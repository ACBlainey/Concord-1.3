# Established Life-Support Reallocation — Aggregation, Withdrawal Authority and Clinical Boundary — Source Resolution 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Date:** 3 October 2026  
**Status:** ACTIVE CROSS-DOMAIN / HEALTH-INTERFACE DEVELOPMENT / SOURCE RESOLUTION / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL  
**Primary interfaces:** Health / Rights / Law / Scarcity Allocation / Withdrawal Authority / Fundamental Aggregation / Emergency / Continuity

## 1. Question

Consider the clean hard case:

- participant A is already receiving life-sustaining support from scarce external resource R;
- A will suffer imminent irreversible fundamental loss if R is withdrawn;
- several other qualifying participants B...N will suffer materially comparable imminent irreversible fundamental loss if R is not reallocated;
- R cannot serve all simultaneously;
- no adequate substitute is presently available;
- treatment/support remains effective for A.

What does existing Concord architecture actually establish?

The question must not be silently replaced by:

- conventional medical triage doctrine;
- utilitarian maximisation;
- first-come-first-served;
- incumbent priority;
- property priority;
- physician discretion;
- “save the greatest number” as an axiom.

The result must be derived from Concord sources.

---

## 2. Health maturity boundary

The V1.3 Health domain currently states:

> **DOMAIN ESTABLISHED / ARCHITECTURE LARGELY UNDEVELOPED**

and:

> **Folder Existence != System Completion**

The Health Development Source Register identifies reusable architectures for:

- participant autonomy;
- supported decision-making;
- diagnostic reasoning;
- bounded clinical/protective authority;
- Contextual Wrapper Architecture;
- outcome learning.

But it explicitly states that the specialist medical operating layer remains undeveloped, including:

- validated clinical knowledge;
- diagnostic criteria;
- treatment/care pathways;
- professional competence/governance;
- service delivery;
- emergency medicine;
- public health;
- health-specific evidence standards.

Therefore this source resolution **cannot define medical futility, prognosis, clinical effectiveness or professional standards**.

Those are Health-domain facts/standards that must later be supplied by validated specialist architecture.

**Aggregation Of Consequence != Aggregation Of Participant Worth**

**Participant Count Can Be Consequence-Relevant**

**Equal Standing Does Not Require Consequence Blindness**

**Greater Beneficiary Count != Automatic Displacement Authority**

### Withdrawal Authority

**Better Aggregate Allocation != Automatic Withdrawal Authority**

**Withdrawal Of Established Support != Mere Absence Of Future Benefit**

**Established Reliance Can Change The Consequence Of Withdrawal**

**Safe Separation Objective != Guarantee Of Harm-Free Separation**

**Best Feasible Separation != Permanent Continuation Until Perfect Alternative Exists**

### Inalienable Participant Standing

Validated:

**Inalienable Standing != Immunity From Legitimate Coercive Authority**

**Standing != Sovereignty Over Law**

**Restriction Of Exercise != Extinction Of Standing**

### Temporal Consent

Validated:

**Past Authority != Continuing Authority**

**Advance Authorisation != Authority To Ignore Present Material Distress**

### Health development sources

Candidate inherited constraints include:

- participant autonomy remains default where required function can be achieved without coercion;
- expertise does not itself create unlimited authority;
- prediction/risk does not itself create authority;
- authority should be minimum necessary, context-specific, provenance-visible and reviewable;
- emergency authority should not silently become ordinary persistent authority.

These are useful authority constraints, not clinical rules.

## 4. Five separate determinations

A life-support reallocation decision must not collapse at least five distinct determinations:

### H1 — Current clinical function

Is support R still materially performing the life/health function for A?

### H2 — Genuine scarcity

Is R genuinely unavailable in sufficient quantity after reasonable ESCP search for alternatives?

### H3 — Comparative allocation consequence

What protected consequences would follow from allocating R to A versus B...N?

### H4 — Withdrawal authority

If allocation favours B...N, what authority exists to terminate A's established support relation?

### H5 — Physical intervention authority

Does removing R require a direct bodily/cognitive intervention that itself needs separate authority?

Therefore:

> **Clinical Function != Scarcity != Allocation != Withdrawal != Direct Intervention**

This is the central decomposition.

---

## 5. Treatment effectiveness must be resolved first

If support no longer performs its intended protected function for A, the case is not the clean aggregation problem.

The support relation may have independently changed or ended.

Therefore:

**Treatment Futility/Non-Function Must Not Be Inferred From Competing Demand**

and:

**Higher Alternative Benefit != Existing Treatment Futility**

Clinical function must be assessed independently.

---

## 6. Prognosis is not worth

Health may eventually establish evidence about:

- likelihood of treatment success;
- expected duration of benefit;
- reversibility;
- treatment burden;
- recovery prospects.

These may alter the consequence model.

But:

**Clinical Prognosis != Participant Worth**

A worse prognosis does not make a participant intrinsically less valuable.

---

## 7. Genuine scarcity must be resolved second

Before considering withdrawal:

- seek another device/resource;
- transfer participants;
- use substitutes;
- share/time-slice where clinically possible;
- expand capacity;
- activate reserve;
- seek external supply;
- alter non-essential resource use;
- examine whether apparent scarcity is configuration-dependent.

Therefore:

**Existing Queue != Proven Irreducible Scarcity**

and:

**Life-Support Withdrawal Must Not Substitute For Reasonable Scarcity Reduction**

---

## 8. Clean comparable case

Only after H1 and H2 are established does the strongest aggregation case appear:

- A's support works;
- B...N would also benefit;
- harms are materially comparable;
- R is genuinely indivisible/scarce;
- no adequate alternatives exist.

Fundamental Aggregation then establishes:

> participant number is a legitimate consequence variable.

But:

> participant number is not a participant-worth variable.

---

## 9. Incumbency

A's incumbency does not mean:

- A is worth more;
- A owns R;
- later claimants have lower standing;
- first possession creates permanent priority.

Therefore:

**First Treatment != Greater Fundamental Standing**

But A's established support means withdrawal is a new consequential act.

Therefore:

**First Treatment != Mere Administrative Position Either**

Incumbency changes the transition, not intrinsic worth.

---

## 10. New claimant symmetry

B...N must not be treated as less real because they arrived later.

**Later Arrival != Lower Participant Standing**

Nor should A be treated as less real because more participants now exist.

The architecture must preserve all claims.

---

## 11. Allocation result is not enough

Suppose aggregation analysis concludes that allocating R to five comparable claimants prevents more equivalent fundamental harms than continuing R for A.

That establishes a strong **allocation reason**.

It does not by itself establish:

> remove R from A now.

Therefore:

**Allocation Preference != Withdrawal Permission**

This is the exact interface exposed by prior testing.

---

## 12. What could supply withdrawal authority?

Possible sources include:

- participant A's valid present consent;
- valid health/law rule governing established life-support reallocation;
- independently justified emergency authority;
- treatment ceasing to function for A;
- safe transfer to adequate substitute;
- another lawful bounded authority source.

At present Concord has not yet developed a canonical Health/Law rule specifically authorising involuntary withdrawal of effective established life support solely because several later claimants could benefit.

Therefore:

> **The current architecture does not yet establish that aggregate consequence alone authorises involuntary withdrawal of effective established life support.**

This is a finding, not a prohibition on future derivation.

---

## 13. Nor does incumbency establish absolute continuation

The reverse conclusion is also unsupported.

Nothing in current Concord architecture establishes:

> once effective life support begins, it may never be reallocated regardless of circumstances.

Withdrawal Authority explicitly rejects permanent entitlement by mere dependency.

Therefore:

> **The current architecture also does not establish an absolute incumbent veto over reallocation.**

The unresolved space is real.

---

## 14. Source-derived present position

For the clean case:

**Aggregation provides a reason for reallocation.**

**Established support creates a separate withdrawal-authority requirement.**

**Current Concord sources do not yet supply a general rule resolving that collision.**

Therefore the correct present classification is:

> **CONSTITUTIONALLY UNDERDETERMINED / REQUIRES HEALTH-LAW AUTHORITY DEVELOPMENT**

This is preferable to importing a hidden rule.

---

## 15. Direct physical withdrawal

Removing life support may require:

- touching participant body;
- removing airway/device;
- stopping infusion;
- disconnecting implanted system;
- altering physiological support.

Therefore:

**Service Withdrawal Can Also Be Direct Physical Intervention**

The authority to stop supplying R may not automatically equal authority to perform every bodily act required to remove R.

---

## 16. Leaving equipment in place

In some cases support can stop without immediate invasive removal.

Example:

- resource flow ceases;
- device remains temporarily in situ.

This may separate:

1. resource reallocation;
2. bodily removal.

Health should determine safe practice.

The architecture should not require harmful physical removal merely to formalise resource ownership.

---

## 17. Participant consent

If A presently and validly consents to withdrawal:

- withdrawal authority becomes much clearer;
- safe transition/palliative/support obligations may remain;
- consent must be informed, competent and non-coerced according to future Health standards.

But:

**Consent To Withdrawal != Obligation To Volunteer**

A must not be morally or institutionally coerced into sacrifice because several others need R.

---

## 18. Refusal

A's refusal to surrender R does not make A selfish, culpable or lower standing.

**Refusal To Die For Others != Misconduct By Default**

This is important for Civil Contact, Health records and Historical provenance.

---

## 19. Pressure to volunteer

The system must not turn aggregate numbers into coercive moral pressure:

> five people will die because of you.

The causal architecture is scarcity, not participant blame.

Therefore:

**Scarcity Outcome != Personal Moral Liability Of Unserved/Incumbent Participant**

and:

**Aggregate Need Must Not Become Coercive Consent Pressure**

---

## 20. Clinician role

Clinical expertise may determine facts such as:

- whether support works;
- clinical compatibility;
- treatment risk;
- prognosis;
- transfer safety.

Expertise does not automatically create constitutional authority to decide whose standing prevails.

Therefore:

**Clinical Expertise != General Allocation Sovereignty**

and:

**Clinical Fact-Finding != Constitutional Authority Creation**

---

## 21. Allocator role

Likewise an allocation body should not determine clinical facts merely to reach its preferred allocation.

The architecture should separate:

**Clinical Assessment**
from
**Scarcity/Allocation Assessment**
from
**Withdrawal Authority**
from
**Review/Adjudication where contested**

Exact institutions remain undeveloped.

---

## 22. Conflict of interest

A clinician responsible for A may have fiduciary/support duties to A.

A system allocator may have population-level duties.

The architecture should not silently merge these roles.

**Different Legitimate Functions Can Require Different Decision Roles**

This is an institutional-design interface.

---

## 23. Emergency timing

If B...N will die within minutes, ordinary review may be impossible.

But:

**Urgency Can Contract Procedure Without Creating New Substantive Authority Values**

Emergency architecture may eventually establish rapid authority.

It must still specify:

- authority source;
- trigger;
- scope;
- provenance;
- review.

---

## 24. Allocation before treatment starts

Suppose A and B...N all arrive before anyone receives R.

There is no established support withdrawal.

Then the problem is cleaner:

**Scarcity Allocation + Fundamental Aggregation**

without Withdrawal Authority.

Where materially comparable, participant count can be consequence-relevant.

Therefore:

**Pre-Initiation Allocation != Established-Support Reallocation**

This distinction is operationally important.

---

## 25. Starting support changes future transition state

If treatment begins for A while B...N are expected imminently, the system should not manipulate timing merely to manufacture incumbency.

**Strategic Initiation != Legitimate Priority Manufacturing**

Likewise delaying A solely to preserve allocator flexibility can itself cause harm.

This requires prospective scarcity protocols.

---

## 26. Temporary trial support

Health may sometimes use time-bounded treatment trials.

A trial must be genuine and clinically/ethically defined in advance.

It cannot simply be a hidden device to make withdrawal easier.

**Time-Bounded Clinical Trial != Rights-Laundering Mechanism**

If conditions for continuation are legitimate, evidence-based and known, the relation may differ from open-ended established support.

---

## 27. Treatment no longer clinically indicated

If validated Health architecture determines treatment no longer serves A's protected health function, termination may derive from clinical function rather than competing demand.

The resource can then enter scarcity allocation separately.

Therefore:

**Independent Treatment Termination -> Resource Availability -> New Allocation**

should remain distinct from:

**New Demand -> Declare A Futile -> Withdraw**

---

## 28. Probability

If A has 90% treatment effectiveness and five claimants each have 20%, simple headcount is insufficient.

Fundamental Aggregation already establishes:

**Expected Outcome Modelling != Fractional Participant Worth**

Health must provide valid clinical probabilities and uncertainty.

Scarcity architecture must keep them explicit rather than inventing worth scores.

---

## 29. Duration

If R preserves A for minutes and B...N for years, duration may be consequence-relevant.

But:

**Expected Duration Of Benefit != Participant Worth**

No universal life-year currency is established by current Concord sources.

---

## 30. Disability

Disability must not be treated as lower standing.

Clinical relevance is limited to actual treatment compatibility/effect where evidence supports it.

**Disability != Lower Allocation Worth**

---

## 31. Age

Age is not a worth score.

Developmental architecture may eventually establish particular protective relations.

But current canonical Ethical Kernel does not establish a simple age multiplier.

**Age != Automatic Fundamental Worth Multiplier**

---

## 32. Social role

A clinician, scientist, parent, official, wealthy donor or high-contribution participant does not receive greater intrinsic allocation worth.

**Social Utility != Participant Worth**

Specific immediate dependency effects may matter separately.

---

## 33. Citizenship and participation level

Fundamental life-support standing should not be silently converted into participation reward.

**Participation Level != Fundamental Life-Support Worth**

and:

**Citizenship != Intrinsic Life Priority**

Any jurisdictional entitlement differences must be separately lawful and cannot redefine standing.

---

## 34. Human / AI / hybrid

The Health domain explicitly adopts substrate-neutral civil function with substrate-specific specialist systems.

Therefore a comparable future case involving:

- biological life support;
- digital cognitive support;
- hybrid support;

must preserve:

**Shared Civil Function Does Not Imply Shared Physical Mechanism**

and:

**Substrate != Participant Worth Class**

Clinical/technical comparability must be established rather than assumed.

---

## 35. Palliative / residual care

If withdrawal is independently authorised and death or major loss is expected, withdrawal should not mean abandonment.

Health should determine appropriate residual care/support.

Cross-domain principle:

**Withdrawal Of One Support Function != Withdrawal Of Participant Standing Or All Care**

---

## 36. Record language

Records should distinguish:

- treatment effectiveness;
- scarcity;
- allocation decision;
- withdrawal authority;
- participant consent/refusal;
- physical withdrawal;
- outcome.

They should not say:

> A was less valuable than B...N.

Instead:

> a bounded resource decision occurred under specified conditions.

**Allocation Outcome != Worth Judgment**

---

## 37. No retrospective moralisation

If A remains on support and five die because no lawful withdrawal authority existed, A should not be recorded as causing their deaths merely by continuing to receive effective support.

Likewise if lawful withdrawal occurs, B...N are not owners of A's sacrifice.

**System Conflict != Participant Moral Guilt**

---

## 38. Need for prospective rules

Because emergency bedside creation of constitutional rules is dangerous, Concord should develop prospective, publicly knowable life-support scarcity protocols.

These should specify:

- clinical fact boundaries;
- scarcity verification;
- allocation factors;
- established-support status;
- withdrawal authority;
- review;
- emergency compression;
- consent;
- documentation;
- reassessment;
- remedy/support.

**Prospective Rule != Automatic Correctness**

But it reduces arbitrary improvisation.

---

## 39. No hidden lottery

If current architecture is underdetermined in a live case, randomisation is not automatically justified merely to avoid deciding.

Lottery is a residual tie-break only after legitimate distinctions are exhausted.

**Constitutional Underdetermination != Automatic Lottery Authority**

---

## 40. No hidden first-come rule

Likewise:

**Constitutional Underdetermination != Automatic Incumbent Priority**

The fact that existing architecture has not yet authorised withdrawal is not the same as proving first-come-first-served is ethically optimal.

Operationally, however, absent lawful withdrawal authority, existing support cannot simply be terminated by assumption.

This is an authority constraint, not a worth judgment.

---

## 41. Current operational implication

Until a valid Concord Health/Law withdrawal rule is developed:

> **A scarcity allocator should not treat a preference to reallocate effective established life support as sufficient authority to perform involuntary withdrawal.**

This protects bounded authority.

It does not constitutionalise permanent incumbent priority.

---

## 42. Candidate future authority question

The next development question is narrower:

> **Can Concord derive a prospective rule that sometimes authorises involuntary withdrawal of effective established life support under genuine scarcity without violating equal standing, anti-instrumentalisation, autonomy, bounded authority or protected-context transition?**

Possible answers remain open:

- yes under tightly bounded conditions;
- no;
- only where support was prospectively conditional;
- only under particular emergency authority;
- only after specified independent adjudication;
- differentiated by treatment/support type.

This document does not pre-decide the answer.

## 46. Strongest current rule

> **Where effective life-sustaining support is already established, genuine scarcity and a better aggregate allocation outcome create a reason to consider reallocation but do not, under current Concord architecture, independently create authority for involuntary withdrawal. Existing support also does not create greater participant worth or an absolute permanent entitlement. The unresolved issue is the lawful source and limits of withdrawal authority in the specific Health context.**

---

## 47. Next development step

Do **not** adversarially test a nonexistent final rule yet.

The next step should be:

**Established Life-Support Reallocation — Candidate Authority Models 001**

That development should construct and compare multiple Concord-compatible candidate models without ranking them prematurely, for example:

1. **Non-Withdrawal Model** — effective established support cannot be involuntarily reallocated solely for aggregate benefit.
2. **Prospectively Conditional Model** — withdrawal can occur only where the support relation was legitimately established under known scarcity/reallocation conditions.
3. **Bounded Emergency Reallocation Model** — independently justified emergency authority can permit withdrawal under strict comparable-harm conditions.
4. **Independent Adjudicative Model** — withdrawal requires rapid independent authority beyond clinician/allocator.
5. **Hybrid Model** — different rules by support type, reversibility, transition mechanism and emergency context.

Each candidate should be tested against Blainey's Laws, IPS, Temporal Consent, Fundamental Aggregation, Withdrawal Authority, bounded authority, anti-instrumentalisation and Health-domain limits.

Only after candidate comparison should a proposed Concord rule be adversarially tested.
