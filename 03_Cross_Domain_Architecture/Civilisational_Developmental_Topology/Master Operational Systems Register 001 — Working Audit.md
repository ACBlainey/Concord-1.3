## Current audit interpretation

The Concord is not accurately described either as a collection of undeveloped domains or as a fully operating civilisation.

Its current state is heterogeneous:
- several systems are mature architectural specifications;
- some have passed substantial adversarial, interface or blind-transfer testing;
- a smaller subset is internally instantiated against the Concord's own development process;
- many specialist civil services still lack their domain-specific operational layer;
- field deployment and longitudinal validation are generally absent.

## Governing development rule

For every residual:

**Existing method -> bounded adaptation/companion -> new mechanism only where no suitable existing architecture supplies the required function.**

Before a high-stakes “missing” classification, apply ESCP and source-resolve relevant V1/V1.1/V1.2/V1.2a provenance.
