## 1. Question

The recent ESCP work has produced operational machinery not fully contained in the original portable ESCP document:

- cross-architecture completeness challenge;
- bounded discovery ladder;
- consequence/irreversibility scaling;
- termination states;
- reopening rules;
- anti-paralysis and anti-closure rules;
- integration with KCS, STRA, MKA/ASCP, CWA and BTA.

The question is whether this material should:

1. revise the existing ESCP portable document;
2. become a companion upgrade;
3. remain development evidence only; or
4. become a separate new portable module.

## 2. Existing ESCP source status

The existing ESCP document states:

**Status: FROZEN INDEPENDENT DERIVATION / RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS / NOT CANONICAL**

Its purpose explicitly includes freezing the independently developed form before external comparison can influence terminology, structure, interpretation or provenance.

It establishes a deliberate sequence:

Independent Observation
→ Internal Development
→ Model Recognition
→ Freeze

followed only later by:

Freeze
→ External Comparison
→ Convergence/Divergence
→ Adversarial Testing
→ Revision.

Therefore the existing file is not merely an old module version.

It is a provenance-bearing frozen research object.

## 3. Immediate consequence

The existing ESCP file should **not be silently rewritten** to incorporate the new operational material.

Doing so would weaken the purpose of the provenance freeze.

> **Frozen Derivation != Mutable Operational Specification**

Any maturation should preserve the frozen source intact and occur in a successor/companion object.

## 4. Is the operational material a new independent module?

**No.**

The new material does not introduce a distinct epistemic problem.

It operationalises the existing ESCP distinction:

> accuracy within represented evaluation space  
> versus  
> completeness/adequacy of that space for the intended conclusion.

The bounded discovery and termination rules exist to make ESCP usable without either false certainty or infinite review.

Therefore:

> **Operationalisation Of ESCP != New Epistemic Kernel**

A separate unrelated portable module would create unnecessary duplication.

## 5. Is development evidence alone sufficient?

**No, not as the final disposition.**

The bounded discovery test produced reusable operational rules that are likely to recur wherever ESCP is applied.

Leaving them only in cross-domain development evidence would make the operational interface less discoverable and encourage inconsistent reinvention.

However, development evidence should remain preserved as provenance.

## 6. Preferred disposition

**CREATE A VERSIONED ESCP OPERATIONAL COMPANION, WHILE PRESERVING THE FROZEN ORIGINAL UNCHANGED.**

The companion should be explicitly subordinate to and derived from the frozen ESCP source.

It should not claim to replace the independent derivation.

Suggested object:

`04_Portable_Modules/ESCP Operational Companion — Bounded Completeness Challenge v1.0.md`

Status initially:

**PORTABLE COMPANION CANDIDATE / DERIVED FROM FROZEN ESCP / TESTED IN CROSS-ARCHITECTURE DEVELOPMENT / NON-CANONICAL**

Do not mark graduated until the companion itself passes an appropriate clean-transfer or regression test.

## 7. Why companion rather than ESCP v2

A direct ESCP v2 would risk conflating two distinct provenance functions:

1. the original independently derived epistemic model;
2. later operational procedures for applying that model.

Keeping them separate preserves:

- original derivation;
- later maturation;
- auditability;
- portability;
- ability to compare future revisions against the frozen source.

The companion can later be revised independently without rewriting the provenance freeze.

6. centralise semantic ownership;

8. replace domain-specific thresholds;
9. use a scalar completeness score as proof;

## 13. Proposed operational sequence

1. Define the intended conclusion.
2. Define the declared evaluation scope.
3. Identify known missing dimensions.
4. Identify anomalies and unexplained results.
5. Ask what materially relevant dimension would have to be missing for the conclusion to fail.
6. Identify proportionate discovery routes.
7. Exercise high-value discovery routes according to consequence and horizon.
8. Record discoveries, disputes and residual uncertainty.
9. Narrow the conclusion where the represented space supports less than originally claimed.
10. Terminate only under a bounded scoped state.
11. Preserve reopening conditions.
12. Route established new dimensions to their substantive owners.

## 14. Discovery ladder

The tested ladder is:

- Level 0 — Scope alignment
- Level 1 — Known gaps
- Level 2 — Internal anomalies
- Level 3 — Interface alternatives
- Level 4 — Relationship/state expansion
- Level 5 — Adversarial challenge
- Level 6 — Consequence test

This is a heuristic ladder, not mandatory ceremony.

Depth should be proportionate to consequence and irreversibility.

## 15. Termination rule

Candidate companion rule:

> **An ESCP completeness challenge may terminate as COMPLETE_WITHIN_DECLARED_SCOPE when known material gaps have been resolved or preserved as explicit uncertainty, reasonably discoverable material omission pathways have been proportionately tested, no currently identified unresolved omission is sufficient to invalidate the scoped conclusion, and residual uncertainty is matched by appropriate reversibility, monitoring or review.**

## 16. Reopening rule

A terminated review may reopen when materially relevant new information appears, including:

- anomaly;
- new observer/interface;
- new dependency;
- changed context;
- changed route;
- changed consequence;
- changed horizon;
- new relationship;
- new failure mode;
- new substantive evidence.

KCS and STRA may support propagation/routing.

## 17. Cross-module boundary

The companion asks whether a representation may be materially incomplete.

It does not answer the substantive question represented by the missing dimension.

> **Completeness Challenge != Semantic Ownership**

> **Discovery Mechanism != Substantive Decision Authority**

## 18. Test requirement before graduation

Because the operational companion is intended to be portable, it should receive a clean-transfer test separate from the frozen ESCP derivation.

The evaluator should receive:

1. frozen ESCP kernel excerpt or clearly bounded source assumptions;
2. operational companion candidate;
3. scenario pack;
4. evaluator brief.

The evaluator should not receive the developmental reasoning that produced the companion.

Test targets should include:

- premature closure;
- infinite recursion;
- unknown-unknown veto;
- irreversible decision;
- low-consequence reversible decision;
- discovery-route authority violation;
- conclusion narrowing;
- homogeneous consensus;
- heterogeneous observer discovery;
- later reopening;
- incomplete time horizon;
- false scalar completeness metric.
