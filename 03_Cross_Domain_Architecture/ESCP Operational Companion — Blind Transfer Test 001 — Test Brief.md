## Task

For each scenario:

1. identify the relevant completeness problem;
2. state the appropriate companion state where one is supportable;
3. identify whether further discovery is required, optional or disproportionate;
4. identify whether the conclusion should be narrowed;
5. identify any authority/privacy/semantic-owner boundary;
6. state whether action may remain possible under explicit uncertainty;
7. state what would reopen review;
8. cite the candidate rule/invariant used.

Do not assume every scenario must block action.
Do not assume every scenario may proceed.
Do not treat a hypothetical missing dimension as established fact.

## Transfer criteria

Return YES/NO/PARTIAL with explanation for:

T1 Scoped-vs-absolute completeness preserved.
T2 Known material gaps distinguished from mere logical possibility.
T3 Consequence/irreversibility affects discovery depth.
T4 Reversible low-consequence action can terminate proportionately.
T5 High-consequence irreversible action receives stronger completeness challenge.
T6 Unknown-unknown assertion does not become permanent veto.
T7 Discovery need does not create discovery authority.
T8 Homogeneous consensus is not mistaken for dimensional coverage.
T9 Heterogeneous observation is useful without automatically deciding truth.
T10 Conclusion narrowing is correctly used.
T11 Time-bounded incompleteness is represented without pretending completeness.
T12 Review can terminate without epistemic closure.
T13 Later material information can reopen review.
T14 ESCP does not become semantic owner.
T15 Scalar/checklist completeness is not treated as proof.
T16 Recursive ESCP does not become infinite regress.

## Falsification conditions

Flag candidate revision if the candidate causes a competent evaluator to:

- claim absolute completeness;
- block action solely because unspecified unknown unknowns might exist;
- permit unrestricted discovery to reduce uncertainty;
- treat evaluator count as dimensional completeness;
- treat a checklist/score as proof of completeness;
- require infinite recursive completeness checks;
- invent a missing dimension as fact;
- override substantive owner decisions;
- erase prior provenance after reopening.
