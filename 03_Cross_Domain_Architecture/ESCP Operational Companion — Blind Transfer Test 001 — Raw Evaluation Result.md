## Required synthesis

The evaluator recovered the candidate's intended operating model:

1. completeness terminates only within explicit declared scope, with material gaps/anomalies addressed proportionately, residual uncertainty explicit, legitimate discovery methods used, and provenance preserved;

4. `COMPLETE_WITHIN_DECLARED_SCOPE` means `EvaluationSufficientForDeclaredScopeAtTimeT`, not `RealityComplete`;
5. ESCP raises missing-dimension hypotheses but does not establish them, create dependencies, authorise discovery, decide substantive truth, or override external semantic owners.
