## Purpose

This companion returns operational refinements exposed by portable blind transfer while preserving the CBERRM kernel:

> **Make the consequence legible; keep responsibility, authority and remedy separate.**

## 1. Candidate opening

Opening an externality candidate is a triage action.

> **Candidate Opened ≠ Escalation Required**

Evidence and materiality determine whether further process is justified.

## 2. Consequence-relative boundary mapping

One incident may contain several intersecting boundaries.

For each claimed consequence identify:
- primary evaluated boundary: the boundary across which that consequence is alleged to propagate;
- secondary boundaries materially relevant to causation, standing, authority or response.

> **One Incident ≠ One Universal Primary Boundary**

## 3. Scoped standing

Where material:

**Standing = <Party, Role, Scope, SourceOfAuthority, Limits, State>**

Roles may include directly affected, authorised representative, consultation/advocacy standing, evidence-bearing third party and disputed/unknown standing.

Authority to raise a concern does not imply authority to receive protected information, negotiate, settle, bind others or accept compensation unless separately granted.

> **Standing to Raise ≠ Authority to Decide**

## 4. Assertion versus case disposition

A claim within a case may be UNSUPPORTED, OVERSTATED, REFUTED or SUPERSEDED without automatically closing the underlying externality case.

> **Assertion Disposition ≠ Case Disposition**

> **Unsupported Assertion ≠ Automatically Separate Externality**

A new assertion becomes a separate externality candidate only where a separately alleged material consequence crosses a boundary.

## 5. Contextual materiality

Materiality is assessed against a declared context/function.

Where closure is not justified, preserve:

**MATERIALITY-UNCERTAIN**

A useful record is:

**MaterialityAssessment = <Consequence, Context, Threshold/Function, State, Evidence, Uncertainty>**

> **One Consequence ≠ One Universal Materiality State**

CBERRM does not invent domain thresholds.

## 6. Causation and responsibility

Permit causal states such as:
- POSSIBLE-CONTRIBUTOR;
- PLAUSIBLE-CONTRIBUTOR;
- SUPPORTED-CONTRIBUTOR;
- UNKNOWN/DISPUTED.

Contributors may be actors, non-agent environmental/system conditions or unidentified sources.

> **Causal Contributor ≠ Responsible Party**

Normative responsibility requires the applicable additional basis.

> **Responsibility Mapping ≠ Quantitative Apportionment**

Where quantitative/legal/financial apportionment is required, hand off to a legitimate domain method.

## 7. Dimensional case state

Evidence/consequence, responsibility, authority/coordination, participation and disposition are different dimensions.

Multiple scoped states may coexist.

Where unresolved, identify the dimension where possible:

**UNRESOLVED[dimension]**

> **Multiple Dimension Classifications ≠ Contradictory Case States**

Portable CB-C identifiers remain optional implementation vocabulary.

## 8. Actor-relative minimum response

Where no shared authority exists, “minimum response” is not selected by an imaginary central sovereign.

Each participant may choose only actions independently legitimate within its own authority.

Shared action requires a legitimate shared interface.

> **Minimum Necessary Response ≠ Manufactured Shared Authority**

## 9. Parallel voluntary coordination

Compatible independently chosen actions may be recorded as:

**PARALLEL-VOLUNTARY-COORDINATION**

This records practical alignment without implying shared government, subordination or formal institution.

## 10. Non-participation

Refusal/non-participation should be recorded only where a relevant participation pathway exists or is offered and the party affirmatively declines or does not participate under the applicable definition.

> **Passive Absence ≠ Refusal**

> **Refusal ≠ Exoneration**

> **Refusal ≠ Automatic Authority Over Refuser**

## 11. Review triggers

For consequential cases record, where useful:

**ReviewTrigger = <Condition/Event, AffectedField, RequiredReview, Provenance>**

Triggers may include new evidence, materiality change, standing change, authority change, consequence change, recurrence or outcome evidence.

Review changes case state without erasing prior provenance.

## 12. Interface ownership

- **FBS** may host bounded negotiation.
- **KCS** may supply evidence/provenance/dependency state.
- **CWA/FPA** may represent contextual interfaces and permissions.
- **Rights Translation/Recognition** may supply specialised standing/rights translation.
- **Judiciary/Governance** applies only where legitimate jurisdiction already exists.

CBERRM does not absorb these functions.

## 13. Authority boundary

This companion does not create jurisdiction, enforcement power, compensation authority, universal materiality thresholds, quantitative apportionment, representative authority, or general authority from a bounded consequence.

## 14. ESCP

> **No Established Contributor ≠ No Contributor Exists**

> **No Shared Authority Found ≠ Proof No Legitimate Interface Exists**

Preserve UNKNOWN/DISPUTED where the evaluation space is incomplete.
