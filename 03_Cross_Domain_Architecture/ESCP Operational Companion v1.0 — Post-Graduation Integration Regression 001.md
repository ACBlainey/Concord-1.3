# ESCP Operational Companion v1.0 — Post-Graduation Integration Regression 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework
**Date:** 3 October 2026
**Status:** POST-GRADUATION REGRESSION / COMPLETE
**Module:** ESCP Operational Companion — Bounded Completeness Challenge v1.0
**Parent:** Evaluation-Space Completeness Problem — frozen independent derivation
**Related architectures:** MKA/ASCP, KCS Change Propagation, STRA, CWA, BTA

## 1. Purpose

Confirm that graduation of the ESCP Operational Companion did not accidentally:

- revise the frozen parent ESCP;
- duplicate or displace ASCP;
- make ESCP a semantic owner;
- make ESCP an authority source;
- make KCS or STRA subordinate to ESCP;
- convert contextual or transition completeness into universal ESCP ownership;
- introduce permanent blocking through unknown unknowns;
- create an unbounded recursive audit requirement.

## 2. Provenance boundary

The frozen parent ESCP remains the source of the epistemic distinction:

> **Accuracy Within Evaluation Space != Completeness Of Evaluation Space**

The graduated companion supplies a bounded operational procedure.

The exact tested candidate remains preserved separately.

**Regression result: PASS**

No provenance overwrite is required.

## 3. MKA / ASCP regression

ASCP remains the specialised authority-space completeness mechanism.

ESCP Companion may ask whether the represented authority space could omit a materially required authority dimension.

It cannot:

- create the missing authority;
- declare an authority legitimate;
- compose authorities;
- override MKA;
- convert uncertainty into permission.

Invariant:

> **Completeness Challenge != Authority Creation**

ASCP remains inside MKA.

**Regression result: PASS**

## 4. KCS Change Propagation regression

The companion may generate a candidate missing-dependency hypothesis.

KCS remains responsible for represented dependency relations and bounded material change propagation.

Sequence remains:

ESCP challenge
→ candidate missing dependency
→ source-grounded investigation
→ KCS update if established
→ candidate downstream review
→ substantive owner re-evaluation.

Invariant:

> **Candidate Missing Dependency != Established Dependency**

**Regression result: PASS**

## 5. STRA regression

STRA may trigger or route reopening of an ESCP completeness review.

ESCP does not own trigger semantics.

> **Review Trigger != Completeness Outcome**

**Regression result: PASS**

## 8. CBPR boundary

## 9. Semantic ownership regression

The companion's common question is intentionally cross-cutting.

That does not make ESCP a universal ontology or master graph.

A missing legal dimension belongs to legal/authority owners.
A missing dependency belongs to the relevant dependency/substantive owners.
A missing context belongs to contextual owners.
A missing transition state belongs to transition/substantive owners.

Invariant:

> **Shared Completeness Question; Distributed Semantic Answers**

**Regression result: PASS**

## 10. Anti-paralysis regression

The companion preserves:

> **Logical Possibility != Operationally Material Completeness Gap**

and:

> **Unspecified Unknown Unknown != Permanent Decision Veto**

Therefore ESCP cannot be invoked merely by asserting that something unknowable might exist.

Materiality, plausibility, consequence and proportionate discovery remain necessary.

**Regression result: PASS**

## 16. S24 clarification containment

Blind Transfer Test 001 identified one non-blocking clarification:

an irreversible evidence-destroying action with no currently identified gap would benefit from an explicit consolidated rule requiring stronger completeness justification and evidence-preservation review.

The tested v1.0 already produced the intended result through existing consequence, irreversibility, preservation and review rules.

Therefore:

- no v1.0 hotfix is required;
- no graduation rollback is required;
- no semantic defect is established;
- the clarification should be recorded as a future revision candidate.

Candidate future wording:

> **Where an action is highly irreversible and would destroy or materially reduce future corrective evidence, absence of a currently identified gap does not by itself justify treating the evaluation space as sufficiently complete; proportionate evidence-preservation and alternative-route review should be performed where reasonably possible.**

This wording is **not part of v1.0**.
