# Mental Health Exceptional Authority — Health-Law-Judiciary Handoff 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework
**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Status:** CROSS-DOMAIN INTERFACE / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL
**Domains:** Health / Law / Judiciary / Civil Security
**Source:** Exceptional Mental Health Intervention — Decision Authorship, Authority Separation and Restoration Architecture 001

## 1. Purpose

This interface prevents exceptional Mental Health cases from collapsing clinical expertise, legal authority and independent review into one institution.

> **Clinical Expertise != Legal Sovereignty**

## 2. Functional ownership

### Health owns
- clinical assessment;
- differential diagnosis;
- decision-authorship/capacity evidence;
- treatment indication;
- treatment risks/alternatives;
- clinical monitoring;
- restoration/recovery evidence;
- clinical records and learning signals.

### Law owns
- the general substantive rule defining when civil liberty or other protected interests may be restricted;
- permitted purposes;
- authority scope;
- prohibited purposes;
- maximum durations/renewal rules;
- legal safeguards and remedies.

### Judiciary owns
- independent resolution of contested continuing authority where required;
- review of whether legal predicates are actually satisfied;
- challenge/appeal;
- remedy for unlawful exercise where established.

### Civil Security owns
- protection of other participants/public where its own lawful predicates are satisfied;
- execution of relevant lawful protective orders/functions.

### Participant retains
- primary self-authority except where a current independently legitimate bounded authority applies.

## 3. Handoff rule

**Health Fact -> Law Predicate Evaluation -> Authority Decision -> Intervention -> Review -> Revalidation/Sunset**

Health does not create the Law predicate by recommending an outcome.

Law does not manufacture the clinical fact needed to satisfy its predicate.

Judiciary does not replace clinical expertise; it evaluates whether the evidence and legal requirements support continuing authority.

> **Evidence Supplier != Automatic Authority Holder**

## 4. Emergency bridge

Immediate Emergency Health may precede formal Law/Judiciary review where its existing predicates are satisfied.

But:

> **Emergency Bridge != Permanent Jurisdiction Transfer**

Once time permits, any continuing liberty restriction must move to the proper legal/review route or end.

## 5. Involuntary assessment handoff

If Law creates a bounded compulsory-assessment authority, it should predefine:
- qualifying protective purpose;
- evidence threshold;
- maximum initial duration;
- permitted assessment scope;
- information access;
- transport/location rules;
- review trigger;
- exit criteria.

Health then evaluates facts within that envelope.

> **Compulsory Assessment Must Be Capable Of Ending In A Finding Of Capacity**

## 6. Continuing detention handoff

Continuing detention/location restriction requires a current Law basis distinct from mere treatment recommendation.

Health may provide evidence about clinical state, risk and authorship.

The legal decision must separately establish that the applicable detention predicates are met.

> **Clinical Recommendation To Remain != Legal Authority To Require Remaining**

## 7. Treatment handoff

Even lawful detention does not automatically authorise treatment.

Treatment authority must identify its own consent/substitute/emergency/legal basis.

> **Lawful Detention != Blanket Clinical Consent**

## 8. Review independence

As duration/intrusion increases, review should become increasingly independent from the initiating institution.

The reviewer must be institutionally and procedurally capable of finding:
- C0/C1 restored/established;
- legal threshold no longer met;
- intervention disproportionate;
- less restrictive route adequate;
- evidence unreliable;
- institutional conflict;
- authority expired.

> **Review That Cannot End Authority Is Not Meaningful Review**

## 9. Evidence packet

A contested-authority review should receive a bounded packet containing:
- exact claimed legal authority;
- exact current intervention;
- current decision-authorship state and scope;
- supporting evidence;
- material contrary evidence;
- participant account;
- relevant clinical differential;
- current risk/time horizon;
- alternatives considered;
- intervention history;
- restraint/seclusion history if any;
- review/sunset dates;
- conflicts of interest;
- advance preferences where relevant.

The packet should not expose unrelated Health information merely because the participant is under review.

> **Judicial Need != General Medical Record Access**

## 10. Mirrored review

For contested authority, use mirrored propositions:

**H1 — current predicates justify the proposed/continuing restriction**

**H2 — current predicates do not justify the restriction / a less restrictive route is adequate / capacity or facts have changed**

Neutral evidence remains neutral.

> **Burden To Review Authority != Burden On Participant To Prove Normality**

## 11. Participant representation

Where consequential liberty restriction is contested, the architecture should support meaningful participant representation/advocacy.

Exact legal representation rules remain Law/Judiciary work.

Support must not be structurally dependent on persuading the participant to accept the institution's clinical conclusion.

## 12. State changes

Material change should propagate through KCS/STRA-style review:

**Clinical State Change
-> Capacity/Authorship Reassessment Where Relevant
-> Law Predicate Re-evaluation
-> Intervention Revalidation
-> Judiciary Review If Required
-> Authority Contract/Change/End**

> **Changed Clinical State Can Invalidate Legal Authority Without Any New Court Finding Being Needed To Create The Fact**

Operational rules must determine how rapidly formal release/change follows.

## 13. Public protection

If risk to others is present, Civil Security/Law authority may coexist with Health authority.

The two must remain separately legible.

A participant may regain C0/C1 capacity while still being subject to an independently lawful public-protection restriction.

> **Capacity Restoration != Automatic Extinction Of Independent Third-Party Protection Authority**

Conversely, mental diagnosis is not required for ordinary public-protection law.

## 14. No institutional laundering

Prohibited patterns include:
- Health calling detention "treatment" to avoid legal review;
- Law calling punishment "clinical care";
- Civil Security using Health diagnosis as substitute for threat evidence;
- Judiciary treating diagnosis as automatic incapacity;
- Health extending restriction because legal review is administratively delayed.

> **Function Must Not Be Renamed To Escape Its Safeguards**

## 16. Result

The exceptional Mental Health branch now has a clean institutional topology:

**Health describes and treats.
Law defines civil authority.
Judiciary reviews contested authority.
Civil Security protects others under its own authority.
The participant retains all authority not legitimately displaced.**

The next Law development should specify candidate predefined envelopes for compulsory assessment and detention rather than leaving those powers implicit.
