# Scarce Resource Allocation — Portable Module v1.0

**Project:** The Concord Framework
**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Status:** PORTABLE MODULE / v1.0 / PMEDG GRADUATION CANDIDATE
**Primary extraction sources:** Fundamental Aggregation; Established Life-Support Reallocation Source Resolution; ESCP; Bounded Contextual Authority; Multi-Key Authority; Constitutional Emergency; Resource Stewardship
**Scope:** substrate-neutral allocation of genuinely scarce rival resources among legitimate competing claims
**Explicit exclusion:** this module does not itself create withdrawal authority over an already established support relation.

## 1. Purpose

Scarcity decisions are vulnerable to hidden shortcuts: a queue can be mistaken for genuine scarcity; an allocation preference for authority; participant count for participant worth; incumbency for ownership; expertise for sovereignty; urgency for unlimited discretion; or a convenient metric for a complete evaluation space.

This module provides a portable sequence for evaluating and allocating a genuinely scarce resource without silently manufacturing participant-worth rankings or authority.

## 2. Core decomposition

A scarcity problem should separate at least:

1. Claim/need state — what legitimate claims or protected consequences exist?
2. Resource function — what can the resource actually do for each claimant?
3. Scarcity state — is supply genuinely insufficient after reasonable alternatives are examined?
4. Comparative consequence — what happens under plausible allocations?
5. Allocation rule/decision — which allocation is supported?
6. Transition/withdrawal — does implementation require terminating an established relation?
7. Direct intervention — does implementation cross another protected boundary?
8. Review/reassessment — have resource state, claims or alternatives changed?

> **Need != Scarcity != Allocation != Withdrawal != Direct Intervention**

## 3. Scarcity must be established

Before imposing fundamental loss, proportionately examine additional supply, substitutes, transfer, sharing/time-slicing where functionally possible, reserve capacity, external supply, capacity expansion, repair/recovery, demand timing, configuration changes, reduction of non-essential competing use, and whether another resource can satisfy the same function.

> **Existing Queue != Proven Irreducible Scarcity**

> **Allocation Conflict Should Not Substitute For Reasonable Scarcity Reduction**

ESCP applies directly: correct allocation inside an incomplete resource space can still be the wrong evaluation. The search burden should be proportionate to consequence and decision horizon.

## 4. Scarcity is contextual and temporal

A scarcity state should represent resource, quantity/capacity, time window, functional constraints, claimant set, substitutes, expansion options, uncertainty, expected future supply/demand, current commitments/established uses and review time.

> **Scarcity Is Contextual And Temporal, Not Merely A Property Of An Object**

## 5. Standing and aggregation

Existing Concord sources establish:

> **Aggregation Of Consequence != Aggregation Of Participant Worth**

> **Participant Count Can Be Consequence-Relevant**

> **Equal Standing Does Not Require Consequence Blindness**

> **Greater Beneficiary Count != Automatic Displacement Authority**

Consequences may therefore be compared without constructing a hierarchy of intrinsic participant value.

## 6. Legitimate consequence variables

Depending on the function, relevant variables may include whether the resource can perform the required function, probability of benefit, magnitude/severity of prevented harm, time sensitivity, duration of functional benefit where relevant, reversibility, substitutes, resource consumption, number affected, uncertainty, downstream dependency and recoverability/reusability.

> **Relevant Consequence Variable != Participant Worth Variable**

> **Expected Outcome Modelling != Fractional Participant Worth**

A participant with a 20% probability of benefit is not worth 0.2 participants. The probability describes uncertainty about allocation consequence.

## 7. Headcount

Where consequences are materially comparable, participant number can matter. But:

> **Headcount != Universal Allocation Algorithm**

And even where aggregation favours one allocation:

> **Allocation Preference != Withdrawal Permission**

## 8. Incumbency and later claimants

> **First Use != Greater Fundamental Standing**

> **Incumbency Can Change Transition State Without Changing Participant Worth**

> **Later Arrival != Lower Participant Standing**

Established use may create reliance, transition and withdrawal-authority questions without creating greater intrinsic worth.

## 9. Hidden worth proxies

Unless independently relevant to actual resource function and constitutionally legitimate, allocation should not use wealth, political influence, donor status, social prestige, occupation/status, citizenship as intrinsic worth, participation level as intrinsic worth, substrate, disability as general worth, age as a general worth multiplier, commercial purchasing power, clinician personal preference or administrative convenience.

Existing source rules include:

> **Disability != Lower Allocation Worth**
> **Age != Automatic Fundamental Worth Multiplier**
> **Social Utility != Participant Worth**
> **Participation Level != Fundamental Life-Support Worth**
> **Citizenship != Intrinsic Life Priority**
> **Substrate != Participant Worth Class**

A characteristic may still matter where it directly changes validated functional consequence; that must be represented as function, not disguised worth.

## 10. Resource stewardship

Stewardship can require avoiding preventable waste, recovery/reuse where safe, matching resource grade to need, reserves, capacity planning and learning from recurring bottlenecks.

> **Resource Stewardship != Authority To Devalue Participants**

## 11. Pre-initiation versus established support

> **Pre-Initiation Allocation != Established-Support Reallocation**

Once a resource is materially supporting a participant or protected function, reallocation may require a new consequential transition.

> **Better Aggregate Allocation != Automatic Withdrawal Authority**

> **Withdrawal Of Established Support != Mere Absence Of Future Benefit**

> **Established Reliance Can Change The Consequence Of Withdrawal**

## 12. Withdrawal boundary

Where implementation requires terminating established support, invoke an independently legitimate withdrawal/transition authority.

> **Allocation Decision != Withdrawal Authority**

If no such authority exists, report the unresolved authority state rather than manufacture it. This is a deliberate portable boundary.

## 13. Direct intervention boundary

> **Resource Reallocation Authority != Automatic Direct Intervention Authority**

Multi-Key Authority should represent separately protected boundaries where required.

## 14. Expertise and roles

Experts determine relevant functional facts such as compatibility, probability, risk, expected performance, substitutes and transition consequences. Expertise does not automatically create the allocation rule.

> **Expertise != General Allocation Sovereignty**

> **Technical Fact-Finding != Authority Creation**

Where consequence is high, separate technical/domain assessment, scarcity verification, allocation reasoning, withdrawal/transition authority and review/adjudication.

> **Different Legitimate Functions Can Require Different Decision Roles**

## 15. Conflict of interest

Allocation should expose material conflicts such as allocator benefit, supplier-created artificial scarcity, ownership of competing resources, conflicting fiduciary roles and political/commercial pressure.

> **Allocation Authority != Permission To Conceal Allocation Conflict**

## 16. Urgency

> **Urgency Can Contract Procedure Without Creating New Substantive Authority Values**

> **Emergency Need != Scarcity Priority**

Emergency architecture may accelerate the route; it does not manufacture the allocation basis.

## 17. Strategic manipulation

> **Strategic Initiation != Legitimate Priority Manufacturing**

Timing or state should not be manipulated merely to create incumbency, preserve allocator flexibility or manufacture queue priority.

## 18. Time-bounded trials

> **Time-Bounded Trial != Rights-Laundering Mechanism**

A trial allocation should serve a real functional/evidential purpose and have prospectively legitimate continuation conditions.

## 19. No hidden first-come rule or lottery

> **Underdetermination != Automatic First-Come Priority**

> **Underdetermination != Automatic Lottery Authority**

If legitimate distinctions are exhausted, enter an explicit tie state. A prospectively justified neutral tie-break may include randomisation, rotation, queue, shared/time-divided use or another rule.

> **Tie-Break Method Should Be Explicit, Not Smuggled In As Neutrality**

## 20. Prospective rules and reassessment

High-consequence recurring scarcity should use prospective, discoverable, reviewable rules where possible.

> **Prospective Rule != Automatic Correctness**

Reassess when supply, claimant state, substitutes, probability/effectiveness, duration, recovery or uncertainty materially changes.

> **Past Allocation != Permanent Allocation Authority**

KCS/STRA can route material change to review.

## 21. Record structure

A consequential allocation record should distinguish resource/function, claimant state, technical facts, scarcity evidence, alternatives searched, consequence comparison, allocation rule, allocation decision, authority, established-use state, withdrawal/transition state, conflicts, uncertainty, tie-break, review time and outcome.

> **Allocation Outcome != Worth Judgment**

## 22. Participant blame

> **Scarcity Outcome != Personal Moral Liability Of A Claimant**

> **System Conflict != Participant Moral Guilt**

Aggregate need must not become coercive pressure to volunteer an established protected resource relation.

## 23. Portable allocation sequence

**Need/Claims -> Resource Function -> ESCP Scarcity Search -> Genuine Scarcity State -> Legitimate Consequence Variables -> Comparative Consequence -> Allocation Rule -> Allocation Decision -> Authority/Transition Check -> Implement If Independently Authorised -> Observe -> Reassess On Material Change -> Record/Learn**

At the critical seam:

> **Allocation Preference -> [NOT AUTOMATICALLY] Withdrawal Permission**

## 24. Candidate state model

`RESOURCE_AVAILABLE`, `APPARENT_SCARCITY`, `SCARCITY_SEARCH_ACTIVE`, `SCARCITY_RESOLVED`, `GENUINE_SCARCITY`, `CLAIMS_INCOMPLETE`, `ALLOCATION_EVALUATION`, `ALLOCATION_PREFERENCE`, `MATERIAL_TIE`, `ALLOCATION_SELECTED`, `TRANSITION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`, `WITHDRAWAL_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`, `DIRECT_INTERVENTION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`, `AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED`, `IMPLEMENTABLE`, `IMPLEMENTED`, `REVIEW_DUE`, `CLOSED`.

## 26. Domain portability

The kernel can apply to intensive-care equipment, medicines, emergency transport, shelter, food/water, electrical capacity, compute, communications bandwidth, repair capability, evacuation capacity, specialist personnel time and manufacturing capacity. Each domain supplies its own functional facts, consequence measures and authority sources.

## 27. Explicit non-claims

This module does not establish a universal numerical utility function, universal QALY/life-year rule, participant-worth score, canonical lottery rule, canonical first-come rule, involuntary life-support withdrawal authority, clinical futility, property law, emergency sovereignty or domain-specific technical standards.

## 28. PMEDG status

The extracted kernel appears substantially portable because its central distinctions are not Health-specific. Before graduation it should undergo source traceability review, non-Health adversarial tests, clean-instance CRADP testing, authority-boundary regression (especially established-support withdrawal), and ESCP completeness testing.

Until then it remains a **PMEDG graduation candidate**.

## 29. Compact rule

> **First prove the scarcity, then compare legitimate consequences without converting them into participant worth, select an allocation under an explicit rule, and separately verify every authority required to implement it.**
