## Purpose

The parent MNC architecture already contains the substantive mechanism. Independent portable transfer supports four bounded operational clarifications.

## 1. Capability floor and capability boundary

Use these terms distinctly:

> **Capability Floor = Minimum Positive Capability Required for Sufficient Function**

> **Capability Boundary/Ceiling = Maximum Justified Extent and Relevant Exclusions**

A complete capability design requires both.

Restrictions such as no delegation, no unrelated data access or no persistence are normally boundary/ceiling conditions rather than positive elements of the capability floor.

This preserves:

> **Minimum Necessary ≠ Minimum Possible**

Under-capability and over-capability remain separate failures.

## 2. Operational discretion and capability expansion

A participant may require judgement in how to exercise an already established capability.

That does not imply authority to enlarge that capability.

> **Operational Discretion Within Capability ≠ Authority to Expand Capability**

Where additional capability is required:

**Changed Function / Conditions → Additional Need → Additional Justification → Minimum Additional Capability**

This applies especially to professional, AI-agent and emergency contexts.

## 3. Logging and legitimacy

Logs, audit trails and provenance records can establish what was granted, assumed or exercised and support later accountability.

They do not themselves establish legitimacy.

> **Logging Capability Exercise ≠ Legitimising Capability Exercise**

A fully logged self-grant can still be unjustified.

A retrospective record cannot substitute for a legitimate basis where prospective justification was reasonably possible.

## 4. Activation-time aggregate review

The parent architecture already requires:

> **Minimum Local Capability ≠ Minimum Systemic Capability**

Portable transfer shows that timing matters.

Where reasonably feasible, before or at activation of consequential capability inspect known:
- overlapping roles;
- existing credentials;
- delegated access;
- persistent permissions;
- linked accounts;
- other capability paths that materially combine with the proposed grant.

> **Locally Bounded New Capability + Existing Capability May Produce Immediate Aggregate Excess**

This is a reasonable-known-capability check, not an omniscience requirement. Hidden or unknown capability remains a review and verification risk.

## 5. Preserved architecture

Nothing here changes:
- Purpose → Function → Need → Minimum Sufficient Capability;
- multidimensional bounding;
- legitimate-basis requirements;
- protected-rights/interests constraints;
- local/systemic analysis;
- formal/effective capability;
- responsibility/capability coupling;
- escalation;
- expiry and termination;
- anti-capture;
- assumed-authority safeguards.

## 6. Evidential boundary

These clarifications arise from one successful blind AI transfer test.

They do not establish:
- objectively correct floors or ceilings;
- empirical reduction of abuse;
- legal compliance;
- cybersecurity effectiveness;
- institutional-performance benefit;
- human usability;
- cross-cultural validity;
- superiority to least-privilege, proportionality or zero-trust frameworks.
