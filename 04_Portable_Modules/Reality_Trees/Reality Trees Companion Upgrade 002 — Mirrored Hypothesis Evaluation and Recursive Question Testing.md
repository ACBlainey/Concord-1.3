## Purpose

This companion adds a bounded operational extension to the existing Reality Trees method.

The parent Reality Trees module already provides possibility-space branching, evidence and uncertainty, separate weighting dimensions, recursive decomposition, testing, revision, branch disposition and root-question reformulation.

This companion does not replace or restate that method.

### Execution boundary

The **compact mirrored operational cycle in this companion is executable from this document alone**. A user does not need the parent Reality Trees module, Blaineyan Reasoning or ESCP merely to construct and use supporting, opposing and neutral/unresolved structures, preserve evidence dependency and proposition scope, revise or abandon hypotheses, or apply the contradiction ladder described here.

The parent Reality Trees module supplies the fuller general-purpose branching architecture, including capabilities outside this companion's deliberately narrow scope. Companion Upgrade 001 supplies additional operational clarification for the parent architecture. Blaineyan Reasoning and ESCP are compatible external interfaces, not hidden dependencies.

Consult the parent architecture when the task requires capabilities this companion does not specify—for example, fuller general Reality Tree construction or parent-specific weighting/status machinery. If such capability is unavailable, do not invent it and attribute it to this companion.

Therefore:

> **Parent Compatibility != Parent Dependency For The Compact Mirrored Cycle**

and:

> **Executable Companion != Complete Restatement Of Parent Architecture**

It adds an explicit **mirrored hypothesis-evaluation form** for situations where a material hypothesis should be actively tested both for and against, and where persistent contradiction or poor fit may indicate that the hypothesis space, evaluation boundary or root question itself requires reconsideration.

The compact principle is:

> **Do not merely ask how strongly a hypothesis is supported. Build enough structure to discover when the hypothesis, hypothesis space or question is wrong.**

---

## 1. When to use the mirrored form

Use the mirrored form where one or more of the following apply:

- premature commitment to a working hypothesis would be consequential;
- confirmation bias is a material risk;
- evidence both supports and challenges the same hypothesis;
- repeated observations may share hidden dependencies;
- several explanations compete or coexist;
- a working hypothesis must remain capable of abandonment;
- the represented alternatives may not span the real explanation space;
- the root question may contain a hidden assumption;
- the evaluated system boundary may exclude relevant causes.

Not every Reality Tree requires mirroring.

> **Mirrored Evaluation Is An Optional Operational Form, Not A Mandatory Reality Tree Structure.**

---

## 2. The mirrored hypothesis unit

For a material hypothesis (H), maintain three explicit evidence states:

### Supporting structure

Evidence, assumptions and intermediate propositions that support:

> **H materially explains the observed state.**

### Opposing structure

Evidence, assumptions and intermediate propositions that support:

> **H does not adequately explain the observed state, is false in the relevant respect, or requires material revision.**

### Neutral / unresolved structure

Evidence that:
- presently supports neither side materially;
- is ambiguous;
- is reliability-limited;
- has unresolved provenance;
- affects several hypotheses without discriminating between them;
- or cannot yet be interpreted safely.

Therefore:

> **Supporting Tree Alone != Complete Evaluation**

and:

> **Evidence != Necessarily Supporting Or Opposing**

The structures may be visual trees, graphs, tables, nested text or another representation that preserves the relationships.

---

## 3. Do not treat the mirror as a forced binary

The opposing structure is not necessarily the logical claim that one specific alternative is true.

Evidence against (H) may support:
- another hypothesis;
- several alternatives;
- a compound explanation;
- an unknown explanation;
- a changed system state;
- or only the conclusion that (H) presently fits poorly.

Therefore:

> **Not H != Automatically Alternative A**

and:

> **Evidence Supports A != Evidence Excludes B**

Two or more hypotheses may coexist.

> **A True != B False**

This matters especially where multiple causes, conditions, actors or mechanisms can be simultaneously real.

---

## 4. Proposition-scope discipline

Evidence should be attached to the proposition it actually supports.

For example:

**Observation E -> Supports Intermediate Proposition X**

does not automatically establish:

**E -> Root Hypothesis H**

The inferential bridge between (X) and (H) must remain visible where material.

Therefore:

> **Strong Evidence For Proposition X != Strong Evidence For Broader Proposition Y**

This prevents high-quality narrow evidence from acquiring unjustified scope merely because it appears within a tree.

---

## 5. Evidence provenance and dependency

Where consequence or complexity warrants it, record for material evidence:

- source/provenance;
- directness;
- reliability;
- relevant context;
- time/state;
- dependencies;
- corroboration;
- contradiction;
- proposition supported/challenged;
- uncertainty;
- later reclassification.

Repeated evidence is not necessarily independent evidence.

If ten observations derive from one source, preserve that ancestry.

> **Evidence Count != Evidential Strength**

> **Dependent Evidence != Independent Corroboration**

Aggregate weighting must not conceal dependency structure.

---

## 6. Weighting

The parent Reality Trees method already distinguishes weighting dimensions.

This companion adds no universal scoring system.

Mirrored structures may use qualitative or domain-appropriate assessment of evidential support, but:

> **Aggregate Weight != Truth**

A high supporting weight does not itself establish the hypothesis.

A high opposing weight does not necessarily prove a specific alternative.

Numerical precision should not be implied unless supported by a legitimate domain-specific method.

---

## 7. Hypothesis abandonability

A working hypothesis must remain capable of being:
- retained;
- strengthened;
- weakened;
- split;
- combined;
- deprioritised;
- suspended;
- or abandoned.

A reasoning process that can only accumulate support for its current hypothesis is not performing mirrored evaluation.

> **A Hypothesis Must Be Capable Of Being Abandoned**

Disposition is evidence-responsive rather than mechanically score-driven. As a minimum:
- **retain/strengthen** where material evidence continues to fit the hypothesis without unresolved contradiction sufficient to require revision;
- **weaken** where support remains but explanatory fit or evidential strength materially declines;
- **split** where one hypothesis contains separable propositions that receive materially different evidential treatment;
- **combine** where multiple hypotheses can coexist and a compound explanation better represents the evidence;
- **suspend** where presently available evidence cannot responsibly discriminate or a material dependency/reliability problem prevents disposition;
- **abandon** where the hypothesis materially fails and retaining it no longer represents the evidence.

These are decision principles, not universal thresholds. Domain-specific methods may legitimately add stronger criteria.

Abandonment is not failure of the method.

Correctly rejecting an initially plausible hypothesis may be a successful result.

---

## 8. Contradiction ladder

Material contradiction or persistent poor fit should trigger progressively wider reconsideration where warranted.

### Level 1 — Evidence interpretation

Ask:
- was the evidence classified correctly?
- is its reliability understood?
- does it support the proposition claimed?
- are dependencies hidden?
- has context/time changed?

### Level 2 — Current hypothesis

Ask:
- does (H) still adequately explain the observations?
- should (H) be weakened, split, combined or abandoned?

### Level 3 — Competing hypothesis space

Ask:
- is a plausible alternative missing?
- are apparently competing hypotheses capable of coexisting?
- is a compound explanation required?

### Level 4 — Evaluation or system boundary

Ask:
- does the represented boundary exclude relevant dimensions, causes, actors, dependencies or contexts?

### Level 5 — Root question

Ask:
- does the question itself contain a false assumption?
- is the apparent problem actually the underlying problem?
- should the root be reformulated?

The ladder is not a requirement to escalate every contradiction through all five levels.

Escalate proportionally when lower-level correction does not adequately explain the mismatch.

Escalation does not require a universal numerical trigger. Move upward when a **material unresolved mismatch remains after the lower level has been reconsidered** and that mismatch could plausibly arise from the next level. Stop escalating when the mismatch is adequately explained for the present purpose, the next level is not materially implicated, or further evaluation requires new evidence or a specialist method. Record the reason for material escalation where consequence warrants it.

## 9. ESCP interface

The Evaluation-Space Completeness Problem remains a separate portable model.

This companion uses an ESCP-compatible trigger:

> **Correct reasoning inside the represented hypothesis space does not establish that the hypothesis space is complete.**

Where persistent contradiction, poor explanatory fit or unexplained observations remain, ask whether relevant possibilities or dimensions are absent from the evaluation architecture.

Therefore:

> **Correct Evaluation Of Incomplete Hypothesis Space != Complete Evaluation**

and:

> **Correct Evaluation Of Incomplete System Boundary != Complete Evaluation**

This companion does not redefine ESCP.

It provides one operational route by which Reality Tree reasoning may discover that ESCP review is warranted.

---

## 10. Blaineyan Reasoning interface

Blaineyan Reasoning remains independent and Reality Trees remain optional within it.

Where both methods are used, the contradiction ladder interfaces naturally with:

**Reconsider -> Re-ground -> Refine**

A possible cycle is:

**Mirrored Trees Expose Poor Fit**  
-> **Reconsider Hypothesis / Boundary / Root**  
-> **Apply ESCP Where Appropriate**  
-> **Re-ground In Evidence**  
-> **Refine Hypothesis Space Or Question**  
-> **Rebuild / Update Mirrored Trees**

Therefore:

> **Mirrored Reality Trees May Operationalise Part Of Blaineyan Reconsideration**

but:

> **Blaineyan Reasoning != Mirrored Reality Trees**

and:

> **Blaineyan Reasoning Does Not Require Mirrored Reality Trees**

## 11. Compound and coexisting hypotheses

The method must represent hypotheses such as:

**A AND B**

as well as:

**A OR B**

where the domain permits them.

A system may fail only when several individually insufficient conditions interact.

A person may have more than one simultaneous medical condition.

An event may involve more than one actor.

Therefore:

> **Single-Hypothesis Framing != Necessarily Single-Cause Reality**

A hypothesis space should not silently impose mutual exclusivity where reality does not.

---

## 12. Temporal and contextual evidence

Evidence should be interpreted within relevant time and state.

A later observation that differs from an earlier observation may mean:
- the earlier observation was wrong;
- the later observation is wrong;
- the underlying state changed;
- the observation conditions changed;
- or the two observations concern materially different contexts.

Therefore:

> **Changed Evidence May Reflect Changed Reality, Not Earlier Reasoning Error**

and:

> **Observation At T1 != Universal Observation Across States**

This is particularly important for dynamic systems.

---

## 13. Intervention as evidence

Where a legitimate domain method permits an intervention, test or experiment, its outcome may update the mirrored structures.

For example:
- a diagnostic test;
- a reversible engineering change;
- a controlled experiment;
- an additional investigation.

But:

> **Reality Tree Identification Of A Test != Authority To Perform The Test**

All applicable specialist, ethical, legal, safety, consent and authority requirements remain external to this companion.

---

## 14. Decision and authority boundary

Mirrored Reality Trees provide structured reasoning support.

They do not independently determine:
- truth;
- guilt;
- diagnosis;
- treatment;
- prosecution;
- punishment;
- repair authority;
- experimental authority;
- policy;
- or other consequential action.

Therefore:

> **Tree Weight != Truth**

> **Tree Weight != Authority**

A receiving domain must supply its own legitimate decision thresholds and authority architecture.

---

## 15. Compact operational cycle

1. **State the present observation/question.**
2. **Represent materially plausible hypotheses.**
3. **Select hypotheses where mirrored testing adds value.**
4. **Construct supporting, opposing and neutral/unresolved structures.**
5. **Map material provenance, dependencies, proposition scope, context and time.**
6. **Assess explanatory fit without collapsing to raw evidence count.**
7. **Retain, weaken, split, combine, suspend or abandon hypotheses as evidence warrants.**
8. **If material contradiction or poor fit persists, move up the contradiction ladder.**
9. **Test hypothesis-space/evaluation-boundary completeness where warranted.**
10. **Reformulate the root question where warranted.**
11. **Re-ground the revised structure in evidence.**
12. **Repeat a bounded cycle or hand off to the relevant specialist method.**

---

## 16. Bounded stopping

A mirrored cycle may stop when:
- material supporting and opposing evidence is represented sufficiently for the present purpose;
- important dependencies and contradictions are visible;
- surviving hypotheses and their limitations are understood;
- the next specialist test/action is identifiable where needed;
- no unresolved mismatch presently warrants wider hypothesis-space or root-question reconsideration;
- further branching would add little material value without new evidence.

The structure may reopen when evidence or context changes.

> **Mirrored Evaluation != Endless Adversarial Branching**

---

## 17. Failure and misuse modes

### One-sided mirroring
Creating an opposing tree in name while only developing the preferred supporting tree.

### Forced binary
Assuming evidence against H proves one selected alternative.

### Evidence multiplication
Counting derivative evidence as independent corroboration.

### Scope inflation
Treating evidence for an intermediate proposition as proof of the root hypothesis.

### Hypothesis lock
Retaining a preferred hypothesis after its evidential structure materially collapses.

### Root immunity
Allowing branches to change while never permitting the root question or evaluation boundary to be reconsidered.

### False numerical precision
Converting qualitative evidence into unsupported probability-like scores.

### Consensus substitution
Treating agreement among evaluators as independent evidence for the underlying proposition.

### Model substitution
Treating an analytical model's confidence as the underlying evidence itself.

### Action leakage
Treating reasoning output as authority for consequential action.

## 21. Specification terms and minimum recording rule

Terms such as **material**, **materially plausible** and **where warranted** are purpose- and consequence-sensitive rather than universal numerical thresholds.

For this companion:
- **material** means capable of changing the interpretation, disposition, evaluation boundary, root question, or consequential handoff for the present purpose;
- **materially plausible hypothesis** means an explanation with enough evidential, logical or contextual basis to warrant representation rather than arbitrary possibility generation;
- **where warranted** means that omission could materially distort the evaluation or conceal a relevant uncertainty/dependency.

At minimum, dependency should be recorded whenever apparently separate evidence shares a source/process and treating it as independent could materially change the evaluation.

These definitions bound judgment without pretending that every receiving domain shares one quantitative threshold.
