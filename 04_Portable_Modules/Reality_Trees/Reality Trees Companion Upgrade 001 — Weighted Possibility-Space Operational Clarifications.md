## Purpose

The parent Reality Trees paper remains the source expression of the method. Portable extraction, source recovery and independent transfer exposed a small set of operational clarifications worth returning without rewriting that source.

## 1. Material branch distinction

Treat branches as materially distinct when separating them changes an important:

- assumption;
- weighting dimension;
- consequence;
- test;
- action;
- or disposition.

If separation changes none of these, the branches can usually remain combined.

## 2. Recursive decomposition

Reality Trees may recursively split broad branches into materially different sub-branches.

Stop decomposing when further subdivision would not materially change weighting, examination, testing, action or disposition for the current question.

> **Recursive Branching ≠ Exhaustive Branching**

## 3. Separate weighting dimensions

Where useful, preserve:

> **Branch Existence ≠ Personal Belief Weight ≠ Evidential Support Weight ≠ Contextual Decision Weight**

These dimensions need not be numerical and should not be silently aggregated.

A branch can be personally preferred while weakly evidenced, or strongly evidenced while contextually unsuitable for a particular decision.

## 4. Status and disposition

> **Branch Status/Weight ≠ Branch Disposition**

Status or weight describes the current epistemic or contextual assessment.

Disposition describes what the reasoning process is presently doing with the branch.

Examples include:

- weakly supported + active;
- uncertain + deferred;
- personally preferred + not selected;
- strongly evidenced + awaiting specialist test.

## 5. Choice under uncertainty

A Reality Tree may support provisional action, investigation or selection while multiple branches remain unresolved.

> **Choice Under Uncertainty Does Not Require Certainty About One Branch**

This is a representational capability, not a claim that the method supplies decision authority or guarantees good decisions.

## 6. Proportional documentation

Two useful documentation depths are:

**Lightweight Reality Tree:** root + branches + relevant weights/constraints + current disposition or provisional choice.

**Extended Reality Tree:** adds assumptions, evidence, consequences, failure modes, tests, provenance and update history where consequence or complexity warrants them.

These are documentation depths, not separate methods.

## 7. Bounded stopping

A current cycle may stop when:

- immediate meaningful alternatives are sufficiently represented;
- important assumptions, weights and uncertainties are visible;
- the next information-gathering or action step is identifiable;
- important pruned or deferred branches are recorded where useful;
- another branch or subdivision is unlikely to materially change the current examination or provisional choice without new information.

The tree may reopen when evidence, conditions or decision context change.

## 8. Boundaries preserved

Nothing in this companion establishes:

- comparative superiority;
- human cognitive benefit;
- universal applicability;
- autonomous decision authority;
- persistent AI branch memory;
- weighted epistemic memory;
- constitutional branching effectiveness;
- or permission for consequential experimentation.

Those require separate evidence or architecture.
