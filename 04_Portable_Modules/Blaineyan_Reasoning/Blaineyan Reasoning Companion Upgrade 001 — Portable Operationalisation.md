## Purpose

The source book remains the substantive theory and provenance record. This companion returns bounded operational clarifications supported by two independent blind AI transfer tests.

It does not replace the book and does not convert its unresolved research hypotheses into established findings.

## 1. Compare without external research

Compare may use internal contrasts among supplied observations, competing framings, functions, assumptions or branches.

External precedents and analogues are useful where legitimately available but are not required.

> **Compare ≠ External Research Dependency**

## 2. Reconsider, Re-ground, Refine

Keep these operations distinct:

**Reconsider** — reassess framing, abstraction, system boundary, assumptions or branch structure.

**Re-ground** — reconnect the revised reasoning to evidence, constraints and known deficits.

**Refine** — produce the updated question, model, design or test and decide whether another bounded cycle is warranted.

> **Reconsider changes the reasoning frame. Re-ground reconnects it to reality. Refine produces the next bounded form.**

## 3. Proportional branch control

Generate materially distinct branches rather than exhaustive variants.

When available attention cannot support every active branch, provisional prioritisation may consider:
- dependency;
- urgency/reversibility;
- decision leverage;
- evidence accessibility;
- risk/failure exposure.

These are aids, not a universal scoring system.

Record worthwhile inactive branches for later review instead of recursively pursuing all of them.

## 4. Generic branch representation

Reality Trees remain optional.

A compact independent representation may use:

**Branch | Epistemic status | Assumptions | Consequences | Evidence needed | Failure modes | Disposition**

> **Blaineyan Reasoning may use Reality Trees; Blaineyan Reasoning does not require Reality Trees.**

## 5. Specialist handoff packet

Where exploratory reasoning reaches a specialist boundary, preserve at minimum:

1. reformulated question/problem;
2. surviving branches/candidates;
3. pruned or deferred branches and reasons;
4. material assumptions;
5. evidence deficits;
6. identified failure modes;
7. unresolved uncertainties;
8. receiving specialist method, role or process.

Exploratory reasoning does not become validation merely because it produced a coherent candidate.

## 6. Epistemic/output labels

Operational labels may include observation, inference, hypothesis, speculative branch, research lead, testable candidate, design candidate, rejected/pruned, unresolved and grounded finding.

Labels may overlap where appropriate, but preserve why each applies.

> **Speculative Branch ≠ Evidence**  
> **Research Lead ≠ Grounded Finding**  
> **Coherent Design Candidate ≠ Validated Design**

## 7. Methodology and substrate

Distinguish:
- underlying cognitive architecture/capability;
- reasoning methodology/scaffold;
- observed reasoning behaviour/output.

A portable implementation should not assume human cognition, a particular AI architecture, persistent memory, Concord adoption or human–AI collaboration.

This is a portability requirement, not proof that different substrates implement identical internal cognition.

## 8. Resource stewardship

The existing standalone resource-stewardship warning remains authoritative within its source role.

Portable testing reinforces, rather than invents, its operational need:

- bounded cycles;
- proportional branch control;
- explicit pause/review points;
- resource monitoring where available;
- deferred-path recording;
- stopping and specialist handoff.

> **Blaineyan Reasoning Is Not a Mandate for Endless Recursion**

## 9. Boundaries preserved

This companion does not establish:
- superiority over other methods;
- empirical novelty;
- human usability;
- universal transfer;
- AI alignment through methodology;
- reasoning as natural law;
- civilisational regeneration through methodology;
- educational benefit;
- human–AI equivalence.

Those remain separate research questions.
