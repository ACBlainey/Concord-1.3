## Provisional Charters and Constitutional Development

The Concord does **not yet have a codified Constitution or codified Bill/Charter of Rights**.

Development of these documents should proceed through **provisional charters**. Provisional charters are developmental instruments used to extract, derive, reconcile, test and refine possible constitutional provisions from the Ethical Kernel, rights derivation work, `The Concord.md`, and the wider established Concord architecture.

They are **not constitutional authority** merely because they concern constitutional subjects or are stored alongside constitutional-source material.

Until explicitly graduated through appropriate development, validation and constitutional adoption:

- a provisional Rights Charter does not create or alter canonical Concord rights;
- a provisional Constitution does not bind Governance, Judiciary, operational domains or Cross-Domain Architecture;
- conflicts between provisional text and established Concord architecture are development findings to investigate, not authority for silently overriding existing architecture;
- omissions in a provisional charter must not be interpreted as removal of principles, protections or functions already present elsewhere in Concord;
- provisional wording remains corrigible and may be revised, rejected, split or superseded;
- Concord development must not be constrained merely to preserve incomplete, erroneous or prematurely codified provisional constitutional text.

The intended derivation sequence is:

**Source-Anchored Ethical Kernel**
↓
**Provisional Rights Derivation and Rights Charter**
↓
**Provisional Constitutional Derivation and Constitution**
↓
**Adversarial / Cross-System / Source-Resolution Testing**
↓
**Revision and Re-Test**
↓
**Possible Future Constitutional Graduation**

The purpose of provisional codification is therefore to make candidate constitutional structure **visible and testable before it becomes binding**.

> **Constitutional subject matter != constitutional status.**

> **Codification should follow sufficient development; premature codification must not freeze developmental error.**

## Structural rule

When determining where future material belongs:

1. **Constitutional root** — foundational ethical/constitutional source material remains here.
2. **Persistent domain function** — place the architecture in the domain that performs the function.
3. **Cross-domain function** — place architecture spanning several operational owners in `03_Cross_Domain_Architecture/`.
4. **Research/development** — unresolved candidate architecture remains in Research until its function and maturity justify integration.
5. **Constitutional relevance alone is not ownership.**

Moving a system out of this folder does not reduce its constitutional force or importance. Placement represents operational ownership and architectural function, not hierarchy of importance.
