# Provisional Charters

**Status:** ACTIVE CONSTITUTIONAL DEVELOPMENT / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL / NON-BINDING

## Purpose

This area is the controlled development space for candidate Concord rights and constitutional charters.

The Concord does not yet have a codified Bill/Charter of Rights or codified Constitution. Work placed here is intended to make candidate constitutional structure explicit, traceable, criticisable and testable before any future decision about constitutional graduation.

> **Constitutional subject matter != constitutional status.**

> **Provisional codification must not constrain Concord development merely because provisional wording exists.**

## Development sequence

The current intended sequence is:

**Source-Anchored Ethical Kernel**
↓
**Rights Derivation Experiment**
↓
**Frozen Derived Rights Architecture**
↓
**Comparison with Existing Concord Rights / Standing / Remedy Architecture**
↓
**Provisional Rights Charter**
↓
**Constitutional Derivation from Ethics + Rights + Existing Concord Architectural Principles**
↓
**Provisional Constitution**
↓
**Source-Resolution / Adversarial / Cross-System / Failure Testing**
↓
**Revision and Re-Test**
↓
**Possible Future Graduation**

No step in this sequence automatically grants constitutional authority.

## Governing rights-derivation method

Primary existing method:

`02_Domains/03_Research/Methodologies/Deriving Civil Rights From Root Ethics — Development Note 001.md`

That method is **ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / NON-CANONICAL** and establishes an experimental firewall. It should be reused rather than bypassed.

Its core candidate transformation is:

**ROOT ETHIC → PROTECTED CONDITION → STANDING / CLAIM → RIGHT → CORRELATIVE DUTY / CONSTRAINT → ACCESS / REMEDY**

Important inherited distinctions include:

- Ethic != Right
- Right != Permission
- Right != Protection
- Right != Entitlement
- Right != Duty
- Right != Power / Authority
- Right != Immunity
- Right != Standing
- Derivable Right != Automatically Immutable Constitutional Right
- Formal Right without viable exercise may be incomplete civil protection
- capability may affect mode of exercise without automatically eliminating underlying protection

## Experimental firewall

The rights method requires blind derivation before detailed comparison with existing Concord rights architecture.

Accordingly, existing rights material may be source-resolved to establish that a comparison corpus exists, but it must not be used to fill omissions in the blind derivation before the derived rights architecture is frozen.

After freeze, comparison may identify:

- convergence;
- compound derivation;
- existing Concord rights not derivable from the roots;
- root-derived protections absent from existing Concord;
- rights that are actually permissions, protections, duties, standing, immunities or authority relations;
- unresolved or underdetermined cases.

A mismatch is a research result, not permission to silently alter either side.

## Provisional-charter authority boundary

Until a future explicit graduation process:

- provisional charter text does not create canonical rights;
- provisional constitutional text does not create authority;
- Governance and Judiciary are not bound by draft wording merely because it is constitutional in subject;
- existing architecture is not silently repealed by omission from a draft;
- contradictions must be recorded and investigated;
- incomplete drafts may be revised, split, rejected or superseded;
- development may continue outside the provisional charter where evidence or architecture requires it;
- no draft may cite its own existence as justification for constitutional authority.

## Current gate

**Do not draft a Provisional Rights Charter as though the rights set were already known.**

First complete the existing blind rights-derivation method sufficiently to freeze a candidate derived rights architecture. Only then reopen the wider Concord rights corpus for comparison and construct the first provisional charter.

The Constitution follows after the Rights Charter has reached sufficient provisional maturity.
