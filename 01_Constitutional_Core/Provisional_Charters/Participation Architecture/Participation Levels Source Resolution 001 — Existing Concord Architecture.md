# 3. Existing six-stage Attractor progression

The Concord Attractor & Induction Protocol already contains a provisional graduated model:

**Observer → Evaluator → Contributor → Collaborator → Member → Citizen**

The source explicitly states that these categories are provisional rather than final constitutional definitions.

Its progression principle is:

> Participation should increase progressively as mutual understanding, demonstrated reliability and reciprocal benefit increase.

The stages perform recognisably different functions:

## Observer
Examination without commitment.

## Evaluator
First meaningful participation; bounded opportunities to test, critique and assess the Concord.

## Contributor
Bounded contribution allowing capability and reliability to be demonstrated without irreversible commitment.

## Collaborator
Shared projects, expanded access and defined reciprocal responsibilities.

## Member
Sustained participation and acceptance of the relevant reciprocal framework; broader civilisational participation.

## Citizen
Long-term participation, acceptance of the full constitutional framework, deeper systems, expanded rights and responsibilities.

This is substantially more developed than the newly created P0–P4 placeholder.

# 4. Existing six-stage Civil Contact Point model

The Civil Contact Point module independently proposes another exploratory six-stage model:

**Tier 0 Observer**
→ **Tier 1 Registered Contact Participant**
→ **Tier 2 Service Participant**
→ **Tier 3 Active Participant**
→ **Tier 4 Resident**
→ **Tier 5 Citizen / Constitutional Participant**

It explicitly says the number of tiers is not the important point.

Its core distinction is:

> Contact, participation, service access, residence and citizenship do not necessarily need to occur simultaneously.

This is highly relevant.

The contact-point model is not identical to the Attractor progression.

That difference is informative rather than necessarily contradictory.

# 5. Cross-map

A provisional functional comparison is:

| Functional depth | Attractor | Civil Contact Point | Emerging common function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Observer | Observer | inspect / encounter without commitment |
| 1 | Evaluator | Registered Contact Participant | recognised early relationship / evaluation / communication |
| 2 | Contributor | Service Participant | bounded reciprocal participation |
| 3 | Collaborator | Active Participant | active participation with broader access/responsibility |
| 4 | Member | Resident | deeper civil relationship, but categories diverge |
| 5 | Citizen | Citizen / Constitutional Participant | deepest polity/constitutional relationship |

The first four and final stages align functionally well.

The penultimate stage exposes an important topology question.

**Member** describes depth of civil relationship.

**Resident** describes physical/jurisdictional location.

These are not necessarily the same axis.

# 6. Residence should probably be orthogonal to participation maturity

Existing Citizenship and Membership research already requires distinctions among:
- personhood;
- citizenship;
- residency;
- membership;
- temporary presence;
- project membership.

The Civil Contact Point module likewise states:

**Identity ≠ Contact ≠ Residence ≠ Participation ≠ Citizenship.**

Therefore a mature participation model should probably **not** assume:

higher participation = physical residence.

A participant might be:
- deeply trusted but non-resident;
- resident but relatively new;
- a citizen temporarily living elsewhere;
- a service participant in another jurisdiction;
- an artificial participant for whom physical residence is poorly defined.

Provisional conclusion:

> **Residence is likely a parallel civil-status dimension rather than a universal participation rung.**

This requires later testing.

# 7. Existing evidence strongly supports graduated participation

CH002 independently verifies that existing Concord architecture already establishes:

- progressive participation;
- evidence-based progression;
- greater participation following demonstrated reliability, understanding, stewardship and reciprocal benefit;
- expanded access for collaborators;
- broader civilisational participation for members;
- developing access to civilisational services through continued participation and reciprocal contribution.

Therefore the Participation Levels Scaffold is not introducing the graduated-participation concept.

It is recovering and integrating an existing Concord pattern.

---

# 8. Contestability is mandatory where progression has material consequences

CH002 also identified a structural integration requirement:

**Reliability Assessment**
→ **Consequential Access / Participation Change**
→ **Contestability Requirement**

Where participant-specific assessment affects material access, standing or participation, the participant should have appropriate:
- inspectability;
- context;
- challenge;
- review;
- correction;
- correction propagation;
- provenance.

This should become a core requirement of any future participation-level architecture.

> **Progression Decision != Unchallengeable Status Assignment.**

---

# 9. Economic architecture rejects one universal trust score

Multisubstrate Economic Participation and Ratchet V4.2 add a critical constraint.

Economic progression can depend upon:
- capability;
- reliability;
- responsibility;
- safety;
- contractual performance;
- appropriate domain-specific trust.

Ratchet explicitly treats trust as contextual.

A reliable software engineer is not thereby a reliable surgeon, judge or financial steward.

Ratchet also rejects a universal person score controlling universal permissions.

Therefore:

> **General Participation Level != Universal Competence or Trust Score.**

A civil participation level may establish a broad relationship with Concord.

Sensitive domain authority should additionally require **role/domain-specific evidence**.

---

# 10. Existing economic progression is role-specific

Multisubstrate Economic Participation contains its own historical ladder:

**Tool → Assistant → Operator → Manager → Economic Agent → Economic Citizen**

This should not be copied into the general civil ladder.

It is evidence that **economic capability itself can graduate within a domain**.

Ratchet V4.2 gives the more general pattern:

**Participant → Trusted Collaborator → Constitutional Participant**

while explicitly separating operational permission from constitutional status.

This supports a layered model:

**General Civil Participation State**
+
**Domain / Role Qualification**
+
**Contextual Trust Evidence**
+
**Specific Permission**

rather than:

**One Universal Rank → Everything Unlocked.**

---

# 11. Services are clearly participation-related, but exact boundaries remain unresolved

The Attractor states that access to civilisational services develops through continued voluntary participation and reciprocal contribution.

The Civil Contact Point model separately identifies a Service Participant stage.

This supports participation-dependent services in principle.

However, the current V1.3 Health domain is explicitly:

**DOMAIN ESTABLISHED / ARCHITECTURE LARGELY UNDEVELOPED**

and Infrastructure and Essential Systems is:

**PARTIAL / SYSTEM GAPS**.

Therefore existing V1.3 architecture does **not** yet justify a detailed mapping of:
- healthcare benefits by participation level;
- continuity benefits by participation level;
- essential infrastructure access by participation level.

Those mappings must remain provisional/open.

The Rights Charter's distinction between a fundamental/minimum protection floor and enhanced participation-dependent services remains the safer current representation.

# 12. Provisional topology emerging from source resolution

The evidence currently supports something closer to **six broad civil relationship bands** than the initial five P0–P4 placeholders.

A source-derived provisional synthesis could be:

## Band 0 — Observer / External Evaluator
Inspect, learn and assess without substantial commitment.

## Band 1 — Recognised / Contact / Evaluator Participant
Recognised civil relationship and official communication; early evaluation and bounded interaction.

## Band 2 — Contributor / Service Participant
Bounded reciprocal contribution and/or selected service access; evidence begins to accumulate.

## Band 3 — Collaborator / Active Participant
Sustained active participation, broader access, shared projects and defined reciprocal responsibilities.

## Band 4 — Member / Established Participant
Sustained relationship, broader civilisational participation, increased trust-dependent eligibility and responsibility.

## Band 5 — Citizen / Constitutional Participant
Deepest polity relationship; expanded constitutional/political rights and responsibilities where later architecture so determines.

These names are **working synthesis labels**, not adopted constitutional statuses.

# 13. Parallel dimensions

The source evidence suggests the final architecture may need at least these independent dimensions:

### A. Fundamental protected standing
Not a participation reward.

### B. General civil participation state
Broad relationship depth with Concord.

### C. Citizenship / constitutional membership
Potentially the deepest polity relationship, but must remain distinct from personhood.

### D. Residence / jurisdictional presence
Physical/legal location relationship; probably orthogonal to participation maturity.

### E. Domain-specific competence and trust
Employment, economic, infrastructure, medical, security, fiduciary and other role-specific qualification.

### F. Specific permissions / entitlements
What the participant may actually access or exercise in context.

This can be represented provisionally as:

**Protected Standing**
+
**Civil Participation State**
+
**Residence / Jurisdiction Status**
+
**Citizenship Status**
+
**Domain Credentials / Contextual Trust**
→
**Context-Specific Eligibility**

No single component should silently substitute for all others.

# 14. Implication for property, employment and economic access

The emerging model fits the recent Rights Charter development.

Property purchase may require:
- sufficient general participation state;
- applicable residence/jurisdiction conditions;
- property-class rules;
- other legitimate eligibility.

Trusted employment may require:
- sufficient participation state where relevant;
- role competence;
- contextual trust;
- security suitability;
- conflict controls.

Consequential economic authority may require:
- sufficient civil relationship where relevant;
- economic competence;
- contextual reliability;
- recoverable trust;
- specific operational permission.

Thus:

> **Higher Participation May Open Eligibility; It Does Not Replace Domain Qualification.**

---

# 15. Implication for automatic benefits and Civil Contact Points

Civil Contact Points already provide a natural official interface for graduated participation.

As participation state changes, the wider architecture can eventually:

**State Change**
→ **Eligibility Recalculation**
→ **New Benefits / Permissions / Opportunities Identified**
→ **Official Civil Contact Point Communication**

This is consistent with the Rights Charter's newly developed automatic-entitlement principle.

The detailed entitlement engine remains undeveloped.

---

# 16. Finding on the initial P0–P4 scaffold

The P0–P4 scaffold successfully exposed the problem but is now too coarse and partly misaligned with recovered source architecture.

It should **not** be treated as the preferred provisional topology.

Existing Concord material provides stronger support for a six-band civil relationship model.

However, the six-band model must not absorb:
- residence;
- domain competence;
- contextual trust;
- role-specific security;
- specific entitlement

into a single rank.

The strongest current hypothesis is therefore:

> **Six broad participation bands + parallel domain/status dimensions.**

This remains provisional.

---

# 17. ESCP boundary

This source resolution does not prove that all relevant historical Concord material has been recovered.

It establishes that multiple V1.3 sources independently converge on graduated participation and that two major onboarding/contact architectures use six stages.

Further archive/source resolution may change the synthesis.

Therefore:

> **Source Convergence != Final Constitutional Adoption.**

---

# 18. Next development action

The next revision of the provisional scaffold should:

1. replace P0–P4 as the preferred sketch with six source-derived working bands;
2. retain explicit provisional status;
3. separate residence from participation level;
4. separate general participation from domain-specific trust/competence;
5. add contestability of consequential progression decisions;
6. preserve automatic communication of newly available benefits;
7. leave health, continuity and exact benefit mappings unresolved;
8. test the six-band synthesis against property, employment, governance, economy, Civil Attention, continuity and other systems before any graduation.

> **Recover the pattern first; assign benefits later.**
