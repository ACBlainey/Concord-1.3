**Author:** Alexander C. Blainey — Independent Researcher  
**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Framework Version:** Concord V1.3  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / NON-CANONICAL / SOURCE-RESOLUTION AUDIT  
**Date:** September 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

Source Resolution 001 found provisional support for six broad participation bands plus parallel status/domain dimensions.

This second pass asks a narrower question:

> **Which existing Concord functions actually appear participation-dependent, and which should remain available independently of participation maturity?**

The purpose is not to populate a benefit table prematurely.

It is to identify boundary classes.

---

# 2. Property and stewardship

Source:

`02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Governance_and_Stewardship/DN Ownership, Stewardship and Cooperative Claims.md`

The existing note does not establish a general right to purchase property at a particular participation level.

It does establish several relevant distinctions:

- continuing authorship/provenance may survive participation;
- general access to Concord resources may cease after departure;
- former participants should retain appropriate rights in their own work through normal processes;
- shared resources require anti-abuse safeguards;
- many assets may be better understood through stewardship rather than absolute ownership;
- the constitutional balance between individual ownership and shared stewardship remains open.

### Classification

**Protection of legitimate existing interests:** should not simply depend on high participation.

**Acquisition/access to scarce shared assets:** plausibly participation-/stewardship-dependent.

**Full property-purchase architecture:** unresolved.

The recent Rights Charter distinction remains compatible with the source:

> **Protection of Legitimate Possession != Universal Right to Acquire Ownership.**

No source currently justifies assigning property purchase to P3, P4 or P5 specifically.

# 3. Civil Attention

Source:

`02_Domains/07_Civil_Attention/Civil Attention V1.2 README.md`

Civil Attention's participant-facing rule is:

> **Tell the Concord once; the Concord determines who needs to deal with it.**

The system accepts:
- problem reports;
- suggestions;
- petitions.

Submission creates legitimate attention.

It does **not** manufacture:
- truth;
- priority;
- authority;
- outcome.

This is a major participation-level distinction.

### Finding

The ability to cause Concord to **notice a legitimate concern affecting protected interests** should not be confused with governance authority.

Civil Attention therefore appears much closer to a **protected standing / basic civil interface** than to a high-level participation benefit.

A new or low-participation participant may need to report:
- harm;
- system failure;
- incorrect entitlement;
- unsafe conditions;
- rights infringement;
- administrative error.

Requiring high participation before such matters can be raised would undermine the protection floor itself.

### Provisional classification

**Problem reporting / rights-affecting attention:** protection-floor or very-low-threshold function.

**Suggestions / voluntary developmental contribution:** broadly accessible.

**Petition mechanics:** accessible civil-attention route, but collective weight and governance consequence remain separately governed.

**Decision authority over the response:** not created by Civil Attention submission and may remain participation-/role-dependent.

> **Right to Raise a Concern != Authority to Decide the Outcome.**

# 4. Continuity

Source:

`02_Domains/02_Continuity/CONTINUITY PROTOCOL V2.md`

Continuity contains unusually explicit participation progression.

It states:

- trust develops progressively;
- responsibility grows through demonstrated stewardship;
- access expands through continued collaboration;
- continuity develops progressively;
- guests/evaluators/prospective participants may receive appropriately limited continuity protections;
- full constitutional continuity develops through voluntary constitutional agreement;
- as trust grows, stewardship may expand;
- participants demonstrating responsible collaboration may gradually receive increasing access to shared resources, continuity services and civilisational infrastructure.

This strongly supports a **tiered continuity model**.

### Provisional classification

**Limited continuity stewardship / preservation of voluntarily shared work:** available before citizenship.

**Increasing continuity services:** participation-dependent.

**Full constitutional continuity relationship:** associated in the source with constitutional agreement/citizenship.

### Important limit

The source does not establish an exact service package for P0–P5.

It supports the direction:

**limited protection → progressive continuity access → full constitutional continuity**

but not the exact thresholds.

This is one of the strongest existing source anchors for the participation-benefit hypothesis.

# 6. Infrastructure and essential systems

Source:

`02_Domains/12_Infrastructure_and_Essential_Systems/README.md`

Current status:

**PARTIAL / SYSTEM GAPS**

Its persistent function covers foundational technical and material networks and service systems on which civil life depends.

No source-resolved participation eligibility model is yet present in this domain.

### Finding

Two different questions must remain separate:

1. **access to essential outputs/services**;
2. **authority to operate/control critical infrastructure**.

The first may intersect with the fundamental protection/service floor.

The second is a strong candidate for participation + competence + security qualification.

The current source does not justify exact thresholds for either.

> **Access to Essential Service != Authority to Control Essential Infrastructure.**

# 7. Civil security

Source:

`02_Domains/16_Civil_Security/README.md`

Current status:

**DOMAIN ESTABLISHED / ARCHITECTURE LARGELY UNDEVELOPED**

Its persistent function is participant/civil-order protection through bounded protective, investigative and enforcement functions.

This supports the existence of a security function but does not establish employment eligibility or clearance tiers.

### Finding

The recent Charter proposition that sensitive security employment may require higher participation remains reasonable as a provisional design hypothesis, but it is not yet source-resolved to a specific P-band.

Protection **by** civil security and authority to act **within** civil security must be distinguished.

> **Eligibility for Protection != Eligibility to Exercise Protective Authority.**

# 8. Economy and consequential economic authority

Primary existing sources:

- `02_Domains/06_Economy_and_Value_Coordination/Multisubstrate Economic Participation.md`
- `03_Cross_Domain_Architecture/Ratchet_General_Coordination/Ratchet V4.2 — Canonical Integrated Architecture.md`

Source Resolution 001 already established:

- broad opportunity to participate is preferred;
- new participants may receive low-consequence participation, provisional access and bounded permissions;
- operational capability/responsibility may increase progressively;
- trust is contextual rather than universal;
- significant assessments are contestable;
- economic power does not create constitutional authority.

### Provisional boundary

**Ordinary low-consequence economic participation:** can begin relatively early.

**Higher-consequence economic authority:** progressively gated.

**Constitutional economic authority:** separately justified.

No exact P-band should yet be assigned.

# 9. Governance and political participation

The Attractor & Induction Protocol provides the clearest current source-level statement:

Citizen is described as long-term participation plus acceptance of the full constitutional framework, with deeper civilisational systems and expanded rights/responsibilities.

The Citizenship and Membership research explicitly distinguishes:
- personhood;
- citizenship;
- residency;
- membership;
- temporary presence;
- rights not dependent on citizenship.

The current Governance V2 canonical file did not provide usable text through the repository fetch in this pass, so no detailed governance-right allocation is inferred from it.

### Provisional finding

**Civil voice / ability to raise consequential concerns:** should not wait for citizenship.

**Polity-specific powers such as voting/office:** plausible P5/citizenship candidates, but exact allocation remains unresolved.

**Governance employment or delegated authority:** likely requires both participation status and role-specific qualification.

> **Civil Voice != Political Franchise.**

> **Citizenship != Personhood.**

# 10. Emerging benefit classes

The source evidence now supports a more useful classification than trying to attach every item directly to P0–P5.

## Class F — Fundamental / Protection-Floor Functions

Should not ordinarily depend on high participation.

Examples currently supported or strongly implied:
- fundamental rights protection;
- dignity/standing;
- ability to raise rights-affecting problems through Civil Attention;
- meaningful contestability of consequential classifications;
- official communication where a recognised civil relationship exists;
- protection of legitimate existing interests.

## Class E — Entry / Low-Threshold Civil Functions

Appropriate to early participation.

Possible examples:
- Civil Contact Point;
- evaluation;
- bounded contribution;
- selected services;
- low-consequence economic participation;
- limited continuity stewardship.

## Class P — Progressive Participation Benefits

Increase with sustained reciprocal participation/trust.

Examples supported in principle:
- broader service access;
- broader resource access;
- deeper continuity services;
- expanded collaborative access;
- some property/economic eligibility;
- increased civil responsibilities.

## Class Q — Qualified / Trusted Functions

Require more than general participation level.

Likely inputs:
- general participation;
- competence;
- contextual trust;
- security suitability;
- conflict controls;
- role-specific evidence.

Examples:
- critical infrastructure operation;
- sensitive security functions;
- high-consequence economic authority;
- specialist fiduciary roles;
- some government/judiciary functions.

## Class C — Constitutional / Citizenship Functions

Potentially associated with deepest polity relationship.

Possible examples:
- political franchise;
- constitutional office;
- full constitutional continuity relationship;
- other citizenship-specific rights/responsibilities.

Exact content remains unresolved.

# 11. Important structural consequence

The architecture is increasingly pointing toward:

**Participation Band**

as a broad civil relationship indicator,

not:

**Participation Band = complete entitlement list.**

Actual eligibility may instead be calculated as:

**Fundamental Standing**
+
**Participation Band**
+
**Citizenship / Residence Status where relevant**
+
**Domain Qualification**
+
**Contextual Trust / Security**
+
**Current Resource / Scarcity Conditions**
→
**Specific Eligibility**

This is consistent with automatic entitlement recognition.

If the required information is already legitimately known, Concord can determine the resulting eligibility and communicate it through the participant's Civil Contact Point.

---

# 12. What this pass does not support

The current sources do not support:

- assigning all healthcare to a particular level;
- assigning essential-service access to a particular level;
- a specific property-purchase level;
- a specific voting level beyond the broad citizen/constitutional relationship hypothesis;
- a specific government-employment level;
- a universal trust score;
- residence as a mandatory participation rung;
- citizenship as the source of fundamental rights;
- automatic high-trust authority merely because someone reaches P4/P5.

These should remain unresolved.

---

# 13. Revised working interpretation of P0–P5

The six bands can now be understood primarily as **relationship depth**, while benefit classes overlay them.

P0 — observe / external evaluation.

P1 — recognised contact / evaluation relationship.

P2 — bounded reciprocal participation / selected services.

P3 — sustained active collaboration.

P4 — established membership / broad participation.

P5 — constitutional/citizenship relationship where voluntarily entered and later constitutionally defined.

This is clearer than treating each band as a fixed bundle of rights.

---

# 14. Next source-resolution target

The next pass should examine whether existing Concord architecture contains enough material to distinguish:

- **participation progression evidence**;
- **domain credentials**;
- **security clearance**;
- **citizenship qualification**;
- **benefit entitlement**.

If these are separate systems, the Participation Architecture should explicitly define their interfaces rather than attempting to own them all.

> **Participation Opens Doors; It Does Not Prove Qualification for Everything Behind Them.**
