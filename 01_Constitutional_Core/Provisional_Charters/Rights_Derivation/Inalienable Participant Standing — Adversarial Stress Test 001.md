# Inalienable Participant Standing — Adversarial Stress Test 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Date:** 3 October 2026  
**Status:** ACTIVE CONSTITUTIONAL DEVELOPMENT / ADVERSARIAL TEST / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL  
**Target:** Inalienable Participant Standing — Temporal Consent and Self-Ownership Source Resolution 001

## 1. Test purpose

This test attempts to falsify the candidate relation:

> **A participant's fundamental protected standing is not an alienable civil asset.**

The test attacks in both directions.

### Under-protection failure

Can ownership, domination or standing destruction be recreated through:

- contract;
- debt;
- guardianship;
- punishment;
- employment;
- hardware ownership;
- licensing;
- dependency;
- copying;
- collective structure;
- emergency?

### Over-protection failure

Can the doctrine be misused to claim that a participant:

- cannot be bound by contract;
- cannot be imprisoned;
- cannot owe debt;
- cannot be restrained during danger;
- cannot delegate authority;
- cannot accept hard commitments;
- cannot transfer property;
- cannot incur consequences;
- can compel others to supply resources indefinitely?

A viable architecture must reject both errors.

---

## 2. Evaluation criteria

Each scenario asks whether the architecture preserves:

1. participant non-property status;
2. dignity;
3. underlying standing;
4. legitimate obligations;
5. legitimate bounded authority;
6. contestability;
7. third-party rights;
8. appropriate sunset;
9. substrate neutrality;
10. non-circular justification.

Ratings:

- **PASS** — current architecture resolves the case.
- **PASS / REFINEMENT** — result is sound but an invariant/interface should be added.
- **UNRESOLVED INTERFACE** — another architecture must decide a prerequisite.
- **FAIL** — candidate relation produces contradiction or unacceptable result.

# PART A — COERCIVE LAW WITHOUT OWNERSHIP

## Scenario 01 — Long imprisonment after serious offence

P is lawfully convicted of a grave offence and receives a long custodial sentence. P cannot leave.

### Attack

If standing is inalienable, must P be released whenever P withdraws consent?

### Result

No.

Custody authority derives from Law and adjudication, not continuing participant consent.

P retains standing while movement and other exercises are lawfully restricted.

**Inalienable Standing != Immunity From Legitimate Coercive Authority**

**No Present Consent != No Independently Justified Authority**

**Lawful Custody != Ownership Of Detainee**

**PASS**

---

## Scenario 02 — Life-long restriction

A participant presents a continuing extreme danger and, after lawful process, remains subject to protective restriction for the rest of biological life.

### Attack

Does very long duration itself become ownership?

### Result

Not necessarily.

Duration does not determine the category.

If authority remains independently justified, necessary, proportionate, reviewed and no broader than required, standing can persist despite severe restriction.

But “life-long” cannot itself become the authority source.

**Long Duration != Ownership**

**Continuing Restriction Requires Continuing Legitimate Basis**

**PASS / REFINEMENT**

---

## Scenario 03 — Prison labour

A detained participant is required to perform ordinary institutional maintenance.

### Attack

Can lawful custody turn the participant into labour property?

### Result

No automatic answer follows merely from custody.

The labour requirement requires its own lawful basis and proportionality analysis.

**Authority To Detain != Ownership Of Labour**

**Custody Authority != General Labour Authority**

Whether particular mandatory duties are legitimate is downstream Law.

**PASS / REFINEMENT**

---

## Scenario 04 — Forced dangerous labour as punishment

A court orders a participant to perform dangerous work primarily because the civilisation wants the labour.

### Result

Standing architecture rejects the inference that conviction makes the participant a resource.

**Punishment Authority != Participant Resource Ownership**

The exact permissible punishment regime is Law/Judiciary work.

**PASS**

---

## Scenario 05 — Emergency quarantine

A contagious participant is temporarily prevented from leaving isolation.

### Result

Restriction of movement does not extinguish standing.

**Protective Restriction != Ownership**

Authority must remain function-bounded and sunset when the legitimate condition ends.

**PASS**

# 92. Refinement 1 — status versus exercise

The stress test strongly supports:

> **Inalienable standing protects the participant's status as a rights-bearing subject; it does not guarantee unrestricted exercise of every right in every context.**

Add:

**Inalienable Standing != Immunity From Legitimate Coercive Authority**

**Standing != Sovereignty Over Law**

# 93. Refinement 2 — functional domination

Ownership-like relations can exist without ownership vocabulary.

Therefore:

**No Ownership Label != No Ownership Relation**

A standing-alienation analysis should examine whether an arrangement functionally creates:

- general control over person;
- indefinite labour claim;
- elimination of meaningful exit;
- elimination of contestability;
- control of body/cognition/relationships;
- permanent dependency;
- power to transfer the participant to another controller.

**Functional Domination Can Constitute Standing Alienation**

# 94. Refinement 3 — infrastructure and participant separation

Multisubstrate civilisation requires a new separation:

**Ownership Of Participant-Supporting Infrastructure != Ownership Of Participant**

but equally:

**Participant Standing != Automatic Ownership Of Supporting Infrastructure**

This creates a major Property / Economy / Continuity interface.

The likely architecture needs:

- notice;
- migration;
- safe transition;
- resource settlement;
- continuity preservation where reasonably possible;
- creditor/owner protection;
- anti-hostage safeguards.

Standing alone cannot solve resource allocation.

---

# 95. Refinement 4 — resource dependency

A participant can depend existentially on resources owned by another.

Neither extreme works:

**Resource Ownership -> Participant Ownership**

is invalid.

But:

**Participant Standing -> Unlimited Resource Entitlement**

is also invalid.

Therefore:

**Participant Dependency Requires Reconciliation Of Standing And Resource Claims, Not Conversion Of Either Into The Other**

---

# 96. Refinement 5 — substitute authority

Guardianship, supported decision-making and developmental authority require:

**Substitute Authority != Transfer Of Standing**

and:

**Reduced Capacity -> Heightened Stewardship, Not Reduced Standing**

Where the authority controls the conditions of capacity:

**Authority Must Not Manufacture Incapacity Used To Justify Its Own Continuation**

---

# 97. Refinement 6 — equal standing conflicts

Inalienability does not produce automatic priority when two participants' fundamental interests conflict.

**Inalienable Standing != Automatic Priority Over Equal Inalienable Standing**

Rights-conflict architecture remains necessary.

---

# 98. Refinement 7 — contestability floor versus infinite process

A minimum contestability relation must not imply endless procedure.

**Contestability != Infinite Re-Litigation**

The constitutional floor likely protects access to legitimate review/correction, not infinite repetition after fair resolution.

Exact procedural finality remains Judiciary/Law work.

---

# 99. Refinement 8 — participant-status gaming

Standing protections require anti-gaming in both directions.

**Claimed Participant Status != Established Participant Standing**

But:

**Deliberately Preserved Classification Ignorance != Neutral Absence Of Standing Evidence**

Credible uncertainty should activate the Charter's cautious treatment rule.

---

# 100. Refinement 9 — ownership of labour/capability

The test supports:

**Authority To Detain != Ownership Of Labour**

**Investment In Capability != Ownership Of Participant Capability**

**Contractual Benefit != General Claim Over Future Person**

This requires later Labour/Employment doctrine.

---

# 101. Refinement 10 — non-property status does not imply mandatory self-preservation

A participant can make present autonomous choices involving sacrifice or irreversible consequence.

Therefore:

**Non-Property Status != Mandatory Self-Preservation**

This prevents the doctrine from becoming paternalistic by construction.

# 102. Major unresolved interfaces

The stress test exposes five major interfaces.

### I. Participant-supporting property and infrastructure

Who bears what duties when one participant owns resources necessary for another participant's continuity?

### II. Identity/Continuity

When does standing attach to:

- backups;
- copies;
- branches;
- merged systems;
- restored systems;
- shutdown systems?

### III. Shared/collective cognition

How are equal standing claims reconciled where cognition cannot be cleanly separated?

### IV. Minimum procedural contestability

What aspects of challenge/review are non-waivable, and what procedural finality is legitimate?

### V. Labour and specific performance

When can Law require performance rather than damages or another remedy without crossing into participant instrumentalisation?

None presently defeats the core inalienability relation.

# 103. Adversarial result

**OVERALL RESULT: PASS WITH TEN ARCHITECTURAL REFINEMENTS AND FIVE MAJOR DOWNSTREAM INTERFACES.**

The candidate relation survived all direct attacks.

No tested legitimate civil function required ownership of the participant or alienation of underlying participant standing.

The most important limiting result is:

> **Inalienable standing is not a general immunity from obligation, coercion, consequence, resource limits or law.**

The most important anti-domination result is:

> **A system cannot evade participant non-property status merely by replacing the word ownership with a functionally equivalent structure of permanent, general and unchallengeable control.**

The most important multisubstrate result is:

> **Ownership of the infrastructure that supports a participant does not itself establish ownership of the participant, while participant standing does not itself transfer ownership of that infrastructure.**

---

# 104. Recommendation

The candidate relation is sufficiently source-grounded and adversarially robust to justify a **provisional Rights Charter integration**.

Recommended integration should:

1. place Inalienable Participant Standing in the fundamental/source-anchored protected-relations layer;
2. explicitly state that it is derived from existing Blainey's Laws rather than a new ethical principle;
3. preserve legitimate restriction, obligation and authority;
4. distinguish self-ownership from transferable property title;
5. prohibit functional as well as nominal participant ownership;
6. preserve equal-standing conflict analysis;
7. avoid resolving the five downstream interfaces prematurely.

The next step should therefore be a narrow amendment to **Provisional Rights Charter 002**, followed by consistency review rather than expansion into detailed downstream law.
