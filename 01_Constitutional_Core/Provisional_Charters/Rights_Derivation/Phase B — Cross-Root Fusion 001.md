# 2. Fusion Group F1 — Protection Against Unjustified Civil Interference

## Inputs

- U1-A — claim against unjustified harmful interference
- U3-A — self-direction within legitimate sphere
- U3-B — claim against arbitrary/functionally unjustified domination
- U3-C — bounded justification of authority
- U3-D — meaningful consent where consent is asserted as justification

## Fusion

These appear to form a compound protection rather than five wholly independent rights.

### Candidate compound right CR-01 — Protected Agency Against Unjustified Interference

**Protected condition:** A participant retains legitimate self-direction and protection against harmful or controlling interference that lacks sufficient justification.

**Supporting roots:** U1 + U3, with U2/U6 likely procedural supports.

**Holder:** participant subject to interference.

**Duty bearers:** participants/institutions exercising consequential interference or authority.

**Claim:** interference must have a legitimate basis proportionate to the protected conditions at stake; authority is not self-justifying.

**Scope:** broad but contextual.

**Limits:** competing protected conditions, necessity, effects on others, capability where relevant, and legitimate obligations.

### Retained sub-relations

- meaningful consent is a possible **legitimating condition**, not identical to the compound right;
- bounded authority justification is partly a **procedural claim** and partly a constitutional constraint;
- non-domination may be best represented partly as an **immunity** from arbitrary power.

**Phase-B classification:** COMPOUND RIGHT + IMMUNITY + AUTHORITY CONSTRAINT.

# 3. Fusion Group F2 — Epistemic Contestability and Civil Correction

## Inputs

- U2-A — against deliberate material deception
- U2-B — epistemic contestability
- U2-C — uncertainty not falsely represented as certainty
- U6-A — meaningful challenge/review
- U6-B — correction of material civil error
- U6-D — accountable provenance
- U7-C — reassessment where restrictions depend on capability
- U7-D — relevant capability evidence rather than status substitution

## Fusion

These form a coherent procedural family, but deliberate deception should remain distinguishable from ordinary error.

### Candidate compound right CR-02 — Consequential Epistemic Contestability

**Protected condition:** A participant materially affected by consequential factual representation or classification can challenge material error and obtain appropriate correction.

**Supporting roots:** U2 + U6; U7 in capability-dependent cases.

**Claim:** consequential civil representation should remain sufficiently attributable, uncertainty-aware, contestable and correctable.

**Access requirements:** enough notice/information to identify the consequential proposition; viable challenge path; evidence submission; correction propagation where error is established.

**Limits:** legitimate confidentiality, privacy, security, urgency and proportionality may constrain disclosure but should not automatically eliminate contestability.

### Candidate distinct right CR-03 — Protection Against Deliberate Consequential Deception

U2-A survives fusion as a narrower substantive claim because intentional material deception is not equivalent to correctable error.

**Supporting roots:** U2, likely U3 where deception manipulates agency.

**Phase-B classification:** CR-02 PROCEDURAL COMPOUND RIGHT; CR-03 NARROW SUBSTANTIVE RIGHT.

# 4. Fusion Group F3 — Review, Remedy and Repair

## Inputs

- U1-C — challenge harmful action
- U6-A — meaningful challenge/review
- U6-B — correction
- U6-C — remedy/repair
- U7-C — reassessment
- U8-B — preservation of valid claims through transition

## Fusion

Review and remedy are related but should not be collapsed.

### Candidate compound right CR-04 — Right to Review of Consequential Civil Action

**Protected condition:** consequential exercises of civil power or decisions affecting protected claims remain challengeable through a viable process.

**Supporting roots:** U6, reinforced by U1/U2/U3/U7 according to subject.

**Claim:** access to appropriate review proportionate to consequence.

### Candidate compound right CR-05 — Right to Appropriate Correction or Remedy

**Protected condition:** established wrongful violation or material civil error is not left without a correction/repair path merely because it has already occurred.

**Supporting roots:** U6 + affected substantive root(s).

**Claim:** context-appropriate cessation, correction, restoration, compensation, rehabilitation, acknowledgment or other feasible repair.

**Limits:** remedy cannot guarantee impossible restoration; finality and repeated reopening may require legitimate thresholds.

### Continuity rule

U8-B appears less like a separate right than a **continuity property of CR-04/CR-05 and other valid claims**: system transition should not silently erase unresolved legitimate standing/remedy.

**Phase-B classification:** TWO RELATED PROCEDURAL RIGHTS + CROSS-CUTTING CONTINUITY PROPERTY.

# 5. Fusion Group F4 — Support, Development and Capability-Sensitive Exercise

## Inputs

- U4-A — reasonable support under serious need
- U4-B — support does not extinguish agency
- U4-C — non-arbitrary consideration of need
- U7-A — capability-sensitive mode of exercise
- U7-B — developmental opportunity
- U7-C — reassessment after capability change

## Fusion

These do not reduce cleanly to one unlimited positive right.

### Candidate right CR-06 — Capability-Sensitive Exercise of Protected Claims

**Protected condition:** where an underlying protection applies, limited or developing capability changes the legitimate mode of exercise rather than automatically erasing the protection.

**Supporting roots:** U7 + U3, with U4 where support is required.

**Claim:** reasonable adaptation/support and reassessment where feasible and relevant.

**Limits:** consequence, actual capability, feasibility, risk and competing protections.

### Candidate entitlement/right CR-07 — Reasonable Support Under Serious Need

**Protected condition:** serious need is not ignored where a legitimate support responsibility and feasible capability exist.

**Supporting roots:** U4, constrained by U3/U5/U7.

**Status uncertainty:** may be an entitlement or contextual positive right rather than invariant universal right.

### Candidate right CR-08 — Reasonable Developmental Opportunity

**Protected condition:** where civilisation assumes developmental responsibility, participants are not arbitrarily denied reasonable opportunity to develop relevant capability.

**Supporting roots:** U7 + U4.

**Status uncertainty:** strongly duty-bearer dependent.

### Derived constraint

**Support != surrender of agency.**

This appears as a constraint on CR-07/CR-08 implementation rather than a separate right.

**Phase-B classification:** ONE STRONG COMPOUND EXERCISE RIGHT + TWO CONTEXTUAL POSITIVE CANDIDATES.

# 6. Fusion Group F5 — Shared Resources and Dependency-Critical Access

## Inputs

- U5-A — unjustified deprivation of dependency-critical shared resources
- U5-B — consideration of materially affected resource interests
- U5-C — future opportunity against reckless depletion
- U8-A — continuity of dependency-critical civil function
- U8-D — adaptive fulfilment after mechanism failure

## Fusion

The common object is not ownership. It is protection of legitimate dependency and stewardship under shared/resource-critical conditions.

### Candidate compound right CR-09 — Protection Against Arbitrary Loss of Dependency-Critical Civil Access

**Protected condition:** a participant materially dependent on a legitimately shared or civilly supplied critical resource/function is not arbitrarily deprived where stewardship obligations apply.

**Supporting roots:** U5 + U8, with U1/U4 potentially relevant by consequence/need.

**Claim:** non-arbitrary stewardship, justified allocation and reasonable continuity/recovery within feasible capability.

**Limits:** scarcity, competing claims, emergency, sustainability, legitimate ownership/control relations and physical impossibility.

### Standing relation SR-01 — Standing in Materially Consequential Stewardship Decisions

U5-B appears more safely classified as **standing** than as a guaranteed right to determine the outcome.

Affected participants should have their material protected interests represented/considered where stewardship decisions impose significant consequence.

### Duty/protection DP-01 — Intergenerational Opportunity Preservation

U5-C does not yet map cleanly to a present individual right-holder.

Preserve as a **stewardship duty / protected future condition** pending later topology work.

**Phase-B classification:** ONE COMPOUND DEPENDENCY RIGHT + STANDING + INTERGENERATIONAL DUTY.

# 7. Fusion Group F6 — Continuity Without Institutional Entrenchment

## Inputs

- U8-A — continuity of critical function
- U8-B — claims through transition
- U8-C — against institutional self-preservation
- U8-D — adaptive fulfilment

## Fusion

U8 does not generate a right to institutional continuity. It generates almost the opposite: continuity belongs to legitimate function and protected participant relations, not to the organisation currently performing them.

### Constitutional immunity/constraint CI-01 — No Authority From Institutional Self-Preservation Alone

An institution's desire, history, capability or existing position does not itself justify continued authority.

**Supporting root:** U8, likely U3.

### Cross-cutting continuity principle CP-01 — Function and Valid Claims Survive Mechanism Change Where Feasible

Where mechanisms change, legitimate functions and unresolved protected claims should be preserved or transferred where possible rather than erased by organisational replacement.

**Status:** principle/constraint, not necessarily freestanding individual right.

**Phase-B classification:** CONSTITUTIONAL CONSTRAINT + CONTINUITY PRINCIPLE, NOT NEW ORDINARY RIGHT.

# 10. Under-determination signal

Phase B provides evidence for the possibility that the ethical roots constrain a legitimate rights architecture without uniquely specifying every conventional civil right.

Several candidate rights require:
- context;
- definitions of participant/standing;
- allocation rules;
- legitimate duty bearers;
- consequence thresholds;
- institutional implementation.

Therefore **Outcome O5 — underdetermination** remains plausible alongside O2/O4.
