# Ten Commandments Comparative Project
## P6 — Ethical Resolution Ladder

The previous crosswalks suggest that the three systems operate at different resolutions. This paper makes that hypothesis explicit before attempting regeneration.

## Proposed levels

### L0 — Minimal ethical root
Highly compressed, substrate-neutral constraints such as Non-Harm, Truth, Autonomy and Stewardship. Blainey's Laws are closest to this level, although several later Concord laws are systemic deductions rather than primitive roots.

### L1 — Universal functional attractor
A root unfolded far enough to express its recurrent function: protection/repair, epistemic integrity, justice/accountability, care, trust, reconciliation, self-regulation, dignity/anti-domination. E3's ten frozen attractors are predominantly L1.

### L2 — Domain principle
Application of L0/L1 to a recurring domain: testimony, intimate fidelity, possession, intergenerational duty, labour/rest, acquisition desire, authority.

### L3 — Concrete behavioural rule
A compact instruction governing behaviour in that domain: do not murder; do not steal; do not bear false witness; do not commit adultery; honour parents; do not covet.

### L4 — Institutional/procedural implementation
Detailed rules defining evidence, sanctions, property transfer, marriage procedure, labour regulation, judicial process and similar mechanisms.

## Where the Decalogue sits

The interpersonal Decalogue is concentrated at **L2–L3**, especially L3. It does not normally state abstract universal roots; it gives memorable behavioural constraints whose underlying functions can be traced upward.

The theological/covenantal commands differ. They contain L2–L3 prescriptions relative to a specific relationship with God and therefore cannot be located purely on a secular ethical ladder without losing content.

Sabbath is unusually multi-level: it is a concrete covenantal observance, but its text also contains social implications for labour, dependants, strangers and animals.

## Initial classification

| Command unit | Dominant resolution |
|---|---|
| exclusive allegiance | L2/L3 covenantal |
| no idols | L3 covenantal |
| no misuse of divine name | L3 speech/authority + covenantal |
| Sabbath | L3 covenantal/social, with unusually rich L2 implications |
| honour parents | L3 intergenerational/relational |
| no murder | L3 behavioural |
| no adultery | L3 relational |
| no theft | L3 property/agency |
| no false witness | L3 evidential/judicial |
| no coveting | L2/L3 motivational |

The Decalogue is therefore not primarily another ten-item L0 kernel. Numerically comparing “ten against ten” obscures the more important structural relation.
