# Unified Paths of Light — E5
## E5-112 — Cross-Tradition Seven-Root Ethical Kernel Freeze

## Status
**E3–E4–E5 SHARED MINIMUM FUNCTIONAL KERNEL FROZEN.**

The following seven roots survived three-way representation testing, ablation and pairwise fusion pressure.

### X1 — Protection From Unjustified Harm
Avoid, prevent, reduce and where possible repair serious unjustified harm.

### X2 — Reality-Responsive Truth
Maintain truthful communication and proportion belief, confidence and consequential judgement to relevant evidence, preserving correction when error is discovered.

### X3 — Protected Agency and Non-Domination
Respect meaningful agency and do not coerce, exploit or arbitrarily subordinate agents where responsible choice is possible.

### X4 — Care Under Need and Vulnerability
Provide proportionate protection or assistance where serious need, dependency or reduced effective agency creates vulnerability and capacity to help exists.

### X5 — Responsible Resource Stewardship
Acquire, control, allocate and use resources responsibly, without exploitation, destructive waste or disregard of legitimate claims and future consequences.

### X6 — Correctability, Accountability and Repair
Preserve mechanisms through which error, wrongdoing and institutional failure can be identified, challenged, corrected and repaired where possible.

### X7 — Capability-Sensitive Development
Support development of judgement and ethical capability, while matching responsibility, intervention and expectations to actual capacity and developmental condition.

# Evidential status

This kernel is not claimed to be:
- the uniquely correct moral system;
- metaphysical natural law;
- exhaustive of any tradition;
- proof that all traditions are equivalent;
- independent of all historical cultural interaction.

It is a **minimum-stable functional intersection** of three differently anchored source-led derivations under the present methodology.

## Why the result matters
The seven roots survive despite:
- different primary corpora;
- different extraction structures;
- different catalogue sizes;
- different higher-order partition geometries;
- E5's independent minimum-compression process;
- deliberate exclusion of Blainey's Laws from construction.

The result therefore supports a stronger testable hypothesis:

> **Distinct ethical traditions may encode different decompositions of a smaller recurrent functional kernel required for durable cooperative agency.**
