# Unified Paths of Light — E9

## E9-1294 — E9 Experimental Closure

## Status

**E9 EXPERIMENT CLOSED.**

### 1. Aim

E9 tested whether the Unified Paths methodology could be applied to an African oral/proverbial ethical corpus without targeting the previously observed seven-function architecture.

### 2. Source result

The intended Christaller-wide traversal could not be completed reproducibly because source access and reconstruction quality were insufficient.

Rather than claim false completeness, E9 froze a bounded Rattray-derived working corpus.

Witness closure:
**R1–R830 classified: Y572 / Z41 / H215 / X2.**

Frozen ethical input:
**AR001–AR571 = 571 admitted occurrences.**

The preserved Y572/AR571 discrepancy remains part of the audit record.

### 3. Normalisation and compression

The derivation proceeded:

**571 admitted occurrences → 557 distinct propositions → parent-child structures → 53 higher parents → architecture separation → 23 provisional substantive parents → minimum-stability compression.**

The process explicitly separated:
- substantive orientations;
- operators/conditions;
- structural conditions;
- domain implementations and mechanisms.

### 4. Frozen Akan roots

The final minimum-stable substantive architecture contains eight roots:

**AK-R1 — Responsible Information Relation**  
**AK-R2 — Development of Effective Capability**  
**AK-R3 — Protected Agency Under Power and Dependency**  
**AK-R4 — Causal Responsibility and Accountable Action**  
**AK-R5 — Protection From Avoidable Harm and Vulnerability**  
**AK-R6 — Sustaining Relationships of Care and Reciprocity**  
**AK-R7 — Responsible Resource Relation**  
**AK-R8 — Preservation Through Adaptive Continuity**

The number eight was not a target.

### 5. Prospective X test

After the Akan architecture froze, the semantic firewall was opened.

Result:

**X1–X7 → Akan: 7 Recovered / 0 Ambiguous / 0 Absent.**

Reverse test:

**AK-R1–AK-R8 → X topology: 8/8 functionally represented.**

The mappings were not forced one-to-one. X5 and X6 in particular were distributed across multiple Akan architectural elements, while AK-R8 distributed across several X functions.

### 6. Principal structural finding

E9 independently reinforces:

> **Functional presence is not partition identity.**

and, more strongly:

> **Functional ethical topology can remain stable while root count, labels, partition boundaries and architectural placement vary.**

### 7. Cross-traditional integration

Post-freeze comparison with E3–E8 found substantial recurrence across:
- harm/protection;
- truth/reality/information;
- agency/power;
- care/support;
- resources/material relation;
- accountability/correction/repair;
- capability/development.

E9 also elevated adaptive continuity and made structural mediation unusually explicit.

### 8. Methodological contribution

E9 established practical extensions for oral/distributed traditions:
- attested-occurrence evidential units;
- provenance and mediation fields;
- variant control;
- witness closure distinct from tradition closure;
- bounded incompleteness;
- architectural separation of roots, operators, structures and implementations.

### 9. Evidential interpretation

E9 strengthens the hypothesis that recurrent ethical functions may reflect persistent constraints of cooperative multi-agent existence.

It does not establish the cause of that recurrence.

Natural-law and evolutionary/survival explanations remain hypotheses requiring broader and more adversarial testing.

### 10. Deferred replication

A clean full-Christaller rerun is specified separately. It must be treated as a new replication and must freeze independently before comparison with E9.

### 11. Closure

E9 began as a difficult test of whether the emerging ethical topology would survive a shift away from the earlier scriptural and philosophical corpora into an African oral/proverbial witness.

It did not reproduce the previous partition mechanically.

That is precisely why the result is useful.

It produced **eight**, not seven, substantive roots; exposed additional operator and structural layers; retained distinctive Akan emphases; and nevertheless recovered the complete seven-function shared topology after the firewall opened.

> **E9 CLOSED.**
