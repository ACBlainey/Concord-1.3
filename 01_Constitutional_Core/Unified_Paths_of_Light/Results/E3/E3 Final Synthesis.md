# Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity — E3
## Final Synthesis

E3 reversed the original Unified Paths of Light anchor. Rather than beginning with Psalms and asking how other traditions compare, it began with the Qur'an, independently extracted its behavioural ethics, froze the resulting catalogue, and only then reopened cross-traditional comparison.

## Compression result

The E3 evidence architecture produced:

**420 audited source records**  
→ **100 provisional normalised principles**  
→ **89 frozen Qur'an-derived principles**  
→ cross-traditional source-quality audit  
→ **5 exact-proposition Universal, 17 Near-universal, 36 Common, 31 Limited**  
→ higher-order ethical compression  
→ **10 independently derived parent attractors**  
→ **10/10 secure across all eight traditions at parent level.**

The difference between the 89-principle prevalence distribution and the 10-attractor result is not contradictory. Exact operational rules diverge more than the higher-order ethical functions they implement.

## Frozen parent attractors

1. Non-Harm and Protection

4. Autonomy, Consent and Agency

6. Trust, Responsibility and Institutional Integrity
7. Peace, Reconciliation and Social Cohesion
8. Self-Regulation, Resilience and Proportionality
9. Stewardship and Responsible Resource Use
10. Dignity, Equality and Anti-Domination

## Central structural finding

Cross-traditional convergence appears layered:

**deep ethical attractor**  
→ **recurring ethical elaboration**  
→ **tradition-specific or civilisation-specific operational safeguard.**

Convergence is strongest at the first level and progressively weaker as formulations become more procedural and institution-specific.

## Anchor-robustness finding

The Qur'an-derived 89-principle catalogue was frozen before the Psalm corpus was reintroduced. Anchor reversal subsequently found substantive functional convergence for 71/89 principles at the broad A+B+C threshold, while residual differences remained.

The higher-order attractor analysis was likewise frozen before formal comparison with Blainey's Laws.

This provides two separation-controlled convergence tests:
1. Qur'an catalogue ↔ Psalm-derived ethics;
2. independently derived E3 parent attractors ↔ Concord Ethical Kernel.

## Interpretation

E3 does not demonstrate that all religions are ethically identical. They are not.

It does not demonstrate that the ten attractors are metaphysically true, uniquely correct, or exhaustive.

It does show that substantial ethical structure survives:
- reversal of the anchor tradition;
- independent extraction;
- normalisation and compression;
- source-quality remediation;
- refusal to force weak provenance into secure matches;
- preservation of residual disagreement.

The most defensible interpretation is therefore that E3 has identified a set of **stable cross-traditional ethical attractors** worthy of further testing as candidate civilisational primitives.
