## Evidential update
E7 is particularly informative because the primary boundary deliberately contained **internally divergent Chinese schools** rather than one doctrinal lineage:
- Confucian/Ru material;
- Daoist material;
- Mohist material.

These sources disagree materially about ritual, hierarchy, intervention, human cultivation, standards and political coordination. Yet blind compression produced a minimum-stable root architecture that functionally recovers the pre-frozen X kernel.

The result therefore strengthens the narrower hypothesis that differently articulated ethical traditions may encode recurrent functional constraints required for durable multi-agent cooperation.

## What E7 does not prove
E7 does not establish:
- metaphysical or physical natural law;
- historical independence among all traditions;
- exactly seven ultimate moral truths;
- exhaustive Chinese ethics;
- identity between Confucian, Daoist and Mohist philosophy;
- that every source passage supports every root;
- that ethical convergence alone guarantees civilisational survival.
