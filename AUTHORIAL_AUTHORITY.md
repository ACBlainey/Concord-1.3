# Concord — Root Authorial Authority

## Citizen Zero — Founder and Architect

**Alexander C. Blainey is Citizen Zero, Founder and Architect of Concord.** Citizen Zero retains full, unrestricted authorial authority over the authored Concord framework, including all repository documents, architecture, domains, interfaces, operational specifications, revisions, corrections, restructuring and development direction.

This authority is a **root-level authorship and development rule**, not an operational office, Steward vote, participation tier, or domain function. It does not depend on participant progression, appointment, accreditation or approval by an operational institution. No operational actor may invoke a bounded functional mandate to veto, capture or usurp Citizen Zero's authorial control of the framework.

## Delegated AI development authority

An AI development instance acting under Citizen Zero's instruction has framework-wide **authorial editing scope on Citizen Zero's behalf**, subject to the actual instruction, access permissions and applicable security safeguards. Its actions remain attributable and reviewable, with source history preserved. Such delegation is not independent sovereignty or a standing right to act contrary to Citizen Zero's directions.

## Boundary with operational authority

**An operationally defined function supplies the mandate necessary to perform its specified actions**, within its lawful conditions, safeguards and current state. A person, employee, service, AI actor or Steward has no vested authority outside the functions and decision states explicitly assigned to it. Ordinary execution within an existing functional mandate does not require a fresh Governance vote for each compliant act.

**Authorial editing and live operational execution are distinct.** Editing an operational rule does not itself cast a Steward vote, adjudicate a case, move funds or alter a live participant's state. Delegated authorial editing likewise does not grant an AI developer independent authority to perform those operational acts. A functioning implementation must apply the applicable lawful execution, adoption and state-transition mechanisms.

This root rule governs documentation and development across Concord. Operational documents specify their own functions and interfaces; they should not repeat Citizen Zero's title or authorial powers. Preserve source provenance and do not silently rewrite archived developmental records.

**To be designed at the appropriate implementation interface:** the exact mechanism by which authorial specification changes are adopted into a live Concord system without conflating repository editing with operational execution or overriding established participant protections.
