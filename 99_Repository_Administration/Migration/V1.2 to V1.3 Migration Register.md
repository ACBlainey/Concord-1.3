## 1. Governing rule

> **Migrate the Living Result; Archive the Developmental Path.**

V1.2 is not being cleaned into V1.3. V1.3 is a successor architecture reconstructed from the current civilisational state discovered during V1.2.

The preserved predecessor remains the authoritative record of its own developmental state.

- **M1 — MIGRATE CURRENT**
- **M2 — MIGRATE GRADUATED RESULT**

- **M4 — MIGRATE ESSENTIAL DEPENDENCY OR SUMMARY**
- **A1 — ARCHIVE IN PREDECESSOR**
- **A2 — ARCHIVE WITH LIVE REFERENCE**

## 3. Status uncertainty

Objects that plausibly became operational before later graduation methodology existed must not be silently demoted.

Use:

**LEGACY STATUS UNRESOLVED — POST-MIGRATION AUDIT REQUIRED**

where source evidence suggests current use but formal later-method certification is absent.

> **Absence of Later-Method Certification != Absence of Earlier Development or Operational Maturity**
