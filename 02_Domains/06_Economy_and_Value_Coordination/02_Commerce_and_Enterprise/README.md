# Commerce and Enterprise

**Status:** PROVISIONAL SUBDOMAIN SCAFFOLD / NON-CANONICAL / LARGELY UNDEVELOPED

## Persistent function

This subdomain concerns participant and enterprise-level productive and exchange activity.

Candidate scope includes:
- enterprise formation and operation;
- business/commercial participation;
- commercial exchange;
- enterprise identity and accountable control;
- commercial permissions/licensing;
- buyer/seller relationships;
- commercial records;
- enterprise lifecycle;
- interfaces with contracts, markets and employment.

## Existing foundations

Ratchet already recognises:
- marketplace exchange;
- contracts;
- consumer-protection requirements;
- marketplace collusion/manipulation;
- corporate participation;
- beneficial-control/responsibility records;
- protected commercial information;
- economic exit.

These are source inputs. Ratchet remains canonical Cross-Domain Architecture and is not duplicated here.

## Major unresolved architecture

- enterprise formation/dissolution;
- organisational/legal forms;
- commercial identity;
- licensing;
- commercial record architecture;
- insolvency/restructuring;
- enterprise succession;
- commercial accountability.

## Law boundary

Commerce operates within legal frameworks. Law owns general legal rules and obligations; Judiciary adjudicates disputes.

> **Commerce != Commercial Law.**

> **Enterprise Identity != Participant Civil Identity.**
