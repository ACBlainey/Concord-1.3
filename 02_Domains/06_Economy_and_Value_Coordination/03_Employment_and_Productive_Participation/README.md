# Employment and Productive Participation

**Status:** PROVISIONAL SUBDOMAIN SCAFFOLD / ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / NON-CANONICAL

## Persistent function

This subdomain concerns work, employment opportunity, role eligibility, productive participation and the interface between participant capability and available civic/private roles.

It is sector-neutral.

Common infrastructure may support:
- Concord/civic employment;
- private/commercial employment;
- apprenticeships;
- supervised work;
- qualification matching;
- role pathways;
- opportunity discovery.

## Current development anchor

`../../../01_Constitutional_Core/Provisional_Charters/Participation Architecture/Concord Employment Eligibility, Opportunity Matching and Role Pathways 001.md`

Current principles include:

> **Eligibility != Appointment.**

> **Targeted Exposure != Secret Employment Market.**

> **Aspiration Should Reveal a Path, Not Merely a Barrier.**

## Interfaces

- Participation Architecture supplies participation/eligibility state.
- Education supplies learning/qualification/competence evidence.
- Historical/Civil Historical Access supplies participant-visible records.
- Civil Contact supplies notifications.
- employing organisations retain legitimate hiring authority.
- Law supplies employment-law frameworks.
- Judiciary supplies dispute resolution.

Private employers should receive minimum necessary verification rather than unrestricted Civil Historical records.

> **Employment Verification != Civil Dossier Access.**
