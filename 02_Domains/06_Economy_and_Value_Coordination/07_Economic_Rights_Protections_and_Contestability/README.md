# Economic Rights, Protections and Contestability

**Status:** PROVISIONAL SUBDOMAIN SCAFFOLD / NON-CANONICAL / PARTIAL

## Persistent function

This subdomain concerns participant-facing protections where economic/commercial classifications, permissions, restrictions or processes materially affect participants.

Candidate scope:
- contestability of consequential economic classifications;
- correction of economic/employment records;
- economic exit;
- protection against inappropriate cross-domain reputation propagation;
- consumer-facing protections;
- employment-process contestability;
- commercial access/restriction review;
- anti-exploitation boundaries.

## Constitutional boundary

Fundamental rights are not created by this subdomain.

The controlling provisional rights work remains in:

`../../../01_Constitutional_Core/Provisional_Charters/`

Economic systems must operate within those constraints rather than redefining them around economic optimisation.

> **Economic Efficiency Does Not Override Fundamental Protected Standing.**
