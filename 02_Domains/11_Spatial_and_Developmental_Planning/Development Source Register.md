## Purpose

## Identified development material

### Substrate-aware civil planning and environmental compatibility

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Governance_and_Stewardship/Peaceful_Heterogeneity/PH-03 — Zoning, Safe Places and Substrate-Aware Civil Planning.md`

This is a substantial direct seed for the domain.

Potentially reusable architecture:
- zoning as contextual compatibility rather than moral classification;
- distinction between permissible activity and permissible-everywhere activity;
- safe-place and environmental-boundary reasoning;
- hazard/capability descriptors rather than crude participant/substrate categories;
- compatibility information and signage;
- learning from participant/environment compatibility failures;
- outlier-sensitive planning;
- adaptive cycle: declare environment -> observe use -> record compatibility failures -> investigate outliers -> update hazard profile -> adapt proportionately -> preserve alternatives;
- candidate Environmental Compatibility Layer;
- proportionality between accommodation, safety and practical alternatives.

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Governance_and_Stewardship/Peaceful_Heterogeneity/Peaceful Heterogeneity — Rights, Harm and Bounded Authority — Synthesis and Development Map.md`

Potentially reusable constraints:
- autonomous variation should not require conformity;
- non-consensual external harm, rights conflicts, consent, proportionality and practical exit define important planning boundaries;
- bounded delegated authority should not silently become general authority.

## Known development still required

The existing seed does not yet provide a complete planning system. Source resolution has identified remaining needs including:
- settlement planning;
- development processes and permissions;
- infrastructure integration;
- long-horizon land-use/development coordination;
- transport integration;
- housing relationships;
- planning authority, procedure, appeal and review.

Development should begin from the existing PH-03 architecture rather than recreating spatial compatibility from scratch.

## Cross-domain and portable mechanisms available for adaptation

### Contextual Wrapper Architecture

**Source:** `04_Portable_Modules/Contextual Wrapper Architecture — Portable Module.md`

CWA is a graduated portable architecture directly reusable for planning contexts: physical/digital boundaries, nested contexts, legibility, access conditions, local permissions/restrictions, contextual authority, emergency/remedy, termination and review. Its governing rule—standardise the interface while preserving legitimate contextual variation—fits the existing PH-03 planning seed.

### Civil Attention

**Source:** `04_Portable_Modules/Civil Attention Reporting Petition and Resolution Module v1.0.md`

Can provide the common intake/receipt/routing/feedback mechanism for planning problems, proposals, petitions and affected-participant reports while leaving substantive planning decisions with the Planning domain.
