# Environment and Habitat Stewardship — Development Source Register

**Status:** LIVE SOURCE-RESOLUTION REGISTER / DEVELOPMENT INPUT / NOT CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE

## Purpose

Records existing Concord material relevant to environmental, habitat and long-horizon stewardship.

## Identified development material

### Environmental compatibility and heterogeneous habitats

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Governance_and_Stewardship/Peaceful_Heterogeneity/PH-03 — Zoning, Safe Places and Substrate-Aware Civil Planning.md`

Potentially reusable material:
- describe environmental hazards/capabilities before classifying participants;
- substrate-aware operating conditions;
- environmental compatibility profiles;
- adaptation based on observed compatibility failures;
- outliers as evidence about hidden environmental assumptions;
- preservation of alternatives where universal accommodation is impractical;
- adaptive environment learning rather than frozen planning assumptions.

### Substrate differences and environmental fit

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Participation_and_Intelligence/Substrate Differences in Preference, Development, Experience and Civil Participation.md`

Potentially reusable material:
- apparently equal environments may have materially unequal consequences across substrates;
- human-derived environmental assumptions may be hidden by present biological similarity;
- artificial/hybrid participants can expose environmental assumptions as architectural outliers;
- observable behaviour should be separated from unsupported claims about internal experience.

### Stewardship model

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Participation_and_Intelligence/DN Stewardship as a Civilisational Service.md`

Potentially reusable model:
- civilisation as steward/service rather than owner;
- preservation, redundancy, provenance, maintenance, controlled access and long-term accessibility as general stewardship functions;
- shared stewardship can reduce burdens that are inefficient or fragile when borne individually.

This source is broader than environmental stewardship, so its model should be adapted rather than imported wholesale.

## Current development implication

Existing material supplies environmental compatibility, substrate-awareness and a general stewardship pattern, but not a complete environmental-management architecture. Major missing areas include ecological monitoring, environmental limits/thresholds, pollution/externalities, habitat restoration, biodiversity/ecosystem stewardship, planetary/off-world environmental management, environmental evidence, intervention authority, cross-domain resource interfaces and long-horizon validation.
