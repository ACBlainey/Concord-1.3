## Purpose

Civilisation Clock Queue Test 001 selected L2-14 for retest because two of its material supporting functions have recently become operational at bounded scale:

- L1-02 Cross-System Dependency and Criticality Management now has live KCS dependency records;
- L1-03 Civilisational State and Transition Representation now consumes those records through the Civil State Map.

L2-14 was not classified as dependency-blocked. The question is therefore different from R1 and R2:

> Does the new dependency/state infrastructure materially improve the integration maturity of civilisational coordination under partial failure, and if so, what residual remains?

This retest is specifically designed to distinguish **upstream blocker removal** from **distributed-function integration improvement**.

L2-14 was classified:

Its supply is distributed across:

- continuity and recovery architecture;

Its recorded material dependencies are:

- L1-12 Cross-System Failure, Resilience and Safeguard Recursion;
- L1-03 Civilisational State and Transition Representation.

The current KCS records also show that L1-12 depends materially on L1-02.

# 2. Minimum Function

A minimum partial-failure coordination capability should allow the civilisation to:

1. identify that a failure is local rather than necessarily global;
2. identify materially dependent functions;
3. distinguish degraded, blocking and failed dependencies;
4. identify alternatives or recovery paths where represented;
5. maintain current state during degradation;
6. coordinate temporary response without silently expanding permanent authority;
7. preserve provenance of the failure and response;
8. propagate corrections/recovery state to material dependents;
9. allow unaffected systems to continue where safe;
10. support post-event learning and revision.

This is coordination under partial failure, not a guarantee that all failures are survivable.

# 4. Effect of New Infrastructure

The new infrastructure materially improves L2-14 in three ways.

### First: failure dependencies can now be explicit

Before KCS operational population, partial-failure coordination relied more heavily on distributed narrative architecture.

The dependency graph now supplies a common representation.

### Second: affected and unaffected functions can be distinguished

Selective propagation prevents a local failure from automatically invalidating the whole civil state.

### Third: recovery can feed a common State Map

The State Map can now represent dependency changes and submit resulting development/review work to the Clock.

These are genuine integration gains.

# 7. Residual Located

The first surviving residual is not another civilisational architecture.

It is an **operational integration/test deficit**:

> Concord has the distributed functions and now has enough shared dependency/state grammar to coordinate them, but has not yet exercised a bounded end-to-end partial-failure scenario.

The minimum next intervention is therefore a test, not a new system.

# 8. Candidate Partial-Failure Test

A future bounded test should select one represented dependency and simulate:

1. owner/capability failure;
2. KCS state transition to DEGRADED or FAILED;
3. identification of direct material dependents;
4. alternative/recovery-path search;
5. determination of unaffected functions;
6. temporary coordination if required;
7. bounded authority check;
8. recovery or substitution;
9. KCS/State Map update;
10. correction/learning propagation;
11. Clock reassessment.

This would test L2-14 much more strongly than another conceptual document.
