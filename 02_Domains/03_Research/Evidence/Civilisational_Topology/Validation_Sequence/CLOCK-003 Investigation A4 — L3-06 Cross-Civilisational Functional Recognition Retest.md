**Status:** CANONICAL RETEST — FROZEN RESULT  
**Date:** September 2026

## Question

Does CBERRM materially change the previous hold on L3-06 Cross-Civilisational Functional Recognition?

## Evidence

The Intercivilisational Architecture already distinguishes:
- communication from sovereign recognition;
- wrapper compatibility from political endorsement;
- rights/personhood standing from sovereignty;
- operational interlocution from state recognition;
- functional cooperation from common government.

Emergent Polities, Sentient Secession and Recognition explicitly develops recognition at multiple resolutions, including operational interlocutor and limited diplomatic interface before sovereign recognition.

CBERRM now supplies a concrete consequential use case: a party may need functional standing in an externality process even while its political, legal or personhood status remains disputed.

## Result

CBERRM closes part of the practical interface deficit.

A civilisation can now represent:

**Functional Contact / Affected Standing / Operational Interlocution**

without inferring:

**Sovereign Recognition / General Jurisdiction / Political Endorsement.**

However, semantic/constitutional equivalence across radically heterogeneous civilisations remains immature, and the recognition paper itself remains active development rather than fully validated architecture.

### Revised state

**L3-06: PRESENT-DISTRIBUTED / DEVELOPING / HOLD PARTIALLY CLEARED.**

It is no longer blocked merely by absence of a concrete functional-recognition use case.

It remains developmentally dependent on semantic/equivalence work for higher-confidence general operation.
