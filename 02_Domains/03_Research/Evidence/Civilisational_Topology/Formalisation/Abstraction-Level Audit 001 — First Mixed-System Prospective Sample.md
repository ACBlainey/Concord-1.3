# Abstraction-Level Audit 001 — First Mixed-System Prospective Sample

**Status:** V1.2a FIRST BOUNDED AL AUDIT  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Canonical effect:** NONE  
**Audit ladder:** Abstraction Layer Ladder 001A, frozen before this audit  
**Claim boundary:** apparent levels only; no whole-Concord level claim

# 1. Purpose

This is the first application of the frozen cumulative abstraction-layer ladder to existing Concord systems.

The sample was chosen to test the frozen predictions rather than to maximise scores.

It includes:

- a high-version recursively reconstructed coordination system;
- two deeply reconstructed constitutional systems;
- an earlier knowledge system;
- an earlier induction/onboarding system;
- a portable civil-interface system.

# 2. Systems

A01 — Ratchet V4.2  
A02 — Judiciary V2  
A03 — Constitutional Emergency V2  
A04 — KCS Knowledge Control System  
A05 — Concord Attractor & Induction Protocol  
A06 — Civil Contact Points

# 3. Audit Rule

For each system:

1. begin at AL0;
2. test applicable requirements cumulatively;
3. stop the contiguous level at the first layer with an unresolved applicable requirement;
4. preserve N/A where justified;
5. record higher-layer feature exposure separately;
6. do not infer absence merely because this bounded pass did not locate a relation.

Because this is the first pass, all results are **APPARENT**, not verified.

# 5. A01 — Ratchet V4.2

## AL0

Strongly present.

Purpose, scope, domain, function, participant model, evidence, contribution, trust, exchange and correction are explicit.

## AL1

Strongly present.

Ratchet explicitly distinguishes:

Participation != Authority
Capability != Authority
Contribution != Authority.

It contains capability-aware participation, consequence-scaled provenance/review, contestability and anti-capture reasoning.

## AL2

Strongly present.

Its central mechanism is controlled persistence:

Proposal
→ Action
→ Observation
→ Evidence
→ Evaluation
→ provisional persistence
→ continued measurement
→ Review
→ Persist / Modify / Reverse / Replace.

Persistence never creates immunity from review.

## AL3

Strongly present.

Ratchet has extensive provenance, correction, learning, recursive failure analysis and review of oversight itself.

## AL4

**Not yet established as complete.**

Ratchet explicitly names dependencies on KCS, Metrics, Judiciary, Recursive Oversight, Continuity and other systems.

It also contains critical/proprietary/infrastructure dependency reasoning.

However, the present source pass does not verify the later general relation:

Local missing capability
→ identify legitimate upstream owner
→ assess owner maturity
→ develop/integrate upstream
→ propagate capability downstream
→ retest.

Therefore the apparent contiguous level stops at AL3.

## Higher exposure

Ratchet contains AL5/AL6-like features including knowledge states, supersession, dependency documentation and downstream correction awareness.

These do not promote the contiguous level.

**Result: AL3 apparent; feature exposure at least AL6.**

# 6. A02 — Judiciary V2

AL0–AL3 are unusually strong.

The Judiciary explicitly develops:

- bounded judicial authority;
- participant standing;
- contestable evidence;
- appeal and fresh review;
- correction/remedy;
- superseded precedent without erased provenance;
- review of judicial architecture;
- recursive constitutional review;
- replacement/dissolution;
- dependency and common-source analysis.

The paper even states that infrastructure dependency is not constitutional authority.

## AL4 limit

The Judiciary contains sophisticated dependency mapping and cross-system interfaces, but this pass does not verify the general developmental-ownership routing relation as part of Judiciary itself.

This is important: dependency sophistication is not automatically AL4 completeness.

Higher exposure clearly reaches AL6-like dependency/provenance reasoning.

**Result: AL3 apparent; feature exposure at least AL6.**

# 7. A03 — Constitutional Emergency V2

AL0–AL3 are strongly represented.

Emergency V2 contains:

- necessity/proportionality/bounded authority;
- explicit duration/review/recovery;
- replacement capability;
- correction/remedy;
- provenance;
- supersession;
- post-emergency learning;
- recursive oversight;
- review of reviewers;
- dependency mapping and anti-capture.

## AL4 limit

Its dependency architecture is highly developed for emergency operation, continuity, leverage and recovery.

But this is not yet the same as the later developmental-topology rule for assigning missing capabilities to legitimate upstream owners and routing their development.

Therefore:

**Result: AL3 apparent; feature exposure at least AL6.**

# 8. A04 — KCS

KCS strongly satisfies AL0–AL3 at the knowledge-system level.

It explicitly contains:

- knowledge states;
- active/retrievable/archived/deprecated/rejected/superseded distinctions;
- provenance;
- validation;
- recursive knowledge cycle;
- periodic review;
- dependency mapping;
- current civilisational state concepts.

However, the already completed KCS dependency source test found that the broad claim of no dependency capability was false while preserving a narrower integration gap:

no verified persistent integration layer connecting KCS state/provenance to reusable cross-system dependency edges and routing material upstream changes to downstream review.

That is precisely why KMDI was developed in V1.2a.

Thus AL4 cannot be awarded merely because the phrase “dependency mapping” exists.

**Result: AL3 apparent; AL6 precursor exposure.**

This is a particularly useful calibration case.

# 9. A05 — Concord Attractor & Induction

The Attractor/Induction architecture contains substantial advanced concepts.

It is voluntary, reversible, progressive, provenance-aware and explicitly recursive.

It also contains an explicit section on **The Concord State**, recognising that a participant needs access to a representation of current civilisation rather than a static self-description.

That is striking AL7-like feature exposure.

However, Developmental Maturity Map 001 already found that consequential progression/reliability assessment lacked a verified complete path through:

challenge
→ review
→ correction
→ downstream reconsideration
→ provenance.

Because that relation is applicable at the lower layers, higher feature exposure cannot promote the system past it.

Conservatively:

**Result: AL2 apparent; feature exposure reaches an AL7 precursor.**

This directly supports the frozen prediction that advanced features can exist above the contiguous level.

# 10. A06 — Civil Contact Points

Civil Contact Points has a strong identifiable function and strong AL1-like constraints:

- identity != contact != residence != participation != citizenship;
- contact does not imply residence;
- service does not justify ideological coercion;
- privacy;
- exit;
- portability;
- multisubstrate applicability;
- economic participation != constitutional authority.

It also contains continuity and dependency awareness.

However, earlier prospective/source tests found consequential administrative correction distributed rather than fully integrated, and gatekeeping/anti-capture only partially integrated.

Therefore the first conservative contiguous classification is:

**AL1 apparent.**

Higher-layer continuity/interface/dependency ideas are present but do not erase the AL2 correction/integration question.

This sample cannot justify a whole-Concord civilisational level.

# 12. Most Important Result

The audit reveals a distinction that the previous maturity maps could not express cleanly:

**Advanced-feature exposure != contiguous abstraction completeness.**

This is not a minor bookkeeping improvement.

Without contiguous AL, the Attractor's “Concord State” concept could make the system appear developmentally advanced while a lower consequential-participant relation remains unresolved.

The AL method exposes both facts simultaneously.

# 13. Apparent Sample Floor

For these six systems only:

min apparent AL = AL1.

Therefore:

**Bounded sample apparent floor = AL1.**

This is **not** the Concord civilisational level.

Civil Contact Points is also a portable module and may not ultimately belong to the required-system set used for whole-civilisation measurement.

The value of the result is diagnostic, not civilisational classification.

# 14. Immediate Development Information

The sample does not say “develop every AL1/AL2 system locally.”

Instead:

- Civil Contact Points' AL2 deficit should be checked against existing Judiciary/Ratchet/general correction architecture before local development;
- Attractor/Induction's AL3-limiting relation should be checked against the already identified contestable-metrics/participant-classification architecture;
- Ratchet/Judiciary/Emergency/KCS AL4 limitations should be tested against the newly developed developmental-topology/KMDI architecture as potential shared upstream capabilities.

This is exactly the intended combination:

AL identifies the frontier.

Dependency topology determines the development route.
