# 1. Question

Does Multisubstrate Economic Participation convert competence/reliability/responsibility assessments into consequential economic authority without an adequate challenge, correction and downstream reconsideration architecture?

# 2. Local Source Result

The source clearly activates the trigger.

Its central question is:

> What can this participant reliably do, and what authority is justified by demonstrated responsibility?

Progression depends upon demonstrated capability, reliability, responsibility, safety, contractual performance and domain-specific trust.

It explicitly states:

> Authority should not increase faster than demonstrated responsibility.

It then identifies graduated capabilities including wallet access, transaction authority, contractual capability, commercial authority, business operation, resource management, employment, investment and full economic participation.

Therefore participant-specific assessments can materially affect operational and economic capability.

# 3. Local Safeguards

The paper already contains important protections:

- domain-specific rather than universal reputation;
- provenance separated from value;
- independent verification;
- economic capability does not automatically create constitutional authority;
- participation is reversible;
- participants can contest decisions;
- participants can request review.

This means CH004 was never a candidate for a total absence of review.

The narrower prospective question was whether successful challenge/review explicitly propagates into correction of the downstream economic state.

The local paper does not substantially specify that propagation.

# 4. Wider Architecture Search — Ratchet V4.2

The source states that the material originated in Ratchet development and that Ratchet should retain the economic interface.

The current canonical Ratchet V4.2 therefore provides the most important external source check.

Ratchet V4.2 contains a much more developed architecture.

## 4.1 Contextual assessment and bounded consequence

Ratchet explicitly rejects:

Evidence
→ Universal person score
→ Universal permissions
→ Permanent social classification.

It instead specifies:

Evidence
→ Contextual assessment
→ Defined decision
→ Bounded consequence
→ Review.

This is an almost direct instantiation of the topology-derived consequential-classification grammar.

## 4.2 Recoverable trust

Ratchet specifies:

Observed behaviour
→ Trust assessment
→ Failure
→ Proportionate restriction
→ Correction
→ Demonstrated reliability
→ Trust recovery.

Thus correction can alter the consequential trust/permission state.

## 4.3 Appeal and correction

Ratchet has an explicit section:

**Appeal and Correction**

and states:

> Participants must be able to challenge significant Ratchet assessments affecting them.

Processes may include:

- correction requests;
- evidence submission;
- independent review;
- appeal;
- adjudication;
- rehabilitation pathways.

The strength of review scales with consequence.

A major exclusion or high-value economic judgement may require substantial procedural protection.

## 4.4 Reversible operational permissions

Ratchet states that operational permissions may be reduced where evidence shows unreliability.

Restrictions should be:

- proportionate;
- contextual;
- reviewable;
- recoverable where appropriate.

It also explicitly preserves unresolved disputes during economic exit.

## 4.5 Governance and escalation

Ratchet governance may determine dispute procedures.

Governance must provide reasoning, preserve evidence, accept review and remain accountable.

Its subsidiarity architecture routes:

local consequence -> local decision where possible;

cross-community consequence -> shared coordination;

system-wide consequence -> system-level governance;

constitutional consequence -> constitutional review.

# 7. Representation Lesson

But the paper is explicitly a companion development paper and states:

> Ratchet V3 should retain only the economic interface.

Therefore the maturity model must distinguish:

**Document maturity**

from:

**System-family maturity**.

PartialDocument(S_d)
+ ExplicitInterface(S_d,S_c)

→ DoNotInferSystemGap(S_d).
