# 1. Purpose

This is the first quantitative analysis performed after the prospective Civilisational Topology hypothesis was frozen.

The purpose is not to prove that the Concord has a fractal topology or a unique attractor.

The immediate questions are narrower:

1. Does the frozen EKC root-to-architecture layer contain measurable structure?
2. Are the earlier informal numerical observations reproducible?
3. Can the formal representation expose relationships that are difficult to see from the ten derivation documents independently?
4. What can and cannot be tested from this first layer?

Across the 8 × 9 complete-case matrix there are 72 possible root-to-architecture cells.

| Threshold | Active cells | Density |

| ≥2.5 | 14 | 0.194 |

This is a very dense bipartite layer.

That matters methodologically.

At the weak/active threshold almost every root touches almost every architecture, so binary connectivity alone contains little discriminatory information.

The useful topology lies primarily in:

- activation strength;
- combinations;
- relative profiles;
- cross-layer relationships not yet encoded.

This is an early indication that a mature Civilisational Topology system should preserve weighted and typed edges rather than reduce the civilisation to connected/not-connected.

# 7. First Demonstrated Topology Capability

The first operational capability prediction can now be stated more concretely.

The topology has exposed that:

> **Information boundaries, governance authority, emergency authority and intercivilisational relations occupy strikingly similar coordinates in the U1–U8 root space despite being substantively different civilisational domains.**

That observation was not encoded as a conclusion in the individual EKC derivations.

The integrated matrix makes it immediately detectable.

The correct next question is not “therefore these are the same system.”

It is:

> **What recurrent architectural motif causes these different domains to occupy similar ethical coordinates?**

Candidate motifs visible in their frozen architecture names and derivations include boundedness, contestability/corrigibility, non-domination, accountability, and preservation of cooperative relations.

This must be checked against the source derivations before being formalised as a motif.

---

# 8. A Second Structural Signal

A3 Adjudication and A7 Participation also show very high root-profile similarity.

Their surface functions differ considerably:

- adjudication resolves contested harm/wrongdoing;
- participation establishes standing across substrates/capabilities.

Their similarity therefore suggests another test:

> Do both instantiate a deeper motif of agency under asymmetric capability or contested standing, bounded by evidence and correction?

Again, the topology does not answer the question.

It identifies where comparative reading should be concentrated.

This is an important distinction between **diagnostic topology** and automated interpretation.

---

# 9. What the First Dataset Cannot Yet Detect

The current graph cannot honestly test:

- cross-system dependency failure;
- correlated infrastructure failure;
- safeguard recursion;
- participant need-to-development paths;
- continuity paths outside A8;
- temporal architectural drift;
- capture propagation;
- self-application of the topology system.

Those relations are not present in the frozen root-to-architecture layer.

Pretending to detect them would violate ESCP and the dataset provenance rule.

Therefore the first capability test is positive but narrow:

> **Integrated representation exposes cross-domain structural similarity and root-coordinate families that are not obvious from isolated derivations.**

The stronger structural-hole and dependency claims remain untested.
