# 2. Family F1

The four architectures are:

- **A1 — Bounded, Purpose-Linked and Contestable Information**
- **A2 — Function-Bounded, Corrigible and Regenerative Authority**
- **A9 — Necessity-Bounded Emergency Bridge**
- **A10 — Plural Coexistence and Interoperability**

Their surface domains are markedly different:

- information;
- ordinary governance;
- emergency power;
- relations between distinct civilisations.

Yet the frozen derivations show substantial recurrence.

---

# 3. F1 Motif M01 — Legitimate Function Before Scope

## A1
Information acquisition, retention, combination, inference, sharing and use must relate to an ethically legitimate function. Scope and duration are proportionate to that function. Materially different reuse requires renewed justification.

## A2
Every consequential authority must identify the legitimate function that justifies its power. Authority extends no further than reasonably required to perform that function. Powers outside the mandate require separate justification.

## A9
The architecture explicitly contains:

Threat Recognition
→ Necessity-Gap Assessment
→ Exceptional Function Definition
→ Minimum Necessary Authority.

Exceptional authority therefore follows the identified function rather than preceding it.

## A10
The architecture uses **Domain-Scoped Interface**: define the minimum shared architecture required for a function. Cooperation does not create unlimited integration or supremacy.

## Motif

[
Legitimate Need
ightarrow
Defined Function
ightarrow
Bounded Scope
]

This motif is strongly supported across all four F1 architectures.

### Candidate name

# **M01 — Function-Bounded Scope**

---

# 4. F1 Motif M02 — Power Does Not Self-Justify

## A1
Technical ability to acquire or use information does not itself legitimate acquisition or use. Informational asymmetry activates stronger accountability.

## A2
Authority-holder preference does not establish legitimacy. Expertise can create function-specific epistemic weight but not general sovereignty.

## A9
Severe threat does not itself grant unspecified emergency authority. The necessity gap must be demonstrated and the exceptional function separately defined.

## A10
Cooperation, difference or sovereignty does not justify domination. The architecture includes asymmetry/dependency review to detect domination hidden inside formally voluntary cooperation.

## Motif

[
Capability/Power

otRightarrow
Legitimate Scope
]

Power requires an independent functional and ethical justification.

### Candidate name

# **M02 — Non-Self-Justifying Power**

# 19. Are F1 and F2 Actually Different?

At first sight they are two motif families.

But a deeper comparison shows overlap.

F1 concerns **legitimate power/flow**.

F2 concerns **legitimate differentiation among participants**.

Both contain:

- bounded scope;
- evidence sensitivity;
- non-domination;
- practical contestability;
- accountability;
- correction;
- temporal revisability.

Agency + Boundary + Interaction + Accountability + Correction + Continuity.

The source-grounded analysis therefore **refines rather than simply confirms** the visual hypothesis.
