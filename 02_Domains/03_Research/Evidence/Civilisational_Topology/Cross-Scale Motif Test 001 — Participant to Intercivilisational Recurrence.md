# 10. Is This Fractality?

Not yet in the strict mathematical sense.

The result supports **architectural self-similarity of relational grammar**.

It does not establish:

- a fractal dimension;
- scale-free degree distribution;
- power-law scaling;
- exact graph isomorphism;
- infinite recursive depth;
- statistical self-similarity under arbitrary rescaling.

Therefore H3 remains open.

However, the result now gives the older Concord use of “fractal” a much more precise candidate interpretation:

> **Fractal architecture may mean recurrence of a stable relational grammar under transformations of scale, while concrete actors, institutions and mechanisms change.**

This is testable.
