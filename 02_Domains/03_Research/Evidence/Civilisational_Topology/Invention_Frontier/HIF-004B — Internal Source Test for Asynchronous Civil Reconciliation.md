# Classification

HIF-004 does **not** survive intact as a wholly absent architecture, because most of its normative constraints already exist.

However its operational core remains unresolved.

Promote narrower residual:

# **HIF-004-R1 — Partition-Tolerant Civil Event Reconciliation**

> When two or more independently legitimate civil actors or systems produce consequential events while unable to observe one another's current state, how can those event histories later be reconciled without silent loss, duplicate consequence, retroactive fabrication of agreement, or unjust consequence to participants caused by the partition itself?

Required inherited constraints:

- preserve both histories and provenance;
- distinguish copies from consequences;
- preserve disputes;
- make correction additive/visible rather than silent;
- protect participants from infrastructure-caused fault;
- escalate irreducible conflicts to bounded review;
- preserve function where safe while reconciliation is pending.
