The experiment now shows why periodic recursion matters.

# 8. Natural Brake

A higher abstraction is useful only if it does at least one of:

- finds an existing solution;
- compresses multiple residuals;
- predicts a missing relation;
- produces a falsifiable capability requirement;
- improves cross-domain reuse;
- exposes a dependency;
- generates a testable candidate architecture.

If it does none of these, abstraction has ceased to add operational information.
