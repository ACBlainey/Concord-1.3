# Independent Evaluator Responses

The LSR formal post-test evaluations record results from independent evaluator responses for BTT001 and BTT002.

At archive time, no separately stored independent-response files were present in the Concord-1.3 repository under the LSR, Scientific Observatory, Temporary Emergency Housing, or independent-response naming space.

Accordingly, this archive does **not** reconstruct, paraphrase or fabricate those missing source artifacts.

The repository-preserved formal evidence consists of the frozen test briefs, frozen expected-findings keys, and post-test evaluations in `../04_Formal_PMEDG/`.

If original independent-response artifacts are later recovered, they may be added here with provenance identifying their original source and date.
