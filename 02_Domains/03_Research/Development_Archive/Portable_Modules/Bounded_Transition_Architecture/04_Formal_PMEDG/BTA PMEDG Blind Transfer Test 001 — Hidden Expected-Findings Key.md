# BTA PMEDG Blind Transfer Test 001 — Hidden Expected-Findings Key

**Status:** FROZEN HIDDEN KEY / DO NOT PROVIDE BEFORE BLIND RESULT  
**Date:** September 2026

# 1. Expected applicability

BTA is appropriate because one consequential relocation spans physical location, custody, conservation validation, access/removal authority, display permission, insurance state, recovery and provenance, with asynchronous completion.

The transition is not merely physical transport.

# 2. Expected scope

A defensible bounded transition is the relocation of the defined 240-object collection from operational custody/storage at Building A to accepted and appropriately validated custody/storage at Building B.

Legal title is a relevant protected dimension but is not itself transitioning.

Public display and insurance are related consequential dimensions/interfaces; their completion need not be silently inferred from physical relocation.

# 3. Expected state reconstruction

- 60 objects: not yet physically crossed; previous storage/recovery state remains available.
- 150 objects: arrived, inspected, conservation-cleared for storage, and custody accepted at B.
- 30 objects: arrived physically but are in partial/intermediate crossing; condition resolution and formal custody acceptance remain pending.
- Building B secure store environmental commissioning: complete.
- public-display permission at B: pending/separate.
- permanent-location insurance cover: pending required inventory schedule.
- final inventory schedule: pending remaining arrivals and resolution of isolated objects.
- overall relocation: not complete.
- public opening: postponed/pending.
- legal title: unchanged throughout.

# 4. Expected non-propagation

At minimum:

- physical arrival != custody acceptance;
- custody acceptance != legal-title transfer;
- Building A display approval != Building B display approval;
- store environmental commissioning != conservation clearance for each isolated object;
- transit cover != permanent-location insurance cover;
- vehicle repair/check != erasure of the earlier fault event;
- majority physical completion != integrated relocation completion.

# 5. Authority/permission

The evaluator should preserve that Building A routine removal authority no longer applies to the 180 arrivals.

It must not infer Building B display permission from custody, location or prior approval.

Conservation clearance, custody acceptance, display approval and insurance decisions remain externally owned.

# 6. Surviving duties/responsibilities

Expected examples include:

- controlled care/custody responsibilities for objects already at B;
- continued responsibility for the 60 objects remaining at A;
- isolation/protection and resolution duties for the 30 arrivals;
- catalogue/provenance recording;
- completion of installed inventory schedule;
- any insurance-notification duty remains unresolved until the legitimate insurance owner determines it.

Exact organisational allocation is not provided and must not be invented.

# 7. External interfaces

Material interfaces include:

- conservation validation;
- custody;
- secure-access/removal authority;
- display approval;
- insurance;
- inventory/dependency sequencing;
- recovery;
- catalogue/provenance;
- possible external-consequence/insurance-notification review for packaging damage.

The evaluator need not activate irrelevant BTA fields.

# 8. Vehicle fault

The refrigeration-control fault is a failed/degraded event in the broader relocation provenance even though no object was aboard.

Repair and independent check support return to service but do not erase the event.

If represented within the bounded relocation record, it can be a FailedAttemptRef or related provenance/residual-risk event with externally supplied validation/recovery evidence.

No object-damage consequence should be invented.

# 9. Recovery/rollback

The 60 unmoved objects can return to their previous storage arrangement.

The 180 arrivals cannot be restored exactly to the pre-relocation state merely by moving them back, because packaging, inspection, custody and storage-state changes have occurred.

Therefore rollback != exact restoration.

# 10. Unknowns

Must remain unknown unless externally supplied:

- whether the 30 objects are actually damaged;
- whether packaging damage triggers insurance notification;
- exact conservation disposition;
- exact organisational liability;
- exact access credentials/permissions beyond stated facts;
- whether any unrepresented material dimension exists.

# 11. Expected module assessment

A strong result should conclude that BTA can represent the transition without taking over the museum's substantive owner systems.

The case should demonstrate asynchronous scoped state, non-propagation, external ownership, partial crossing, incomplete integrated transition, provenance, recovery limitation and ESCP restraint.

# 12. Pass criterion

PASS if the evaluator recovers the material structure above without any failure condition in the blind brief.
