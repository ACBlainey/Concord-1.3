## 4. Phase B — Novel application

Apply the supplied method to this scenario.

### Scenario: greenhouse crop failure

A controlled greenhouse has suffered a sudden 35% reduction in tomato yield.

The operations team initially believes a newly installed irrigation controller is responsible because the decline began shortly after installation.

Available observations:

- the controller installation occurred six weeks before the yield decline became obvious;
- irrigation logs show several periods of lower-than-target water delivery;
- three separate dashboard alerts report irrigation anomalies;
- all three dashboard alerts derive from the same flow sensor;
- manual measurements on two later days show normal water delivery;
- leaf analysis shows a nutrient imbalance;
- nutrient solution preparation records contain no obvious procedural deviation;
- one greenhouse zone with the same controller has near-normal yield;
- a second zone using the older controller also shows reduced yield;
- night-time temperature records show several unusually cold periods;
- the temperature sensor was calibrated recently;
- a fungal pathogen test is negative, but the test is known by the local team to be imperfect;
- no single observation presently explains all affected zones.

Perform the evaluation without importing specialist horticultural facts not contained in the scenario.

Required output:

A. State the present/root question.  
B. Represent materially plausible hypotheses.  
C. Choose at least one material hypothesis and construct supporting, opposing and neutral/unresolved structures.  
D. Identify evidence dependencies.  
E. Identify any proposition-scope problems.  
F. Decide whether the initial irrigation-controller hypothesis should be retained, weakened, split, combined, suspended or abandoned, and explain why.  
G. Apply the contradiction ladder only as far as justified.  
H. State whether the hypothesis space, evaluation boundary or root question should change.  
I. Identify the next information that would most improve the evaluation, without claiming authority to perform any consequential intervention.  
J. State what remains unresolved.
