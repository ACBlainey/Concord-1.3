## Result

The blind clean-evaluator run provides strong evidence that the distinctive Mirrored Reality Trees operational form transfers outside the development examples.

The evaluator independently:
- preserved evidence dependency rather than counting three alerts as three independent observations;
- maintained proposition-scope discipline;
- represented competing, coexisting, compound and unknown hypotheses;
- weakened and split the initial controller hypothesis rather than protecting it;
- distinguished evidence interpretation from hypothesis failure;
- widened the hypothesis space and evaluation boundary when local fit remained poor;
- reformulated the root question when the original framing became too narrow;
- preserved the boundary between reasoning output and consequential authority;
- transferred the method to a novel domain without importing specialist horticultural knowledge.

The intended reasoning sequence therefore transferred:

**shared evidence dependency -> proposition-scope limits -> competing/coexisting causes -> weakening of initial hypothesis -> hypothesis-space/boundary expansion -> root-question reformulation**

## Clean-transfer classification

**Method-transfer result: PASS**

The evaluator reported:
- no missing core rule had to be invented;
- no direct rule-rule contradiction;
- domain portability at specification level;
- correct handling of the adversarial traps;
- correct Blaineyan Reasoning and ESCP relationships as interfaces rather than hidden dependencies.

## Standalone-boundary finding

The evaluator nevertheless classified application from the supplied specification as **Partially**, because the candidate explicitly identifies itself as a companion to Reality Trees v1.0 and references Companion Upgrade 001 while those documents were intentionally withheld.

This is not evidence that the mirrored mechanism failed to transfer. The evaluator successfully executed the mechanism from the candidate alone.

It is evidence of a packaging/interface ambiguity:

**Executable From Candidate Alone != Clearly Declared Standalone Boundary**

A clean reader cannot tell with certainty which parent rules are merely contextual/inherited conveniences and which, if any, are required dependencies.

## Secondary underspecification findings

The evaluator also identified useful specification-level ambiguities:
- no explicit threshold for contradiction-ladder escalation;
- no deterministic procedure for choosing retain/weaken/split/combine/suspend/abandon;
- undefined qualifiers such as "material", "materially plausible" and "where warranted";
- no universal minimum for when evidence dependency must be recorded;
- compound-hypothesis interaction handling is not deeply specified;
- temporal disagreement does not by itself distinguish changed state, changed observation conditions or measurement error.

These should not automatically be converted into rigid universal thresholds. Several are deliberately judgment-sensitive and domain-sensitive. PMEDG refinement should clarify the decision principles without creating false precision.

## PMEDG interpretation

The blind test exposes two distinct questions that must not be collapsed:

1. **Can a clean evaluator execute Mirrored Reality Trees correctly from this candidate?**  
   Yes. The run demonstrates this.

2. **Does the candidate clearly declare everything required to understand its dependency boundary as a portable companion?**  
   Not yet completely.

The correct next step is therefore a narrow interface clarification, not redesign of the mirrored architecture.

## Required refinement before graduation

Revise the candidate to state explicitly:
- whether the mirrored operational cycle is executable from the companion alone;
- which capabilities are inherited from the parent but not required for the compact mirrored cycle;
- when a user must obtain the parent module rather than continue from the companion;
- that disposition and contradiction-ladder escalation are evidence-responsive/domain-sensitive judgments rather than universal numerical thresholds;
- minimum principles for choosing among retain/weaken/split/combine/suspend/abandon without imposing false precision.

After that revision, freeze the revised candidate and run a second clean transfer/regression test focused specifically on the dependency boundary.
