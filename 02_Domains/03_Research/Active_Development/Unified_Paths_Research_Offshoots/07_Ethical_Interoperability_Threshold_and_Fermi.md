# Ethical Interoperability Threshold and the Fermi Question
## Speculative Research Offshoot

### Core hypothesis
There may be a minimum ethical/civilisational architecture required for stable interoperability between independently developed high-capability intelligences.

Such an architecture might include sufficient constraints on harm, deception, domination, resource conflict, uncorrectable power and developmental asymmetry to permit reliable recognition, negotiation and coexistence.

### Testable form without assuming extraterrestrial contact
Ask what minimum ethical functions two independently developed advanced agents would require to sustain:
- communication;
- treaty formation;
- reciprocal assurance;
- resource exchange;
- non-domination;
- conflict de-escalation;
- correction after error.

### Fermi extension
If advanced extraterrestrial civilisations exist, an interoperability threshold could be explored as one possible filter on open contact.

This is presently speculative and is not evidence that extraterrestrial contact occurs.

### Quarantined speculative branches
Ideas concerning selective contact with ethically developed individuals, removal from Earth, teleportation, simulation-level intervention, metaphysical agents or “prison planet” interpretations require independent evidence and must not be treated as deductions from the ethics programme.

### Development value
The rigorous component is the interoperability-threshold problem. The Fermi application should remain a clearly labelled extension.
