# Unified Paths of Light — Research Offshoots
## Provenance Register and Development Boundary

## Purpose
This folder preserves hypotheses and research directions that arose while conducting E4–E6 comparative ethics work.

They are **offshoots of the ethics programme**, not findings of E4, E5 or E6 unless separately demonstrated there. Their presence here preserves provenance while preventing speculative ideas from being silently folded into completed experiments.

## Development rule
Each offshoot begins as a sketch. If selected for development it should pass through the normal Concord development process: question → hypothesis → prior-work check → operationalisation → predictions → falsifiers → experiment/research → evaluation → integration or rejection.

## Experimental firewall
Offshoots must not be used to reinterpret completed derivations retrospectively.

Where an offshoot generates a prospectively testable prediction for a future ethics extraction, that prediction may be registered **before** the relevant corpus is opened. The extraction itself must remain blind to the predicted result.
