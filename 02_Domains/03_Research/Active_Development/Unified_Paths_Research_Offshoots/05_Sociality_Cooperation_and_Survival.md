# Sociality, Cooperation and Empirical Survival Advantage
## Biological and Civilisational Offshoot

### Origin observation
Highly social organisms can achieve extraordinary abundance, ecological reach and collective capability. Human technological dominance is also built upon unusually deep social coordination.

### Hypothesis
Some ethical functions may be cultural/cognitive expressions of more general constraints required for stable cooperation among interdependent agents.

### Required caution
Sociality is not identical to ethics. Eusocial insects, herd animals and human societies implement coordination through very different mechanisms. Numerical abundance is also not a single measure of evolutionary success.

### Test programme
Compare levels/forms of social interdependence with mechanisms functionally analogous to:
- harm restraint;
- reliable signalling;
- agency/role boundaries;
- care for dependants;
- resource allocation;
- correction of coordination failure;
- capability development.

This requires external biological and social-science research before it can support the natural-law hypothesis.
