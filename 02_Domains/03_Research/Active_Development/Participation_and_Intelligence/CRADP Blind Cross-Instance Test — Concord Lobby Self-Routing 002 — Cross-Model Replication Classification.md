## 3. Corrective-target replication

### Orientation

**REPLICATED**

Claude found that the Orientation board provides useful first-time sub-routes and preserves reading/evaluation without commitment.

### Uncertain assessment unit

**REPLICATED WITH WATCH-POINT**

Claude correctly used:

**Developmental State & Assessment -> I do not know what the relevant system or assessment unit is**

without converting the phenomenon into a collective agent, person or institution.

However, it identified mild classification pressure from the candidate-unit enumeration itself. The explicit non-inference guard successfully neutralised that pressure in the test.

### Autonomy vs Assessment

**REPLICATED**

Claude distinguished:

- choice/control/refusal/exit/consent -> Autonomy & Consent;
- evidence supporting autonomy dimensions -> Developmental State & Assessment.

It regarded the matching route-distinction language as particularly effective.

### Challenge vs formal review

**REPLICATED**

Claude distinguished:

- substantive challenge/evidence -> Challenge, Evidence & Correction;
- formal attention/independent review -> Report, Petition & Review.

It also confirmed that a weak signal can enter formal attention without becoming a verified finding.
