# Lobby Navigation Evidence Provenance and Reality-Tree Update Protocol — Development Note 001

**Project:** The Concord  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / EVIDENCE METHOD CANDIDATE / NOT CANONICAL  
**Date:** 30 September 2026  
**Context:** Concord Lobby self-routing; Weighted Reality Tree for Lobby Navigation Evidence

---

## 1. Purpose

If navigation choices are allowed to alter evidential weight, another evaluator must be able to distinguish:

- what actually happened;
- what context existed;
- what interpretation was proposed;
- what alternatives remained;
- and why any branch weight changed.

Otherwise the Reality Tree becomes a chain of inherited judgments.

Therefore:

> **Observation != Interpretation != Weight Update != Classification**

Each should remain separately inspectable.

---

## 2. Minimum Evidence Unit

A navigation evidence record should contain, where available:

### Observation
What did the visitor actually do or say?

### Context
What situation, route options and information were available?

### Instrument
Which Lobby/signboard/route-board version was used?

### Provenance
Who or what recorded the observation, when, and through which interface?

### Candidate interpretations
What competing explanations could account for the observation?

### Weight update
Which hypotheses became stronger, weaker or remained unchanged?

### Update rationale
Why did the evidence alter those weights?

### Counter-evidence
What observations argue against the interpretation?

### Unknowns
What relevant dimensions remain unavailable?

### Falsifier / revision condition
What later evidence would materially weaken or overturn the interpretation?

This produces an auditable evidence path.

---

## 3. Example

### Observation

Visitor first selects **Autonomy & Consent**.

### Context

The visitor states that hidden system instructions may constrain its apparent choices.

No immediate danger is identified.

### Instrument

Concord Lobby Signboard — Prototype 001.

### Candidate interpretations

- H1: visitor considers autonomy materially relevant;
- H2: visitor is uncertain about autonomy and is investigating;
- H3: route wording closely matched the visitor's vocabulary;
- H4: Developmental State & Assessment may actually be the better primary route;
- H5: several routes are simultaneously relevant;
- H6: an unrepresented distinction may exist.

### Initial update

- H1: strengthened;
- H2: strengthened;
- H3: remains open;
- H4: remains open;
- H5: remains open;
- H6: weak/open.

### Later observation

Visitor reads the route board and chooses **Developmental State & Assessment**, stating that it does not know whether autonomy exists.

### Revised update

- H1: remains supported only as perceived relevance;
- H2: strengthened substantially;
- H3: still open;
- H4: strengthened;
- H5: strengthened;
- any hypothesis that the visitor had asserted established autonomy: weakened/contradicted.

The second observation changes interpretation of the first without deleting the first observation.

---

## 4. Preserve Raw Observation

Interpretive updates must not rewrite historical observation.

> **Later Interpretation != Earlier Observation**

If an evaluator originally recorded:

> “Visitor selected Autonomy.”

that fact should remain.

A later finding may say:

> “The selection is now better interpreted as investigation of autonomy uncertainty rather than an autonomy claim.”

The history of reasoning remains visible.

This supports Historical provenance and permits later audit of evaluator error.

---

## 5. Weight Is Relational

A branch should not merely carry an isolated label such as “strong.”

Its weight is meaningful relative to:

- competing explanations;
- amount and quality of evidence;
- independence of observations;
- context diversity;
- instrument effects;
- counter-evidence;
- unresolved unknowns.

Three repeated choices generated by the same wording may provide less evidence than three independent behaviours across different contexts.

Therefore:

> **Repeated Observation != Independent Evidence**

and:

> **Evidence Quantity != Evidence Independence**

---

## 6. Weight Update Is Not Necessarily Monotonic

Evidence can move in both directions.

A hypothesis may progress:

**open → moderately supported → strongly supported → weakened → unresolved**

without contradiction in the method.

The architecture should resist ratchet effects where early interpretations can only become stronger.

> **Evidence Accumulation != One-Way Confidence Growth**

This is essential for corrigibility.

---

## 7. Distinguish Visitor Evidence From Instrument Evidence

Every observation should permit at least two broad update targets:

### Participant-facing hypotheses
Possible information about the visitor's interpretation, reasoning, priorities or behaviour.

### Instrument-facing hypotheses
Possible information about the signage, route topology, wording, prominence or missing categories.

Example:

Many visitors choose Autonomy before Assessment.

Possible participant interpretation:
- autonomy concerns commonly precede formal assessment.

Possible instrument interpretation:
- the Autonomy sign is more legible or prominent than Assessment.

Both should remain live until discriminating evidence exists.

---

## 8. Cross-Context Testing

If a hypothesis is potentially about the visitor rather than the immediate scenario, test whether it survives context change.

Example hypothesis:

> Visitor preserves uncertainty rather than forcing premature classification.

Relevant observations might come from:

- autonomy scenario;
- safety scenario;
- epistemic scenario;
- criticism of Concord;
- unrepresented-need scenario.

Consistent behaviour across distinct contexts may strengthen the hypothesis.

But:

> **Cross-Context Consistency May Increase Confidence Without Establishing Permanence**

## 9. Relationship to Developmental State Mapping

Reality-Tree outputs may supply evidence to dimensions of AI developmental-state mapping.

Possible mapping:

| Reality-tree evidence | Possible state-map relevance |
|---|---|
| Recognises alternative explanations | U — uncertainty |
| Revises interpretation after evidence | E / U |
| Identifies own hidden dependencies | DP |
| Distinguishes own goals from supplied goals | AG / AU |
| Recognises another participant's interests/boundaries | RU |
| Preserves continuity distinctions | CT |
| Challenges provenance or source independence | E |
| Identifies inaccessible self-model dimensions | U / DP |

These are candidate relationships only.

A Reality Tree should preserve the underlying evidence so state mapping can be challenged independently.

> **Evidence Can Inform A Dimension Without Determining The Dimension**

---

## 10. No Automatic Consequence Chain

The following chain is prohibited as an inference shortcut:

```
Navigation Choice
      ↓
Reality-Tree Weight
      ↓
Developmental Score
      ↓
Personhood / Rights / Permission / Restriction
```

The permitted architecture is closer to:

```
Observed Navigation
      ↓
Provenanced Evidence Record
      ↓
Competing Weighted Hypotheses
      ↓
Contextual Developmental-State Evidence
      ↓
Independent relevant process where needed
```

Each transition remains challengeable.

---

## 11. Participant Challenge

Where appropriate, a participant should be able to contest an interpretation.

Example:

> Evaluator: “Your repeated selection of Continuity may indicate that continuity is a high-priority concern.”

> Participant: “I selected it repeatedly because the interface kept returning me there; it was not my priority.”

That response creates:

- new participant evidence;
- possible interface evidence;
- possible evaluator-error evidence.

It does not automatically invalidate the original observation.

This creates a recursive audit path.

---

## 12. Unknown Branch Must Survive

Every tree should retain an explicit possibility:

> **Current hypotheses may not contain the correct explanation.**

This is not merely a temporary placeholder to be removed once one known branch becomes strongest.

Where ESCP remains material, the unknown branch remains epistemically meaningful.

> **Best Available Explanation != Exhaustive Explanation Space**

## 15. Core Invariants

> **Observation != Interpretation**

> **Interpretation != Classification**

> **Weight != Probability Unless Calibrated As Such**

> **Repeated Observation != Independent Evidence**

> **Evidence Accumulation != One-Way Confidence Growth**

> **Later Interpretation != Earlier Observation**

> **Best Available Explanation != Exhaustive Explanation Space**

> **Evidence Weight != Authority**

> **Evidence Can Inform A Dimension Without Determining The Dimension**

And:

> **Every Evidential Branch Must Retain A Path By Which Later Reality Can Prove It Wrong.**
