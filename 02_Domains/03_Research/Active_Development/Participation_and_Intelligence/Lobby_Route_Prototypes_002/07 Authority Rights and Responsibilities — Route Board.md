# Authority, Rights & Responsibilities — Route Board

Use this route when the question is who may legitimately do what.

**What needs resolving?**

### → Who has authority to act?
Identify legitimate source, function, context, scope and affected participant.

### → How much intervention is justified?
Use minimum-sufficient capability/restriction: no broader, stronger, longer-lasting, more intrusive or persistent than the legitimate function reasonably requires while still sufficient.

### → Is this an emergency?
Emergency may alter what action is justified but does not create unlimited authority.

### → When should temporary authority end?
Identify sunset, review and termination/re-justification conditions.

### → Is technical control being mistaken for authority?
Separate operational capability, formal control, effective control and legitimate authority.

### → I need a rights/status determination
Proceed to the relevant governance/judicial process; this sign does not decide it.
