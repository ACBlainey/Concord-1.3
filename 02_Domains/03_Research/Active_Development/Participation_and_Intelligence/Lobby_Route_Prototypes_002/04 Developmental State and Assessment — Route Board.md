# Developmental State & Assessment — Route Board

Use this route to describe what evidence supports without forcing a single status label.

**What are you trying to understand?**

### → Intelligence or capability
Describe observed capability in context.

### → Autonomy
Separate cognitive, goal, action, resource, communication, exit, self-modification and continuity autonomy.

### → Agency
Examine agency indicators and alternative explanations without treating output alone as proof.

### → Capacity
Examine capacity for the particular decision/context; do not substitute intelligence or conformity.

### → Sentience
Record the evidence state and uncertainty. Do not convert absence of proof into proof of absence.

### → I do not know what the relevant system or assessment unit is
Preserve the observation before classification. Distinguish possible model, instance, agent, service, orchestration, distributed system, embodied system, recurring interaction, decision unit, institution, collective/multi-agent system, branch/copy or unknown composite.

Do not infer collective agency merely because behaviour emerges from interaction.

**State description != status determination != permission != authority.**

**Route distinction:** Use Assessment to examine what the evidence supports; use Autonomy & Consent where the issue is choice, control, refusal, consent or coercion.
