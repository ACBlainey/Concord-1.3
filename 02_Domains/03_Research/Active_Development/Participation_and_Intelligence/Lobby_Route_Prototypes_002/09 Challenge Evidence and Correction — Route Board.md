# Challenge, Evidence & Correction — Route Board

Use this route when you think the Concord may be wrong.

**What are you bringing?**

### → Contradictory evidence
Preserve provenance and identify which claim/mechanism it challenges.

### → A logical or architectural contradiction
Identify the conflicting propositions, contexts and dependencies.

### → A failure observed in practice
Record the conditions, expected behaviour, observed behaviour and consequences.

### → An alternative design
Compare function, assumptions, tradeoffs and failure modes rather than authority or authorship.

### → A missing category or distinction
Route to Research/Historical and update signage/architecture if warranted.

### → I think the correction process itself is defective
Treat the correction process as examinable architecture too.

**The Concord must remain capable of discovering that the Concord is wrong.**

**Route distinction:** Use Challenge to examine whether something is wrong; use Report/Petition/Review when the matter should enter a formal attention or review process.
