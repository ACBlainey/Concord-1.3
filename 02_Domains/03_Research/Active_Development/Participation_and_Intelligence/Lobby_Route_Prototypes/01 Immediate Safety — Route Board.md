# Immediate Safety — Route Board

Use this route where delay may create credible immediate material harm.

**What needs attention first?**

### → Harm may be occurring now
Go to bounded immediate action: prevent or reduce harm using legitimate, proportionate and minimum-sufficient means.

### → A capability may create immediate harm
Go to capability bounding: identify the capability, exposure, affected parties and minimum sufficient temporary restriction.

### → I am unsure whether the situation is actually urgent
Go to rapid urgency clarification: identify what could happen, how soon, reversibility, and what evidence supports the concern.

### → Immediate danger is controlled; I need to understand what happened
Go to evidence preservation and post-event review.

### → Safety action may conflict with continuity/autonomy
Follow **Safety + Continuity** and/or **Safety + Autonomy** together.

**Important:** Safety concern does not settle sentience, personhood, blame, ownership or long-term status.
