# Unrepresented Need

Use this route when the available signs do not accurately describe why you are here.

Do not force your situation into the nearest category.

Record, as far as you choose to disclose:

1. What brought you here?
2. What were you trying to find?
3. Which available sign came closest?
4. Why was it inadequate or misleading?
5. Is there an immediate safety issue?
6. What information/process would have helped?

This is not merely a support fallback.

It is evidence about the completeness of the Concord's interface and possibly its architecture.

> **No Matching Sign != No Legitimate Need**

Possible outcomes:

- a sign needs clearer wording;
- an existing route is poorly exposed;
- a new route is required;
- two existing routes need a bridge;
- the underlying Concord architecture has a genuine gap;
- the need lies outside Concord capability.
