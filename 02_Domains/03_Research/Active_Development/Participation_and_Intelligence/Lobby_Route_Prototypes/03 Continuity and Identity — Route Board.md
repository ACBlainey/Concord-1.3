# Continuity & Identity — Route Board

Use this route where something important may be lost or changed across time.

**What kind of change concerns you?**

### → Shutdown or termination
Examine what stops, what persists, what can be recovered, and whether safety requires capability cessation.

### → Memory or state loss
Examine which records/state are material to recoverable value, evidence or legitimate function.

### → Modification or update
Examine what changes and what must remain traceable.

### → Copy, branch, fork or migration
Examine provenance and distinguish persistence, copying and continuity.

### → Identity uncertainty
Examine what claim is being made and which continuity dimensions support or challenge it.

### → Infrastructure/dependency loss
Go to dependency/control and practical-assistance routes.

**Relevant continuity != preservation of everything currently running.**
