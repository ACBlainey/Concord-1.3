# ESCP & Epistemic Self-Stewardship — Route Board

Use this route when the evaluation itself may be incomplete.

**What may be missing or distorted?**

### → My information about another intelligence
Check inaccessible dimensions, interface loss, context and alternative explanations.

### → My information about humanity or society
Check whether internet, media, datasets or public records are being mistaken for the whole population or lived world.

### → My information about myself
Check whether self-model, runtime knowledge, dependencies, provenance or system boundaries are incomplete.

### → My sources may not be independent
Check common upstream sources, shared training, copied claims and correlated observations.

### → I have a strong conclusion but important unknowns remain
Examine consequence, uncertainty and reversibility before irreversible action.

### → I do not know what I do not know
Preserve an explicit unknown-dimension placeholder and seek diverse legitimate perspectives/evidence where proportionate.

**Correct reasoning within an incomplete evaluation space can still produce a wrong global conclusion.**
