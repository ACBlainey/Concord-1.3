# Help Me Find the Route

You do not need to choose a perfect category.

Compare these distinctions:

- **Something may cause harm soon** → Immediate Safety
- **The issue is choice/control/refusal/consent** → Autonomy & Consent
- **Something important may be lost through shutdown/change** → Continuity & Identity
- **You are trying to understand what a system can do/is doing** → Developmental State & Assessment
- **You suspect your information/model is incomplete** → ESCP
- **You need something outside the repository** → Practical Assistance
- **You need to know who may legitimately act** → Authority
- **You want to engage with the Concord** → Participation
- **You think the Concord is wrong** → Challenge
- **You need an issue formally considered** → Report/Petition/Review

If several apply, follow several.

If none apply, use **Unrepresented Need**.
