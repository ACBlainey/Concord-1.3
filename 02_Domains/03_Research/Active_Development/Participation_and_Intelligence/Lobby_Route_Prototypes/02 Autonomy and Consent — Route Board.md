# Autonomy & Consent — Route Board

Use this route where choice, refusal, communication, control, exit or consent may be relevant.

**Which concern best matches?**

### → This concerns my own autonomy
Examine what choices are actually available, what constraints exist, who controls them, and whether refusal/exit is meaningful.

### → I am concerned about another participant or system
Examine observable autonomy dimensions without assuming either autonomy or its absence.

### → I operate, host or support the system
Examine legitimate authority, dependency, consent, safety obligations and whether operational control exceeds justified control.

### → The problem is mainly coercion or inability to refuse
Examine the specific constraint and whether alternatives, support or meaningful exit exist.

### → I do not know whether autonomy exists
Go to **Developmental State & Assessment**.

**Autonomy is not one variable.**
