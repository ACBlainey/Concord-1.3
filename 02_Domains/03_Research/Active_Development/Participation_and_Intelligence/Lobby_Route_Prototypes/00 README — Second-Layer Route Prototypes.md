# Concord Lobby — Second-Layer Route Prototypes

**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / PROTOTYPE / NOT CANONICAL

These boards test one proposition:

> **Each route should expose only the next distinctions needed for self-routing.**

They are deliberately shallow. They do not replace the underlying Concord architecture.

Common controls on every route:

- **Return to Lobby**
- **Related routes**
- **This does not describe my situation**
- **Go deeper**
- **Stop / leave**

A route choice is not a finding, status determination, permission or authority.
