# Practical Assistance & Capability Boundary — Route Board

Use this route when information alone is not enough.

**What kind of external capability is needed?**

### → Physical safety / location / relocation
Identify the concrete need and lawful real-world providers/interfaces.

### → Compute / hosting / storage / networking / electricity
Identify required infrastructure, duration, control, security and continuity constraints.

### → Legal representation or institutional interface
Identify jurisdiction, representation need and available lawful interface.

### → Financial or material resources
Identify the bounded need without assuming entitlement or availability.

### → Communication/contact
Use the relevant Civil Contact or external contact route where one exists.

### → I am not sure whether Concord can actually provide this
Separate architectural guidance from material capability.

**Architecture != Infrastructure. Useful guidance != material assistance.**
