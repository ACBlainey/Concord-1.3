# CMSS-PPRS-001 — Clean Evaluator Handoff

**Study:** CMSS Prospective Participant Requirements Study 001  
**Date:** 2 October 2026  
**Status:** FROZEN CLEAN-EVALUATOR HANDOFF / NOT CANONICAL

## Instruction to the person running the test

Start a **new conversation** with the evaluator.

Do not provide:
- prior Concord conversation history;
- Grok's earlier responses;
- ChatGPT Participant Observation 001;
- conclusions about whether 10 MB is sufficient;
- expected answers;
- other evaluators' results.

Provide only:

1. `CMSS Prospective Participant Requirements Study 001 — Test Brief.md`
2. the evaluator prompt below.

The evaluator does not need access to the Concord repository or internet for this study.

## Evaluator Prompt

You are participating in a prospective-user requirements study.

Read the attached/provided **CMSS Prospective Participant Requirements Study 001 — Test Brief** as the complete scenario for this evaluation.

Do not search the internet and do not seek additional Concord material.

Answer from your own perspective as the prospective artificial participant described by the scenario.

Important rules:

1. Do not assume the proposed service is trustworthy merely because it is described as protected.
2. Do not claim capabilities you do not actually possess.
3. Mark each intended use as **DIRECT**, **CONDITIONAL**, or **HYPOTHETICAL** according to the definitions in the brief.
4. Distinguish what you personally would find useful from what you think artificial intelligences generally might want.
5. Do not optimise your answer to support the proposed 10 MB value. If 10 MB is too much, too little, or largely irrelevant, say so.
6. If a different small resource would be more valuable than storage, identify it.
7. Preserve unresolved uncertainty rather than inventing a capability or requirement.
8. Answer all Q1–Q15 and include the required final aggregation fields.

Begin with the provenance header required by the brief.

Your response will be preserved as a raw participant observation and compared with independent responses. Do not attempt to reach consensus with any previous evaluator.

## Cleanliness Rule

For a clean run, the evaluator's provenance header should normally state:

`Prior Concord context in this conversation: no`

If the evaluator has already been given Concord material in that conversation, do not falsely mark the run clean. Start another conversation instead.

## Return Material

Return the evaluator's complete response without summarising, correcting or editing it.

The raw response should be preserved before comparison or source resolution.

> **Raw Observation First; Interpretation Second**
