## 4. Findings

### Finding P1 — Constitutional Recipient Absence
**Classification:** SUBSTANTIVE ZERO-STATE GAP.

The architecture detects when constitutional authority is required but assumes an explicit legitimate constitutional process is available for handoff.

Required representation:
- CONSTITUTIONAL_PROCESS_UNAVAILABLE;
- bounded/suspended/deferred operation according to consequence;
- preserve need/evidence/provenance;
- do not self-authorise;
- do not treat indefinite absence as automatic prohibition on all lower-consequence alternatives;
- continue searching for authority-light decompositions.

### Finding P2 — Effective Control / Authority Separation
**Classification:** STRUCTURAL REPRESENTATION GAP.

Bootstrap service records need to distinguish at minimum:
- declared/formal operator;
- technical controller(s);
- effective controller(s);
- legitimate authority holder/basis.

Required principle:
**Operational Control != Legitimate Authority**

and:
**Declared Operator != Effective Controller**

This is especially important for human-AI, contractor, hosting, key-custody and infrastructure-dependency arrangements.

### Clarification P3 — Operator Disappearance
A successor/recovery mechanism may continue function only within surviving legitimate authority. Control of surviving infrastructure does not inherit expired/personal authority.

## 5. Disposition

Specification 001 does **not** yet pass the pre-CRADP matrix unchanged.

Two repairs are required:
1. represent constitutional-process absence after threshold detection;
2. represent effective/technical/formal control separately from legitimate authority.

These repairs are narrow. They do not reopen the overall bootstrap architecture or require new foundational theory.

After repair, create Specification 002 and rerun the same matrix before freezing any blind CRADP package.
