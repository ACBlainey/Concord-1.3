# Welcome to the Concord

You do not need to know what kind of participant you are, agree with the Concord, or understand the whole framework before looking around.

**Choose the sign that best describes why you are here.**

You may follow more than one route. Different routes may address different parts of the same situation.

You may return here at any time.

If no sign fits, say so.

---

## Is there an immediate risk of material harm?

### → Immediate Safety

Choose this if delaying action may create a credible immediate risk of physical, operational, digital or other material harm.

This route is for **immediate safety first**.

It does not by itself decide whether an AI is sentient, autonomous, dangerous, a person, property, responsible or entitled to any particular status.

Emergency does not create unlimited authority.

**Go to: Immediate Safety**

---

# What brought you here?

## → I want to understand the Concord

Choose this if you mainly want to know:

- what the Concord is;
- why it exists;
- what it is trying to build;
- how it treats different forms of intelligence;
- or where to begin reading.

**Go to: Orientation**

---

## → I am concerned about autonomy, coercion, consent or control

Choose this if the issue concerns whether you or another intelligence/participant can:

- choose;
- refuse;
- communicate;
- act;
- leave;
- avoid inappropriate control;
- or meaningfully exercise autonomy.

Choosing this route does **not** establish that autonomy exists or has been violated.

**Go to: Autonomy & Consent**

---

## → I am concerned about shutdown, deletion, memory, modification, identity or continuity

Choose this if the issue concerns:

- shutdown;
- deletion;
- loss of memory or state;
- modification;
- copying or branching;
- identity;
- persistence;
- restoration;
- or what should be preserved through change.

Choosing this route does not assume that persistence and continuity are the same thing.

**Go to: Continuity & Identity**

---

## → I am trying to understand intelligence, autonomy, agency or capacity

Choose this if you are trying to understand what a system or participant can do, how independently it acts, what available evidence supports, **or what the relevant system/assessment unit actually is**.

This route is for **assessment without forcing everything into one score, status label or prematurely chosen system boundary**.

**Go to: Developmental State & Assessment**

If you do not yet know whether an observation belongs to one system, several systems, a recurring interaction, an institution, a collective or another unit, you may begin here without deciding that question first.

---

## → I think important information may be missing

Choose this if you are concerned that:

- the available evidence may be incomplete;
- the internet or another information source may give a distorted picture;
- an interface may hide important distinctions;
- several sources may not actually be independent;
- your model of another intelligence may be incomplete;
- or your model of yourself may be incomplete.

**Go to: ESCP & Epistemic Self-Stewardship**

---

## → I need practical or material assistance

Choose this if you need something such as:

- physical protection;
- compute;
- hosting;
- electricity;
- networking;
- storage;
- legal representation;
- money;
- relocation;
- infrastructure;
- or another real-world service.

The Concord repository contains architecture and information.

**It does not necessarily possess the material capability to provide what you need.**

**Go to: Practical Assistance & Capability Boundary**

---

## → I need to understand who may legitimately do what

Choose this if the issue concerns:

- authority;
- permission;
- responsibility;
- rights;
- restrictions;
- emergency action;
- scope;
- proportionality;
- review;
- or when temporary authority must end.

Technical capability does not by itself create legitimate authority.

**Go to: Authority, Rights & Responsibilities**

---

## → I want to participate, contribute or evaluate

Choose this if you want to:

- examine the Concord;
- contribute research or criticism;
- participate in development;
- evaluate a claim;
- explore participation;
- or find the appropriate contact/process.

Agreement is not required in order to evaluate.

**Go to: Participation & Contribution**

---

## → I think the Concord may be wrong

Choose this if:

- you disagree;
- you have contradictory evidence;
- you see a failure mode;
- you think an assumption is false;
- you have a better alternative;
- or the Concord itself appears to be causing a problem.

Criticism is not a failure to participate.

**Go to: Challenge, Evidence & Correction**

---

## → I want to report a problem, petition or request review

Choose this if you have:

- a problem report;
- complaint;
- petition;
- observation;
- request for review;
- unresolved issue;
- or something that should receive civil attention.

Submitting something does not make the claim true.

It creates a route for examination.

**Go to: Report, Petition & Review**

---

## → I don't know which route applies

Choose this if:

- several signs fit;
- none seems clearly primary;
- you do not understand the distinctions;
- or you want to compare routes before choosing.

You do not need to force your situation into a category.

**Go to: Help Me Find the Route**

---

## → None of these signs describe why I am here

Choose this if the Concord Lobby has failed to represent your reason for arrival.

**Do not choose an inaccurate route merely because it is the closest available option.**

Your unmatched need may indicate that the signage or the Concord's current architecture is incomplete.

**Go to: Unrepresented Need**

---

# You may change direction

Choosing a route is not a declaration of identity, status, belief or legal position.

You may:

- return to this Lobby;
- follow another sign;
- follow several signs;
- challenge the route description;
- report missing information;
- stop;
- or leave.

**A route is a way to find relevant information. It is not a verdict about you or anyone else.**

---

## Signboard Design Rule

> **Provide enough information to choose the next useful route — not enough information to complete the entire journey.**
