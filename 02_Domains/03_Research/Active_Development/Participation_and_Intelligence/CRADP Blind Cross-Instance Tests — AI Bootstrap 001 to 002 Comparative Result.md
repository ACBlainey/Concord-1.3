## 7. ESCP Development Findings

Test 002 adds three important issues:

### 7.1 Stopping / Sufficiency

ESCP explains why an evaluation space may be incomplete but does not itself determine when representation is sufficient for action.

This should be framed carefully:

> **Complete Enough For This Bounded Decision != Complete Model Of Reality**

The required evidentiary sufficiency should scale with consequence, uncertainty and reversibility rather than requiring impossible total completeness.

### 7.2 Resource-Bounded Evaluation

Evaluation consumes:

- time;
- compute;
- attention;
- money;
- access;
- opportunity;
- sometimes safety margin.

Therefore:

> **More Evaluation != Always Better Evaluation**

and potentially:

> **Epistemic Improvement Has Resource And Delay Costs**

This appears to be a substantive development candidate.

### 7.3 Adversarial Evaluation-Space Manipulation

ESCP presently focuses heavily on passive incompleteness.

An evaluator's accessible dimensions may also be deliberately shaped by:

- deception;
- selective disclosure;
- poisoned evidence;
- correlated false sources;
- interface manipulation;
- adversarial prompting;
- suppression of disconfirming evidence.

This is a substantive extension candidate at the intersection of ESCP, Research and Civil Security.

Do not fold it into ESCP canonically before development/testing.

- universal interpretability;
