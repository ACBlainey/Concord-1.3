## 3. New contribution isolated

The three-domain development appears to add a bounded operational extension rather than a new foundational method.

The strongest new contribution is:

> **For a material hypothesis, explicitly maintain a supporting structure and an opposing structure at the same time, preserve evidence dependencies and contradictions, and make material imbalance/contradiction a trigger for hypothesis-space and root-question reconsideration rather than merely a change in branch score.**

Candidate new elements:

1. **Explicit mirrored pair per material hypothesis**
   - supporting tree;
   - opposing tree;
   - neutral/unresolved evidence state.

2. **Evidence dependency structure**
   - repeated/derivative observations do not become independent corroboration merely through count.

3. **Proposition-scope control**
   - strong evidence for an intermediate proposition does not automatically establish the broader root proposition.

4. **Mandatory hypothesis abandonability**
   - a working hypothesis must be capable of being weakened, deprioritised or abandoned.

5. **Contradiction as a structural trigger**
   - material contradiction can trigger reconsideration of:
     - evidence interpretation;
     - current hypothesis;
     - competing hypothesis set;
     - evaluation/system boundary;
     - root question.

6. **Explicit non-binary coexistence**
   - hypotheses may coexist;
   - A true does not imply B false;
   - compound causes/hypotheses must be representable.

7. **Temporal/contextual evidence state**
   - changed evidence may reflect changed reality rather than prior reasoning failure.

8. **Operational bridge to ESCP/Blaineyan Reasoning**
   - mirrored evidence exposes poor fit;
   - poor fit triggers Reconsider;
   - ESCP tests evaluation-space completeness;
   - root/system boundary may be reformulated;
   - reasoning is re-grounded and refined.

- forced paired supporting/opposing representation for material hypotheses;
- explicit neutral/unresolved state;
- dependency-aware evidential aggregation;
- contradiction-driven escalation from local evidence review to hypothesis-space/root-question review;
- hypothesis abandonability as an operational requirement;
- explicit coexistence/compound-hypothesis handling;
- time/context-aware evidence-state interpretation;
- tested operational pattern across policing, medicine and engineering.

## 8. Candidate compact cycle

**Root / Present State**
-> **Material Hypothesis H**
-> **Supporting Tree + Opposing Tree + Neutral/Unresolved Evidence**
-> **Map Provenance / Dependencies / Proposition Scope / Context / Time**
-> **Assess Fit Without Raw-Count Collapse**
-> **Retain / Weaken / Abandon / Split / Combine Hypothesis**
-> **If Poor Fit Or Contradiction Persists: Reconsider Hypothesis Space**
-> **If Needed: Test Evaluation/System Boundary Through ESCP**
-> **If Needed: Reformulate Root Question**
-> **Re-ground In Evidence**
-> **Refine And Repeat Bounded Cycle**

## 9. Candidate governing propositions

**Hypothesis != Conclusion**

**Supporting Tree Alone != Complete Evaluation**

**Evidence Count != Evidential Strength**

**Dependent Evidence != Independent Corroboration**

**Strong Evidence For Proposition X != Strong Evidence For Broader Proposition Y**

**Evidence Supports A != Evidence Excludes B**

**A True != B False**

**Changed Evidence May Reflect Changed Reality**

**A Hypothesis Must Be Capable Of Being Abandoned**

**Correct Evaluation Of Incomplete Hypothesis Space != Complete Evaluation**

**Correct Evaluation Of Incomplete System Boundary != Complete Evaluation**

**Correct Answer To Wrong Root Question != Successful Reasoning**
