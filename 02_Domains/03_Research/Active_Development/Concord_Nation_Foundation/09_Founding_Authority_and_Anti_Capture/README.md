# Founding Authority and Anti-Capture

Research how any temporary or transitional authority used during Concord founding could be justified, bounded, reviewed and relinquished.

This area should examine founder power, emergency powers, transition bodies, constitutional transfer, authority expiry, anti-entrenchment safeguards, conflicts of interest, self-validation, institutional capture and mechanisms preventing those who design the founding process from becoming permanently sovereign by default.

> **Founding Necessity ≠ Permanent Authority.**

> **Power cannot cite its own existence as evidence that it ought to continue.**
