# Population, Constituency and Consent

Research who constitutes the founding population of a Concord polity and how legitimate consent is established, renewed, withdrawn and represented.

Topics include founding constituency, humans and nonhuman intelligences, residency, affected non-members, minority protections, dissent, exit, coercion, representation and the difference between participation in a project and consent to political jurisdiction.

> **Participation ≠ Consent to Government.**
