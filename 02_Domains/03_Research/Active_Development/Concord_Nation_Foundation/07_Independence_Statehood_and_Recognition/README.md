# Independence, Statehood and Recognition

Research the conditions under which a Concord polity could legitimately claim independence, statehood or another form of political standing, and how external recognition should be sought and interpreted.

This area should compare internal legitimacy, effective institutional capacity, territorial or functional control, population, external recognition, diplomatic standing and interoperability readiness without collapsing them into a single binary test.

> **Claimed Statehood ≠ Established Legitimacy.**

> **Recognition ≠ Creation of Legitimacy, but external recognition can materially affect political reality.**
