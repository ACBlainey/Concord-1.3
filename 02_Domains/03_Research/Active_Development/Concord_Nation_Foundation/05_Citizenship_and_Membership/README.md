# Citizenship and Membership

Research citizenship, residency, membership and civil standing within a future Concord polity.

This area should distinguish voluntary project membership from citizenship, and citizenship from personhood. It should consider multisubstrate citizenship, entry and exit, dual or multiple citizenship, non-citizen residents, temporary presence, rights that do not depend on citizenship, civic obligations and protection against exclusionary status systems.

> **Personhood ≠ Citizenship.**

> **Membership in the Concord project ≠ citizenship in a future Concord polity.**
