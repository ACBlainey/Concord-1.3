# Transition From Voluntary Community to Polity

Research the transition thresholds between a voluntary research/community project and an operating political community.

Key questions include when voluntary coordination becomes governance, when shared infrastructure becomes jurisdictional infrastructure, what consent must be obtained before authority changes character, how transitional institutions remain temporary, and how participants can refuse or exit a proposed political transition.

> **Community Coordination ≠ Political Jurisdiction.**

> **Institutional Growth must not silently manufacture governing authority.**
