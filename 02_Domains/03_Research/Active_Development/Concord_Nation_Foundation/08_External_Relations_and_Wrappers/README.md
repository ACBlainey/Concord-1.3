# External Relations and Wrappers

Research how an emerging or established Concord polity would interact with surrounding states, civilisations, international organisations, disputed entities and non-state actors.

This area should connect Nation Foundation work to Concord Interoperability Wrappers, Peaceful Distance, diplomacy, trade, migration, recognition, disputes, shared infrastructure and sovereignty-neutral interfaces.

> **External interoperability does not require forced internal uniformity.**

> **Diplomatic communication ≠ automatic recognition of sovereignty.**
