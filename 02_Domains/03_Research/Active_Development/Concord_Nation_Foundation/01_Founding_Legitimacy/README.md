# Founding Legitimacy

Research the legitimate basis on which a Concord polity could be founded.

Core questions include the source of founding authority, the distinction between civilisational design and political legitimacy, voluntary association, constituent power, limits on founder authority, and the evidence required before any claim to govern can be justified.

> **Civilisation Design ≠ Authority to Found a State.**

> **A coherent constitution ≠ legitimate jurisdiction over a population or territory.**
