# Open Questions

Maintain unresolved questions, contradictions, research dependencies and candidate tests for Concord Nation Foundation work.

Initial questions include:

- What minimum conditions distinguish a voluntary civilisational community from a polity?
- What makes founding consent sufficiently legitimate across human and nonhuman participants?
- Can a Concord polity exist partly through digital or functional jurisdiction without conventional territorial sovereignty?
- What conditions justify a declaration of independence?
- What standards of statehood should the Concord apply to itself and to others?
- How should external recognition interact with internal legitimacy?
- How can founder authority be prevented from becoming permanent by inertia?
- What rights belong to dissenters, nonparticipants and minorities during political transition?
- How should a failed founding attempt unwind safely?
- When is constitutional forking preferable to continued shared governance?
- Which dependencies remain after secession and require wrappers?
- What evidence would falsify a proposed claim to legitimate Concord political authority?

This folder should remain a live research frontier rather than a holding area for questions that have effectively already been decided.
