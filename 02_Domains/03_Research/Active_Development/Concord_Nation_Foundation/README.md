# Concord Nation Foundation

**Project:** The Concord V1.1  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / NOT CANONICAL  

This development area examines how the Concord could legitimately progress from a civilisational design framework and voluntary community into an operating polity, jurisdiction, nation or state without allowing the act of designing a civilisation to manufacture political authority over people, intelligences, territory or infrastructure.

The central founding question is:

> **How can the Concord legitimately progress from a proposed civilisational architecture and voluntary community into an operating polity, jurisdiction or state without allowing the act of designing a civilisation to manufacture authority over the people, intelligences, territory or infrastructure that civilisation would govern?**

A core methodological safeguard is:

> **The Concord must not demand standards of legitimacy, consent, representation, statehood or recognition from other emerging polities that it exempts itself from satisfying.**

This creates a reciprocal test between Concord analysis of other emergent polities and any future Concord claim to political existence.

## Development areas

1. `01_Founding_Legitimacy` — legitimate basis for founding authority and political existence.
2. `02_Population_Constituency_and_Consent` — who constitutes the founding population and how consent is established and preserved.
3. `03_Territory_Digital_Jurisdiction_and_Presence` — physical, digital, infrastructural and multisubstrate jurisdiction.
4. `04_Constitutional_Ratification` — transition from design document to legitimately ratified constitutional order.
5. `05_Citizenship_and_Membership` — citizenship, residency, membership, rights and exit.
6. `06_Transition_From_Voluntary_Community_to_Polity` — thresholds and safeguards for movement from association to polity.
7. `07_Independence_Statehood_and_Recognition` — independence, declarations, statehood criteria, recognition and external legitimacy.
8. `08_External_Relations_and_Wrappers` — diplomacy, Peaceful Distance and interoperability with surrounding polities.
9. `09_Founding_Authority_and_Anti_Capture` — founding power, temporary authority, constitutional transfer and anti-capture safeguards.
10. `10_Forks_Secession_and_Peaceful_Distance` — internal divergence, constitutional forks, secession and post-fork interoperability.
11. `11_Adversarial_Testing` — edge cases, failure modes, illegitimate shortcuts and reciprocal tests.
12. `12_Open_Questions` — unresolved research questions and future work.
