# Forks, Secession and Peaceful Distance

Research how legitimate divergence within a future Concord polity could be handled without assuming permanent political unity is always desirable or legitimate.

Topics include internal accommodation, constitutional forks, autonomy, secession, minority protection, consent, division of assets and obligations, residual dependencies, post-fork wrappers, Peaceful Distance and the conditions under which separation reduces rather than relocates conflict.

> **Political Separation ≠ Peaceful Distance.**

> **A successful fork requires an interoperability architecture for everything that remains connected after political separation.**
