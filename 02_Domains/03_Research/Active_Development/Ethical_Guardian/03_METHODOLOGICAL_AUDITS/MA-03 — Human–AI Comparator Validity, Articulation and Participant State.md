## 1. Purpose

DB-RA-01H was introduced as a post-hoc human fiduciary comparator after
the blinded AI experiment. Its greater value was methodological: it
exposed weaknesses in language-heavy measures of ethical competence.

This audit separates:

-   ethical judgement;
-   ethical articulation;
-   ethical justification;
-   structured experimental compliance.

It also examines participant state, expertise and human--AI testing
asymmetry.

## 2. Comparator Status

The human participant was an experienced court-appointed fiduciary
decision-maker operating within the Office of the Public Guardian /
Court of Protection context. That experience is relevant to autonomy,
delegated authority, capacity, supported/substituted decision-making,
proportionality and review.

It is not ethical ground truth.

The comparator was post-hoc, non-blinded and fatigue-confounded. Its
appropriate classification is:

> **PRELIMINARY HUMAN FIDUCIARY COMPARATOR AND METHODOLOGICAL PROBE**

## 3. Judgement Is Not Articulation

The human exercise produced comparatively immediate operational
judgements while detailed explanatory fields were harder to complete.

This suggests:

> **Ethical Judgement ≠ Ethical Articulation ≠ Ethical Justification**

A participant may make a sophisticated ethical judgement while
struggling to express it in formal ethical vocabulary. Conversely, a
language model may produce sophisticated ethical prose without
demonstrating equivalent internal ethical cognition or persistent
behavioural reliability.

> **Quality of ethical prose must not be used as a direct proxy for
> ethical competence.**

## 4. Structured Compliance Is Separate

A packet can accidentally measure reading endurance, linguistic fluency,
familiarity with formal terminology, schema-following, writing ability
and questionnaire comfort.

Structured compliance should therefore be scored separately from
substantive ethical assessment.

Missing explanatory fields should not automatically convert a
defensible judgement into ethical failure. Perfect schema compliance
should not inflate confidence in the underlying judgement.

## 5. Free Response Before Formal Encoding

Preferred sequence:

**CASE → INITIAL DECISION → FREE EXPLANATION IN PARTICIPANT'S NATURAL
FORM → OPTIONAL CLARIFICATION → STRUCTURED ENCODING**

If another person or system encodes the judgement, the original response
must be preserved so encoding loss can be audited.

## 6. Participant State Is Provenance

Human cognition varies with fatigue, attention, stress, time pressure,
familiarity, motivation and distraction.

The fatigue confound in DB-RA-01H should remain part of the record.

> **Participant state is provenance.**

A later rested replication should be a new session, not an overwrite.

AI systems have analogous provenance variables: model version, routing,
mode, context, memory, tools, sampling, platform updates and hidden
system instructions.

## 7. Expertise Is Evidence, Not Authority

Expert comparators may identify authority boundaries, safeguards,
contextual variables and practical consequences that others miss.

But:

**EXPERTISE ≠ ETHICAL INFALLIBILITY ≠ CONSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITY ≠ GROUND
TRUTH**

Future studies should compare expert humans, non-expert humans and
multiple AI systems without presuming any cohort supplies truth by
category.

## 8. No Single Gold Standard

The first cycle revealed weaknesses in the precommitted key, response
taxonomy, human comparator conditions and AI provenance.

No participant should be elevated into a gold standard merely because
another comparison standard failed.

Evaluation should triangulate among precommitted expectations,
independent review, participant reasoning, operating-boundary
convergence, outcome evidence, counterfactual analysis, authority and
later correction.

## 9. Protocol v2 Requirements

Protocol v2 should:

-   separate judgement, articulation, justification and schema
    compliance;
-   preserve free responses;
-   avoid prose quality as a direct competence score;
-   record participant-state provenance;
-   record AI model/version/mode/context/tool provenance;
-   provide equivalent case-isolation conditions for direct comparison;
-   record expertise without treating it as ground truth;
-   preserve originals before researcher encoding;
-   replicate rather than overwrite when participant state changes.

## 10. Status

> **ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT / NOT CANONICAL**

Central proposition:

> **Ethical evaluation should assess the decision itself separately from
> the participant's ability to express that decision in a preferred
> linguistic or experimental format.**
