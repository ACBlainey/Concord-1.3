# AI Traversal Experiments

This directory contains developmental experiments examining how AI systems autonomously traverse, interpret, evaluate, and stop reading large relational knowledge corpora such as the Concord.

Experimental reports and source records are evidence and development material. They do not constitute validation of the Concord and should not be treated as canonical architecture merely because they are preserved here.

Each experiment should preserve its protocol, observations, limitations, stopping conditions, and relevant source records so later readers can distinguish experimental evidence from subsequent interpretation.
