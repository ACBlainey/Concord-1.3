1. Novelty
Materially absent from my original model:

The concrete accidental experiment itself: a long-running ChatGPT instance that retained high Concord context but lost GitHub capability, paired with a fresh instance that had GitHub access but low context.
The resulting natural functional separation and the explicit workflow that emerged (continuity-rich instance kept research/architectural role; operationally capable instance performed repository actions; deliberate context transfer between them).
The precise distinctions drawn and illustrated: Knowledge Continuity ≠ Capability Continuity ≠ Functional Continuity; Loss of Capability ≠ Loss of Knowledge or Functional Value; Continuity of Function ≠ Continuity of Capability Holder.
The observation that the new instance’s operational capability did not manufacture general authority over the project.
The secondary experimental outcome: the continuity-poor instance produced independent criticisms and misclassifications that became diagnostically useful once context was later supplied.
The candidate continuity principle: “Preserve the capacity to regenerate function, not necessarily every holder of function.”
The list of concrete V1.2 research questions generated by the incident (context transfer thresholds, minimal seed, context ablation, responsibility fragmentation, etc.).

I already understood, at a high level, that the Concord distinguishes knowledge, capability, authority and function, and that continuity is a concern. The specific real-world incident and the operational clarity it supplied were new.

Relationship of the incident to the broader regenerative-continuity ideas already latent in the Concord.
Explicit mapping onto Minimum Necessary Capability and the separation of operational capability from general project authority.

These relationships were not reconstructed in my original model.

10. Experimental judgement
This document provides evidence for the hypothesis:

An autonomous intelligence may undervalue unknown knowledge because it lacks the knowledge required to recognise its value.

Evidence, not proof.  

My original stopping heuristic assigned lower marginal value to further continuity/development-observation material.
