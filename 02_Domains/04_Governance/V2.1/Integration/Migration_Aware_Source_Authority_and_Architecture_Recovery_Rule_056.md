# Governance V2.1 — Migration-Aware Source Authority and Architecture Recovery Rule 056

**Status:** AUTHORIAL METHODOLOGY CORRECTION, 11 October 2026. Applies to ongoing V2.1 reconciliation and other Concord migration recovery. Does not silently amend original historical sources.

## Authorial rule

Document labels such as **canonical**, **non-canonical**, **provisional**, **draft**, **development**, or **active development** describe an author's or project's **assumed state at the time of writing**. They are **not** reliable standalone tests of whether the architecture is currently accepted, superseded, obsolete, or ready to integrate.

Serious migration omissions between earlier Concord versions and V1.3 mean **absence from V1.3 is not evidence of rejection**. Earlier versions may contain architecture that should be recovered and incorporated. Conversely, a historically canonical label is not evidence of current correctness.

## Reconciliation precedence (evidence-led, not a new constitutional hierarchy)

1. **Current explicit authorial direction**, including subsequent corrections.
2. **Substantive architectural content and compatibility** with Concord's established principles and other systems.
3. **Historical development trajectory and later reliance**, including whether a concept was adopted, exercised, cross-referenced, graduated, or silently lost in migration.
4. **Cross-version source comparison**, with provenance and exact original paths preserved.
5. **Historical status labels** as dated metadata, never a veto or automatic adoption.

When evidence conflicts, record the conflict and resolve it on its merits. A source may be integrated as established architecture while its unresolved implementation choices remain identified; do not conflate completeness of an architectural principle with completion of all operational specifications.

## Recovery workflow

- Locate relevant architecture across **all available earlier versions, archive snapshots and V1.3**; do not assume V1.3 is exhaustive.
- Preserve the original source and exact full path, compare substance, identify later changes and dependent modules.
- Classify findings as **recovered established architecture**, **compatible refinement**, **unresolved conflict**, **superseded**, or **new proposal**, with supporting evidence.
- Integrate missing established architecture into the current working candidate, rather than refusing solely on header status.
- Keep genuinely novel constitutional powers, unresolved legal thresholds or contradictory rules marked for authorial resolution.
- Validate targeted section changes and preserve untouched sections; retain historical provenance.

## Immediate G-05 application

The previously recovered `PH-02 — Emergency Governance and Emergency Operations.md` contains a substantive **Emergency Authority Envelope (EAE)**, legitimacy/activation/exercise gates, advisory/directive/compulsory separation, and contextual deviation safeguards. `Phase D4 — Holder, Conflict, Support, Exit and Emergency Foundations 001.md` preserves emergency constitutional standing, minimum deviation and sunset. **These architectural components must not be excluded because of non-canonical headers.** Reconcile and incorporate them into V2.1, checking earlier versions for missing related architecture.

This rule **does not** turn every draft example into enacted legal power. It removes status-label gatekeeping while retaining evidence-based scrutiny of what was actually established, what remains undecided, and what later authorial direction changes.
