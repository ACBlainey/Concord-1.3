# G-03 — Quorum and Triadic Decision Validity: Narrow Resolution Candidate 047

**Status:** PROVISIONAL analysis for authorial decision. This is not an adopted quorum rule and does not amend Governance V2 Canonical.

## Existing source-supported rules

- A normal triad has three Steward-owned voting positions.
- Two matching F votes yield F; two matching A votes yield A; otherwise U, **subject to independently valid quorum and authority**.
- The same F/A/U arithmetic applies to the three constituent resultants of an authorised virtual triad; the nine individual votes are never pooled.
- A lawful substitute occupies **one** original Steward position, not an extra vote.
- Material-conflict recusal, absence, invalid authentication, and insufficient-information abstention are different causes. Insufficient-information abstention does not create an automatic substitution vacancy.
- Decision-event identity uniqueness, competence, forum, and commit-time authority are separate requirements.

## The unresolved question

Does a three-position triad require **three validly occupied positions** before it may make a consequential decision, or can an independently authorised degraded mode act with only two valid positions?

This is a **quorum/constitution question**, not a change to the two-matching-vote arithmetic.

## Conservative default candidate (requires authorial approval)

**Q-1: Full constitution by default.** All three original positions must have eligible, independently authorised, uniquely identified holders (ordinary or lawful substitutes) before a consequential triadic decision may commit. An eligible Steward may cast U or abstain for insufficient information without vacating the position. An invalid or vacant position does not count as an authorised U ballot.

**Q-2: No implicit degraded quorum.** If a position cannot be lawfully filled, the decision remains procedurally unconstituted; even F/F from the other two does not automatically confer legal effect. A future exception must have an **independently established** mandate, conditions, scope and audit, rather than being inferred from urgency, a favourable majority, or the geometry.

**Q-3: Constituent independence.** In an expanded virtual triad, each constituent must pass its own constitution and participation gates before its F/A/U resultant can count. A missing constituent is not silently treated as a U resultant. The final virtual decision also requires its own valid resultant-level procedure and authorisation.

**Q-4: Distinct outcome classes.** `INVALID_CONSTITUTION`, `UNRESOLVED_VALID`, `AGAINST_VALID`, `FOR_VALID` and `DECISION_BOUNDARY_UNKNOWN` are different states. A system must not convert procedural failure into a substantive Against or Unresolved.

## Small discriminating examples

| Case | Arithmetic | Validity under Q-1–Q-4 |
|---|---|---|
| Three authorised holders cast F/F/U | F | May commit if all other gates pass |
| Three authorised holders cast F/U/U | U | Valid unresolved outcome |
| Third position vacant; two holders cast F/F | F arithmetically | **Cannot commit** absent separately lawful degraded quorum |
| Material conflict; lawful screened substitute fills third position; F/F/A | F | May commit if all gates pass |
| Third Steward abstains for insufficient information; other two cast F/F | F | May commit if position remains valid and other gates pass; record abstention cause |
| Third position failed authentication; F/F elsewhere | F arithmetically | **Cannot commit** without independent remedy or lawful degraded quorum |
| One virtual constituent unconstituted; two valid constituent resultants F/F | F arithmetically | **Cannot commit** unless a separately established resultant-level exception permits it |

## Reason for candidate

The three-position structure and the existing lawful substitution mechanism make **full constitution** the simplest conservative default. It prevents quorum being manufactured by absence, conflict, selective authentication failure, or selective non-participation. It does not require all three Stewards to agree, or all three to cast F/A.

## Exact authorial choice requested

**Approve full constitution of all three positions as the ordinary consequential quorum, with no implicit two-position degraded mode?** If approved, amend the reconciled Chapter 12 and Chapter 132 candidates accordingly, retaining an independently authorised emergency route as a separate competence rather than silently weakening triadic quorum.

No new permanent authority, constitutional amendment, or emergency power is created by this candidate.
