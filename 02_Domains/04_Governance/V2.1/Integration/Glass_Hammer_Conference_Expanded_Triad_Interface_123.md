# Contextual Conference — Expanded Triad and Substitution Interface 123

**Status:** developmental source reconciliation, 13 October 2026. No new authority or executable implementation.

## Core reconciliation

Stage 122 establishes independent isolated Steward voting as the ordinary procedure, while allowing conference through an independently legitimate contextual exception, including circumstances not yet foreseen. Conference is a communication and deliberation context, **not** a source of authority, a ballot, a substitute appointment, or a new governance forum.

A conference may not revise an official committed ballot, reopen consumed authority, change a determined F/A result, create a host execution grant, or disclose sealed ballots merely because participants are present. Any permitted pre-commit information exchange, including provisional positions, requires its own contextual information rules. Whether conference can occur after partial ballot commitment remains unresolved; no automatic permission or blanket prohibition is established.

## Expanded triads

An origin-excluded expanded decision retains three independently valid adjacent constituent triads, each yielding one F/A/U resultant. The virtual decision uses the **three resultants**, never a pooled nine-ballot majority. An origin-included arrangement retains the three mixed constituent-resultant structure but remains subject to source-resolved identity and independence constraints. Conference cannot cure invalid participant mapping or create additional resultant votes.

A conference may be scoped within a constituent triad or, if independently justified, across multiple contexts. Attendance does not create a vote or common jurisdiction. Cross-constituent information sharing requires explicit CWA/CIBB boundaries; correlated influence is a real risk to be evaluated rather than assumed absent. The precise permissible conference scope for each decision class remains open.

## Substitution

The GTP vertex-adjacent and fractal-ring candidate systems address **one legally vacant position**. Conference does not declare a vacancy, appoint a replacement, bypass eligibility or randomness, or turn a committed ballot or disliked outcome into a vacancy. The selected substitute inherits only the bounded decision-specific position through independent authorisation, not the original Steward's wider office.

A valid abstention must not be silently converted into vacancy. The first-ring pool consists of five other vertex-adjacent positions; the second ring has ten nominal candidates if the first has none eligible; further ring enumeration is unresolved. If one eligible candidate remains, select that candidate; if more than one, use an auditable unbiased random draw subject to legitimate contextual security exceptions.

## Shared competence and emergency

Information coordination among GTP regions is distinct from voluntary joint execution, binding shared competence and independently authorised emergency preservation. Conference participation, GTP adjacency and majority sentiment do not bind an unconsenting independent jurisdiction. Binding cross-jurisdictional effects require their own lawful common-competence or other valid authority.

A paused Civilisation Clock cannot delay independently authorised emergency protection. Neither conference nor emergency urgency automatically changes the ballot or jurisdiction rules.

## Owner integration

Governance and Bounded Contextual Authority own legitimacy and mandate. CWA owns the conference context; CIBB owns protected projections. The triadic decision owner records independently authorised, single-use immutable ballots and constituent resultants. GTP substitution rules own vacancy and selection. Judiciary/STRA own review routing and adjudication boundaries. CBPR/BTA/host owners control execution and transition effects; Historical preserves protected provenance.

The anonymous 0–3 commit count remains informational only. It cannot trigger conference, disclose votes, block a remaining Steward or grant authority.
