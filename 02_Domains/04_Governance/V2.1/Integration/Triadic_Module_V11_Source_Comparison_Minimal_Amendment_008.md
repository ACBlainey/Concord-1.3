## Verified source provisions

- **§2:** Three decision-makers hold equal decision authority; initial independent reasoning before deliberation.
- **§3:** Triads can be decision units inside larger systems and at multiple governance levels.
- **§6:** Two or three For authorise; two or three Against reject; one For, one Against, one Abstain has no majority. No majority means no authorised state change.
- **§§8–9:** Abstention is diagnostic and distinct from an Against vote.
- **§10:** No-majority safety default has both restraint and failure-to-act risks.
- **§18:** Twelve-step portable procedure defines authority, participant independence, votes, dissent, escalation and review.
- **§21:** Refers to a distinct `Triadic Governance Decision Protocol` as primary voting source and `Governance Operating Protocol` for decision-cycle architecture; these are named leads, not independently verified standalone documents here.
- **§22:** Experimental status, empirical testing required.

**Not found as an explicit rule in this source:** an expanded three-member forum substituting for **one** original decision position; conservation of original-position voting weight; two-stage resultant aggregation; recursive weight conservation; or origin-included GTP mapping. Absence here is not proof of absence in all historical Concord materials.

## Minimal compatible amendment: optional companion

### C-008-01 — Substitution contract

Where the surrounding lawful governance architecture expressly permits, an original triad position may be exercised through an independently constituted triad. Its members internally deliberate and produce **one** resultant For, Against or Unresolved for the original position. The original position retains its single vote and bounded authority. No internal member acquires an additional original-level vote.

### C-008-02 — Original voting semantics preserved

Apply the original module's majority rule within the authorised internal triad. Map a valid internal majority For→For, majority Against→Against and no majority→Unresolved. **Unresolved** is an interface output indicating no operative For/Against resultant; it is not an assertion that the original module's term **Abstain** has been replaced or that every procedural invalidity equals abstention. At the receiving triad, treat Unresolved as a non-affirmative/non-opposing decision position for arithmetic only, with cause and procedural validity separately recorded. Do not alter the source's no-majority default or emergency exception boundaries.

### C-008-03 — Weight and authority conservation

If original triad P consists of positions P1/P2/P3, and P2 is lawfully substituted by Q1/Q2/Q3, then `result(Q)` occupies **P2 only**. Its original-level voting weight remains **one of three positions**. Its authority cannot exceed P2's original mandate. This is not a grant of a fractional portion of all executive or constitutional authority. Recursion preserves this invariant at every interface but requires separate lawful authorisation and stopping rules.

### C-008-04 — No pooled ballots

Where three original positions are each exercised through a constituent triad, the three **resultants** form the final triad. Never count the nine internal votes as nine equal original-level votes. Example: F/F/A, F/F/A, A/A/A yields F/F/A→For, not pooled 4F/5A→Against.

### C-008-05 — Scope, eligibility and audit

Record original position, authority source, decision question, constituent appointments, quorum, identity, independence, conflicts, internal positions, resultant, review, expiry and termination. A mathematical majority from an invalid forum cannot acquire legal effect. This companion does not itself authorise substitution or create a quorum rule.
