# Chapter 132 §§132.1–132.5 — GTP Integration 003

**Status:** Proposed targeted replacements, pending author approval and full chapter reconciliation.

## Verified original text and tensions
- **§132.1:** calls GTP a “neutral hierarchical territorial partitioning” and an application/module, not foundational.
- **§132.2:** safeguards constitutional principles if GTP is modified, rejected or replaced.
- **§132.3:** geometry does not confer rights or legitimacy.
- **§132.5:** describes hierarchical nested jurisdiction while retaining competence and constitutional boundaries.

The author's clarified spatial concept is a conceptual tessellation existing at every resolution, used concurrently at multiple scales; geometric parent/child is not authority hierarchy. GTP is foundational to Concord's *current spatial coordination implementation*, not the source of constitutional rights.

## Proposed §132.1 replacement
GTP is Concord's adopted multiresolution triangular spatial reference and coordination mesh. Every spatial subdivision exists conceptually regardless of whether it is operationally used for a task. Selection of operating resolution does not create or abolish triangles. Geometry may inform administrative coverage, jurisdictional analysis, representation, logistics and resource coordination, but confers no authority by itself.

## Proposed §132.2 replacement
GTP is foundational to the current Concord spatial governance implementation. Constitutional rights, ethical constraints, consent, bounded authority and legitimate decision procedure do not derive from geometry and must survive any properly authorised change in spatial implementation. Modifying or replacing the adopted reference system requires explicit constitutional change control, continuity mapping, provenance and rights-preserving transition; it cannot happen by silent reclassification.

## Proposed §132.3 clarification
Spatial parent/child means geometric containment and subdivision **only**. Any constitutional priority, institutional delegation or federated authority derives from a separate lawful instrument, not the size or nesting of a GTP triangle.

## Proposed §132.4 clarification
Any number of spatial resolutions may operate concurrently where their task, competence and externalities justify it. Operational subdivision or consolidation responds to workload, with hysteresis to prevent chattering; the underlying conceptual tessellation remains unchanged.

## Proposed §132.5 replacement
Nested GTP spatial layers permit coordination at local, cross-cell and wider scales, including overlapping fields. They do **not** imply superior and subordinate governments. Local decisions stay with the lowest capable legitimate decision field; cross-boundary externalities may require separately constituted shared decisions. The R/A/B/C origin-included or origin-excluded expanded-triad voting rules apply only when an authorised decision forum exists, with three constituent results forming the virtual final triad.

## Preserve §§132.6–132.12
Population variation, geographic neutrality, boundary revision, digital communities, interplanetary settlement, legitimacy and the chapter principle must be preserved and reconciled, not erased.
