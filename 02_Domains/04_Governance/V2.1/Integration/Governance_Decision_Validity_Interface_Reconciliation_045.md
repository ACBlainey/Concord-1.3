# Governance V2.1 — Decision Validity Interface Reconciliation 045

**Status:** PROVISIONAL integration note. Reconciles existing V2.1 Integration 006, 018 and Bounded Voting Context Candidate 016 with Chapters 12 Candidate 012 and 132 Candidate 010. No original canonical amendment or new quorum rule.

## 1. Source-limited corrections to earlier development status

Integration 006 was written before several authorial clarifications. Its G-01 and G-02 entries should **no longer be treated as wholly open**:
- **G-01 node representation:** at a regular shared GTP node, six separate Steward positions belong to six different triangles. No joint office or governance body; physical offices may lie elsewhere inside their own triangle.
- **G-02 repeated origin Steward:** prohibited. An origin-included expanded virtual triad has three distinct origin Stewards, each occupying one mixed constituent position, with nine distinct holders across the three constituent triads.

These points are resolved **at the authorial design level**, not a claim that constitutional adoption or all eligibility checks are complete. The old use of *vertex* in source-preserving development notes should be read as **node** under Terminology Standard 035.

## 2. Gate order for consequential decisions

1. **Competence:** identify the mandate and exact question; GTP mesh or node adjacency is never jurisdiction.
2. **Forum:** identify the lawful ordinary triad, expanded virtual triad, shared-competence institution or emergency forum. A substitution ring is a **candidate pool**, not a forum.
3. **Constitution and participation:** verify each original or substitute Steward position, independent eligibility, recusal cause, identity uniqueness across the complete decision event, appointment, independence and quorum.
4. **Substantive resolution:** calculate F/A/U only within valid constituent triads and combine constituent resultants where authorised. Never pool nine ballots. A valid unresolved U is distinct from invalid constitution or missing authority.
5. **Commit:** revalidate authority, event boundary, candidate identity and expiry immediately before consequential commitment; then close vote-specific authority and preserve provenance.

An apparent F or A cannot bypass an invalid earlier gate. An unresolved event boundary triggers `DECISION_BOUNDARY_UNKNOWN`, not a new vote simply because the system issues another identifier.

## 3. Three distinct expansion interfaces

**Candidate expansion:** A single original Steward position is vacant under a lawful cause. Use node-ring selection: first ring ordinarily five positions; second ordinarily ten additional positions; one eligible candidate fills by default; two or more are selected by unbiased audited draw. Exhaustion advances outward; independently established severe capture can justify exceptional widening. **Exactly one original voting position** is filled.

**Constituent expanded triad:** An independently authorised three-to-one resultant occupies **one** receiving decision position. The three constituent voters are not added to the receiving triad's voting denominator.

**Wider governance / emergency coordination:** A high-consequence local event with non-local effects may justify separately authorised cross-triangle or multi-scale coordination. It does not automatically grant a vacancy substitute wider jurisdiction, nor does an emergency label erase the original forum's rights or procedural safeguards.

These are different authority objects and must not be conflated.

## 4. Explicit unresolved gates

- **G-03:** quorum and procedural invalidity for ordinary and expanded triads, recusal and missing holders. Preserve the three original positions; do not invent a lower quorum threshold.
- **G-04:** instrument of binding common competence across independently authorised jurisdictions.
- **G-05:** bounded emergency preservation: triggering threshold, authorising office, minimality, expiry, restoration, challenge and remedy.
- **Decision boundary:** event ancestry and independently authorised reconsideration/appeal, as specified provisionally in 018.
- **Implementation:** fair randomness, manipulated eligibility exclusions, topology snapshots and cross-resolution mapping.

The above remain interfaces to develop **proportionately**, not reasons to retest every GTP geometry or triadic ballot permutation.

## 5. Recommendation

Treat the node-ring substitution and origin-included identity architecture as **provisionally source-reconciled**. Prioritise G-03/G-04/G-05 and decision-event boundary as narrow normative questions for authorial resolution. Maintain source provenance, avoid invented law, and do not alter original Governance V2 Canonical.
