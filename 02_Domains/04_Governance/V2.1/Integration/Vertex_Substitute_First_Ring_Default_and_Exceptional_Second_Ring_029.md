# Vertex-Adjacent Substitute Selection — Authorial Resolution 029

**Status:** Authorial clarification, provisional V2.1 integration. Supersedes the unresolved single-eligible-candidate policy in Operational Candidate 027 and Adversarial Matrix 028.

## Ordinary substitution pool — first ring

For a lawfully vacant Steward voting position A1 of triangle A at abstract vertex V, the five Steward positions of the five other triangles incident at V constitute the **first-ring alternate pool**. Each belongs to its own triangle; no common vertex office or governing body exists.

After independently valid conflict, eligibility, authority and complete-decision-event identity screening:

- **Five to two eligible:** Select one by a fair, auditable, tamper-resistant uniform random draw.
- **Exactly one eligible:** **That Steward fills the vacant position by default**, provided all substantive eligibility and authority conditions remain satisfied. No random draw is necessary; one eligible candidate does not acquire additional authority.
- **Zero eligible:** Ordinary first-ring substitution cannot proceed. Consider exceptional expansion only under a separately legitimate trigger and scope, or withhold/postpone under the applicable decision rules.

A dislike of the sole eligible candidate, anticipated vote or political preference is **not** a reason to bypass this default and expand.

## Exceptional expanded candidate pool — second ring

Each of the five neighbouring triangles has **two additional Steward positions** at its other two abstract vertices, yielding up to **ten further candidate positions** for exceptional substitution. These are not the five positions incident at V, and are not a new governing tier. Verify actual distinct participant identities and geometry before counting the pool.

This is an **exceptional candidate-pool expansion**, not a horizontal transfer of governance jurisdiction, a three-to-one extended-triad resultant, or a change to the three-position original triad. It remains a **single substitute for A1's single decision position**.

The authorial statement allows exceptional expansion *if needed*, but does not yet specify the exact legal trigger, whether second-ring candidates may be used when an eligible first-ring Steward exists, or the second-ring selection algorithm. Pending that resolution, the safe provisional interface is:
1. Use the first-ring pool normally.
2. If first-ring eligibility is exhausted or a separate independently justified exceptional circumstance is established, verify the lawful expansion basis.
3. Form the second-ring candidate set from the other two Stewards of each of the five incident neighbour triangles.
4. Apply the same eligibility, conflicts, one-person-one-position, bounded-authority, decision-event and audit gates.
5. Select one under a separately authorised fair selection procedure. **Do not silently assume** a specific ordering, weighting or mixing rule between rings.
6. Close temporary authority at the end of the single decision event.

## Important anti-capture qualification

With a single eligible first-ring candidate, appointment is deterministic. The earlier claim that capture certainty requires control of all five **assumes all five remain eligible and an uncompromised random draw**. An attacker who can manipulate disqualifications or the conflict determination may narrow the pool to one. Independent eligibility review and auditable exclusions are therefore essential.

## Abstention routing remains unchanged

Material conflict recusal can open a substitute-eligible position. Insufficient-information abstention triggers evidence gathering, not replacement on that ground. Absence and incapacity require separately lawful vacancy triggers.

## Open procedural issues

The exact exceptional expansion trigger, selection among eligible second-ring candidates, whether a justified exceptional expansion may occur despite a sole eligible first-ring Steward, independence of candidate screening, quorum, event boundaries and zero-eligible fallback remain for further development. No original canonical file is changed.
