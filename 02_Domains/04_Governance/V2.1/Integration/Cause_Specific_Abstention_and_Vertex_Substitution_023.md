# Cause-Specific Abstention and Vertex Substitution — Authorial Clarification 023

**Status:** Authorial clarification, provisional V2.1 integration, not canonical enactment. Complements `Integration/GTP_Vertex_Adjacent_Random_Substitute_Authorial_Mechanism_022.md`.

## Distinguish abstention causes

**Conflict-of-interest abstention or recusal.** A Steward who is conflicted with respect to a specific decision cannot lawfully exercise that position for the matter. This creates a **decision-specific substitute-eligible vacancy**, subject to the existing conflict, eligibility, appointment and quorum safeguards. The candidate pool comprises the five distinct triangle-owned Steward positions sharing the original Steward's abstract GTP vertex. Following lawful eligibility screening, one substitute is selected by an auditable, tamper-resistant random draw and receives only the original position's bounded vote-specific authority.

**Insufficient-information abstention.** A Steward who withholds a vote because evidence is incomplete, unreliable or not understood does **not** create a substitute-eligible vacancy merely by abstaining. The appropriate response is to obtain evidence, request clarification, consult expertise, update the decision record and re-evaluate under the lawful existing decision process. This cannot be bypassed by appointing a new voter more willing to decide.

## Decision routing

1. Record abstention or recusal and its **specific cause**, without converting all abstentions into an Against or Unresolved vote.
2. Where conflict of interest is established, initiate the bounded random vertex-adjacent substitution procedure. Ensure the original Steward cannot also cast a vote in the same position.
3. Where information is insufficient, suspend or withhold consequential resolution and route to evidence gathering and re-evaluation; do not invoke substitution on that cause alone.
4. Where causes are mixed, contested or unknown, establish the operative cause through the lawful review mechanism before selecting a substitute or treating a result as final.
5. Apply independent quorum, jurisdiction, identity uniqueness, vote-specific authority and commit checks throughout.

## Anti-capture safeguard

A conflicted Steward's substitute is not preselected by the recusing Steward or the convening forum. A fair random draw among eligible vertex-adjacent Stewards reduces predictability of the replacement. For one compromised alternate in a five-person eligible pool, a single uniform draw has 1/5 selection probability, **conditional** on the draw, candidate screening and vacancy trigger being uncompromised. Do not allow manipulated conflict declarations or repeated draws to create a preferred replacement.

## Interaction with existing governance

This is **one-position temporary substitution**, not the three-to-one extended-triad resultant and not an automatic jurisdictional expansion. The same one-Steward-one-position-per-decision and no-geometry-derived-authority constraints apply.

The user identifies existing conflict-of-interest abstention provisions; their exact original source wording and location have **not yet been independently verified in this record**. Reconcile with those provisions before finalising the constitutional chapter.

## Outstanding procedural questions

Precise conflict finding/challenge authority; insufficient-information deadlines and escalation; evidence refresh and lawful continuation versus separate decision; selection audit and quorum. None of these gaps authorises automatic replacement for an information-based abstention.
