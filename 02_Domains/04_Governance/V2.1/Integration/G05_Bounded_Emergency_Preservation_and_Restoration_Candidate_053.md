## Distinguish urgency from exceptional authority

**Urgent ordinary action:** an independently valid majority acts earlier than the next normal Civilisational Clock cycle **within its existing mandate**. This does not require or imply an extraordinary emergency office.

**Exceptional emergency preservation:** where ordinary lawful capacity is insufficient to prevent serious imminent harm, a *separately established* emergency mandate may authorise narrowly defined protective measures. Neither the label 'emergency' nor a GTP scale or adjacency creates that mandate.

## Candidate validity gates

1. **Trigger and evidence:** documented serious, time-sensitive threat, likely consequences, affected rights and why ordinary competent action cannot adequately prevent harm. Mere inconvenience, disagreement, an Against majority or Unresolved outcome is not enough.
2. **Authority:** identify the existing constitutional/legal source, empowered participants, territorial and functional bounds, and the legal procedure for activation. **No source means no inferred exceptional power.** Immediate protective action may still be possible under independently existing ordinary duties.
3. **Necessity and proportionality:** use the least rights-intrusive effective measures within the mandate, with clear protected objectives, resource limits and safeguards for nonparticipants.
4. **Coordination without capture:** neighbouring or wider GTP areas may share sensing, evacuation support, logistics and information at multiple scales. Binding commitments of independent areas require their own lawful basis, not geographic proximity or an expanded substitute lottery.
5. **Time and termination:** log activation time and relationship to Civilisational Clock transitions; set a short bounded expiry or objective termination condition, with independently authorised renewal if absolutely necessary. End exceptional authority as soon as the predicate ends.
6. **Review and restoration:** preserve contemporaneous evidence, decisions, dissent, affected-party rights, audit and challenges. Restore ordinary competent governance; address compensation, remediation and wrongful-action remedies through valid processes.

## Example: nuclear station incident

An incident in triangle A threatens downwind areas B and C. A's authorised Stewards can act urgently within A's pre-existing powers, including an already valid majority without waiting for an outcome-irrelevant third ballot when applicable rules allow. B and C may independently take protective measures under their own powers; all may coordinate monitoring and evacuation logistics. A shared emergency command **does not appear automatically** because the plume crosses GTP boundaries. Any order binding B or C must identify a separately valid emergency or common-competence mandate. Widening A's node-ring candidate pool to substitute one conflicted Steward is a different mechanism entirely.

## Decision provenance

Maintain separate records for: ordinary urgent timing; activation of any exceptional authority; each specific measure; cross-area consent/delegation or legal basis; expiry/renewal; and restoration/review. A vote marked A remains A and a vote marked U remains U; neither may be reclassified to manufacture emergency permission.

## Open authorial choices

- Which pre-existing Concord offices or provisions, if any, may authorise exceptional preservation, and what independently verifiable activation threshold applies?
- What maximum duration, renewal oversight and post-incident review are required?
- Which rights cannot be suspended, and what immediate remedies or emergency challenges remain available?

**Recommended narrow principle:** emergency action is lawful only under a bounded pre-existing authority; emergency **coordination** can widen spatially without widening **jurisdiction**. Any extraordinary authority expires and returns to ordinary governance with audit and remedies.
