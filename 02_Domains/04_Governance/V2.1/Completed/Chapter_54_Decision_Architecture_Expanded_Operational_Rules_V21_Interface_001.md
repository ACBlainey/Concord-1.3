## Proposed V2.1 clarification

For an expanded triadic decision, add a proposal-level decision-topology record: origin R; adjacent A/B/C; origin-included or excluded mode; eligible members of each constituent triad; authorisation and conflicts; three individual constituent majority results; virtual-triad result; any unresolved position and reason; implementation scope; minority reasoning; sunset and review. Never substitute the pooled count of nine ballots for the three resultant votes. If constituent results are F,F,U the virtual result is F; F,A,U is Unresolved. Expansion must not change the proposal's scope or resource authority without the material-amendment process already specified by V2 §§54.4 and 54.11.

## Integration / validation requirement

Determine confidentiality levels for individual votes versus public resultant votes, consistent with §§54.28–54.30.

## Provenance and non-drift constraints

Author-confirmed expanded triadic voting (October 2026) and the GTP spatial-mesh clarification are supplemental to existing V2 protections. Do not silently rewrite other provisions, convert Parallel Stewardship into voting office, imply automatic hierarchical authority from geometric nesting, or treat unresolved votes as substantive rejection. This is a draft for ten-chapter sectional assembly, not an adopted amendment.
