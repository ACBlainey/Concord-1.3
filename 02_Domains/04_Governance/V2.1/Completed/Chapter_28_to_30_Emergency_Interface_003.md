# Chapters 28–30 — Exact Emergency Interface 003

**Status:** Provisional subsection-level integration plan; source: Governance V2 Canonical Chapters 28–30. Original provisions remain operative in the source manuscript.

## Chapter 28 §28.3 — Succession Order
**Existing:** V2 permits primary/secondary successors, emergency delegate, temporary collective body and fallback; explicitly asks how to handle compromised identity, communications, successor incapacity and corrupted records. **Preserve.**

**Proposed insertion after existing succession list:** A successor occupies only the specified Steward position after the relevant pre-existing succession rule establishes identity, eligibility, independence, competence, scope and expiry. Geometric adjacency and six-Steward vertex representation alone do not appoint a successor. A Parallel Steward is advisory unless separately and validly appointed. A substantive Against vote or principled abstention is not vacancy. Any substitution must be logged and reviewable.

## Chapter 29 §29.2 — One Steward Unavailable
**Existing:** V2 explicitly allows delay, emergency substitute, delegated replacement, escalation or a predefined degraded decision rule, and distinguishes temporary absence from vacancy. **Preserve.**

**Proposed insertion after distinction:** Classify absence, vacancy, recusal, abstention, failed authentication, compromised independence and lack of evidence separately. If two eligible positions lawfully vote the same way, a three-position triad can have a valid majority notwithstanding the third unresolved position, subject to existing quorum rules. A failed decision is not grounds to shop for a new forum. An origin-included/excluded expansion is a separate, specifically authorised procedure, not a default replacement for ordinary triad succession.

## Chapter 30 §30.2 — Emergency Deadlock
**Existing:** V2 forbids pretending a majority exists and lists evidence, substitute, escalation, specialised emergency body, preservation action or constitutionally established pathway. **Preserve.**

**Proposed insertion after the existing options:** Record the distinction between a substantive majority Against and U (no valid majority). For a virtual triad, F/F/U→F, A/A/U→A, F/A/U→U. Escalation may change the forum only by a separately authorised mechanism and may not silently change the decision's scope or authority. If status quo creates serious danger, use only an independently legitimate, narrow emergency preservation route, with time limits and audit. Do not treat a Parallel Steward's dissent as a fourth vote or a substitute appointment.
