# G-03 — Civilisational Clock and Contextual Majority Effect 049

**Status:** PROVISIONAL authorial resolution of timing principle; not constitutional adoption. Builds on authorial direction 048. Original Governance V2 Canonical remains untouched.

## Authorial resolution

A substantive triadic majority may become **mathematically determined** when two independently valid Steward positions vote alike (F/F or A/A), even if the third position's vote is outstanding. The third vote can no longer change that majority, but its later entry remains material to **provenance, dissent, reasons and accountability**.

**Operative timing is separate from majority determination.** Many Concord actions are governed by the **Civilisational Clock** and may take effect at the next applicable cycle rather than immediately. Where the situation is urgent, an already validly authorised majority can trigger timely action without waiting for a third vote that cannot alter the result. Context determines the applicable action window; urgency does not itself confer jurisdiction, implementation powers or emergency authority.

## Three operational classes

1. **Routine / clock-scheduled:** Record the determined majority and any missing ballot. Queue implementation for the next legally applicable Civilisational Clock transition or other established effective-time rule. A later official third vote completes the record but does not change a two-vote majority.
2. **Urgent within existing mandate:** Where lawful urgency conditions are satisfied, the determined majority may permit action before the next ordinary cycle. Preserve why the urgent timing was necessary, what mandate permits it and which consequences were authorised. Do not manufacture a separate emergency jurisdiction.
3. **Outcome not determined:** Split F/A or insufficient independently valid votes cannot be described as a determined majority. Continue deliberation, seek the missing official ballot, or use a separately authorised one-position substitute or expanded resultant.

## Provenance and provisional ballots

An absent Steward's position is recorded as **OUTSTANDING**, not automatically as U, Against or a disqualifying third ballot. A lawfully appointed temporary Steward, expanded resultant or provisional position vote may occupy that position only under its own bounded procedure.

If a provisional ballot is used, the returning original Steward's **OFFICIAL** ballot can supersede it as the current authoritative vote for that position, while the provisional ballot remains preserved in immutable history. Supersession changes **position-level provenance**, not a mathematically fixed majority already reached by the other two valid positions. It does not automatically undo lawful actions already taken. A challenge, reconsideration or remedy must use a separately legitimate process.

**No double count:** At most one current ballot/resultant per original position. Steward identity must remain unique throughout the complete decision event.

## Validity safeguards

- The two majority-forming positions must be validly occupied, eligible, authenticated and acting within a competent forum and mandate.
- This is not a blanket declaration that every two-vote case may be committed: applicable law must permit the decision to be operative while the third position is outstanding, and all remaining validity/commit gates must pass.
- Distinguish **OUTCOME_DETERMINED**, **RECORD_INCOMPLETE**, **EFFECTIVE_AT_NEXT_CLOCK**, **EFFECTIVE_URGENTLY** and **OUTCOME_UNDETERMINED** as orthogonal states, not interchangeable votes.
- No third Steward may be excluded deliberately to manufacture timing advantage. Record contact/availability efforts and permit the lawful later official ballot.
- A valid substantive **Against** majority is equally determinative; urgency cannot relabel it as Unresolved to bypass rejection.
- For virtual triads, distinguish the three resultant positions from their constituent Steward ballots. An independently valid resultant-level majority may be mathematically determined, but unresolved constituent constitution or authority cannot be silently bypassed.

## Minimal remaining authorial/implementation interfaces

1. Which decisions may operate on a determined two-position majority with third ballot outstanding, and which classes legally require full participation?
2. Which Civilisational Clock transition governs each class, and which urgency thresholds authorise earlier implementation?
3. What is the official-vote completion window and archival/finalisation policy when a Steward never returns?

These are bounded **timing and validity interfaces**, not a need for further geometric enumeration.
