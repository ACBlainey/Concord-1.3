## Source and existing protections

**Local Governance V2 source:** V2 §§198.1–198.20, local lines 49119–49377.

The chapter already defines geographic, functional, institutional, personal, technological, issue-specific and overlapping jurisdictions, including positive/negative conflicts.

## Proposed V2.1 clarification

A GTP spatial address identifies where a decision's effects and participants lie, but is neither proof of authority nor the sole dimension of jurisdiction. A proposal must identify its subject, participants, geographic reach, material externalities, time and delegated authority. Overlapping GTP fields do not automatically merge voting rights. Resolve positive jurisdiction conflicts through the applicable constitutional conflict procedure, not by treating the larger triangle as superior. A negative jurisdiction conflict requires a legitimate referral or bounded coordination path, not an improvised general power. Origin-included or origin-excluded expanded triads must document the legal authority to decide the specified issue.
