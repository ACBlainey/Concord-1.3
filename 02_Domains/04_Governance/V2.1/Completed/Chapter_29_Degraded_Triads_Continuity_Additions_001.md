# Chapter 29 — Degraded Triads: V2.1 Continuity Additions 001

**Status:** Proposed additions to V2 Chapter 29, not a replacement. **Section 03 (Chapters 21–30).** Source: local Governance V2 Canonical (1).md, Chapter 29 lines 14073–14214, especially §§29.2, 29.5–29.6. Original chapter must be retained in sectional assembly.

## Proposed §29.V21.1 — Capability and failure classification
Before activating extraordinary voting, identify whether the difficulty is: temporary absence, vacancy, communication failure, loss of secure authentication, recusal, incapacity, lack of evidence, compromised independence, or principled abstention. Record urgency and whether ordinary delay is safe. Do not treat these conditions as interchangeable.

## Proposed §29.V21.2 — Bounded continuity sequence
Where lawful and proportionate, prefer (a) normal triad, (b) restoration or short safe delay, (c) independently authorised substitute, (d) reconstitution of valid triad, (e) origin-included or origin-excluded expanded voting if the decision field and authority justify it, (f) bounded further mesh expansion or lawful emergency preservation route. This is an **assessment order**, not a mandatory escalation ladder or transfer of jurisdiction. Selection depends on the problem, authority and consequence field.

## Proposed §29.V21.3 — Parallel and substitute Stewards
A Parallel Steward's independent advisory role does not automatically make them a substitute voting Steward. Any conversion to an authorised decision position requires an independent succession or appointment basis, eligibility, conflicts review, scope, expiry and record. Never substitute solely to reverse an unwanted vote.

## Proposed §29.V21.4 — Expanded triad in degraded conditions
An expanded decision must specify three constituent triads and three resultant positions; each constituent triad votes independently, and the final virtual triad votes on the three results. The originating R may be excluded (A/B/C vote) or included (2A+1R, 2B+1R, 2C+1R). Individual votes are not pooled. If a constituent cannot produce a valid majority, its result is Unresolved; a valid two-result majority may still determine the virtual result.

## Proposed §29.V21.5 — Failure of the exceptional route
If a valid decision cannot be constituted within the permitted time and scope, record non-authorisation and use only separately legitimate emergency preservation powers. Further expansion must not continue indefinitely or manufacture an affirmative majority. Reconstitute normal decision capability promptly.

## Reconciliation flags
The V2 text permits emergency substitute, delegated replacement, escalation and predefined degraded decision rules (§29.2), while limiting unilateral power (§29.6). Precise appointment authority and the six-Steward vertex eligibility map remain unresolved; do not present the above sequence as already enacted constitutional procedure.
