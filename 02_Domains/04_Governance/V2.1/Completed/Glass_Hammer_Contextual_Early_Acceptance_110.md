# Glass Hammer — Contextual Early Acceptance 110

**Status:** provisional interpretation and fixture, not a constitutional amendment.

## Author clarification

The third vote's timeliness is **contextual**. Two matching official Steward votes establish a clear outcome. Whether the system may **accept the result early for progression** depends on a legitimate contextual justification. Emergency urgency is an identified example; future eligible circumstances cannot be exhaustively enumerated. The third Steward's separate ballot remains recordable and does not change the two-vote outcome.

## Minimal extension, not architectural conflict

1. **Determination**: two matching F or A ballots determine the arithmetic result.
2. **Acceptance**: ordinarily allow the third Steward to participate; where an independently justified context warrants early progression, accept the already-determined result before third-ballot arrival.
3. **Third ballot**: the third Steward retains a separately context-guarded, single-use submission route. Late records are evidence/provenance and do not reopen or reverse the accepted outcome.
4. **Execution**: accepting a decision does not create external action authority. Host-side action requires its own legitimate current authority and context.
5. **Clock**: urgent acceptance is evaluated by the operational state machine, not by waiting for a variable or paused Civilisation Clock.

## Safeguards and non-prescription

- No fixed list of eligible contexts is prescribed. The example fixture accepts a `basis` string and `early_context_justified` boolean supplied by an external owner; these are **test inputs, not trustworthy authority evidence**.
- Emergency alone is not a universal override of rights, necessity, proportionality or competent jurisdiction.
- Early acceptance must have a documented reason, an identifiable authorised evaluator and a challenge/review path before production graduation.
- The analogy is explanatory. Neither a six-digit state code nor the literal hammer is a technical requirement.
- The Stage 110 adapter is deliberately separate from Stage 108 to avoid silently changing previously verified fixtures.
- The original 093 late-third-vote provenance behaviour and Stage 108 immediate-close behaviour are resolved conceptually by separating **result determination** from **contextual acceptance**; production-level reconciliation and source-owned policies remain open.

## Verification

16 Stage 110 tests cover ordinary waiting, emergency and other contextual early acceptance, third ballot before/after acceptance, single-use constraints, no outcome reversal and execution separation.
