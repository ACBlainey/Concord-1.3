# Chapter 132 — GTP Constitutional Portability Clarification 002

**Status:** Editorial correction to the provisional Chapter 132 patch 001; do not silently supersede canonical V2. For Section 14 (Chapters 131–140).

## The precise conflict
V2 §132.1 calls GTP an optional implementation module and a *hierarchical* territorial partition. V2 §132.2 insists the constitutional principles must remain usable even if GTP is modified, rejected or replaced. V2 §§132.3 and 132.11 correctly state that geometry does not create legitimacy.

The author's clarified GTP concept is a **permanent conceptual multiresolution triangular mesh** in which every spatial subdivision always exists, with multiple operating resolutions and overlapping coordination. Geometric parent/child is *not* governance superior/subordinate. Triadic Stewardship and expanded voting use specific edge-adjacent triangles.

**These facts do not logically require deleting V2 §132.2's constitutional portability safeguard.** Distinguish the status of GTP as Concord's adopted/default spatial architecture from the source of rights and legitimacy.

## Proposed replacement of §§132.1–132.5 (subject to approval)

### 132.1 — GTP spatial reference architecture
GTP provides Concord's adopted conceptual multiresolution triangular spatial reference and coordination mesh. All tessellation levels exist as a spatial abstraction; activating a resolution means choosing the scale at which a particular task operates, not creating or deleting spatial cells.

### 132.2 — Constitutional independence and spatial continuity
GTP is foundational to the **current Concord spatial governance implementation**, but geometry is not the source of constitutional rights, ethical standing, lawful authority or legitimacy. The constitutional safeguards must remain valid if a future properly authorised revision modifies or replaces the spatial implementation. Such a change requires explicit change control, impact assessment and migration of spatial references; it must not occur by silent reinterpretation.

### 132.3 — Geometric relations are not command relations
Spatial parent/child refers only to tessellation resolution and containment. No governance office derives a power to direct another solely because its cell is spatially larger. Separately enacted institutional hierarchy, constitutional priority or lawful delegation may still exist; those arise from law, not GTP geometry.

### 132.4 — Concurrent scale and adaptive coverage
Multiple resolutions may be operational simultaneously, including overlapping coordination fields. Workload changes may alter operational coverage, with hysteresis to avoid oscillation; geometric subdivision is permanent. Jurisdiction, competence, rights, consent, resource authority and externalities determine the lawful decision field.

### 132.5 — Edge adjacency and authorised expanded triads
For origin R, edge-adjacent A, B and C may support (i) origin-excluded constituent triads A/B/C, or (ii) origin-included mixed constituent triads 2A+1R, 2B+1R, 2C+1R. Each constituent produces one resultant vote; the three results form the final virtual triad. The nine individual ballots are not pooled. The six-Steward vertex mapping and selection rules remain open.

## Preservation requirements for §§132.6–132.12
Preserve and reconcile population variation, geographic neutrality, boundary revision, digital communities, interplanetary settlement, legitimacy and the chapter principle. Do not delete those provisions simply because §§132.1–132.5 need repair.

## Source-status distinctions
- **Verified V2:** constitutional independence, geography does not create legitimacy, population variation and other limits.
- **Author-confirmed:** permanent conceptual tessellation, non-hierarchical spatial relations, simultaneous resolutions, triadic expansion.
- **Proposed editorial reconciliation:** 'foundational to current spatial implementation' with constitutional portability retained.
- **Unresolved:** whether this exact constitutional interface is acceptable to the author; six-Steward vertex role map.

**No V2 canonical change is authorised by this patch.**
