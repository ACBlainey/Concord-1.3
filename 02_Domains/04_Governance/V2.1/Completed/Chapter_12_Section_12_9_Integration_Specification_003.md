# Chapter 12 §12.9 — Expanded Triad Integration Specification 003

**Status:** Draft exact-subsection addition, not canonical. **Source:** Governance V2 Canonical, §12.9, “Temporary Expanded Triads”; V2 §12.5 on non-transfer of jurisdiction.

## Preserve §12.9 opening and safeguards
The original section permits a temporary expanded decision structure, requires participating jurisdictions, decision authority, voting rules, duration, scope, implementation responsibility, review and termination, and forbids unrelated responsibilities. **Retain all those provisions.**

## Insert after the existing §12.9 list of required fields
**Proposed §12.9.A — GTP origin and adjacency.** For a qualifying GTP-based expanded decision, identify the inverted originating triangle R and its three edge-adjacent triangles A, B and C. A spatial cell is a reference field, not an independent grant of jurisdiction. The authority to convene and act must be independently verified.

**Proposed §12.9.B — Two permitted constituent arrangements.** In origin-excluded mode, the three constituent decision groups are the three separately authorised three-Steward triads of A, B and C. In origin-included mode, each constituent group is a three-position mixed triad consisting of two authorised positions from A (or B or C respectively) and one authorised position from R. R receives no fourth resultant position. Eligibility, conflicts and whether a person may fill more than one mixed position require explicit constitutional rules; do not infer them from geometry.

**Proposed §12.9.C — Two-stage voting.** Each constituent triad produces exactly one resultant position: For (F), Against (A) or Unresolved (U). The three constituent results form a virtual final triad; the final result is F if at least two results are F, A if at least two are A, and otherwise U. Nine underlying individual votes must never be pooled into one majority. A resultant U records why it arose and does not become an automatic A.

**Proposed §12.9.D — Bounded application.** The record must specify the legitimate decision field, the origin-included/excluded mode, the three constituent compositions, evidence and decision version, each constituent result, final result, jurisdiction, resource authority, implementation and sunset. Repetition or recursive expansion to manufacture a majority is prohibited. Any broader authority requires a separately valid decision and delegation.

**Proposed §12.9.E — Local autonomy and shared consequences.** An expanded triad may coordinate information and genuinely shared resources without overriding lawful independent local decisions. Cross-jurisdictional voting requires an identified common competence; a count of neighbouring regional preferences is not itself jurisdiction.
