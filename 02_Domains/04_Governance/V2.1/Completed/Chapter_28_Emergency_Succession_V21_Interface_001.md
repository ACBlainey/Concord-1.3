# Chapter 28 — Emergency Succession: V2.1 Interface Patch 001

**Status:** Provisional additive patch, not canonical; intended for Section 03. Preserve all unaffected original V2 text.

## Source and existing protections

**Local Governance V2 source:** V2 §§28.2–28.7, local lines 13947–14072.

Succession is continuity rather than opportunity; restoration and no emergency inheritance are already explicit.

## Proposed V2.1 clarification

An unavailable or compromised Steward may be replaced only through a pre-authorised, position-specific succession mechanism. A Parallel Steward's advisory function does not automatically supply succession authority. A GTP vertex's six-Steward representation is a pool of potential roles, not a universal succession list. Each appointment records reason, position, competence, conflict check, jurisdiction, expiry and restoration. Where a triad cannot be lawfully reconstituted, assess exceptional origin-included/excluded expansion only if its own authority and scope conditions are satisfied. Spatially larger GTP triangles are not inherently superior offices. A contrary substantive vote or principled abstention is not itself a vacancy.

## Integration / validation requirement

Verify V2 §28.3 succession order against the actual six-Steward vertex office map before establishing any default nominee.

## Provenance and non-drift constraints

Author-confirmed expanded triadic voting (October 2026) and the GTP spatial-mesh clarification are supplemental to existing V2 protections. Do not silently rewrite other provisions, convert Parallel Stewardship into voting office, imply automatic hierarchical authority from geometric nesting, or treat unresolved votes as substantive rejection. This is a draft for ten-chapter sectional assembly, not an adopted amendment.
