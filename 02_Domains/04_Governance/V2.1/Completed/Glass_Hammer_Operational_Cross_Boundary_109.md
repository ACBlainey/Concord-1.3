## Stage 109 boundaries

1. **Individual ballot:** the owning state machine performs a context-guarded, single-use event. An office/credential alone is insufficient. Its unique event ID does not become transferable authority.
2. **Triadic resolution:** a second matching F or A determines the result in the simplified fixture. Abstention U is neither F nor A. Three ballot records can remain unresolved.
3. **Host execution:** a resolved outcome is evidence, not authority to perform a separate action. A host must independently revalidate current execution context and authority. The integration fixture returns `ELIGIBLE_FOR_HOST_REVALIDATION` rather than executing any effect.
4. **CRSTL:** a read-only transition projection records source-shaped local transition metadata. It cannot mint authority, establish a global causal order or block an emergency awaiting a paused Civilisation Clock.
5. **State Map:** any future projection is descriptive, not an authority issuer.

## Known gaps and source fidelity

- Stage 108 closes ballot acceptance after a matching two-vote result. Earlier 093 allows a third Steward ballot as **provenance-only** after a resolved result. This is a **real discrepancy**, not proof of compatibility. Reconcile it in the next stage with a separate state path for late official ballot evidence, without allowing a second authority exercise or result mutation.
- Stage 108 uses externally asserted eligibility/context booleans and does not cryptographically authenticate Steward identity, bind an actual contextual wrapper, or implement physical effects.
- Stage 109 CRSTL projection does not implement a Clock service, distributed reconciliation or cross-domain causality.
- The analogy's three rooms and three cases illustrate independent Steward decisions, not a requirement to serialize the three ballots or to use six-digit passwords.
- The original BCA section 30A.4 allows continuous bounded functions. Individual consequential acts must be evaluated against their current justified context without silently converting a continuing function into a standing authority balance.
- The precise constitutional abstention/late-vote treatment remains subject to primary-source resolution.
