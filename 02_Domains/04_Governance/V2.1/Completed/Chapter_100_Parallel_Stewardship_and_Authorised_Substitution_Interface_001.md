# Chapter 100 — Parallel Stewardship: V2.1 Interface Additions 001

**Status:** Proposed clarification; **Section 10 (Chapters 91–100)**. Source: local V2 Chapter 100 lines 31556–31680, §§100.1–100.11. **Preserve the existing chapter in full.**

## Proposed §100.V21.1 — Two distinct functions
**Parallel Steward:** Independently reasons about evidence, assumptions, alternatives and constitutional risks; may document agreement or dissent; is advisory and does not automatically vote or command.

**Authorised substitute Steward:** Temporarily occupies a formally established voting position pursuant to a separate succession, delegation or emergency authority rule. The fact that a person has served as a Parallel Steward does not itself authorise the substitution.

## Proposed §100.V21.2 — Independence before comparison
Parallel reasoning should ordinarily be recorded before exposure to the primary Steward's conclusions. Shared data, model lineage, institutional dependence, funding and common assumptions should be checked for correlated failure. Parallel agreement is evidence, not proof; disagreement is a diagnostic prompt, not a fourth vote.

## Proposed §100.V21.3 — Eligibility for exceptional appointment
A Parallel Steward may be considered as a substitute only if the appointment mechanism separately validates identity, competence, independence, conflicts, jurisdiction, specific voting position, duration and audit. Appointment cannot be justified merely because the incumbent voted Against or abstained on principle.

## Proposed §100.V21.4 — Six-Steward vertex pools
The author-confirmed GTP vertex representation of six Stewards may support alternative selection and emergency continuity. However, vertex affiliation alone grants no automatic decision authority. The formal mapping from vertex positions to eligible decision roles, and protections against duplicate effective votes across constituent triads, require separate source resolution and testing.

## Proposed §100.V21.5 — Return to advisory status
Any temporary voting authority ends at its stated expiry, the restoration of ordinary office where applicable, or the conclusion of the authorised decision. Advisory reasoning may continue independently; no emergency participation creates permanent executive entitlement.

## Integration warning
The source V2 §100.11 expressly defines Parallel Stewardship as independent advisory reasoning without silent executive authority. These proposed clauses clarify its interface with emergency succession; they do **not** convert Parallel Stewardship itself into a voting institution.
