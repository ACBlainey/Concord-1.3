## Proposed V2.1 clarification

The lowest capable legitimate decision field is selected by consequences, competence, rights, resources and jurisdiction rather than GTP triangle size alone. GTP's finer and coarser spatial resolutions coexist conceptually; changing the operational resolution of coverage does not create or extinguish spatial triangles. Where workload warrants subdividing an operating service area, introduce hysteresis to prevent repeated reallocation. This is a workload and coordination adaptation, not the automatic creation of superior or inferior governing authorities. Multiple scales and overlapping decision fields may operate concurrently when their authority boundaries are explicit.

## Integration / validation requirement

Specify hysteresis metrics and how operational reassignment affects accountability without making geographic coverage a proxy for constitutional legitimacy.

## Provenance and non-drift constraints

Author-confirmed expanded triadic voting (October 2026) and the GTP spatial-mesh clarification are supplemental to existing V2 protections. Do not silently rewrite other provisions, convert Parallel Stewardship into voting office, imply automatic hierarchical authority from geometric nesting, or treat unresolved votes as substantive rejection. This is a draft for ten-chapter sectional assembly, not an adopted amendment.
