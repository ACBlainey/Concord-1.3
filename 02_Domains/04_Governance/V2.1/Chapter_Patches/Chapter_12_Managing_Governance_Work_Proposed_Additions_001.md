# Chapter 12 — Managing Governance Work
## V2.1 provisional replacement/addition clauses — 001

### 12.V21.1 — Lowest capable level
Governance decisions shall ordinarily remain at the lowest capable, legitimate decision level. Capability requires relevant knowledge, jurisdiction, resources, procedural integrity and ability to account for material external effects. A wider geographic problem does not automatically establish a wider authority. Mere disagreement between capable regions is not a reason to escalate.

### 12.V21.2 — Spatial field and two principal expanded configurations
Let R be the originating GTP triangle and A, B, C its three edge-adjacent triangles. GTP is a spatial mesh: spatial parent/child is not a governance chain of command.

**Origin excluded:** Each of A, B and C constitutes an authorised three-Steward triad and independently produces one resultant vote. R contributes no voting position. The three resultant votes constitute the final virtual triad.

**Origin included:** Constitute three independent mixed triads: (2 A + 1 R), (2 B + 1 R), (2 C + 1 R). Each independently produces one resultant vote. The three resultant votes constitute the final virtual triad. The origin does not contribute a fourth resultant vote.

The exact eligible officeholders must be selected according to documented authority, conflicts and independence checks. GTP vertices are represented by six Stewards in the wider mesh; this permits alternative configurations but confers no automatic additional ballot.

### 12.V21.3 — Vote aggregation
Each three-position constituent triad resolves to **For** if at least two valid votes are For; **Against** if at least two valid votes are Against; otherwise **Unresolved**. The final virtual triad applies the same rule to the three constituent results. The nine individual ballots **shall not** be pooled into a single nine-person majority. Unresolved is not silently treated as Against. No valid majority means no new state-change authorisation through that vote.

### 12.V21.4 — Exceptional continuity and expansion
Parallel Stewardship supplies independent advisory reasoning and does not itself grant executive voting authority. A substitute must receive separately defined, bounded voting authority through the applicable succession mechanism. Absence, recusal, incapacity and compromised independence may justify delay, substitution, reconstitution or further mesh expansion where independently justified. Principled abstention must not be bypassed merely to obtain approval. Recursive expansion requires a defined voting topology, scope, eligibility, finite stopping rule, sunset and audit.

### 12.V21.5 — Large-area governance
Distinguish **coordination**, **independent local decisions**, **genuinely shared aggregated decisions**, and **hybrid arrangements**. Five regions experiencing a hurricane may share forecasts and emergency resources while making distinct evacuation-policy decisions. Four regions voting for mandatory evacuation do not automatically override a fifth region voting against it. Participant rights, emergency protections, cross-boundary impacts and resource obligations remain separately assessable. Common infrastructure with material cross-boundary consequences may require a properly authorised joint decision.

### 12.V21.6 — Required record
Record originating spatial address and scale, scope, reason for expansion, each constituent group's membership and conflicts, three constituent results, final result, abstention causes, minority reasoning, authority basis, affected regions, implementation boundaries, sunset and review path.
