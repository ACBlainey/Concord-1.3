# Chapter 150 — Governance and Central Coordination: V2.1 Interface Patch 001

**Status:** Provisional additive patch, not canonical; intended for Section 15. Preserve all unaffected original V2 text.

## Source and existing protections

**Local Governance V2 source:** V2 §§150.1–150.10, local lines 41292–41417.

Enumerated central authority, residual local authority, scope containment and emergency limits are explicit.

## Proposed V2.1 clarification

Separate four functions: information sharing; coordination of shared logistics; independent local policy decisions; and a specifically authorised common decision over indivisible externalities. A hurricane affecting five regions does not turn a 4-to-1 regional policy preference into authority over the fifth region. However, shared evacuation corridors, flood infrastructure or scarce common resources may require a separately constituted decision with explicit scope and rights safeguards. A broad GTP coordination field is not a central executive office merely because its spatial resolution is coarser.

## Integration / validation requirement

Define the threshold for material cross-boundary externalities and the lawful forum for truly joint decisions; avoid assuming every shared impact requires identical local policy.

## Provenance and non-drift constraints

Author-confirmed expanded triadic voting (October 2026) and the GTP spatial-mesh clarification are supplemental to existing V2 protections. Do not silently rewrite other provisions, convert Parallel Stewardship into voting office, imply automatic hierarchical authority from geometric nesting, or treat unresolved votes as substantive rejection. This is a draft for ten-chapter sectional assembly, not an adopted amendment.
