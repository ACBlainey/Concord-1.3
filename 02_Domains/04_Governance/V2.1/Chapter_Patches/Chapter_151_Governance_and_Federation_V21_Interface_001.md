## Source and existing protections

**Local Governance V2 source:** V2 §§151.1–151.12, local lines 41420–41553.

The chapter allows coexistence of local, regional and wider authority; enumerated and residual competences; shared and exclusive functions; jurisdiction conflicts and exit.

## Proposed V2.1 clarification

Distinguish institutional federation from GTP's non-hierarchical geometric mesh. Federation may have legally defined competence allocations, but geometric parent/child triangles alone do not create that allocation. Concurrent GTP spatial resolutions may support federated coordination without becoming a chain of command. In overlapping decision fields, identify whether competence is exclusive, shared, advisory or operationally coordinated; record the lawful basis before applying any aggregated vote. A virtual triad aggregates only the three constituent votes in an authorised expanded decision—not all neighbouring regions' independent political decisions.
