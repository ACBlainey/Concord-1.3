# Chapter 30 — Emergency Abstention and Decision Failure: V2.1 Additions 001

**Status:** Proposed additions; **Section 03 (Chapters 21–30)**. Based on local V2 Chapter 30 lines 14215–14367, especially §§30.1–30.5. Preserve existing V2 text.

## Proposed §30.V21.1 — Result versus procedural condition
Every three-position triad produces **For**, **Against**, or **Unresolved**. For and Against require at least two valid corresponding votes; otherwise Unresolved. Record the cause: principled abstention, absence, recusal, invalid authentication, evidential insufficiency or other cause. **Against is a substantive majority decision; Unresolved is not.**

## Proposed §30.V21.2 — Emergency does not convert abstention
Abstention is not consent, opposition, vacancy or automatic grounds for replacing a voter. A substitute may be considered only if an independent rule legitimately authorises substitution for the actual cause. A reasoned abstention may be the constitutionally correct outcome. A valid two-vote majority is not defeated merely because the third position abstains.

## Proposed §30.V21.3 — Expanded vote with unresolved constituent
For three resultant positions (F,A,U), the final result is U; for (F,F,U), it is F; for (A,A,U), it is A. Where an unresolved result blocks decision and delay creates serious harm, the system may consider the pre-authorised emergency mechanisms in V2 §30.2, but may not invent a majority or repeatedly expand until the preferred result is obtained.

## Proposed §30.V21.4 — Non-action and emergency preservation
Where no legitimate majority exists, no state change is authorised by the attempted vote. Where maintaining the status quo is itself dangerous, only a separately lawful, narrowly scoped emergency preservation route may act; it requires evidence, audit, sunset and rapid independent review. Inaction's consequences must be recorded, as V2 §30.4 already recognises.

## Proposed §30.V21.5 — Anti-laundering
Escalation changes the decision forum only within a specific legitimate delegation. A larger GTP triangle has no automatic superior authority by virtue of spatial scale. A failed local vote does not create a general power to govern that region.
