# Origin-Included Eligibility — Resolution of Repeated-Origin Hypothesis 013

**Status:** V2.1 design validation against explicit authorial clarification; not a live or blind test.

## Resolution of Test 012

Test 012 compared H1 (one originating Steward repeated across all three mixed constituent triads) with H2 (three distinct originating Stewards). The author has now specified that **each Steward can act in only one occupied voting position across the entire decision**, and that each voting authority is a bounded, single-use event.

- **H1:** PROHIBITED. Its 44/64 pivotality result remains a mathematically correct diagnostic of a now-disallowed configuration; it must not be treated as a lawful alternative.
- **H2:** PASSES the distinct-origin identity constraint *if* the six neighbouring positions are also occupied by six distinct eligible Stewards. H2 is not thereby authorised; mandate, quorum, jurisdiction and GTP office mapping still require validation.

## Gate sequence

1. Identify one decision and its vote-event boundary.
2. Verify the competence, mandate and forum of the receiving decision.
3. Verify every substituted position and the legality of its constituent triad.
4. Resolve distinct Steward identities **across all occupied positions**, not merely inside each constituent triad.
5. Verify independence, conflicts, recusal and quorum under separate rules.
6. Resolve internal triads to single resultants; then resolve the final three positions.
7. Terminate vote-specific authority when that vote is complete. Record implementation under any separate mandate.

**Disposition:** Repeated-origin eligibility question is resolved **prohibited** by the author. Remaining open: six-Steward vertex map, eligibility, appointment, quorum, decision boundaries, independent mandates and implementation powers.
