## Reconciliation with authorial clarifications 020–023

**New concrete specification, not falsely attributed to historical text:** five other triangle-owned Stewards at the same abstract GTP vertex form the local alternate pool; a fair random draw chooses one eligible substitute; one position and one vote-specific authority only; one-Steward-one-position uniqueness; anti-capture analysis.
