# Origin-Included Virtual Triad — Repeated-Origin Influence Test 012

**Status:** Provisional arithmetic investigation. Not a source-verified eligibility rule, constitutional authorisation, empirical prediction or PMEDG graduation.

## Architecture being tested

Three mixed constituent triads are `A=[A1,A2,R_A]`, `B=[B1,B2,R_B]`, `C=[C1,C2,R_C]`. Each produces one For/Against/Unresolved resultant. The three resultants form the final virtual triad. Each constituent resultant has **one** final decision position. Nine internal ballots are never pooled.

The source leaves open whether `R_A`, `R_B` and `R_C` must be distinct eligible originating Stewards, can repeat, or are allocated according to the GTP six-triangle vertex relationship.

## Hypothesis H1: One originating Steward reused in all three constituent triads

Assume `R_A=R_B=R_C=R` and six distinct neighbouring Steward voters `A1,A2,B1,B2,C1,C2`. Assume binary F/A ballots, valid appointments, and two-of-three majority for both stages. There are seven unique individuals, nine ballot positions, and `2^7=128` unique individual ballot configurations.

To measure repeated-origin influence, hold the six neighbouring votes fixed and compare final results with R=F versus R=A. Of the `2^6=64` possible neighbouring configurations, **44** change final result when R changes, and **20** do not. The rate `44/64 = 68.75%` is a **conditional pivotality fraction under exhaustive equally enumerated binary neighbour configurations**, not a probability that R will control real decisions.

The same enumeration yields 64 For and 64 Against final results across all 128 unique-individual configurations. Symmetric final-result frequencies do **not** imply balanced individual influence.

## Hypothesis H2: Three distinct originating Stewards

Assume `R_A`, `R_B`, `R_C` are distinct, and all six neighbouring Steward positions are distinct. There are nine unique voters and `2^9=512` possible binary configurations. Each original position appears once; individual ballot influence is structurally symmetric within its own constituent triad, subject to the two-stage composition. This does **not** independently prove the arrangement is geometrically or legally authorised.

## Adversarial examples

**Repeated R decisive:** Each neighbour pair is split F/A. In every mixed triad the R vote breaks the tie. R=F gives F/F/F→F; R=A gives A/A/A→A. One individual therefore determines all three final resultants in this case despite the rule that R receives no separate fourth resultant.

**Repeated R not decisive:** Each neighbour pair is F/F. Each constituent result is F regardless of R, so final result remains F.

**Distinct R positions:** With split neighbour pairs, three independent R voters supply the three constituent resultants. Final result follows two-of-three of those distinct R positions rather than a single repeatedly counted individual.

## Constitutional interpretation

The authority-conservation invariant remains true in H1: each constituent triad supplies only one resultant. However, **conserving the weight of a resultant does not guarantee independence of the people who produce the resultants**. These are separate constraints.

The exact GTP office/vertex eligibility map must determine whether H1 is permitted, prohibited or avoidable. It would be premature to adopt H2 solely because its influence distribution is preferable.

**Disposition:** H1 is a measurable concentration-of-influence risk; H2 is an alternative design hypothesis. Neither is approved as an operational rule. Governance V2 Canonical and portable-module originals remain unchanged.
