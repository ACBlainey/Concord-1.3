# Fractal Substitute Expansion — Contextual Regression Matrix 032

**Status:** Provisional conceptual test cases, not independently executed blind tests. Based on Authorial Resolution 031.

| ID | Case | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| F01 | Five first-ring Stewards eligible | Uniform random selection of one |
| F02 | One first-ring Steward eligible | Sole eligible Steward fills original position by default |
| F03 | Zero first-ring eligible, ten second-ring Stewards eligible | Expand to second ring; uniformly select one of ten |

| F12 | Nuclear power station emergency with cross-border effects | Activate independently authorised emergency and multi-jurisdiction coordination; do not confuse with vacancy substitution |
| F13 | Nuclear emergency and one local Steward lawfully recused | Treat emergency coordination and single-position substitution as two separately authorised interfaces |
| F14 | Random draw is manipulated despite many candidates | Withhold; ring size cannot cure compromised selection |
| F15 | Insufficient-information abstention in emergency | Do not relabel as recusal solely to replace dissent; use lawful urgent evidence/decision pathways |
| F16 | Further ring has 20 geometric positions but duplicate participants | Actual eligible distinct pool, not nominal position count, governs selection |

## Invariants

One decision position, one vote-specific bounded authority, no standing vertex body, no geometric grant of jurisdiction, cause-coded abstention, no outcome-dependent redraw, and full decision-event identity uniqueness. Wider governance emergency action is distinct from a wider substitution pool.

## Remaining formalisation

GTP higher-ring topology, independence assessment, security escalation thresholds, ring-combination rule, authoritative randomness, quorum and event boundaries.
