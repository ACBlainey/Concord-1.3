## III. Potential conflicts or ambiguities requiring explicit reconciliation

**C-006-01 — Authority wording in original Chapter 12 §12.4:** “Expand participation to match the consequence boundary before expanding hierarchy” and Chapter 12 §12.11 “Vertical Escalation” retain hierarchical terminology. They may be read as institutional escalation, not GTP geometric hierarchy. Chapter 151 permits constitutionally established federal hierarchy. Add an explicit distinction in a later targeted edit, rather than removing legitimate legal escalation.

**C-006-02 — Quorum and U:** Two matching positions give an arithmetic resultant, but §§9.14, 12.9 and 29.2 make legal effect conditional on quorum and eligibility. No final quorum policy or complete procedural state machine is established. Do not silently equate U with vacancy, abstention or procedural invalidity.

**C-006-03 — R participation independence:** The origin-included procedure uses one R position per mixed triad. Whether this may be the same person across constituent triads and how independence is preserved remain undecided. Repeated influence could materially affect results.

**C-006-04 — Geometry-to-office mapping:** Six Stewards at a vertex is acknowledged but not mapped to triangle-office representation. This is an implementation blocker, not a reason to invent positions.

**C-006-05 — Common competence:** The candidate texts correctly distinguish coordination from binding shared jurisdiction, but no complete authorisation template or precedence rule is yet present.

**C-006-06 — Emergency authority:** An unresolved vote is not itself an emergency. Preservation powers require independent threshold, authorisation, expiry and post-action review. Keep separate from decision arithmetic.
