## What PH-02 actually establishes

- **Emergency does not create authority:** prior legitimate authority plus valid trigger can activate bounded temporary power; it does not create sovereignty.
- **Emergency Authority Envelope (EAE):** emergency class E, authorised actor A, affected rights R, severity/scope S, time T, oversight O, validation/review V and remedy M.
- **Three separate tests:** (1) legitimacy of the authority class, (2) valid activation, (3) legitimate particular exercise. One does not prove the next.
- **Consent to an authority envelope is not blanket consent to every exercise.** PH-02 proposes intelligible explicit civic consent to defined authority classes, subject to constitutional constraints, necessity and proportionality.
- **Prediction does not grant authority.** Forecast aggregate harm reduction is insufficient, especially where imposed harm falls disproportionately on specific people.
- **Advisory, directive and compulsory authority are different classes.** Directive does not automatically permit force or criminalise noncompliance; compulsory enforcement requires separate higher authority.
- **Contextual deviation matters:** emergency instructions may be departed from for relevant reasons; deviation alone is not proof of incapacity or unlawful conduct.

## What Phase D4 §11 actually establishes

- Emergencies alter operational priorities, **not constitutional principles**.
- Emergency governance remains constitutional governance; participant standing is not erased.
- Minimum sufficient temporary interference, necessity, proportionality, function-specific authority, accountability, records, correction and automatic sunset are identified.
- Exceptional authority terminates when its qualifying justification ends; continuation needs renewed justification.
- This document explicitly says it is **not** an adopted rights charter.
