## Three independent pathways, not one escalation ladder

| Pathway | Trigger | Authority source | Scope | Ending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ordinary urgent execution** | An already valid decision must take effect before the next normal clock transition | The same pre-existing ordinary mandate plus its valid urgency timing rule | Only what that ordinary mandate already permits | Ends with completion of action; no exceptional office is created |
| **Nonbinding cross-area emergency coordination** | Harm or risk affects multiple GTP areas | Each area's own authority to communicate, warn, prepare and offer lawful support | Shared information and voluntary support, not compulsory external orders | Ends when operational need ends |
| **Exceptional preservation power** | Serious time-sensitive harm cannot adequately be prevented under ordinary powers | **Must be separately found and verified in existing law or expressly created by later authorial constitutional work** | Minimum necessary temporary protective measures, within explicit rights, geography, duration and review constraints | Automatic expiry/restoration and independent review required by its mandate |

A high-consequence incident can involve all three simultaneously, but none automatically authorises the next.

## Decision and implementation guards

- A valid F/F or A/A majority can determine outcome while third vote provenance remains outstanding; the actual lawful operative time depends on the Civilisational Clock or a valid urgent exception.
- A valid Against outcome is not a vacant position; an Unresolved outcome is not an emergency mandate. Neither can be relabelled to force an action.
- Widening a node-ring substitute pool changes the **candidate set for one original Steward position**, not the territorial or functional extent of emergency authority.
- No standing multi-triangle command structure arises from adjacency, common hazard, simultaneous action or larger GTP scale.
- A participant may act within independently valid self-protective powers, but this audit does not invent the content of those powers.
- Any exceptional preservation action needs contemporaneous trigger evidence, authorising source, necessity, least-intrusive scope, time boundary, provenance, review and restoration.

## Source-limited result

**Resolved at architecture level:** urgent timing, coordination and exceptional emergency powers are distinct; exceptional authority cannot be inferred from urgency, vote arithmetic, GTP geometry or neighbouring participation.

**Not resolved:** whether Concord's existing constitutional material actually establishes exceptional preservation authority; if so, who can activate it, thresholds, sunset, non-derogable rights, challenge and remedies. These require source recovery and/or authorial direction before Chapter 12 or 132 can assert a binding rule.
