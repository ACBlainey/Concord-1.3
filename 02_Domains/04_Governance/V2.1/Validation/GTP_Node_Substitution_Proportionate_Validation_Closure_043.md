# GTP Node Substitution — Proportionate Validation Closure 043

**Status:** PROVISIONAL engineering closure for this development pass; not final constitutional graduation.

## Authorial scope decision

Do **not** enumerate every possible geometric, security or voting permutation. GTP and triadic decision making already have extensive development histories. The present purpose is to integrate a **bounded, context-sensitive substitution mechanism**, not to reopen or re-prove the whole governance architecture.

## Findings sufficient for provisional integration

1. At a regular six-triangle node, the first ring contains five other triangle-owned Steward positions; the second contains ten positions at the other two nodes of those five triangles.
2. At a node with a different incident degree, enumerate the actual topology rather than fabricate a six-triangle pattern.
3. Node-centred geometric discovery for third and later rings removes the arbitrary originating-triangle asymmetry observed in the original proposed frontier on a synthetic triangular mesh.
4. Each ring uses the same selection method: zero eligible candidates → continue outward when lawfully appropriate; one → fill by default; two or more → independently screened, unbiased, auditable random selection.
5. Independently established severe capture may justify earlier wider-ring selection. A high-consequence emergency, such as a nuclear power station incident with non-local effects, may require separately authorised wider emergency governance.
6. Expansion adds **candidate options**, never extra votes, new jurisdiction or permanent authority. Each valid substitute occupies one original Steward position for one bounded decision event.
7. The term **node** is standard in Concord governance; *vertex* remains only a mathematical or historical synonym.

## Validation completed

- Synthetic regular triangular lattice: first ring 5, second ring 10.
- Six-origin comparison: origin-excluded frontier produced differing ring memberships; node-centred frontier produced identical outward triangle sets.
- Synthetic altered five-incident-triangle node: first ring 4, second ring 8; outward discovery consistent across all five origin choices.

These are geometry checks, not deployment or security proofs. No real spherical GTP mesh, actual identity registry or cryptographic draw was exercised.

## Minimal remaining deployment gates

- Validate candidate identities, conflicts, vacancy authority and full decision-event participation uniqueness.
- Establish auditable fair randomness and protection against manipulated exclusions.
- Use a coherent mesh snapshot, with explicit handling if resolutions or topology differ.
- Verify quorum and decision-event boundary before consequential commitment.
- Keep security and emergency expansion independently authorised and reviewable.

## Closure decision

**Close the open-ended ring-permutation exploration for now.** Retain the node-centred third-ring definition as a provisional implementation convention, with exceptional topology or security issues handled as they arise. Return effort to reconciliation of Chapters 12 and 132 and their interfaces with bounded voting, emergency coordination and the GTP mesh. Do not modify original Governance V2 Canonical.
