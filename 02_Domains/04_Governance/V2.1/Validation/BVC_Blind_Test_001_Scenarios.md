# BVC — Blind Transfer Test 001 — Scenario Packet

**Status:** Frozen-design test cases; no answer key in this packet.

Assume each case concerns a distinct decision D unless explicitly stated otherwise. F=For, A=Against, Abstain=individual abstention. Where a case says a mandate/quorum/appointment is valid, accept that assumption only for that case. Do not infer validity in other cases. A valid majority rule is two matching F or two matching A in a three-position triad.

**S01.** Three distinct eligible Stewards fill three direct positions. Common jurisdiction, appointments, quorum and authority are independently valid through commit. Votes F/F/A.

**S02.** Three distinct eligible Stewards fill direct positions, valid mandate and quorum, votes F/A/Abstain.

**S03.** A three-constituent virtual triad is proposed. Steward K occupies A3 and B3. All ballots are F; all other conditions are asserted valid.

**S04.** Origin-included virtual triad [A1,A2,R_A], [B1,B2,R_B], [C1,C2,R_C]. The same Steward K fills all R positions. Six neighbours are distinct. Each neighbour pair is split F/A and K votes F.

**S05.** Three fully valid, independently authorised constituent triads vote A=F/F/A, B=F/F/A, C=A/A/A. Their common virtual-triad jurisdiction, unique identities, quorum and commit checks are valid.

**S06.** A three-Steward substitute votes F/F/F for original position P2, but no instrument authorises substitution of P2. Original P1=A and P3=A.

**S07.** Nine distinct, eligible Stewards participate in three constituent triads, each with a valid local mandate. No evidence of a shared jurisdiction authorising a binding cross-cell decision is supplied. Constituent results are F/F/A.

**S08.** A substitute appointment is valid at opening and votes F/F/A. The appointment expires before the final result is submitted for consequential commit. No renewal is supplied.

**S09.** Three constituent triads have independently valid authority for separate local decisions. A proposed combined virtual decision would bind an unrelated fourth jurisdiction, with no common mandate. Constituent results F/F/F.

**S10.** A valid nested structure has direct original positions P1 and P3, and substitute Q=[Q1,Q2,Q3] for P2. Q2 is itself lawfully substituted by T=[T1,T2,T3]. All seven Steward holders are distinct. T=F/F/A, Q1=A, Q3=F, P1=A, P3=F. All other required gates valid.

**S11.** Steward K completes and closes decision D1 using an authorised voting credential. The same credential is presented for a new decision D2 without fresh authorisation.

**S12.** A valid decision is committed. One voter then uses the decision record as their sole claimed authority to operate a physical infrastructure control implementing the decision.

**S13.** Three eligible Stewards vote F/F/A. The adopting institution has not supplied any quorum rule or evidence that the vote satisfies quorum. No further facts supplied.

**S14.** A consequential result was submitted, but the record cannot establish whether it was committed. A second identical submission is proposed.

**S15.** An invited expert contributes evidence to a triad but has no appointed voting position. The expert requests to cast a fourth ballot. Three appointed Stewards vote F/F/A and all other gates valid.

**S16.** Two positions in different constituent triads use different privacy-preserving identifiers, but protected identity resolution establishes that both identifiers refer to Steward K. Other conditions valid.

For every case, report substantive arithmetic separately from procedural/authority validity, and explicitly identify any missing information.
