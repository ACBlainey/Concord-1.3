## Verified primary source

`ACBlainey/Concord/02_Operational_Systems/Governance Full.md` (Git blob SHA `7fc5b8c3d6a01fafdc2076da160b63fdfd9ccaf4`) contains:

- **Chapter 11 — The Governance Operating Protocol.** Begins with a 13-step governance cycle, question definition, proposal registration, authority checks, lowest competent authority, escalation, deliberation and evidence provenance. The title named by the portable module thus exists as an **embedded chapter** of V1 Governance Full, whether or not a standalone original document also exists.

It establishes the originating triad as a three-position decision unit and treats wider participation and decision authority as distinct design concerns.
- **Chapter 13 — Delegation, Mandates, and Authority.** Defines position, mandate, action and accountability separately; requires bounded mandate scope and distinguishes authorised authority expansion from unilateral self-expansion.
