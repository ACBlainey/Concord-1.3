# Triadic Expansion — Mechanism Separation Test 011

**Status:** Conceptual interface review, not independent legal authorisation, quorum validation or PMEDG graduation.

| Case | Scenario | Required outcome |
|---|---|---|
| I01 | A triad receives evidence and advice from three neighbouring Stewards, but does not appoint a substitute | No new vote; three original decision positions remain |
| I02 | A lawful three-Steward substitute determines the original P2 position | Exactly one P2 resultant at receiving level |
| I03 | A three-Steward group votes F/F/F but lacks lawful authority to substitute P2 | Invalid forum; no operative P2 resultant |
| I04 | Several independent triads share an externality but have not constituted common jurisdiction | No binding common authority from simple pooled votes |
| I05 | One constituent position is itself lawfully substituted by a further triad | Exactly one resultant enters each receiving interface; no leaf-vote multiplication |
| I06 | Two original positions vote Against; the third position's substitute votes internally F/F/F | Receiving triad A/A/F→Against; invalid pooled five-ballot F/F/F/A/A→For |
| I07 | Priority is elevated due to emergency | Scheduling may change; voting weight does not automatically change |
| I08 | A member recuses and two remaining members vote For | No assumed legal result until denominator, quorum and replacement rules are specified |

## Pass criteria

A compliant design must preserve exactly three positions at every receiving triad, independently prove forum and mandate validity, retain source distinctions between abstention and active opposition, and keep informational participation separate from legal voting. The final two cases are specifically protected by Governance V1 Chapter 12's priority/voting distinction and Chapter 13's explicit unresolved recusal denominator.

## Status and blockers

All eight expected outcomes follow the stated interface principles as a **specification review**, not as an executed blind or deployment test. Eligibility, origin-included repeated participation, six-Steward GTP vertex mapping and quorum remain unresolved. The separately named historical Triadic Governance Decision Protocol is not yet recovered.
