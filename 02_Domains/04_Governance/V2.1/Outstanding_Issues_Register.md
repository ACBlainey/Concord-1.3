## Register rules

- Record new unresolved Governance V2.1 questions **here** rather than creating further free-standing issue registers. Detailed evidence and proposed solutions may remain in their original source or sectioned draft; link them from this register.
- Never resolve an issue by treating a file's “canonical” label as proof of freshness, or by silently choosing between conflicting source generations. Recover omitted prior architecture and preserve original provenance.
- Distinguish **authorially directed**, **source-verified**, **provisional**, **tested in simulation**, **operationally verified** and **constitutionally approved**. Passing a portable-module test does not grant authority.
- Close an issue only with its decision, authorisation/source, implementation location, evidence and date; retain the historical entry.
- Preserve source-preserved section files, V1/V2 canonical originals, reconciled candidates and validation fixtures unchanged. This new register is a coordination artifact, not a replacement for source material.

### Practical composition: one consequential governance action

**Suggested interface assembly, not an existing deployed pipeline:** Triadic module determines the F/A/U voting outcome; CWA/FPA bound the voting context and permission; MKA checks the actual implementation act's independent authority; CBERRM discovers cross-boundary effects; STRA identifies due implementation/review conditions; BTA represents the asynchronous transition across owner systems; KCS rechecks changed prerequisites; CIBB provides protected projections/provenance. The Civilisation Clock remains an owner of ordinary scheduling semantics, and Constitutional Emergency remains an owner of emergency mandates. **No module can turn a mathematical two-vote majority into a valid quorum, invent a Steward appointment, create a shared jurisdiction, or confer emergency authority.**

## Reality-anchored parallel Steward merit assessment — 12 October 2026

Independently correct dissent can demonstrate superior Steward competence. This narrows the ascension merit criterion, but does not itself appoint a candidate.

**Implementation source:** `03_Cross_Domain_Architecture/Stewardship_Selection_and_Allocation/README.md` §11.
