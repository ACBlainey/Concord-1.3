# Governance V2.1 — Consolidated Outstanding Issues Register

**Status:** Working single-point backlog for later resolution; not constitutional canon, an authority grant, or a substitute for preserved source files.  
**Updated:** 12 October 2026.  
**Scope:** Known open constitutional, integration, validation and graduation issues identified in the V2.1 sectional manuscripts, reconciled candidates, G03/G05 integration records and validation registers. This is a living index, not a claim that every historical Concord source has been exhaustively audited.

## Register rules

- Record new unresolved Governance V2.1 questions **here** rather than creating further free-standing issue registers. Detailed evidence and proposed solutions may remain in their original source or sectioned draft; link them from this register.
- Never resolve an issue by treating a file's “canonical” label as proof of freshness, or by silently choosing between conflicting source generations. Recover omitted prior architecture and preserve original provenance.
- Distinguish **authorially directed**, **source-verified**, **provisional**, **tested in simulation**, **operationally verified** and **constitutionally approved**. Passing a portable-module test does not grant authority.
- Close an issue only with its decision, authorisation/source, implementation location, evidence and date; retain the historical entry.
- Preserve source-preserved section files, V1/V2 canonical originals, reconciled candidates and validation fixtures unchanged. This new register is a coordination artifact, not a replacement for source material.

## Explicit decisions already supplied — do not reopen as generic unknowns

1. **12 Oct 2026:** Two matching valid Steward votes fix the substantive For/Against outcome; the third vote is required for provenance but cannot reverse the majority.
2. **12 Oct 2026:** Most ordinary approved decisions are implemented at the next applicable Civilisation Clock transition, allowing the third vote to arrive where practicable.
3. **12 Oct 2026:** Independently authorised emergency or time-critical decisions implement immediately upon a lawful two-vote majority, without waiting for the third vote or the Civilisation Clock.
4. **Previously established:** GTP geometric parent/child is not a governing hierarchy; every spatial resolution always exists; operational levels can coexist.
5. **Previously established:** Triadic resultants conserve original position weight; nine ballots are not pooled; CAP ≠ PERM ≠ AUTH.

**Important distinction:** These authorial directions settle their stated principles. They do not implicitly settle constitutional quorum, appointment, authenticated authority or implementation validity.
