## Initial findings

1. **Do not reinvent the permission/authority/effect boundary.** FPA, MKA, CBPR and BTA already supply complementary grammars. Their composition needs concrete owner and host verification, not a new governance-only parallel system.
2. **Review and sunset have reusable triggers.** STRA and LSR can supply state/event/functional termination triggers without making the pausable Civilisation Clock a required emergency timer.
3. **Cross-boundary recognition is not common jurisdiction.** CBER provides a strong candidate discovery and contestation interface while keeping shared-competence creation constitutionally separate.
4. **Privacy and provenance already have independent domain owners.** CWA/KCS/Historical schemas should be referenced before inventing a new central governance identity or evidence archive.
5. **Research materials can narrow many epistemic issues but are not validated operational controls.** Independent calibration, capture resistance and participant privacy still need explicit testing.
