# Policing Dual Review and Independent Authority Audit — Basic Architecture 001

**Project:** The Concord  
**Domain:** Civil Security  
**Date:** 2 October 2026  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / BASIC POLICING ARCHITECTURE / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL

## 1. Purpose

The emerging Concord legal grammar applies Bounded Contextual Authority to both participants and institutions.

Policing therefore cannot be designed as a system that investigates only the participant who is suspected of wrongdoing.

Whenever Civil Security exercises consequential authority, the same event creates two legitimate objects of examination:

1. the conduct that caused the policing response; and
2. the legality, necessity, proportionality and integrity of the policing/system response itself.

> **The Suspect Is Not The Only Subject Of Legal Review.**

## 2. Dual-review principle

Candidate architecture:

**Incident -> Participant-Conduct Review + Civil-Authority Review**

The first asks:
- what happened;
- whether a prohibited act occurred;
- whether the participant possessed consent, BCA or another legal defence;
- what culpability, mitigation or aggravation remains.

The second asks:
- why policing intervened;
- what information was reasonably available at each stage;
- what authority was activated;
- whether its trigger was satisfied;
- whether the intervention remained necessary and proportionate;
- whether the least intrusive reasonably sufficient action was used;
- whether authority expanded without justification;
- whether authority ended when its function ended;
- whether evidence, records and participant rights were handled lawfully.

These reviews concern the same event but are not the same inquiry.

## 3. Symmetry of BCA

The same basic authority architecture applies to both sides.

**Participant Interferes With Another -> Participant BCA Tested**

**Police Interfere With Participant -> Police BCA Tested**

Institutional status does not exempt Civil Security from the legal grammar it applies to others.

**Police Status != General Authority**

**Law-Enforcement Function != Presumption Of Lawful Conduct**

**Good Institutional Purpose != Valid Authority For Every Action**

## 4. Independent review

A system should not possess final authority to determine the legality of its own consequential conduct.

Internal review remains useful for:
- immediate supervision;
- operational learning;
- error detection;
- professional standards;
- rapid correction.

But internal review is not sufficient where the institution's own authority, conduct, evidence handling or systemic incentives are materially in question.

Candidate rule:

> **Consequential exercises of policing authority must remain independently reviewable by a body that is not operationally subordinate to, controlled by, or dependent upon the policing unit whose conduct is under review.**

**Internal Review != Independent Review**

**Self-Investigation != Sufficient Final Accountability**

**Institutional Expertise != Exclusive Jurisdiction Over Institutional Misconduct**

## 5. Independence must be functional

Independence should not be merely nominal.

A reviewing body should not be treated as independent merely because it has:
- a different name;
- a different office;
- a separate reporting form;
- nominal statutory separation.

Future architecture should test independence across:
- appointment/removal;
- funding/control;
- access to evidence;
- investigative capability;
- authority to compel preservation/disclosure where lawful;
- reporting;
- conflicts of interest;
- career dependency;
- operational command;
- ability to refer matters onward;
- ability to publish or preserve findings subject to legitimate information protections.

**Organisational Separation != Functional Independence**

## 6. Routine review versus escalation

Not every police interaction requires a separate external investigation.

The architecture should instead make police authority inherently reviewable and preserve sufficient provenance for proportionate review.

Possible model:

**Ordinary Interaction -> Automatic Authority/Decision Provenance -> Routine Supervisory/System Review**

**Consequential Intervention -> Stronger Recorded BCA Basis -> Independent Review Availability**

**Serious Harm / Death / Major Rights Restriction / Credible Misconduct Indicator / Systemic Pattern -> Mandatory Independent Review Trigger**

Exact thresholds remain unresolved.

## 7. Independent evidence access

Independent review cannot function if the reviewed institution exclusively controls the evidence needed to assess itself.

Relevant records may include:
- authority activation;
- grounds known at the time;
- orders/instructions;
- searches;
- restraints;
- detention transitions;
- evidence access;
- use of force;
- communications;
- relevant system logs;
- participant complaints;
- later modifications to records.

Access remains subject to CIBB, privacy, protected information and legitimate purpose boundaries.

**Independent Review Authority != Universal Information Access**

The reviewer receives the information necessary for its legitimate review function through an appropriate bounded authority envelope.

## 8. Review of the system, not only the individual officer

An adverse event may arise without individual misconduct.

Review should therefore distinguish:
- individual action;
- supervision;
- training;
- policy;
- equipment;
- information quality;
- staffing/capacity;
- incentives;
- procedural design;
- technology;
- legal ambiguity;
- institutional culture;
- recurring pattern;
- cross-domain failure.

Candidate principle:

> **Case Review Should Test Both Actor Conduct And System Conditions.**

This avoids forcing every failure into either "bad officer" or "no problem."

**No Individual Misconduct != No System Failure**

**System Failure != Automatic Individual Culpability**

## 9. Judiciary role

The Judiciary provides independent adjudication where legal rights, liability, authority or contested facts require judicial determination.

It should be able to evaluate:
- the alleged underlying offence;
- the participant's claimed justification/defence;
- police intervention;
- arrest/apprehension authority;
- search/access authority;
- detention;
- evidence handling;
- prosecution conduct where relevant;
- continuing restrictions;
- remedies for unlawful civil action.

Thus a criminal or comparable case should not conceptually be:

**State Evaluates Accused**

but:

**Independent Judiciary Evaluates The Legally Relevant Conduct And Authority Claims Of All Parties Before It**

The participant may be found to have acted unlawfully while police also exceeded authority.

The participant may have acted lawfully while police acted lawfully on reasonable information available at the time.

The participant may have acted unlawfully while later police conduct was also unlawful.

These findings are logically independent.

## 10. No circular institutional validation

The following reasoning is insufficient:

**Police Say Police Action Was Lawful -> Therefore Police Action Was Lawful**

Likewise:

**Prosecution Relies On Police Action -> Therefore Police Action Was Valid**

Authority must be source-resolvable and independently testable.

Where the validity of evidence depends upon an earlier authority exercise, Law/Judiciary should be able to examine that upstream authority rather than treating institutional provenance as self-validating.

## 11. Learning loop

Independent review should not exist only to allocate blame.

Findings should be capable of producing:
- individual remedy;
- correction of records;
- changed practice;
- training changes;
- policy correction;
- legal clarification;
- system redesign;
- technology correction;
- escalation of recurring patterns;
- evidence for Research/Historical analysis where appropriately protected.

**Accountability + Learning -> Reduced Recurrence**

Review architecture must preserve the distinction between learning from failure and excusing unlawful conduct.

## 12. Initial invariants

PDR-01 Suspect != Only Subject Of Review.  
PDR-02 Police Status != General Authority.  
PDR-03 Internal Review != Independent Review.  
PDR-04 Self-Investigation != Sufficient Final Accountability.  
PDR-05 Organisational Separation != Functional Independence.  
PDR-06 Independent Review Authority != Universal Information Access.  
PDR-07 No Individual Misconduct != No System Failure.  
PDR-08 System Failure != Automatic Individual Culpability.  
PDR-09 Participant Unlawfulness != Police Lawfulness.  
PDR-10 Participant Lawfulness != Police Unlawfulness.  
PDR-11 Each Authority Exercise Requires Independent Legal Justification.  
PDR-12 Review != Presumption Of Misconduct.  
PDR-13 Institutional Record != Self-Proving Truth.  
PDR-14 Accountability != Only Punishment.  
PDR-15 Judicial Independence Must Include Independence From The Authority Being Adjudicated.

## 13. Development chain

**Incident**  
-> **Immediate Bounded Protective Response Where Necessary**  
-> **Participant-Conduct Investigation**  
+ **Police-Authority Provenance**  
-> **Legal Classification Of Participant Conduct**  
+ **Legal Classification Of Civil Authority Exercise**  
-> **Independent Review Where Triggered / Requested / Required**  
-> **Independent Judiciary Where Adjudication Is Required**  
-> **Participant Liability / Exoneration / Remedy As Applicable**  
+ **Officer/Institutional Liability Or Exoneration As Applicable**  
+ **System Learning / Correction Where Indicated**

This should be developed alongside Law rather than allowing policing architecture to create its own independent theory of lawful authority.
