## 1. Purpose

This note reconciles the restored Judiciary mechanism **Constitutional Adversary Stewardship: Justice as Fiduciary Advocacy** with later V1.3 development of the Civil Security policing lifecycle, Mirrored Reality Trees (MRT), the Independent Prosecution Function, prosecution-threshold functional sunset, Judicial Evidential Depth and Archived Observation Access, and CIBB-style bounded information access.

It does not rewrite Judiciary V2 Canonical. The restored source remains preserved as current Judiciary source material.

## 2. Preserved source principle

The restored Judiciary source states that prosecution and defence are not merely advocates for their side. Their fiduciary hierarchy is:

1. fiduciary to justice — primary duty to truth;
2. fiduciary to the Constitution;
3. fiduciary to integrity of the judicial system;
4. fiduciary to the represented party.

It also proposes a single independent evidentiary body so prosecution and defence work from the same evidentiary foundation rather than competing through concealment.

**Prosecution Fiduciary != Conviction Advocate**

**Defence Fiduciary != Acquittal Advocate**

**Fiduciary Role != Permission To Distort Evidence For Assigned Side**

**Success != Conviction Rate**

**Success != Acquittal Rate**

The retained adversarial value is independent challenge and interpretation. The rejected value is victory through evidential distortion, concealment or asymmetric possession.

## 3. Reconciliation of evidence gathering

The restored source describes the independent evidentiary body as gathering all available evidence. Later Civil Security development establishes that police have a legitimate investigative function and must neutrally investigate what happened rather than construct a case against a selected suspect.

These architectures should not create duplicate general investigative authorities.

### Civil Security

Civil Security performs primary incident investigation under bounded investigative authority. It gathers evidence, develops and tests materially plausible hypotheses, uses MRT where appropriate, seeks inculpatory and exculpatory evidence, preserves provenance and dependency, identifies alternative actors/hypotheses, and transfers the reconstructable whole evidential state when the referral condition is reached.

### Independent evidentiary function

On referral/prosecution/adjudication, an independent evidentiary function becomes custodian of the judicial evidential state. It:

- receives the whole evidential state;
- preserves evidence and provenance;
- verifies reconstructability;
- prevents prosecution or defence from controlling the existence of evidence;
- provides legitimate symmetric access to both fiduciaries;
- maintains access/disclosure provenance;
- identifies material omissions or integrity defects;
- obtains or exposes deeper archived evidence where new lawful adjudicative authority permits;
- routes material new evidence into threshold and hypothesis reassessment;
- operates under independent judicial review.

It does not become a second general police force.

**Independent Evidentiary Function != General Investigative Police Authority**

Where new evidence creates a genuinely new investigative question, Civil Security retains the investigative function.

## 4. Evidence existence, custody and access

The common evidentiary foundation can now be expressed:

**Evidence Creation / Observation**
-> **Legitimate Preservation**

-> **Bounded Investigative Access**
-> **Whole Evidential State Transfer**
-> **Independent Judicial Evidentiary Custody**
-> **Purpose-Bounded Symmetric Fiduciary Access**

**Evidence Preserved != Universal Disclosure**

**Common Evidentiary Foundation != Unbounded Information Access**

Where privacy, safety, protected-space, third-party or other legitimate constraints apply, CIBB-style minimum-necessary projection may provide functionally equivalent access without exposing irrelevant protected information. Any asymmetric restriction capable of affecting fairness or evidential meaning requires independent review.

## 5. Fiduciary symmetry

Prosecution and defence may begin from different assigned propositions, but neither owns the evidence.

The prosecution fiduciary may argue that evidence establishes the legal propositions required for liability. The defence fiduciary may argue those propositions are not established, contrary propositions are established, justification applies, attribution fails, or another hypothesis better explains the evidence.

Both remain responsible for material truth-bearing evidence.

A prosecution fiduciary discovering decisive exculpatory evidence must not conceal it. A defence fiduciary discovering decisive inculpatory evidence must not distort or conceal it merely to manufacture an acquittal.

**Opposing Examination + Shared Truth Duty -> Adversarial Error Detection Without Victory-At-All-Costs Advocacy**

## 6. MRT as evidential reasoning interface

The graduated MRT portable companion supplies a mechanism the restored source did not possess when written.

### Investigative MRT

Civil Security asks: **What happened, and which materially plausible explanation best fits the evidence?**

It may test the current suspect, alternative suspects, multiple actors, accident, justification, system error, no offence, and other materially plausible hypotheses.

### Fiduciary MRT examination

Prosecution and defence fiduciaries may independently examine the same evidential state through mirrored structures. Their classifications are not binding on each other or Judiciary.

### Judicial MRT

Judiciary may construct its own mirrored evidential/legal structure.

**Police MRT != Judicial MRT**

**Prosecution MRT != Judicial MRT**

**Defence MRT != Judicial MRT**

Judiciary receives prior reasoning and evidence but remains responsible for its own adjudicative analysis.

## 7. Judicial MRT should be proposition-level

Criminal adjudication should not ordinarily collapse immediately into one giant **Guilty <-> Not Guilty** mirror.

Candidate judicial structure:

- Did the relevant event/act occur?
- Is the act attributable to this participant?
- Are the legally required elements established?
- Is a lawful justification or defence established/relevantly raised?
- What culpability state is established?
- Are aggravating or mitigating propositions established?

Each material proposition receives supporting/opposing evidential examination before the legal conclusion is derived.

**Event Established != Attribution Established**

**Attribution Established != Every Legal Element Established**

**Prohibited Act Established != Absence Of Justification**

## 8. Deeper adjudicative evidence

The independent evidentiary function may expose or obtain additional evidence once a broader evidential envelope is lawfully justified.

Possible sources include archived police bodycam, dashcam and vehicle-camera footage, relevant CCTV, interview/custody recordings, operational and dispatch logs, historical sensor records, system reliability/error metrics, relevant longitudinal records, third-party evidence, and evidence concerning materially plausible alternative actors.

This is not prosecution-owned access. It serves adjudicative truth-finding and is legitimately available to both fiduciary sides subject to bounded access rules.

## 9. Evidential reversal before judgment

The judicial process must remain capable of discovering that prosecution should not continue.

Example:

1. Police evidence legitimately satisfies the prosecution threshold.
2. Prosecution begins.
3. Deeper authorised evidential access exposes archived CCTV.
4. The CCTV establishes a reliable alibi for the accused.
5. The same evidence indicates another materially plausible actor.
6. Attribution to the accused collapses.
7. The prosecution threshold is no longer satisfied.

Candidate transition:

**Material New Evidence -> Current Attribution Hypothesis Collapses -> Prosecution Threshold Reassessed -> Threshold No Longer Met -> Prosecution Authority Sunsets -> Current Prosecution Halts**

Separately:

**Alternative-Actor Evidence -> Civil Security Investigative Handoff -> Fresh/Expanded MRT Investigation -> Independent Referral If Warranted -> Independent Prosecution Threshold For That Participant**

**Judicial Process Begun != Duty To Continue Unsupported Prosecution**

**New Suspect != Inherited Prosecution**

## 10. Judiciary is not passive

Judiciary need not be merely a passive chooser between two submitted interpretations.

Where Judicial MRT exposes a material unresolved contradiction, dependency, omitted proposition, alternative actor or evidential gap, Judiciary may require legitimate evidential resolution within adjudicative authority.

**Judicial Evidential Inquiry != General Police Authority**

The Judiciary asks only what must legitimately be resolved for the matter before it.

## 11. Evidentiary-body independence

The independent evidentiary function creates concentration-of-power risk. Candidate safeguards include independence from both fiduciaries, evidential provenance, auditable access history, reviewable inclusion/exclusion decisions, protection against selective indexing, transparent evidence-status changes, CIBB-compatible bounded access, ability for both fiduciaries to challenge omission/classification/access restriction, judicial oversight, no outcome-performance incentive, no authority to manufacture a preferred hypothesis, and separation between evidence custody and adjudicative decision.

**Evidence Custodian != Owner Of Evidential Meaning**

**Evidence Custody != Adjudicative Authority**

## 12. Relationship to prosecution threshold

**Evidence Function -> What Evidence Exists / Is Reliably Available?**

**Prosecution Function -> Is Independent Adjudication Justified?**

**Judiciary -> What Legal/Factual Finding Is Established?**

These functions interact but must not collapse into one authority.

## 15. Development conclusion

The restored Constitutional Adversary Stewardship architecture and newer V1.3 policing/prosecution/evidence architectures are strongly compatible.

The major source-level concept already existed: prosecution and defence should operate as fiduciaries of justice from a common independent evidentiary foundation.

The newer work supplies operational depth: neutral police investigation, MRT, evidence dependency/proposition scope, bounded authority transitions, independent prosecution threshold, protected evidential archives, stage-dependent evidential depth, CIBB-compatible access, threshold sunset, investigative handback, and Judicial MRT.

The principal reconciliation required is institutional:

> **The independent evidentiary body should not duplicate Civil Security's general investigative authority. It should provide independent evidential custody, integrity, symmetric legitimate access and adjudicative-stage evidential development under bounded judicial authority.**

This candidate reconciliation should be stress-tested before any change is made to the canonical Judiciary source.
