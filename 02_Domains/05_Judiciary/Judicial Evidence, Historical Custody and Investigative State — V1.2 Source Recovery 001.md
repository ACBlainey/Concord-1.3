# Judicial Evidence, Historical Custody and Investigative State — V1.2 Source Recovery 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Date:** 6 October 2026  
**Status:** SOURCE RECOVERY / JUDICIARY–LAW–CIVIL SECURITY INTERFACE / NON-CANONICAL  
**Recovered from:** V1.2 Historical Source Resolutions 002, 008 and 023; Judiciary V2 lineage  
**Interfaces:** Judiciary; Law; Civil Security; Historical; CIBB; KCS; Research

## 1. Recovery finding

The current V1.3 chain is already structurally coherent:

**Civil Security establishes the evidential state**
→ **Independent Prosecution tests whether adjudication is justified**
→ **Judiciary determines the contested legal outcome**

V1.2 does not reveal a missing parallel justice system.

It reveals a mature **evidence-memory and investigative-state boundary** that should constrain all three functions.

The key recovery is that an event, observation, evidentiary object, interpretation, investigative hypothesis, legal finding and historical record are different states and must not silently collapse into one another.

## 2. Event-to-finding chain

Candidate semantic chain:

**Reality / Event**
→ **Observation**
→ **Recorded Evidence**
→ **Transformation / Derivative**
→ **Interpretation**
→ **Investigative Hypothesis**
→ **Prosecution Assessment**
→ **Judicial Finding**
→ **Historical Record**

Each arrow may introduce:
- uncertainty;
- incompleteness;
- transformation;
- authority;
- correction;
- dispute.

> **Evidence != Event**

> **Interpretation != Evidence**

> **Investigative Hypothesis != Judicial Finding**

> **Historical Record != Continuing Legal Force**

## 3. Custody versus authority

V1.2 Judiciary/Historical work gives a strong invariant:

> **Custody Of Law Is Not Authority To Determine Law**

Generalised:

> **Custody Of A Record != Authority Of The System That Created Or Uses The Record**

Historical may preserve evidence, provenance, decisions and dissent without becoming investigator, prosecutor or court.

## 4. Evidentiary provenance

Where consequence warrants, evidence should retain enough provenance to reconstruct:
- origin;
- collector/source;
- time/context;
- preservation;
- chain of custody;
- transformations;
- summarisation;
- redaction/anonymisation;
- computational generation;
- model/tool/version;
- translation;
- uncertainty;
- known limitations;
- access/use history where relevant.

> **Preserved Content Without Material Provenance Can Be Evidentially Incomplete**

## 5. Raw evidence

> **Raw Evidence != Complete Reality**

A camera, sensor, witness or other observation can accurately capture part of reality while omitting the dimension that matters.

ESCP therefore applies even to high-fidelity evidence.

## 6. Raw and derived evidence

Keep distinct:
- protected raw source;
- transformed source;
- redacted derivative;
- anonymised derivative;
- machine classification;
- human/agent interpretation;
- evidentiary claim.

> **Derived Evidence Must Not Be Represented As The Raw Observation From Which It Was Produced**

## 7. Transformation lineage

A privacy transformation should reduce exposure without erasing evidentiary lineage.

> **Redacted Evidence != Original Evidence**

> **Anonymised != Necessarily Non-Identifiable Forever**

Where legitimate, the relation between derivative and protected original should remain reconstructable without requiring unrestricted exposure of the original.

## 8. Minimum necessary retrieval

V1.2's black-box evidence work supports:

**Legitimate Question**
→ **Purpose**
→ **Authority/Permission Check**
→ **Evidence Scope**
→ **Minimum Necessary Extraction**
→ **Redaction/Anonymisation Where Appropriate**
→ **Bounded Output**
→ **Access/Transformation Provenance**

> **Need For Evidence != Authority For Maximum Exposure**

Historical/CIBB may execute a legitimate retrieval without becoming the authority that justified it.

## 9. Access versus replication

> **Evidence Access Event != Evidence Replication Event**

A bounded query that leaves the source protected creates a different future correction/privacy problem from copying the material into another system.

Where a copy is created, its lineage and correction state may matter.

## 10. Correction propagation

> **Correction Of Source Evidence != Automatic Correction Of Every Derived Copy**

If evidence has propagated, correction architecture should know which consequential derivatives may have become stale.

This interfaces with KCS change propagation.

## 11. Epistemic status survives transfer

Moving information from support, health, civil records or another domain into investigation must not upgrade it.

> **Transfer Into Investigative Context != Upgrade From Statement/Inference/Hypothesis To Fact**

The receiving system inherits the evidence object, not a stronger epistemic status.

## 12. Suspicion is state, not identity

> **Suspicion != Persistent Participant Identity**

A participant can move through:
**not subject → investigated → suspected → cleared / charged / otherwise resolved**

Historical preservation of the earlier state must not make it the participant's permanent current state.

## 13. Clearance

> **Clearance Should End The Operational Force Of Suspicion Without Erasing Accountability-Critical History**

A false suspicion may remain historically valuable because it exposes:
- investigative error;
- model failure;
- authority misuse;
- evidentiary misinterpretation;
- system bias.

The evidential meaning can invert:

> **False Suspicion Is Evidence About The Investigative System, Not Evidence Against The Cleared Participant**

## 14. Final finding does not rewrite earlier uncertainty

Where a case is eventually resolved:

> **Later Resolution != Earlier Certainty**

Historical should preserve genuine alternative hypotheses and evidence states that existed at the earlier time.

This prevents hindsight reconstruction.

## 15. Prediction

> **Prediction Of Conduct != Record Of Conduct**

> **Model Branch != Historical Event**

> **Predictive Risk != Guilt**

If predictive investigative material is preserved for audit/research, its hypothetical/model status must remain explicit.

## 16. Observation density

Investigation can change the data environment.

**Model Flags Participant**
→ **More Observation**
→ **More Recorded Incidents**
→ **Apparent Stronger Model Evidence**

Therefore:

> **Recorded Event Frequency != Underlying Event Frequency Without Observation Context**

and:

> **Observation Density Can Be Produced By Surveillance Density**

Authority-generated data should not be misrepresented as independent evidence.

## 17. Recursive investigative evidence

> **Investigative Recursion != Independent Evidence Accumulation**

Model-on-model or inference-on-inference chains should preserve dependency lineage.

A downstream confidence score does not magically become a direct observation.

## 18. Model provenance

Where machine reasoning materially affects investigation, preserve enough to distinguish:

**Input → Model/Version → Output → Investigator Interpretation → Evidential Action → Authority Decision**

> **Model Output != Investigator Conclusion**

> **Investigator Conclusion != Judicial Finding**

## 19. Disconfirming evidence

V1.2 strongly supports preservation of material evidence against the favoured hypothesis.

This aligns with V1.3 Mirrored Investigative Reality Trees.

> **Investigative Provenance Should Preserve Material Disconfirming Evidence, Not Merely The Evidence Supporting The Final Theory**

## 20. Historical replay

Testing an investigative method on old cases must restrict the simulated evaluator to information actually available at the historical decision point.

> **Historical Outcome Leakage != Predictive Validation**

This is particularly important for AI/model evaluation.

## 21. Rejected capabilities

A high-risk investigative method may be historically retained even when deployment was rejected.

> **Historical Preservation Of Rejected Capability != Permission To Reactivate It**

Executable models/tools may require stronger custody than ordinary documentary records.

## 22. Rights-cost evidence

Investigative effectiveness is not the whole evaluation space.

Material outcomes can include:
- privacy loss;
- false accusation;
- chilling effects;
- discriminatory impact;
- unnecessary investigation;
- support avoidance;
- loss of trust.

> **Investigative Effectiveness != Net Legitimacy**

Historical preserves relevant evidence; legitimate authorities determine legal/constitutional consequence.

## 23. Rehabilitation and restoration

> **Historical Record Of Transgression != Permanent Civil Disqualification**

> **Historical Transgression Record != Proof Of Persistent Deficit**

A participant can move through:
**transgression → accountability → rehabilitation → restored standing**

Historical memory can preserve the chain while current systems use the current legitimate state.

## 24. Historical memory must not become extra-judicial punishment

> **Historical Discoverability Must Not Silently Create Punishment Beyond Legitimately Authorised Consequences**

> **Historical Survival Of A Sanction Record != Continuing Sanction**

Access/discoverability therefore matter as much as physical retention.

## 25. Wrongful finding

Where a finding is overturned or materially corrected, Historical should preserve:
- original state;
- evidence/reasoning available then;
- challenge;
- correction/overturning;
- current legal state;
- provenance.

> **Correction Of A Finding != Erasure Of The Historical Event**

But:

> **Preservation Of The Old Finding != Permission To Treat It As Current**

## 26. Dissent and minority reasoning

Judiciary V2 treats dissent as civilisational memory.

> **Preservation != Endorsement**

Minority reasoning can later expose overlooked evidence, alternative rights analysis, changed circumstances or grounds for correction.

Historical discoverability of dissent supports judicial learning without turning dissent into precedent automatically.

## 27. Legal/semantic versioning

A judicial/legal record should be interpretable against the rule/version that applied at the relevant time.

> **Current Legal Meaning != Historical Legal Meaning By Default**

Later amendment should not silently rewrite the semantic context of earlier action.

## 28. Dual provenance

Justice architecture often requires two linked provenance tracks:
1. **evidence provenance** — what information existed and how it changed;
2. **authority provenance** — who accessed/acted, under what authority, scope and time.

> **Evidence Provenance != Authority Provenance**

Both may matter to adjudication.

## 29. Domain disposition

### Civil Security
Owns bounded protective/investigative action and creation/preservation of the investigative evidential state.

### Prosecution / Law
Owns independent prosecution-threshold assessment and legal framing under Law.

### Judiciary
Owns adjudication, legal findings, judicial review and remedies within its authority.

### Historical
Owns durable temporal custody/provenance functions where assigned, without inheriting judicial/investigative authority.

### CIBB / information architecture
Supports purpose-bounded retrieval/projection.

### Research
May study cases/methods under separate legitimate authority; historical custody is not automatic research permission.

## 30. V1.3 disposition

No new parallel justice architecture is required from V1.2.

Recover this evidence-state/custody boundary as an interface constraint on the already coherent V1.3 justice chain.

The full V1.2 Historical development remains provenance.

## 31. Central recovered rule

> **A Just Justice System Must Preserve Not Only What It Ultimately Decided, But What Was Observed, How Evidence Was Transformed, What Was Uncertain, What Authority Was Exercised, What Alternatives Existed, And How The Participant's Legal State Later Changed — Without Allowing Historical Memory To Become Permanent Suspicion Or Extra-Judicial Punishment.**
