# Development Status

**Status:** ACTIVE / SOURCE-PRESERVING RECONSTRUCTION / NOT YET VALIDATED

This document reconstructs and extends the existing Concord Judiciary papers while preserving their substantive ideas, mechanisms, reasoning, safeguards and unresolved questions.

The purpose of Judiciary V2 is not to shorten the earlier Judiciary architecture.

It is not an editorial condensation.

It is not an attempt to replace detailed constitutional reasoning with a smaller collection of principles.

The purpose is to preserve the existing Judiciary architecture while integrating constitutional developments that have emerged through the continuing development of the Concord, particularly through the reconstruction of Governance, the development of Ratchet, recursive constitutional analysis, failure challenge, and examination of the interfaces between constitutional systems.

Where earlier Judiciary material remains compatible with the developing constitutional architecture, it is preserved.

Where additional safeguards are required, they are added.

Where boundaries between constitutional systems require clarification, they are made explicit.

Where a mechanism remains uncertain, it remains identified as uncertain rather than being silently resolved.

Where an architecture appears promising but has not been validated, it remains a candidate architecture rather than being presented as demonstrated fact.

The resulting Judiciary therefore remains subject to:

- constitutional reconciliation;
- cross-system review;
- adversarial examination;
- failure challenge;
- simulation where appropriate;
- empirical testing where possible;
- implementation testing;
- recursive review;
- and future correction.

The Concord does not presume that a constitutional architecture becomes correct merely because it has been written.

**Architecture is not proof.**

**Architecture ≠ Validation.**

Canonical status means current, not final.
