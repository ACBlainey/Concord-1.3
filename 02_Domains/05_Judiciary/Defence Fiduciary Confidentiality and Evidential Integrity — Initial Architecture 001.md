# Defence Fiduciary Confidentiality and Evidential Integrity — Initial Architecture 001

**Project:** The Concord
**Domain:** Judiciary / Law
**Date:** 3 October 2026
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / FIDUCIARY CONFIDENTIALITY ARCHITECTURE / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL

## 1. Source position

Source resolution of the current Concord V1.3 Judiciary found:

- Constitutional Adversary Stewardship establishes prosecution and defence as fiduciaries of justice, the Constitution, the judicial system and then the represented party;
- Judiciary V2 requires protection of legitimate confidentiality where necessary;
- the common-evidence architecture rejects evidential concealment and victory-at-all-costs advocacy;
- no located V1.3 source presently defines a specific self-incrimination rule, legal-professional privilege, defence-client privilege, or detailed compelled-disclosure architecture.

Accordingly, this note does **not** claim that conventional attorney-client privilege or a conventional right against self-incrimination is already Concord law.

It develops a candidate architecture from existing Concord principles.

---

## 2. The apparent conflict

A defence fiduciary must be capable of receiving candid information from the represented participant.

But the fiduciary is also not permitted to corrupt the evidential state merely to manufacture an acquittal.

If every communication to the defence fiduciary automatically became disclosable evidence, meaningful representation would be damaged.

A participant could not safely:

- describe uncertainty;
- disclose embarrassing facts;
- test alternative explanations;
- admit mistakes;
- ask whether conduct was unlawful;
- explain fears or vulnerabilities;
- identify weaknesses in their own account;
- prepare for questioning;
- seek legal interpretation.

Conversely, if the defence relationship became a mechanism for hiding independently existing evidence, the fiduciary architecture would fail.

The architecture therefore requires a boundary between:

**Protected Representational Communication**

and:

**Independently Existing Evidence / Evidential State**

---

## 3. Core distinction

Candidate principle:

> **Communication created or disclosed for the legitimate purpose of obtaining fiduciary legal representation is not automatically transformed into evidence available to the opposing fiduciary or Judiciary merely because the defence fiduciary learns it.**

At the same time:

> **A protected fiduciary relationship does not change the existence, provenance or lawful discoverability of evidence that exists independently of that communication.**

Therefore:

**Knowledge Given To Defence Fiduciary != Automatic Common Evidence**

but:

**Telling Defence Fiduciary About Existing Evidence != Destruction Of That Evidence's Independent Existence**

Example:

A participant privately tells the defence fiduciary:

> I was at location X.

The communication itself may be protected representational communication.

But independently existing CCTV, access logs, witnesses or physical evidence concerning location X remain ordinary evidence subject to legitimate evidential processes.

The defence fiduciary may not destroy, falsify, conceal or interfere with those independent sources.

---

## 4. Why confidentiality serves truth rather than defeating it

Confidentiality is not merely a private benefit.

It can improve the judicial truth-finding architecture by allowing the participant to communicate candidly with a fiduciary who can:

- identify legal issues;
- identify factual contradictions;
- prevent false or misleading claims;
- test explanations;
- identify evidence requiring legitimate examination;
- explain consequences;
- advise against fabrication;
- prepare accurate participation;
- identify justification or mitigation;
- recognise when the current legal framing is wrong.

Thus:

**Protected Communication Can Support Truth-Finding Without Becoming Common Evidence**

This is compatible with the restored fiduciary hierarchy because the defence fiduciary serves justice partly by making meaningful, competent representation possible.

## 5. Four provisional information classes

### Class A — Common independently existing evidence

Evidence already legitimately within the common evidentiary state or independently obtained through lawful evidentiary processes.

Examples may include:

- CCTV;
- bodycam;
- physical evidence;
- system logs;
- witness statements;
- forensic evidence;
- documents obtained independently;
- archived sensor records.

The defence fiduciary cannot remove such evidence from the common state.

### Class B — Independently existing evidence identified through protected communication

The participant tells the defence fiduciary about an external evidence object/source not yet represented in the common evidentiary state.

The communication and the external evidence are distinct objects.

The fiduciary must not destroy, alter or fabricate the external evidence.

Whether and how the fiduciary must cause the independent source to enter the common evidentiary state requires a bounded procedural rule; it cannot be solved by pretending the external evidence ceased to exist.

### Class C — Protected representational communication

Communication generated for legitimate legal/fiduciary representation.

This may include:

- participant account;
- questions;
- legal advice;
- defence preparation;
- hypothesis testing;
- discussion of weaknesses;
- strategy consistent with fiduciary duties;
- private explanation necessary for representation.

Candidate default:

**Protected Representational Communication != Common Evidentiary Object**

### Class D — Abuse of fiduciary channel

Communication or conduct whose function is not legitimate representation but use of the fiduciary relationship to facilitate ongoing/future unlawful harm, evidence destruction, fabrication, coercion, concealment operations or corruption of judicial process.

The precise boundary requires Law.

The existence of confidentiality must not itself manufacture authority for unlawful conduct.

**Confidentiality != Authority To Facilitate Evidential Corruption**

---

## 6. The defence fiduciary's truth duty

The defence fiduciary's truth duty does not require volunteering every protected participant statement into the common evidentiary state.

It does require that the fiduciary not:

- knowingly present a false factual proposition as true;
- fabricate evidence;
- alter evidence;
- destroy evidence;
- knowingly misrepresent the content/provenance of common evidence;
- suppress evidence already under a legitimate disclosure obligation;
- instruct others to conceal/destroy evidence;
- use protected communication to construct a knowingly false evidential narrative.

This creates an important distinction:

**Duty Not To Mislead != Duty To Disclose Every Protected Communication**

A fiduciary may legitimately require the prosecution to establish its case even where the represented participant has privately provided information adverse to their own position.

The defence fiduciary's role is not to become an involuntary witness against the participant.

---

## 7. Fiduciary withdrawal / non-assertion problem

A difficult case arises where the participant insists that the defence fiduciary present a proposition the fiduciary knows, through protected communication, to be false.

The fiduciary should not present the false proposition as truth.

Candidate responses may include:

- refuse to make the false assertion;
- narrow the representation to propositions that can legitimately be advanced;
- advise the participant;
- seek protected judicial guidance through an appropriate mechanism;
- withdraw/transfer representation where necessary and where doing so does not itself improperly disclose protected information.

The exact procedural route requires later development.

**Representation Duty != Duty To Present Known Falsehood**

---

## 8. Existing evidence discovered physically through defence

Another difficult boundary is where the participant physically gives the fiduciary an independently existing evidential object.

The object is not identical to the communication surrounding it.

Examples:

- a device;
- a document;
- a physical object;
- a recording.

Taking custody may change provenance or evidential availability.

The architecture should not allow evidence to become undiscoverable merely by moving it into a fiduciary protected space.

Candidate invariant:

**Transfer Of Existing Evidence Into Fiduciary Custody != Transformation Into Protected Communication**

The precise custody/disclosure procedure requires Law and evidence-integrity rules.

---

## 9. Self-incrimination remains unresolved

No located V1.3 source establishes the scope of a Concord right against compelled self-incrimination.

This note therefore does not invent one.

It identifies the question as a separate constitutional/legal development requirement:

> To what extent may a participant be compelled to provide testimonial, informational, biometric, physical, digital, cryptographic or other evidence against themselves?

These categories should not be silently treated as equivalent.

Any eventual rule must be reconciled with:

- Autonomy & Consent;
- BCA;
- privacy/protected-space principles;
- public safety;
- evidential integrity;
- meaningful representation;
- fiduciary confidentiality;
- independent adjudication;
- CIBB;
- proportionality;
- rights classification.

Until developed:

**Fiduciary Confidentiality != Complete Self-Incrimination Doctrine**

## 10. Common evidence does not mean common private thought

The common evidentiary foundation should contain legitimate evidence relevant to adjudication.

It should not require universal exposure of:

- participant private thought;
- fiduciary legal analysis;
- protected representational dialogue;
- internal hypothesis testing;
- draft arguments;
- legitimate preparation material,

unless a separately established lawful rule makes a particular item evidentially accessible.

Therefore:

**Common Evidentiary Foundation != Common Cognitive Workspace**

This applies symmetrically.

Prosecution and defence may each have protected reasoning/preparation spaces while sharing the underlying legitimate evidential foundation.

Their private reasoning is not itself evidence merely because it concerns evidence.

---

## 11. MRT and protected reasoning

Fiduciary MRT work may include:

- alternative interpretations;
- speculative hypothesis testing;
- weakness analysis;
- contradiction exploration;
- possible defences;
- possible prosecution responses.

If every exploratory branch became an evidential admission, MRT would become unusable for rigorous reasoning.

Therefore:

**Fiduciary MRT Hypothesis != Evidential Assertion**

**Exploratory Reasoning != Admission Of Fact**

When a fiduciary formally relies upon an external evidence object or factual proposition before Judiciary, the evidential/provenance rules governing that reliance apply.

---

## 12. Evidentiary completeness interaction

Protected communication creates a legitimate limit in the evidence space.

The Evidentiary Completeness Audit should represent the existence of a protected category where material without exposing its protected content.

For example:

**PROTECTED REPRESENTATIONAL MATERIAL — NOT PART OF COMMON EVIDENTIARY STATE**

rather than pretending no such protected information class exists.

However, completeness audit cannot use ESCP as a pretext to penetrate protected communications merely because they might contain useful evidence.

**ESCP Risk != Authority To Search Fiduciary Communication**

This mirrors:

**Unknown Unknowns != Authority To Search Everyone**

---

## 13. Independent evidentiary body boundary

The independent evidentiary body does not automatically receive defence-client communications.

Its common evidentiary custody applies to evidence legitimately within the adjudicative evidence state.

It should not become a universal repository of every communication generated by legal representation.

**Independent Evidence Custody != Custody Of All Fiduciary Communications**

This protects both meaningful representation and institutional separation.

---

## 14. Symmetry and asymmetry

Prosecution and defence fiduciaries share a truth duty, but their participant relationships are not necessarily identical.

The defence fiduciary may require protected communication with the participant whose liberty, rights, property or status are directly exposed to adjudicative consequence.

The prosecution fiduciary represents the lawful prosecution function rather than possessing an equivalent private accused-client relationship.

Therefore formal symmetry of fiduciary ethics does not require identical confidentiality relationships.

**Equal Fiduciary Truth Duty != Identical Information Relationship**

This asymmetry should be explicit rather than hidden.

---

## 15. Participant control

Protected communication belongs to a legitimate representational function, not to the fiduciary as personal property.

Future Law should define:

- who may authorise disclosure;
- when consent is valid;
- whether protection survives change of fiduciary;
- whether it survives case closure;
- how joint/multiple representation works;
- how incapacity is handled;
- what happens after participant death/retirement where relevant;
- how AI/hybrid participants exercise the protection;
- exceptional boundaries.

The current architecture does not resolve these.

## 17. Candidate operating boundary

**External / Independently Existing Evidence**
-> common evidentiary rules, provenance, access and disclosure

**Participant -> Defence Fiduciary Protected Communication**
-> protected representational space

**Protected Communication Identifies External Evidence**
-> communication remains protected
-> external evidence retains independent existence
-> applicable Law determines any duty/process concerning the external source

**Participant Transfers External Evidence Into Fiduciary Custody**
-> object remains evidence
-> evidence-integrity/custody rule applies
-> communication may remain separately protected

**Participant Requests False/Fabricated Presentation**
-> fiduciary refuses corruption
-> protected resolution/withdrawal route as Law later defines

---

## 18. Development conclusion

The apparent conflict between fiduciary truth duty and defence confidentiality can be substantially resolved by separating:

**truth-bearing evidence that exists independently**

from:

**communication and reasoning generated within the legitimate representational relationship.**

The defence fiduciary does not serve justice by becoming a second prosecutor against the participant.

Nor does the defence fiduciary serve the participant by corrupting the evidential state.

The candidate middle architecture is:

> **Protect candid representation; preserve independent evidence; prohibit falsehood and evidential corruption; keep the common evidentiary foundation distinct from protected fiduciary communication.**

The next validation step should stress-test difficult boundary cases before developing any constitutional self-incrimination rule.
