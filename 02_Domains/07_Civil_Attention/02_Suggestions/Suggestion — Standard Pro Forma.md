# Concord Suggestion — Standard Pro Forma

**Submission type:** Suggestion

## What are you suggesting?
Describe the idea or proposed improvement.

## What might it improve?
Describe the current situation, opportunity or need that led to the suggestion.

## Who or what could be affected?
Identify relevant participants, services, systems, places or processes if known.

## Why might the suggestion help?
Explain the expected benefit or improvement.

## Possible disadvantages, risks or trade-offs
Add any you can identify. It is acceptable not to know them.

## Supporting information
Provide examples, evidence, sketches, documents, links or other useful material if available.

## Related Issue or previous suggestion
Add any known Civil Attention reference or related work.

## Contact / feedback preference
Provide a permitted contact route if desired or required.

---

**For Civil Attention processing:** A proposed solution is an input for legitimate evaluation, not authority to implement it. Merit and priority must be assessed through the appropriate domain process.
