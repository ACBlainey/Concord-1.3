## Basic V1.3 principles framework

`Defence Basic Principles Framework 001.md`

The first V1.3 Defence framework establishes only the presently supportable foundations:
- non-aggression and neutrality without defensive incapacity;
- participants as the civilisation Defence exists to protect;
- self-defence/defence of others as the constitutional foundation beneath collective Defence;
- defensive action as Bounded Contextual Authority;
- the same legal/authority mechanism scaling from individual defence to coordinated civilisational defence;
- voluntary defensive contribution rather than citizenship-based conscription;
- consensually accepted, bounded defensive obligations;
- a right of retreat at every scale, constrained only by legitimately accepted duties;
- compatibility with evacuation, continuity and Concord diaspora;
- dual-role civil systems and participants where practical;
- bounded threat-relevant information sharing rather than Defence-wide civil surveillance;
- qualification, functional sunset, review and accountability.

Detailed military implementation remains explicitly unresolved until a real capability base and threat environment exist.
