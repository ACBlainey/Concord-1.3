# Medical Assessment, Qualification and Accreditation Topology 001

**Project:** The Concord
**Domain:** Education
**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL
**Dependencies:** Education Domain Basic Framework 001 / Medical Education and Qualification Architecture 001 / Clinical Function and Competence Contract 001
**Interfaces:** Historical / Health / Civil Contact

## 1. Purpose

Define how medical competence may be assessed and qualifications legitimately issued without creating an unnecessary institutional monopoly or allowing self-asserted competence to become consequential authority.

The topology separates:
- teaching;
- supervised development;
- assessment;
- qualification/accreditation;
- enduring record custody;
- clinical use.

> **Teaching != Assessment != Accreditation != Clinical Authority**

These functions may coexist operationally, but their authorities must remain distinguishable.

## 2. The object being accredited

The primary object is demonstrated competence against a defined standard/version, not attendance at an institution.

A qualification may aggregate multiple competencies, but should remain traceable to what was actually demonstrated.

> **Credential != Competence By Assertion**

> **Course Completion != Qualification Unless Required Competence Was Demonstrated**

## 3. Open assessment route

Where safe and practicable, a participant should be able to present for assessment regardless of how they learned.

Valid preparation may include:
- formal education;
- independent study;
- apprenticeship;
- prior recognised learning;
- supervised practice;
- simulation;
- mixed routes;
- substrate-appropriate learning.

Consequential practical competence may still require evidence that cannot be produced by theory examination alone.

> **Open Assessment != Lower Assessment Standard**

> **Alternative Learning Route != Alternative Clinical Standard**

## 4. Assessment authority

An assessor must be competent to evaluate the capability being assessed.

But competence to perform a clinical function does not automatically establish competence to assess it.

> **Competence To Perform != Automatic Competence To Assess**

Assessment competence may require:
- understanding of the target standard;
- ability to observe/evaluate relevant performance;
- calibration against assessment criteria;
- recognition of evidence limits;
- conflict management;
- provenance/accountability.

## 5. Qualification authority

Qualification authority is the bounded authority to attest that specified evidence satisfies a specified qualification standard.

It is not ownership of the profession.

> **Authority To Award Qualification != Authority To Control All Learning Routes**

> **Qualification Authority != Clinical Authority**

A qualification authority should be able to justify:
- which standard it applied;
- which evidence it accepted;
- who/what performed material assessment;
- how integrity was protected;
- what scope the resulting qualification actually attests.

## 6. Multiple legitimate qualification providers

Concord should permit multiple legitimate assessment/accreditation providers where they can demonstrate equivalent conformance to the relevant standard.

This avoids making one institution the sole doorway to a civil function.

> **Common Standard != Single Institution**

> **Multiple Accreditors != Multiple Competence Standards**

Different providers may use different teaching or assessment mechanisms while still demonstrating the same required capability.

## 7. Accreditation of the accreditor

A system with multiple qualification providers still requires a way to establish that their attestations are trustworthy.

This should focus on conformance of the assessment/accreditation process rather than institutional prestige.

Possible evidence:
- assessor competence;
- assessment validity;
- standard/version fidelity;
- integrity controls;
- auditability;
- outcome/error signals;
- conflict handling;
- correction/appeal;
- provenance.

No accreditor should become legitimate merely by declaring itself legitimate.

> **Accreditor Status != Self-Authorising Authority**

The exact civil authority topology for recognising accreditors remains provisional.

## 8. Independence and conflict

Teaching and assessment may occur within the same organisation, but the system should represent the conflict where the teacher/provider benefits from passing the learner.

Separation may be required where consequence or conflict warrants it.

> **Institutional Integration != Independence**

> **Conflict != Automatic Invalidity**

The response should be proportionate: disclosure, independent sampling, external assessment, second assessment or stronger separation as risk increases.

## 9. High-consequence assessment

Higher-consequence functions may justify stronger evidence.

Possible controls include:
- multiple assessors;
- independent assessor;
- direct observation;
- simulation under failure conditions;
- supervised real-world evidence;
- external moderation;
- blind/standardised components;
- provenance review;
- re-assessment of borderline/contested outcomes.

> **Higher Consequence -> Stronger Evidence Requirement Where Proportionate**

This does not imply that every high-consequence function requires the same assessment topology.

## 10. Composite qualifications

A broad qualification may compose many competence attestations.

Composition should preserve:
- component identity;
- standard/version;
- currency;
- scope;
- dependencies;
- any missing or limited component.

> **Qualification Composition != Erasure Of Component State**

If one component becomes outdated, the entire historical qualification need not disappear. Current eligibility for affected functions can change while unaffected competence remains.

## 11. Historical custody

Once validly awarded, the enduring qualification/accreditation event is preserved by Historical.

Historical preserves:
- qualification;
- component scope where relevant;
- issuer;
- assessment provenance;
- standard/version;
- award date;
- later patches/revalidation;
- correction;
- suspension/withdrawal where legitimately determined;
- supersession.

> **Historical Custody != Qualification Authority**

> **Historical Record != Current Clinical Authority**

Historical preserves what occurred and the authoritative credential state; it does not independently decide whether a participant may treat a current patient.

## 12. Active Education state

Education holds active:
- learning state;
- pending assessments;
- remediation;
- requalification;
- educational patches;
- competence gaps;
- contested assessment process.

On completion, enduring accreditation/provenance transfers into Historical custody while Education retains only what is needed for active educational function.

## 13. Participant verification

A participant should be able to prove relevant qualification without exposing their full educational history.

Possible bounded questions:
- does participant P currently satisfy CFCC X?
- is qualification Q valid for scope S?
- is a function-critical patch outstanding?
- is supervision required?

PLE can support minimum-disclosure verification.

> **Qualification Verification != Full Educational Disclosure**

## 14. Challenge and appeal

A failed or disputed assessment should have a contestation route.

Possible grounds include:
- factual error;
- procedural failure;
- assessor conflict;
- inaccessible/irrelevant barrier;
- incorrect standard/version;
- evidence omitted or misrepresented;
- assessment not measuring the intended competence.

Appeal should not mean automatic pass.

> **Right To Challenge != Right To Qualification**

> **Assessment Authority != Immunity From Review**

Where the dispute concerns substantive clinical competence, review should use evidence appropriate to that capability.

## 15. Correction, suspension and withdrawal

A qualification may require correction, scope limitation, suspension or withdrawal where legitimately established.

Possible reasons include:
- qualification issued in error;
- fraud;
- invalid assessment;
- material competence evidence later found unreliable;
- function-critical current requirement no longer satisfied.

These states must distinguish historical fact from current eligibility.

> **Qualification Suspended != Qualification Never Existed**

> **Historical Achievement != Permanent Current Eligibility**

Misconduct unrelated to competence should not be relabelled as educational incompetence merely to simplify exclusion.

## 16. Competence concern after qualification

A later concern should trigger proportionate review, not automatic cancellation.

Evidence may point to:
- competence gap;
- outdated knowledge;
- protocol defect;
- equipment/system failure;
- unusual case;
- supervision failure;
- misconduct;
- no actual deficiency.

Mirrored Reality Trees can help test competing explanations.

> **Competence Concern != Proven Incompetence**

If an educational gap is found, targeted remediation/reassessment should normally address the demonstrated gap.

## 17. Anti-gatekeeping safeguards

The accreditation system should not require:
- membership of a professional association merely for status;
- purchase of teaching from the assessor where equivalent learning exists;
- arbitrary time served unrelated to competence;
- prestige-based institutional hierarchy;
- unnecessary repetition of demonstrated capability;
- disclosure of irrelevant personal/educational history.

Legitimate practical-experience requirements remain permissible where they are evidence of the capability being assessed.

> **Gate Competence Where Consequence Requires It; Do Not Gate Access To Learning Or Assessment Without Independent Need**

## 18. External qualifications

An external qualification should not be accepted or rejected solely because of origin.

Education should map it against the relevant current competence standard.

Possible outcomes:
- equivalent;
- equivalent with scope limitation;
- evidence incomplete;
- targeted assessment required;
- educational patch required;
- practical validation required;
- not equivalent.

> **Foreign/External Credential != Automatic Equivalence**

> **Different Educational Route != Automatic Non-Equivalence**

## 19. AI/digital assessors and practitioners

Assessment may use AI/digital systems where their own assessment capability is validated for the function.

> **Automation Capability != Assessment Authority**

> **Model Confidence != Competence Evidence**

Where a digital participant itself seeks qualification, the assessment should test the required functional capability rather than assume competence from architecture, model class or claimed benchmark.

## 20. Minimal qualification record

A qualification attestation may include:
- QualificationRef;
- ParticipantRef;
- Competence/CFCC refs;
- Scope;
- StandardVersion;
- EvidenceClasses;
- Assessor/AssessmentFunction provenance;
- QualificationAuthority;
- AwardedAt;
- CurrencyDependencies;
- PatchState;
- Limitations;
- CurrentCredentialState;
- ContestationState where material;
- HistoricalProvenanceRef.

The public/contextual projection should disclose only what the legitimate function requires.

## 22. Remaining unresolved topology

Still unresolved:
- which civil function recognises/accredits qualification authorities;
- exact assessor qualification standards;
- exact independence thresholds;
- specialty-specific assessment;
- sanctions for fraudulent accreditation;
- inter-civilisation credential recognition;
- deployable credential schema.

These should be resolved without allowing recognition of accreditors to become ownership of medicine or monopoly control of education.


## 23. Accreditation authority resolution — 4 October 2026

The previously unresolved authority question is resolved:

> **Education Owns Educational Accreditation**

This includes recognition/accreditation of medical qualification authorities, assessment functions and assessors where accreditation is required.

Education may distribute execution among multiple legitimate providers, but the civil accreditation function remains within Education. Health does not accredit medical qualifications merely because it consumes competence evidence, and Historical does not accredit merely because it preserves the resulting record.

> **Distributed Accreditation Providers != Distributed Domain Ownership**

> **Health Recognition Of Qualification != Health Accreditation Authority**

> **Historical Custody Of Credential != Historical Accreditation Authority**

Education accreditation remains bounded by the competence/function standard and cannot create clinical authority.

The unresolved-topology item concerning which civil function recognises/accredits qualification authorities is therefore closed. Detailed institutional implementation remains open.
