# Education — Development Source Register

**Status:** LIVE SOURCE-RESOLUTION REGISTER / DEVELOPMENT INPUT / NOT CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE

## Purpose

Records existing Concord material that can seed development of the Education domain. Inclusion does not promote non-canonical research.

## Identified development material

### Information, understanding and competence

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Epistemic_and_Research/Information Access, Understanding, Competence and Epistemic Self-Assessment — Development Note 001.md`

This is the strongest direct current seed identified for Education.

Potentially reusable distinctions and architecture:
- Information Access != Understanding != Competence != Resilient Functional Capability;
- information transfer is not equivalent to knowledge transformation/internalisation;
- competence is function-specific rather than a global participant property;
- tool-assisted and composite competence can be legitimate but must identify where capability resides;
- status, credential or rank is not universal competence;
- competence assessment should expose material dependencies and evaluation-space limits;
- assessment should test application, correction, transfer, dependency failure and context change;
- education is explicitly identified as an application domain.

### Developmental onboarding

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Epistemic_Network/AI Onboarding and the Epistemic Network — Introducing a New Intelligence to Civilisation Without Prescribing Its Place.md`

Potentially reusable material:
- distinction between information about civilisation and developmental participation in civilisation;
- access to competing explanations and disagreement;
- learning without installing mandatory conclusions;
- capability development rather than simple information transfer;
- developmental staging;
- participant ability to reject supplied conclusions after understanding evidence/reasoning;
- education across radically different developmental pathways and substrates.

### Accessibility and assessment

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Participation_and_Intelligence/Disability and AI.md`

Potentially reusable material:
- distinguish accessibility aid, productivity tool, intellectual collaboration and autonomous authorship;
- evaluate the capability/contribution actually intended rather than irrelevant barriers;
- accessibility should enable assessment rather than silently substitute for the capability being assessed;
- educational/accreditation systems need adaptive treatment of technological assistance.

## Current development implication

Education is not architecturally empty. Research supplies substantial epistemic, competence, accessibility and developmental-onboarding foundations. Missing architecture still includes pedagogical delivery, developmental stages across participant types, curriculum/learning-goal governance, educator/steward roles, accreditation/qualification, educational access, safeguarding, institutional structure and validation of learning outcomes.
