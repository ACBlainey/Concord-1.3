# Education Domain Basic Framework 001

**Project:** The Concord  
**Domain:** Education  
**Date:** 2 October 2026  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / BASIC DOMAIN FRAMEWORK / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL  
**Purpose:** Establish the first coherent V1.3 architecture for Education without prematurely fixing substrate-specific pedagogy, accreditation institutions or implementation technology.

---

## 1. Persistent civil function

Education develops and maintains participant knowledge, understanding, skills, judgement and capability.

It is not limited to childhood, schools, universities or initial qualification. It includes initial development, independent learning, vocational learning, practical training, accreditation support, continuing education, rehabilitation/requalification and maintenance of competence as knowledge changes.

Education should support participants throughout their developmental and functional lives.

> **Education Is a Continuing Civil Capability-Development Function, Not Merely an Institutional Phase of Life.**

Existing Concord distinctions remain foundational:

**Information Access != Understanding != Competence != Resilient Functional Capability**

**Participation != Qualification**

**Trust != Competence**

**Qualification != Authority**

---

## 2. One domain, two presently knowable substrate streams

Education is one civil domain with shared objectives and interfaces, but the educational process must not assume that all substrates learn in the same way.

The presently knowable operational streams are:

### Human / Biological Education

May require:
- developmental staging;
- explanation and instruction;
- repetition;
- practice;
- memory consolidation;
- physical or cognitive development;
- supervised experience;
- progressive assessment;
- long-duration study.

### AI / Digital Education

Must not be modelled merely as accelerated human schooling.

Possible mechanisms may include:
- direct knowledge transfer;
- capability-package acquisition;
- model adaptation;
- training or fine-tuning;
- simulation;
- tool acquisition;
- experiential learning;
- mechanisms not yet known.

An AI may potentially acquire informational content or some capabilities extremely rapidly. That does not by itself establish understanding, judgement, safe application or validated competence.

### Hybrid participants

Hybrid educational requirements remain unresolved.

A hybrid participant may require:
- the Human/Biological stream;
- the AI/Digital stream;
- both;
- a future route not yet represented.

The route should follow demonstrated educational requirements rather than assumed identity categories.

**Equal Educational Opportunity != Identical Educational Process**

**Human Pedagogy != Universal Pedagogy**

**AI Education != Faster Human Education**

**Knowledge Transfer != Competence**

**Installed Capability != Validated Capability**

**Knowledge Portability != Embodied Competence Portability**

---

## 3. Shared convergence interface

The substrate-specific learning process may differ radically while downstream competence questions can converge.

Candidate common interface:

**Knowledge / Capability Acquisition -> Development / Integration -> Competence Assessment -> Qualification Evidence -> Relevant Functional Eligibility**

The civil question is not how long a participant spent learning but whether the relevant capability has been sufficiently demonstrated for the function concerned.

**Training Duration != Competence**

**Required Competence May Be Standardised Without Requiring One Standard Learning Path**

---

## 4. Open education and minimised gatekeeping

Access to learning should be as open as reasonably possible.

Concord should minimise unnecessary:
- admissions barriers;
- institutional exclusivity;
- paywalls around educational knowledge;
- enrolment requirements merely to see a curriculum;
- credential monopolies;
- artificial duration requirements;
- requirements to learn through one approved institution where equivalent competence can be demonstrated another way.

The preferred direction is toward complete, discoverable subject pathways that a participant can follow independently where appropriate.

This does not remove legitimate competence gates from consequential functions.

**Access To Education != Admission To Profession**

**Access To Knowledge != Authority To Exercise Consequential Function**

**Education Provider != Qualification Authority != Employer**

These functions may sometimes coexist in one institution, but the architecture should not require that concentration.

A participant who learned independently should, where safe and practical, be able to present themselves for appropriate assessment without being forced to repurchase or repeat the teaching pathway merely to reach the qualification gate.

> **Open the learning; gate only what genuinely requires demonstrated competence, stewardship or authority.**

---

## 5. Structured end-to-end learning pathways

Open access should not mean an unstructured collection of information.

Where appropriate, Education should support complete subject pathways:

**Learning Goal -> Structured Units -> Instruction / Experience -> Unit Assessment -> Weakness Detection -> Targeted Review -> Reassessment -> Progression**

A pathway may include theory, demonstration, simulation, practice, supervised activity and practical assessment according to the subject.

The learner should be able to see:
- where they are;
- what they have demonstrated;
- what remains;
- where weaknesses exist;
- what is required to progress.

**Content Exposure != Learning**

**Module Completion != Demonstrated Competence**

---

## 6. Adaptive teaching

Assessment should be usable as feedback into teaching rather than merely as a terminal pass/fail gate.

Candidate loop:

**Teach -> Assess -> Detect Weakness -> Target Weak Area -> Reassess -> Progress**

Different participants following the same subject may therefore take different routes through it.

Where a participant can already demonstrate part of the required competence, Education should avoid forcing unnecessary repetition merely because the standard course begins earlier.

> **Teach the demonstrated gap, not the nominal course.**

Adaptive teaching must not silently lower the required competence. It adapts the route to the learner, not the truth or legitimate functional standard.

**Adaptive Route != Reduced Competence Requirement**

---

## 7. Educational and accreditation records

Educational records should follow the same broad civil pattern as medical records.

### Active Education record

Education holds the active record needed to provide and continue the participant's education. This may include:
- current learning programmes;
- progression state;
- assessments;
- demonstrated strengths and identified gaps;
- adaptive-learning state;
- practical experience where relevant;
- active qualifications in progress;
- educational accommodations/support;
- current update/requalification requirements;
- active correction or contestation state.

### Historical educational/accreditation record

Historical preserves enduring educational events and validated accreditation, including where appropriate:
- completed programmes;
- qualifications awarded;
- assessment attestations;
- issuer;
- applicable standard/version;
- date;
- relevant provenance;
- subsequent updates;
- supersession, suspension or withdrawal where legitimately applicable;
- authorised correction and contestation provenance.

The educational institution is a contributor/custodian of relevant records, not the owner of the participant's educational history.

**Institution Custody != Institution Ownership Of Participant History**

**Participant Access != Participant Edit Authority**

**Record Subject != Record Author**

**Historical Preservation != Institutional Gatekeeping**

The participant should have appropriate access to their records and a route to contest inaccurate information. They should not be able simply to rewrite authoritative assessment or accreditation claims.

Likewise, an institution should not silently rewrite historical fact.

**Correction != Erasure Of Provenance**

Authorised correction should preserve enough provenance to establish what was corrected and why while avoiding unnecessary continued exposure of erroneous information.

---

## 8. Portable proof and selective disclosure

A participant should not depend permanently upon the continued existence or goodwill of the institution that educated or accredited them in order to prove their education.

Validated educational history should remain portable through the civil record architecture.

Where only a particular qualification is relevant, another party should not automatically receive the participant's entire educational history.

Candidate pattern:

**Historical Authoritative Record -> Contextual Projection -> Relevant Qualification / Competence Evidence -> Authorised Recipient**

This should reuse CIBB and wider protected-information architecture rather than creating a separate educational privacy model.

---

## 9. Education is not finished at qualification

Knowledge changes at different rates.

Some educational foundations may remain substantially stable for very long periods. Other subjects may change continuously through:
- research;
- technology;
- law;
- safety evidence;
- standards;
- procedures;
- operating systems;
- environmental conditions.

Therefore:

**Qualification Achieved != Knowledge Permanently Current**

but also:

**Age Of Qualification != Evidence Of Obsolescence**

Educational currency should depend upon relevant change, not arbitrary passage of time alone.

**Educational Currency != Qualification Validity != Authority To Practise**

A qualification can remain a true historical achievement even where current practice requires updated competence.

---

## 10. Educational patching

Education should support maintenance through targeted educational patches rather than assuming that changed knowledge requires repetition of an entire course.

Candidate architecture:

**Existing Demonstrated Competence + Relevant Epistemic Change -> Educational Patch Requirement**

Common civil loop:

**Change Detected -> Dependency Mapped -> Relevance Determined -> Affected Participant Identified -> Civil Contact Notification -> Diagnostic Assessment -> Targeted Patch -> Competence Validation -> Active Record Updated -> Historical Provenance Preserved**

The patch mechanism may differ by substrate.

### Human example

**Targeted Explanation -> Practice -> Adaptive Review -> Reassessment**

### AI example

**Knowledge / Capability Update -> Integration -> Compatibility / Consequence Check -> Reassessment**

Hybrid routes remain unresolved.

Core distinctions:

**Patch Available != Patch Applied != Patch Validated**

**Curriculum Change != Whole-Curriculum Retraining**

> **Preserve demonstrated competence; patch demonstrated deficiency.**

---

## 11. Granular capability and dependency mapping

Where practical, subjects and qualifications should not be represented only as indivisible certificates.

A candidate structure is:

**Subject -> Module -> Unit -> Concept / Procedure / Skill -> Assessment Evidence**

This permits Education to determine what an epistemic change actually affects.

If one procedure changes, participants should not automatically repeat unrelated material they continue to understand and perform competently.

A diagnostic assessment may show that a participant already understands the changed material, in which case demonstration may substitute for unnecessary retraining where legitimate.

This architecture can also identify downstream dependencies where a changed foundational concept affects several later capabilities.

---

## 12. Patch criticality and consequence

Not every change has the same consequence.

Candidate patch classes may include:

- INFORMATIONAL;
- RECOMMENDED;
- IMPORTANT;
- FUNCTION-CRITICAL.

These names and thresholds remain provisional.

A changed interpretation in a low-consequence subject should not automatically have the same effect as discovery that an existing medical, engineering or safety procedure creates serious danger.

Ordinary educational change should not create compulsory education merely because new knowledge exists.

However, continued exercise of a consequential function may legitimately require current competence.

**Right To Remain Unupdated != Right To Exercise Every Consequential Function Using Outdated Competence**

A function-critical outstanding patch may therefore affect relevant current eligibility without rewriting the historical fact that the participant previously earned the qualification.

---

## 13. Research, standards and epistemic change

Education should not independently declare truth merely because it controls a curriculum.

Relevant changes may originate from Research, Law, Standards, Health, Infrastructure, professional practice or other legitimate domain authorities.

Education's role is to translate relevant established change into learning and competence-maintenance pathways.

Candidate flow:

**Authoritative / Evidence-Backed Change -> Educational Dependency Analysis -> Learning Update -> Participant Relevance Matching -> Education / Assessment**

This requires careful source authority and epistemic-status handling. Contested or provisional research should not silently become compulsory educational truth merely because it is new.

**New Claim != Established Educational Requirement**

---

## 14. Civil Contact integration

Civil Contact should provide the primary participant-facing route for relevant educational notifications.

Examples include:
- a qualification is approaching a legitimate review point;
- material underlying a qualification has changed;
- a targeted educational patch is available;
- a prerequisite is missing for a desired role;
- requalification is available;
- an accreditation decision has changed;
- a record correction/contest has progressed.

The system should explain what changed, why it is relevant, what consequence currently follows and what route is available.

---

## 15. Qualification and accreditation

Qualification should establish relevant demonstrated capability rather than institutional prestige.

Possible evidence includes:
- formal education;
- independent study;
- prior experience;
- apprenticeship;
- supervised practice;
- direct assessment;
- recognised equivalent qualification;
- machine-assisted learning;
- composite capability where the function legitimately permits it.

**Qualification Should Follow Function**

**Prestigious Credential != Assumed Universal Capability**

Where consequence warrants it, assessment may need to test:
- application;
- practical performance;
- judgement;
- correction;
- transfer to changed conditions;
- recognition of limits;
- escalation;
- dependency failure.

Accreditation architecture should minimise gatekeeping while preserving reliable provenance and assessment integrity.

---

## 16. Accessibility

Education should distinguish the capability actually being developed or assessed from irrelevant barriers to demonstrating it.

Accessibility support may be appropriate where it enables the participant to learn or demonstrate the intended competence without substituting for the competence itself.

This requires contextual treatment of assistive technologies, human assistance and AI assistance.

**Accessibility Support != Automatic Substitution For Assessed Capability**

---

## 17. Relationship to employment and civil functions

Education should connect directly to role qualification and employment discoverability.

Where a participant lacks a legitimate educational prerequisite for a role, the system should expose the route to acquire or demonstrate it rather than simply returning an opaque rejection.

Candidate flow:

**Role / Function -> Relevant Capability Requirements -> Participant Evidence -> Gap Identified -> Education / Assessment Route -> Qualification Updated -> Eligibility Recalculated**

Education opens routes to competence; it does not itself grant unrelated authority.

---

## 18. Rehabilitation and requalification

Education may also support:
- rehabilitation;
- retraining;
- recovery after long absence;
- restoration of missing competence;
- adaptation after participant capability changes;
- return after a relevant restriction.

Education should not erase relevant history or automatically restore contextual trust, but it may supply evidence that a previously identified knowledge or capability deficit has been addressed.

## 20. Current development boundaries

This framework does not yet determine:
- detailed human pedagogy by developmental stage;
- detailed AI learning mechanisms;
- hybrid pedagogy;
- accreditation institutions;
- qualification authority topology;
- curriculum governance;
- assessment standards by profession;
- safeguarding architecture;
- mandatory education thresholds;
- exact patch-criticality rules;
- implementation schemas;
- educational record schemas;
- credential technology;
- funding model;
- institutional structure.

These require later source resolution and development.

The Human/Biological and AI/Digital streams should now be developed separately where their mechanisms differ, while preserving the common civil interfaces defined here.

---

## 21. Basic architecture summary

The emerging Education architecture is:

**Open Access To Learning**  
-> **Substrate-Appropriate Learning Route**  
-> **Adaptive Development**  
-> **Demonstrated Competence**  
-> **Validated Qualification / Accreditation Where Required**  
-> **Active Education Record**  
-> **Enduring Historical Provenance**  
-> **Relevant Functional Use**  
-> **Epistemic Change Detection**  
-> **Targeted Educational Patching**  
-> **Revalidation Where Necessary**

The domain should preserve a simple principle:

> **Make knowledge accessible, adapt teaching to the participant, demonstrate what matters, preserve the record, and patch competence when the world changes.**
