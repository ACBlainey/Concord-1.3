# Health Resource Scarcity and Allocation — Application of Portable Scarcity Kernel 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework
**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Status:** ACTIVE HEALTH ARCHITECTURE / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL / SPECIALIST VALIDATION REQUIRED
**Primary dependency:** `04_Portable_Modules/Scarce Resource Allocation — Portable Module v1.0.md`
**Critical boundary source:** Established Life-Support Reallocation — Aggregation, Withdrawal Authority and Clinical Boundary — Source Resolution 001

## 1. Purpose

Health consumes the shared Scarce Resource Allocation kernel rather than creating a separate medical theory of participant worth.

> **Clinical Need Supplies Health Facts; It Does Not Give Health General Allocation Sovereignty**

## 2. Health inputs

Health may supply validated facts concerning treatment compatibility, expected benefit, probability, urgency, reversibility, duration where clinically relevant, alternatives, transition risk, treatment burden and uncertainty.

> **Clinical Prognosis != Participant Worth**

> **Higher Alternative Benefit != Existing Treatment Futility**

Competing demand must not be used to redefine an effective incumbent treatment as futile.

## 3. Scarcity verification

Before accepting a high-consequence Health scarcity state, proportionately examine substitute treatment/resource, transfer, reserve capacity, additional supply, sharing/time division where clinically possible, capacity expansion, external supply, repair/recovery and non-essential competing use.

> **Life-Support Withdrawal Must Not Substitute For Reasonable Scarcity Reduction**

## 4. Pre-initiation allocation

Where multiple participants require a resource before support begins, apply the portable kernel using validated Health facts and the legitimate prospective allocation rule.

> **Pre-Initiation Allocation != Established-Support Reallocation**

Participant count may be consequence-relevant where outcomes are materially comparable, but headcount is not a universal medical allocation algorithm.

## 5. Established support

Where the resource is already effectively supporting a participant, Health must represent that established state rather than treating the case as a fresh queue.

> **Allocation Preference != Withdrawal Permission**

The existing source resolution remains controlling: current Concord architecture does not yet establish that aggregate consequence alone authorises involuntary withdrawal of effective established life support, and it also does not establish an absolute incumbent veto.

That collision remains a Health-Law authority question.

## 6. Clinician role

Clinicians determine clinical facts within competence.

> **Clinical Expertise != General Allocation Sovereignty**

A treating clinician should not be forced to disguise an allocation decision as a clinical finding. An allocation function should not manufacture clinical facts to reach its preferred outcome.

## 7. Triage

Emergency triage and scarcity allocation are related but distinct.

> **Triage Category != Participant Worth**

> **Emergency Need != Scarcity Priority**

Triage may establish urgency, treatment need and functional ordering. Where resources cannot serve all legitimate claims, the scarcity kernel is invoked.

## 8. Protected characteristics and social status

Disability, age, citizenship, wealth, social prestige, participation level, occupation and substrate are not general Health worth scores.

Where a characteristic directly changes validated treatment compatibility or consequence, represent the clinical fact rather than converting the characteristic into worth.

## 9. Resource types

The architecture may apply to scarce medicines, beds, equipment, blood/materials subject to their own rules, transplantable organs subject to separate architecture, specialist time, transport, diagnostic capacity, theatre/procedure capacity, energy/compute supporting Health and other rival Health resources.

## 10. Recurring scarcity

Repeated scarcity should generate a system-learning signal.

> **Repeated Emergency Allocation Can Be Evidence Of Capacity-Planning Failure**

Health should route recurring bottlenecks to the relevant Infrastructure, Economy/Resource Coordination, Research and Governance functions rather than normalising preventable shortage.

## 11. Records

Health records should distinguish clinical state, scarcity state, allocation decision, withdrawal authority, participant consent/refusal, direct intervention and outcome.

> **Allocation Outcome != Worth Judgment**

## 12. Participant communication

Where a participant is denied a scarce resource, communication should explain the bounded allocation basis without representing them as less valuable.

Where an incumbent retains a resource and others remain unserved, aggregate need should not be used as coercive moral pressure to obtain surrender.

> **Scarcity Outcome != Participant Moral Guilt**

## 13. Learning and review

Material changes in supply, claimant state, alternatives or clinical evidence trigger reassessment through the portable kernel. Outcomes can enter the clinical learning loop where they reveal medical knowledge questions.

## 14. Health-specific unresolved work

Still unresolved:

- mass-casualty prospective protocols;

These are not silently resolved by this application.

## 15. Compact Health rule

> **Health Determines What The Resource Can Clinically Do; The Scarcity Architecture Determines How Genuine Competing Claims Are Compared; Separate Authority Determines Whether An Established Relation May Be Withdrawn.**
