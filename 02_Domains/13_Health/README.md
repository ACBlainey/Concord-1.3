# Health

**Status:** DOMAIN ESTABLISHED / PARTICIPANT-DIRECTED CORE HEALTH ARCHITECTURE ESTABLISHED / SPECIALIST AND IMPLEMENTATION LAYERS UNDER DEVELOPMENT

## Persistent function

This domain understands, protects, maintains, restores or appropriately supports participant health and functioning through health-specific knowledge and practice.

Health is substrate-neutral as a civil function, not substrate-identical in physical mechanism.

> **Shared Health Function != Shared Physical Mechanism**

The living Health domain is:

It preserves the earlier medical transfer-test findings including:

> **Clinical Consensus != Independent Evidence**

### 4. Participant-directed diagnostic testing

`Participant-Directed Diagnostic Testing — Risk-Banded Access and Pathway Review 001.md`

Makes diagnostic access proportionate to actual test risk and complexity rather than professional tradition.

> **Access To Diagnostic Evidence != Requirement For Prior Doctor Permission**

> **Review Required != Doctor Required**

### 5. Protocol-driven self-treatment

`Protocol-Driven Self-Treatment and Automated Therapeutic Authorisation 001.md`

Allows validated bounded treatment pathways to proceed without mandatory human professional mediation where diagnosis, indication, participant-specific safety and legitimate therapeutic authority are sufficiently resolved.

> **Confirmed Pathology != Automatic Requirement For Doctor Encounter**

> **Professional Escalation Should Be Triggered By Clinical Need, Not Routine Administrative Sequence**

### 6. Longitudinal and preventive Health

`Longitudinal Health Monitoring — Baselines, Pattern Detection and Preventive Review 001.md`

Establishes participant-owned routine metrics, personal baselines, trend detection, recurrent-episode analysis, bounded always-running review and periodic comprehensive Health review.

> **Continuous Observation != Continuous Institutional Authority**

> **Repeated Local Resolution != Global Explanation**

> **No Symptoms != No Health Issue**

This layer also implements the wider Concord cross-domain principle of prevention and earliest proportionate support.

## Participant-directed Health lifecycle

The current architecture supports:

**Daily life / preventive monitoring**
→ participant-owned longitudinal evidence
→ baseline/trend/pattern review
→ participant concern or bounded trigger
→ Smart Diagnostic Navigation
→ participant-directed diagnostic evidence
→ updated Reality Tree
→ protocol-driven self-treatment where sufficiently resolved
OR
→ professional/generalist/specialist involvement where actual clinical judgement or intervention is required
→ monitoring
→ resolution, reassessment, rehabilitation or continuing support.

Professional expertise remains essential where needed but is not treated as a universal administrative permission layer.

## Authority boundary

Health architecture preserves:

> **Health Support != Participant Ownership**

> **Expertise != Sovereignty**

> **Reasoning Capability != Clinical Authority**

> **Clinical Function != Scarcity != Allocation != Withdrawal != Direct Intervention**

> **Continuous Observation != Continuous Institutional Authority**

Consent, authority, competence, record access and consequential intervention remain separately bounded.

## Records and contextual access

The architectural direction is established but requires a dedicated implementation specification.

The intended model is:
- participant longitudinal Health record;
- participant ordinary access;
- contextual minimum-necessary projection for an active Health function;
- professional access derived from the legitimate care relationship rather than professional status alone;
- provenance-preserving contribution back to the longitudinal record;
- contraction/termination of active access when the function ends.

A dedicated Health Record and Contextual Access Architecture remains required.
