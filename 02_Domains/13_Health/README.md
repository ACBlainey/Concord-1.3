# Health

**Status:** DOMAIN ESTABLISHED / SUBSTRATE-NEUTRAL CORE ARCHITECTURE ESTABLISHED / SPECIALIST LAYERS UNDER DEVELOPMENT

## Persistent function

This domain understands, protects, maintains, restores or appropriately supports participant health and functioning through health-specific knowledge and practice.

Health is substrate-neutral as a civil function, not substrate-identical in physical mechanism.

> **Shared Health Function != Shared Physical Mechanism**

## Current V1.3 architecture

The first common operating architecture is now established:

`02_Domains/13_Health/Health Core Architecture 001 — Substrate-Neutral Service, Evidence, Authority and Care Lifecycle.md`

It defines the common Health lifecycle and boundaries for intake, triage, evidence, diagnostic reasoning, consent, capacity, exceptional authority, intervention, monitoring, outcome, reassessment, closure and cross-domain handoff.

It also establishes the separation:

> **Clinical Function != Scarcity != Allocation != Withdrawal != Direct Intervention**

and preserves the requirement for separate biological/human and digital/AI specialist layers.

## Development boundary

The Health core does not create specialist medical knowledge by architecture.

Still under development are:

- Health service topology and access;
- specialist clinical evidence and diagnostic criteria;
- professional competence and governance;
- biological/human Health specialist architecture;
- digital/AI Health specialist architecture;
- emergency medicine and public health;
- records/confidentiality architecture;
- rehabilitation and continuing support;
- Health-Law established-support withdrawal authority;
- implementation and empirical validation.

> **Architectural Completeness != Medical Knowledge Completeness**
