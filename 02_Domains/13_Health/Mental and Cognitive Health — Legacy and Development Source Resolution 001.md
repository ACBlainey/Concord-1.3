## 1. Finding

V1.2 did not contain a mature ordinary mental-health service architecture. Its own domain-discovery work classified Health and Care as a major missing operational domain.

However, V1.2 contains substantial cross-substrate development on standing, capacity, impairment, supported decision-making, fiduciary support/guardianship, restoration of autonomy, cognitive privacy, supporter conflicts of interest and disagreement versus incapacity.

This material is highly relevant to Mental and Cognitive Health, but its original machine-ethics/guardianship context must not be mistaken for clinical mental-health architecture.

## 2. Primary V1.2 source

`08_Active_Development/01_DEVELOPMENT_NOTES/02_MACHINE_ETHICS_AND_GUARDIANSHIP/Personhood, Capacity, Fiduciary Support and Derived Responsibility Across Substrates — Development Note 001.md`

Important surviving propositions include:

> **Personhood != Capacity**

> **Capacity != Competence**

> **Competence != Authority**

> **Impairment != Loss Of Standing**

> **Support != Ownership**

> **Guardianship != Permanent Control**

The source develops capacity as:

**CAPACITY(entity, function, context, time)**

This is directly useful to Health because it resists global binary classifications where evidence concerns only a particular decision, function, context and time.

It also develops temporary/transitional impairment, minimum-scope support, supported consent, supporter accountability, restoration of autonomy as impairment resolves, and testing for supporter incentives to preserve incapacity status.

**Migration value: HIGH.**

## 4. Adversarial guardianship source

Relevant propositions include:

> **Disagreement With A Guardian Must Not Itself Constitute Evidence Of Ethical Incapacity**

## 8. Candidate living principles

Strong design inputs are:

1. Standing != Current Mental/Cognitive Capacity.
2. Capacity is decision/function/context/time specific.
3. Impairment != Loss Of Standing.
4. Support != Ownership.
5. Support Need != General Substitute Authority.
6. Disagreement != Incapacity.
7. Obedience != Competence.
8. Support should contract as independent agency returns.
9. Supporter benefit from continued control is a conflict of interest.
10. Temporary/transitional impairment requires reassessment.
11. Domain-specific impairment must not be generalised without evidence.
12. Cognitive privacy remains a protected boundary, subject to independently legitimate limits.

## 9. Important non-equivalences for development

**Mental Illness != Incapacity**

**Diagnosis != Authority**

**Distress != Incapacity**

**Risk != General Control**

**Disagreement != Incapacity**

**Support != Guardianship**

**Guardianship != Ownership**

**Need For Treatment != Consent To Treatment**

**Need For Support != Loss Of Privacy**

**Behavioural Difference != Pathology By Definition**

**Clinical Function != Law Enforcement**

These are development constraints, not claims that every edge case is already resolved.

- exact Health-Law-Judiciary interfaces.

## 11. Recommended development order

Do not begin with involuntary treatment.

First develop ordinary participant-directed Mental/Cognitive Health around:
participant-directed access -> experience/distress/function assessment -> competing explanations and physical/medical differential -> decision-specific capacity -> supported decision-making -> voluntary treatment/support -> privacy/confidentiality -> longitudinal/fluctuating state -> recovery/adaptation -> learning.

Then add the exceptional branch:
acute crisis -> immediate harm -> contested capacity -> independently justified temporary authority -> minimum intervention -> review -> restoration of ordinary participant authority.

This prevents exceptional psychiatry from defining the ordinary mental-health system.
