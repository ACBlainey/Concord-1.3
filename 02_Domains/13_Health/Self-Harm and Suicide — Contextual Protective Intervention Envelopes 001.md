# Self-Harm and Suicide — Contextual Protective Intervention Envelopes 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework
**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL / SPECIALIST AND LEGAL VALIDATION REQUIRED
**Domain:** Health
**Interfaces:** Law / Judiciary / Civil Security / Emergency Health
**Primary sources:** Mental Health Crisis and Exceptional Authority Source Resolution; Self-Defence and Defensive Authority; Bounded Authorisation; Rights Derivation; Temporal Consent; Inalienable Participant Standing; Custody as a Protected Context

## 1. Purpose

Self-harm and suicide intervention should be highly contextual.

The architecture should define, in advance wherever reasonably possible, the factual and authority envelopes within which particular protective actions may occur.

This follows the Concord self-defence model:

**Legitimate Protective Context + Protective Function + Necessity -> Bounded Protective Authority**

subject to proportionality, least harmful adequate means, current evidence, review and functional sunset.

> **Protective Purpose != Unlimited Protective Authority**

## 2. Why envelopes

A broad rule such as "prevent suicide" is under-specified. It does not state:
- what evidence activates intervention;
- whether the participant has decision capacity;
- how immediate the threatened harm is;
- what action is actually authorised;
- whether the action is reversible;
- what alternatives exist;
- how long authority persists;
- what causes escalation;
- what causes de-escalation;
- who reviews the decision.

Predefined envelopes reduce improvised coercion while allowing rapid action when time genuinely matters.

> **Precompute The Authority Rule; Verify The Current Facts**

## 3. Envelope dimensions

A protective envelope should represent at least:

**<Observed State, Evidence Reliability, Harm Magnitude, Immediacy, Specificity, Capability/Opportunity, Decision-Specific Capacity, Voluntariness/Coercion State, Available Alternatives, Reversibility, Protective Function, Permitted Acts, Prohibited Acts, Review Requirement, Sunset Trigger>**

Authority attaches to the actual bounded relation, not to the label "suicidal", "mentally ill" or "high risk".

> **Risk Label != Authority Envelope**

## 4. Context bands

The following bands are architectural candidates, not clinical diagnostic criteria.

### SH-0 — no material current self-harm signal

Examples may include historical information with no supported present concern.

Default: ordinary participant authority and ordinary Health confidentiality.

No exceptional protective authority arises merely from history.

> **Past Crisis != Present Crisis Authority**

### SH-1 — distress / thoughts / low-specificity concern

Evidence may indicate distress or thoughts of self-harm without a supported immediate plan/action.

Permitted route should ordinarily remain voluntary:
- offer assessment;
- support;
- safety planning;
- participant-chosen contacts;
- follow-up;
- treatment options;
- environmental/support changes.

No automatic detention, forced treatment or restraint.

> **Thought != Attempt**

### SH-2 — elevated concern / credible developing risk

Evidence supports materially elevated risk, for example increasing specificity, preparation, deteriorating state or other validated indicators, but immediate action is not yet established.

Priority may increase. Active assessment, support and voluntary protective arrangements may be appropriate.

Capacity should be assessed only where relevant to the actual decision.

Compulsory action still requires its own independent authority.

> **Elevated Risk != Automatic Coercive Authority**

### SH-3 — imminent serious self-harm with ordinary authority still obtainable

Serious harm appears sufficiently imminent/time-sensitive, but the participant can still engage and valid consent/support may be obtained within the safe action window.

Use supported consent and voluntary protective action where adequate.

> **Imminence != Automatic Incapacity**

Emergency authority is not activated merely because clinicians prefer a different choice.

### SH-4 — imminent grave harm + temporary authority gap

An attempt/action is occurring or grave harm is sufficiently imminent that delay materially increases harm, while ordinary authority cannot safely be resolved in time because capacity, communication, consciousness, uncertainty or circumstances create a genuine authority gap.

Existing Emergency Health may support minimum temporary protective action.

Potential acts, only where necessary, may include:
- interrupting immediate harmful action;
- emergency stabilisation;
- minimum necessary restraint;
- emergency transport;
- minimum necessary assessment.

> **Emergency Rescue != General Continuing Authority**

> **Authority To Interrupt Immediate Harm != Authority To Control The Participant Indefinitely**

### SH-5 — post-interruption / stabilised but unresolved

The immediate act has been interrupted or the time-critical state has changed.

This is a mandatory authority-reassessment point.

> **Stabilised != Continuing Emergency Authority**

Possible routes:
- ordinary voluntary care;
- supported decision-making;
- independently authorised short assessment;
- another Law/Judiciary route if legitimately established;
- release from exceptional Health authority.

The fact that SH-4 authority existed does not itself justify SH-5 detention/treatment.

### SH-6 — competent, informed, sustained grave self-harm intention outside immediate authority gap

This is the unresolved constitutional envelope.

If a participant demonstrates decision-specific capacity, understands material consequences, is not acting under a currently established coercive/impairing condition sufficient to invalidate the decision, and the immediate emergency authority gap has ended, existing Concord sources do not yet establish whether a separate civil protective override exists.

> **Competent Grave Self-Harm Intention != Automatically Resolved By Emergency Authority**

No Health actor should invent continuing authority merely because this envelope is difficult.

This state requires explicit Rights/Law constitutional resolution.

## 5. Escalation

Escalation from one envelope to another requires material evidence that the predicates of the new envelope are satisfied.

> **Failure Of A Less Restrictive Intervention != Automatic Authority To Escalate**

> **Continued Refusal != Automatic Escalation Trigger**

> **Prior Envelope != Current Envelope**

Examples of material state change may include new action, increased immediacy, newly credible plan/capability evidence, loss/change of decision-specific capacity, or failure of an environmental condition that changes immediate danger.

## 6. De-escalation

Authority must contract when predicates contract.

> **Protective Authority Must Be Able To Move Down As Well As Up**

De-escalation should not depend on the participant saying what the authority holder wants to hear.

> **Agreement With Clinician != Required Evidence Of Recovery**

Observable state, capacity, risk evidence and current context should be reassessed.

## 7. Proportionality

Following Self-Defence:

> **Proportionality != Mechanical Equality Of Force**

The question is what intervention is reasonably necessary and proportionate to the protective function under current circumstances.

A high magnitude of possible harm does not erase probability, immediacy, evidence reliability, alternatives or participant standing.

> **Severe Possible Outcome != Unlimited Preventive Authority**

## 8. Reasonable perception and uncertainty

As in defensive authority, review should consider the evidence reasonably available at the time rather than only hindsight.

> **Claimed Risk != Established Protective Context**

Reasonable error and reckless/fabricated/unsupported claims must remain distinguishable.

Uncertainty may sometimes justify reversible precaution where consequences of waiting are catastrophic and the factual basis is credible, but:

> **Uncertainty != Unlimited Authority**

## 9. Reversibility

Where adequate alternatives exist, prefer interventions that preserve future choice and can be reversed.

Reversibility does not trump effectiveness where the only adequate action is irreversible, but it is authority-relevant.

> **Equivalent Protective Effect -> Prefer Lower Irreversibility And Lower Rights Intrusion Where Reasonably Possible**

## 10. Predefined permitted-act envelopes

Each context band should eventually map to an explicit action matrix.

The matrix should identify whether each act is:
- ordinarily permitted;
- permitted only with participant consent;
- permitted under bounded emergency authority;
- requires separate Law/Judiciary authority;
- prohibited within that envelope.

Acts should include:
- conversation/assessment offer;
- supporter contact;
- record access;
- environmental intervention;
- removal of immediate hazard;
- transport;
- required location;
- physical restraint;
- chemical restraint;
- treatment;
- communication restriction;
- disclosure;
- search/seizure;
- continuing observation.

> **Envelope Classification != Blanket Permission**

## 11. Independent keys remain independent

Even within SH-4:

> **Emergency Protective Authority != Every Intervention Key**

If restraint is unnecessary, it is not authorised merely because transport is.

If treatment can wait until consent is restored, rescue authority does not automatically authorise treatment.

If public protection is not the function, Health does not acquire policing authority.

## 12. Advance crisis envelopes

Where a participant experiences recurrent crises, they should be able to pre-specify preferences within legitimate limits.

Examples:
- early warning signs they recognise;
- people to contact;
- preferred safe locations;
- interventions they know help;
- interventions known to worsen state;
- communication methods;
- medication preferences/adverse effects;
- environmental changes;
- preferred voluntary escalation routes.

This can create a participant-authored response envelope before capacity or communication deteriorates.

> **Advance Planning Can Convert Future Uncertainty Into Presently Authored Guidance**

Its legal force remains governed by Temporal Consent and applicable Law.

## 13. Anti-gaming and anti-capture

Predetermined envelopes must not become a checklist that can be gamed to manufacture authority.

Evidence provenance matters.

Repeated institutional classification into a higher envelope should be reviewable for bias or systematic overreach.

> **Envelope Definition != Permission To Manufacture Its Predicates**

Likewise, a participant should not be required to perform institutional agreement as the price of de-escalation.

## 14. Documentation

For exceptional intervention preserve:
- observed facts;
- evidence sources;
- uncertainty;
- current envelope;
- authority basis;
- permitted act used;
- alternatives considered where time allowed;
- why action was necessary;
- review time/trigger;
- changes causing escalation/de-escalation;
- participant account;
- eventual sunset.

## 15. Review test

Adapted from self-defence review:

1. Did the claimed protective context exist or reasonably appear to exist?
2. What harm was threatened?
3. How immediate was it?
4. What was known about decision-specific capacity?
5. Was intervention necessary?
6. Was a less restrictive adequate route available?
7. Was the action proportionate to the protective function?
8. Did the actor remain inside the applicable envelope?
9. Did the envelope materially change?
10. Did intervention continue after its justification ended?

## 17. Development status

SH-0 through SH-5 are substantially source-derived structural envelopes, but exact clinical thresholds require specialist validation.

SH-6 remains deliberately unresolved.

The next step should be a **Self-Harm Protective Action Matrix** mapping the envelopes against specific actions and authority keys, followed by adversarial scenarios to test false positives, fluctuating capacity, malicious reporting, recurrent crises and institutional overreach.


## 18. SH-6 constitutional source resolution

SH-6 has now been source-resolved in:

`01_Constitutional_Core/Provisional_Charters/Rights_Derivation/Competent Grave Self-Harm — Autonomy, Life, Harm and Protective Authority Source Resolution 001.md`

Result: **PARTIALLY DETERMINATE**.

Existing Concord strongly supports temporary, proportionate and preferably reversible protective intervention while a genuine time-critical authority or decision-validity gap remains. It also strongly requires that authority derived from that gap contract as capacity, communication, information, voluntariness and temporal stability become adequately resolved.

The sources do not presently establish a general continuing Health power over a genuinely competent, informed and sufficiently voluntary sustained grave self-directed choice. Nor do they establish an unrestricted positive right to assisted death.

SH-6 is therefore refined as a **constitutional handoff state**, not an ordinary clinical detention state.

> **SH-6 Should Be Difficult To Enter Because Its Predicates Are Demanding, Not Because The Participant Must Agree To Live**

> **If Exceptional Authority Exists, Its Envelope Should Precede The Case Rather Than Be Invented Around The Participant**

Before SH-6 classification, the system must establish that the emergency authority gap has ended, decision-specific capacity is present, material consequences/alternatives are understood, coercion and reversible invalidating causes have been proportionately examined, temporal stability is sufficient for the context, third-party protection is separated, and prior emergency authority is not merely being inherited.
