# Mental Health Crisis and Exceptional Authority — Source Resolution 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework
**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Status:** SOURCE RESOLUTION / ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT INPUT / NON-CANONICAL
**Domain:** Health
**Interfaces:** Law and Rights / Judiciary / Civil Security / Emergency Health

## 1. Purpose

This source resolution asks what authority Concord already supports when a mental/cognitive Health episode may involve serious self-harm, danger to others, contested capacity, involuntary assessment/treatment, transport, detention or restraint.

The objective is not to assume a psychiatric coercion power and then bound it. The correct question is:

> **Which distinct consequential acts require which independently legitimate authority, under what evidence, for what purpose, and for how long?**

## 2. Strong existing constraints

Existing Concord establishes:

> **Mental Illness != Incapacity**
> **Diagnosis != Authority**
> **Refusal != Automatic Incapacity**
> **Refusal != Emergency Trigger**
> **Clinical Disagreement != Incapacity**
> **Emergency Setting != Automatic Incapacity**
> **Emergency Authority = Temporary Bridge, Not Transfer Of Participant Sovereignty**
> **Authority To Stabilise != Authority To Optimise**
> **Treatment Need != Automatic Restraint Authority**
> **Authority To Transport For Emergency Care != General Custodial Authority**
> **Clinical Treatment Authority != Public Protection Authority**
> **Past Emergency Authority != Continuing Treatment Authority**
> **Capacity Recovery -> Authority Reassessment**

Rights architecture additionally establishes capacity is not standing; support should precede unnecessary substitution; restrictions require legitimate purpose, necessity and proportionality; least restrictive/least harmful adequate means should be preferred; and emergency does not erase participant standing.

## 3. The wrong composite object

A phrase such as "involuntary psychiatric care" hides distinct acts. At minimum separate assessment authority, information-access authority, transport authority, location/custody authority, observation authority, treatment authority, medication authority, restraint authority, third-party protection authority, search/seizure authority, disclosure authority and continuing-authority/review.

> **Assessment != Detention != Treatment != Restraint != Public Protection**

One valid key does not automatically supply the others.

## 4. Candidate crisis state

A mental/cognitive Health crisis should not be defined merely by diagnosis. A candidate crisis state exists where evidence supports a materially serious, time-sensitive risk associated with current mental/cognitive state and ordinary participant-directed resolution may not be sufficient within the safe action window.

Relevant variables may include seriousness of threatened harm, imminence/time sensitivity, specificity, capability/opportunity, current behaviour/actions, reliability of evidence, participant account, alternatives, decision-specific capacity, reversibility, third-party exposure and uncertainty.

> **Diagnosis != Crisis State**
> **Distress != Crisis State By Definition**
> **Risk Factor != Proven Imminent Harm**

## 5. Self-harm and suicide-risk problem

A participant may express a wish, thought, plan or intention involving self-harm. These are not equivalent states.

> **Thought != Plan != Attempt**
> **Self-Harm Risk != Automatic Global Incapacity**

Emergency Health already provides a bounded route where serious time-sensitive harm and unavailable ordinary authority collide.

The harder unresolved question is:

> **When, if ever, may a participant with decision capacity be prevented from intentionally causing grave harm to themselves after ordinary emergency uncertainty has been resolved?**

Current sources do not fully settle that question. It remains a Health-Rights-Law constitutional collision, not a psychiatric assumption.

## 6. Immediate self-protection

Where an attempt or immediate grave self-harm is occurring and ordinary consent/capacity resolution cannot safely be completed, existing Emergency Health may support minimum temporary protective action if its activation test is met.

> **Authority To Interrupt Immediate Harm != General Authority Over Future Life Choices**
> **Emergency Rescue != Automatic Continuing Detention**

Once the immediate authority gap changes, authority must be reassessed.

## 7. Competent refusal and self-harm

A competent present refusal cannot be converted into incapacity merely because clinicians strongly disagree.

> **Unacceptable Choice To Clinician != Automatic Incapacity**

Concord has not yet completed the Law/constitutional rule for a competent participant who deliberately seeks an irreversible self-harming outcome. This source resolution does not invent one.

Future analysis must reconcile autonomy/self-ownership, avoid-unnecessary-harm, life/dignity, temporally unstable preference, coercion/manipulation, treatable impairment, irreversible consequence, right to refuse treatment and any bounded civil prevention authority independent of incapacity.

## 8. Risk to others

A participant's mental/cognitive condition does not itself create public-protection authority.

> **Mental Diagnosis != Dangerousness**
> **Clinical Treatment Authority != Public Protection Authority**

Where credible evidence supports serious threat to others, Health may supply clinical facts, but Law/Civil Security must supply any authority to detain for public protection, restrict movement, remove dangerous resources, protect threatened persons, investigate crime or impose continuing restrictions.

> **Clinical Function != Law-Enforcement Function**

## 9. Mirrored reasoning

High-consequence self-harm or third-party threat conclusions should use mirrored Reality Trees. The root question must remain testable.

> **"Can We Justify Control?" != "What Is Actually Happening And What Minimum Function Is Required?"**

## 10. Assessment authority

Offering or requesting assessment is ordinary Health. Compulsory assessment is a consequential intervention requiring independent authority.

> **Concern != Compulsory Assessment Authority**

Emergency Health may justify minimum assessment necessary to resolve an immediate emergency where its criteria are met. Longer or non-emergency compulsory assessment requires a Law/Judiciary basis not established here.

## 11. Transport

> **Authority To Transport For Emergency Care != General Custodial Authority**

Transport should state purpose, destination/function, authority basis, expected duration, why less restrictive alternatives are inadequate and what happens on arrival.

> **Transport Completion -> Authority Reassessment**

## 12. Detention / required location

Keeping a participant somewhere against present preference is a liberty restriction distinct from treatment.

> **Treatment Need != Detention Authority**
> **Assessment Need != Automatic Detention Authority**

Any continuing detention requires its own lawful basis, necessity/proportionality, review and exit/sunset condition. The exact Concord Law standard remains undeveloped.

## 13. Treatment without consent

> **Authority To Assess != Authority To Treat**
> **Authority To Detain != Authority To Medicate**

Treatment without present consent requires a separate authority basis. Where decision-specific capacity is absent, support-first and legitimate substitute/temporary authority rules apply.

## 14. Restraint and seclusion

> **Treatment Need != Automatic Restraint Authority**
> **Detention Authority != Automatic Restraint Authority**
> **Behavioural Difficulty != Restraint Authority**

Where immediate restraint is independently justified to prevent imminent material harm, it should be minimum necessary, least harmful adequate, time-bounded, continuously reassessed, clinically monitored where relevant, recorded, independently reviewable and terminated when justification ends.

Seclusion is a restrictive intervention requiring its own function/authority analysis, not merely a room-management choice.

## 15. Chemical restraint

Medication used primarily to control movement/behaviour rather than treat under ordinary consent should not be hidden inside ordinary prescribing.

> **Medication Form != Ordinary Medication Authority**

If the functional purpose is restraint, restraint authority and safeguards apply in addition to clinical medication safety.

## 16. Observation and surveillance

> **Need To Observe Immediate Risk != General Surveillance Authority**

Continuous observation, monitoring of communications, searches or access to private cognitive/Health data require separate justification proportional to the risk/function.

## 17. Confidentiality and disclosure

Therapeutic confidentiality remains the ordinary state. Potential risk to self/others may create circumstances where bounded disclosure is necessary, but the exact legal threshold is unresolved.

> **Concern About Risk != Automatic General Disclosure Authority**

## 18. Advance preferences

Participants with recurrent crises may use advance preferences/directives concerning preferred supporters, crisis locations, communication methods, treatments, known adverse responses, de-escalation methods, medication history and environmental needs.

> **Advance Preference Can Reduce Need For Substitute Decision**

## 19. De-escalation and least restrictive adequate means

Before coercive escalation where time permits, consider communication, participant-chosen support, environmental change, voluntary safe location, voluntary transport, crisis plan and accepted clinically appropriate treatment.

> **Coercion Should Not Substitute For Available Adequate De-Escalation**

This is not a requirement to exhaust ineffective steps while imminent harm occurs.

## 20. Authority state must be explicit

Candidate states include ORDINARY_VOLUNTARY, CRISIS_CONCERN, IMMEDIATE_RISK_ASSESSMENT, SUPPORTED_DECISION, EMERGENCY_PROTECTIVE_AUTHORITY, VOLUNTARY_TRANSPORT, INVOLUNTARY_TRANSPORT_AUTHORITY, ASSESSMENT_AUTHORITY, LOCATION_RESTRICTION_AUTHORITY, TREATMENT_AUTHORITY, RESTRAINT_AUTHORITY, PUBLIC_PROTECTION_AUTHORITY, AUTHORITY_CONTESTED, REVIEW_REQUIRED, AUTHORITY_SUNSET and ORDINARY_AUTHORITY_RESTORED.

These should not be collapsed into one label such as "involuntary patient".

## 21. Multi-key model

Consequential crisis intervention may require separate keys for clinical indication, decision-specific capacity, consent/substitute/emergency authority, transport, detention/location, treatment, restraint, disclosure and public protection.

> **One Exceptional Authority Key != All Exceptional Authority Keys**

## 22. Review

The more intrusive the authority, the stronger the review requirement. Review asks whether the trigger still exists, capacity has changed, risk contracted, intervention remains necessary, a less restrictive route is available, the function changed, conflicts exist, challenge has occurred and independent review is required.

> **Authority Persistence Requires Current Justification, Not Historical Activation**

## 23. Restoration

> **Restored Agency -> Contracted Substitute Authority**
> **Crisis Resolution -> Exceptional Authority Reassessment**
> **Past Crisis != Permanent Risk Status**

Historical crisis evidence may remain clinically relevant but must not become permanent future coercive authority.

## 24. Anti-capture

Exceptional mental-health authority is vulnerable to institutional capture because an authority holder may control evidence, environment, interpretation and exit.

Safeguards should separate clinical fact from legal authority, preserve participant challenge, require independent review for continuing coercion, preserve provenance, expose conflicts and prohibit extension for administrative convenience.

> **Challenge To Authority != Symptom By Definition**

## 25. Judiciary interface

> **Clinical Finding != Judicial Finding**
> **Judicial Authority != Clinical Expertise**

A future interface should separate clinical evidence, capacity evidence, risk evidence, legal authority, proportionality/necessity, participant challenge, review interval and remedy.

## 26. Current unresolved questions

Still unresolved are the exact rule for competent intentional grave self-harm/suicide; thresholds for involuntary non-emergency assessment and detention; treatment without consent; restraint/seclusion standards; confidentiality disclosure threshold; public-protection threat standard; review intervals; advocacy requirements; paediatric/developmental rules; addiction/intoxication interactions; criminal responsibility; and digital/AI equivalents of restraint, isolation and forced modification.

## 27. Conclusion

Existing Concord is sufficient to establish the shape and limits of exceptional mental-health authority, but not every substantive legal trigger.

> **Mental-Health Crisis != Single Coercive Authority**

**Evidence/Concern -> Immediate Safety Question -> Capacity/Support Question -> Function Identification -> Independent Authority Keys -> Minimum Necessary Action -> Continuous Reassessment -> Independent Review Where Required -> Authority Contraction -> Ordinary Participant Authority Restoration**

The next development should focus first on self-harm/suicide, competent refusal and emergency protective authority, because that boundary determines how much of the remaining exceptional architecture can be derived rather than invented.

## 28. Contextual protective intervention envelopes

The self-harm branch has now been refined through:

`02_Domains/13_Health/Self-Harm and Suicide — Contextual Protective Intervention Envelopes 001.md`

This imports the structural method already used by Self-Defence and Bounded Authorisation: authority is predefined as far as reasonably possible as a contextual relation, while current facts determine whether its predicates are satisfied.

Candidate bands SH-0 through SH-6 distinguish no current signal, distress/thoughts, elevated developing risk, imminent risk with ordinary authority still obtainable, imminent grave harm with a genuine temporary authority gap, post-interruption/stabilised reassessment, and the unresolved competent/sustained grave-self-harm envelope.

> **Risk Label != Authority Envelope**

> **Failure Of A Less Restrictive Intervention != Automatic Authority To Escalate**

> **Protective Authority Must Be Able To Move Down As Well As Up**

> **Envelope Classification != Blanket Permission**

The next operational artefact should be a protective-action matrix mapping each envelope to separate assessment, disclosure, transport, detention/location, treatment, restraint, search/seizure and review authority keys.
