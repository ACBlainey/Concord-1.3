Potentially reusable candidate constraints:

## Current development implication

These sources can seed patient/participant autonomy, supported decision-making, outcome learning and support boundaries. They do not provide clinical medicine, public-health, service-delivery, professional-governance or health-capability architecture; those remain genuine development gaps.

## Cross-domain and portable mechanisms available for adaptation

### Contextual Wrapper Architecture

**Source:** `04_Portable_Modules/Contextual Wrapper Architecture — Portable Module.md`

CWA explicitly uses hospitals as an example of combined physical spaces, medical-record permissions, professional roles and emergency states. Reusable mechanisms include nested access, action-specific permission, minimum-necessary permission, permission provenance, emergency/remedy interfaces and termination. Health-specific consent, clinical authority and confidentiality rules remain to be developed.

### Civil Attention

**Source:** `04_Portable_Modules/Civil Attention Reporting Petition and Resolution Module v1.0.md`

Can supply a domain-neutral intake and accountable routing layer for complaints, safety reports, service problems and participant proposals without turning the intake system into clinical decision authority.


### Reality Trees — diagnostic reasoning

**Source:** `04_Portable_Modules/Reality Trees.md` and associated Concord Reality Tree methodology.

Reality Trees are a directly relevant reusable reasoning method for Health, especially diagnostic reasoning. A health-specific adaptation can preserve competing causal/diagnostic branches rather than forcing premature convergence.

Candidate diagnostic grammar:

**Symptoms / observations -> competing Reality Tree branches -> supporting and disconfirming evidence -> targeted investigation -> branch revision -> bounded diagnostic conclusion -> treatment/response -> outcome feedback -> tree correction**

Potentially reusable functions:
- represent multiple competing diagnostic hypotheses;
- make causal assumptions and branch dependencies explicit;
- preserve uncertainty and unresolved alternatives;
- identify observations/tests capable of discriminating between branches;
- incorporate contradictory evidence without silently erasing earlier reasoning;
- revise diagnostic models as new evidence or outcomes arrive;
- preserve provenance of why a branch was strengthened, weakened or rejected.

**Boundary:** Reality Trees provide a diagnostic-reasoning architecture. They do not provide medical knowledge, validated diagnostic criteria, clinical competence, treatment evidence or professional qualification. Any Health adaptation must be validated against appropriate clinical evidence and must not treat internal logical coherence as proof of medical correctness.

### Bounded Contextual Authority — clinical authority, consent and exceptional intervention

**Source:** Concord Bounded Contextual Authority architecture in `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Governance_and_Stewardship/`, together with the Contextual Wrapper Architecture and related authority-justification work.

Bounded Contextual Authority is highly relevant wherever healthcare creates temporary or context-specific asymmetries of knowledge, capability, access or decision-making power.

Potential Health applications include:
- informed consent and refusal;
- supported decision-making and fluctuating capacity;
- emergency treatment;
- professional scope of practice;
- access to medical records and sensitive information;
- safeguarding;
- isolation or other public-health interventions;
- temporary authority where a participant cannot presently exercise a required decision;
- explicit termination/review of exceptional authority when its functional justification ends.

Candidate Health authority grammar:

**Clinical/Protective Need -> Legitimate Function -> Is Authority Required? -> Minimum Effective Proportionate Authority -> Defined Clinical Context and Scope -> Provenance/Consent or Exceptional Justification -> Action -> Review -> Authority Contracts or Ends When Justification Ends**

Key inherited constraints:
- expertise does not itself create unlimited authority;
- prediction or risk does not itself create authority;
- authority should be justified by the required function;
- scope should be minimum necessary and context-specific;
- exceptional authority should be provenance-visible and reviewable;
- emergency authority should not silently become ordinary persistent authority;
- participant autonomy should remain the default wherever the required function can be achieved without coercive authority.

**Boundary:** Bounded Contextual Authority supplies an authority-control architecture, not medical competence or a complete health-law framework. Health development must still determine clinically and legally valid consent/capacity standards, emergency thresholds, professional responsibilities, safeguards and review mechanisms.

## Revised Health development implication

Health should no longer be treated as beginning only from generic support and wrapper constraints. Concord already supplies reusable architecture for at least two central Health functions:

1. **diagnostic reasoning** through Reality Trees; and
2. **legitimate bounded clinical/protective authority** through Bounded Contextual Authority.

Together with CA-01, supported decision-making, CWA, Civil Attention and outcome-learning architecture, this constitutes a meaningful cross-domain foundation.

The remaining gap is therefore more precisely the **specialist medical operating layer**: validated clinical knowledge and diagnostic criteria, treatment and care pathways, professional competence/governance, service delivery, emergency medicine, public health, prevention, population-health functions, health-specific evidence standards, implementation and empirical validation.
