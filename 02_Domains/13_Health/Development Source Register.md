# Health — Development Source Register

**Status:** LIVE SOURCE-RESOLUTION REGISTER / DEVELOPMENT INPUT / NOT CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE

## Purpose

Records existing Concord material that can inform development of Health without claiming that a health-service architecture already exists.

## Identified development material

### Participant support, autonomy and capacity

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Layer_Zero_and_Minus_Zero/Candidate_Architecture/CA-01 Participant Support and Autonomy Architecture.md`

Potentially reusable material:
- voluntary/supportive intervention boundaries;
- participant autonomy during support;
- refusal of support should not automatically escalate authority;
- support-system exit can be a successful outcome;
- support must remain functional if predictive/metrics systems fail;
- false-positive vulnerability and emergency-override concerns;
- cross-substrate support requirements.

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Layer_Zero_and_Minus_Zero/Source_Systems/00_SYNTHESIS_AND_SOURCE_DOCUMENTS/Systems Extracted from the Layer-Zero and Layer −0 Development Cycle.md`

Potentially reusable material:
- Handrail Civil Support Architecture;
- Supported Decision-Making and Capacity Architecture;
- trajectory analysis and predictive support;
- case learning;
- support gradients;
- separation of protective architecture from architectures that increase institutional power.

### Learning from outcomes

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Evidence/Layer_Zero_Development/4 Adversarial Critical Examination — 12 Candidate Principles from the Layer-Zero - Layer −0 Development Cycle.md`

Potentially reusable candidate constraints:
- outlier, contradictory and unsuccessful outcomes should be examined as possible evidence of hidden assumptions or missing pathways;
- indicators should not automatically become conclusions;
- repeated failure should trigger reassessment rather than automatic repetition/escalation;
- evidence significance should depend on evidence rather than rarity alone.

## Current development implication

These sources can seed patient/participant autonomy, supported decision-making, outcome learning and support boundaries. They do not provide clinical medicine, public-health, service-delivery, professional-governance or health-capability architecture; those remain genuine development gaps.

## Cross-domain and portable mechanisms available for adaptation

### Contextual Wrapper Architecture

**Source:** `04_Portable_Modules/Contextual Wrapper Architecture — Portable Module.md`

CWA explicitly uses hospitals as an example of combined physical spaces, medical-record permissions, professional roles and emergency states. Reusable mechanisms include nested access, action-specific permission, minimum-necessary permission, permission provenance, emergency/remedy interfaces and termination. Health-specific consent, clinical authority and confidentiality rules remain to be developed.

### Civil Attention

**Source:** `04_Portable_Modules/Civil Attention Reporting Petition and Resolution Module v1.0.md`

Can supply a domain-neutral intake and accountable routing layer for complaints, safety reports, service problems and participant proposals without turning the intake system into clinical decision authority.
