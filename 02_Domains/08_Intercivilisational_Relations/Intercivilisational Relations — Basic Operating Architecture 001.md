# Intercivilisational Relations — Basic Operating Architecture 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Date:** 6 October 2026  
**Status:** BASIC OPERATING ARCHITECTURE / ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / NON-CANONICAL  
**Source basis:** V1.3 migrated civilisational-wrapper and recognition architecture; V1.2 Intercivilisational Relations migration map; Historical Source Resolutions 018 and 041  
**Interfaces:** Constitutional Core; Law; Judiciary; Defence; Economy; Historical; Research; Civil Contact; Cross-Domain Architecture

## 1. Persistent function

Intercivilisational Relations manages legitimate relationships between the Concord and distinct civilisations, polities and other sufficiently independent civil entities.

Its purpose is not to make other civilisations Concord-like.

Its purpose is to make bounded coexistence and useful interaction possible without silently converting interaction into authority, recognition, jurisdiction, assimilation or political merger.

> **Interoperability Without Forced Internal Uniformity**

## 2. Domain boundary

This domain may coordinate:
- communication;
- diplomacy;
- recognition state;
- negotiated relations;
- peaceful distance;
- interoperability;
- rights/standing translation interfaces;
- bounded agreements;
- cross-boundary case routing;
- externality interfaces;
- optional mediation where separately authorised;
- interfaces to trade, security, law and shared systems.

It does not thereby acquire:
- government over another civilisation;
- adjudicative authority;
- military authority;
- authority to redesign another civilisation internally;
- ownership of shared history;
- automatic mediation authority;
- sovereignty by authorship of protocols.

## 3. Central non-conversion rules

> **Common Protocol != Common Government**

> **Wrapper Adoption != Concord Oversight**

> **Design Authorship != Operational Authority**

> **Technical Compatibility != Political Subordination**

> **Diplomatic Communication != Sovereign Recognition**

> **Negotiation != General Legitimisation**

> **Interaction != Jurisdiction**

> **Externality != Jurisdiction**

> **Standing To Raise A Claim != Proof Of Responsibility**

> **Recognition != Binary Variable**

These distinctions should remain explicit state rather than being inferred from one another.

## 4. Civilisational wrapper

A civilisational wrapper is an interoperability boundary/interface.

It may provide common ways to represent:
- participants/representatives;
- permissions;
- context;
- rights/standing;
- information exchange;
- responsibility;
- agreements;
- disputes;
- externalities;
- exit/termination.

The wrapper is not a supracivilisational government.

> **The Concord Provides Architecture; Use Of That Architecture Does Not Transfer Sovereignty To The Concord**

## 5. Relationship state

An intercivilisational relationship should not be represented by one allied/hostile/recognised flag.

Material dimensions may include:
- communication status;
- interoperability status;
- recognition type/scope;
- agreement state;
- trust/evidence state;
- jurisdiction claims;
- effective control where relevant;
- responsibility allocation;
- disputes;
- peaceful-distance state;
- security posture;
- economic interface;
- information-sharing scope.

These dimensions can change independently.

## 6. Recognition has resolution

Recognition can concern different things:
- existence;
- sentient/moral standing;
- communication standing;
- document/credential acceptance;
- operational capacity;
- political community;
- diplomatic status;
- sovereignty;
- particular territorial/jurisdictional claims.

> **Recognition Of X != Recognition Of Every Claim Made By X**

> **Recognition Of Sentience != Recognition Of Sovereignty**

> **Recognition Of A Polity != Acceptance Of Every Claimed Border**

Recognition decisions should be corrigible and provenance-preserving.

## 7. Communication before recognition

The domain should permit bounded communication where legitimate without requiring prior settlement of every political-status question.

> **Communication Interface != Treaty Recognition**

This is especially important for emergent polities, disputed entities, hostile boundaries, emergencies and first-contact-like cases.

## 8. Agreements

Agreements should preserve:
- parties;
- authorised representatives;
- scope;
- effective time;
- commitments;
- information permissions;
- responsibility allocation;
- review;
- amendment;
- expiry/termination;
- disputes;
- provenance.

> **Agreement In Function A != Agreement In Function B**

> **Agreement Document != Complete Agreement Relationship**

## 9. Peaceful distance

Peaceful distance is a legitimate relationship state.

Two civilisations may choose limited interaction, narrow interoperability or deliberate non-integration without that automatically constituting hostility or failure.

> **Absence Of Integration != Absence Of Relationship**

> **Peaceful Distance != Forced Isolation**

Where fundamental rights or cross-boundary harms create legitimate interfaces, peaceful distance does not erase those questions; it bounds the relationship rather than pretending no relationship exists.

## 10. Bounded negotiation

Functionally bounded negotiation should preserve:
- the problem being negotiated;
- participant/representative authority;
- admitted subjects;
- permitted information;
- decision scope;
- commitments;
- unresolved issues;
- expiry/termination.

> **Participation In A Bounded Negotiation != General Political Alignment**

Failed negotiation remains information rather than becoming a null event.

## 11. Optional mediation

Neutrality, competence or protocol authorship does not create mediation authority.

Mediation requires separate:
- consent/legitimate basis;
- scope;
- authority;
- accountability;
- information boundary;
- termination.

> **Ability To Mediate != Authority To Mediate**

## 12. Cross-boundary externalities

Where consequences cross boundaries, preserve separately:
- standing;
- evidence;
- causality;
- materiality;
- responsibility;
- jurisdiction;
- response authority;
- mitigation capacity;
- participation/refusal;
- case state.

> **Causation != Responsibility != Jurisdiction != Mitigation Capacity**

A party's refusal to participate neither erases the consequence nor grants another party unlimited authority over it.

## 13. Rights translation

Different civilisations may encode personhood, standing, rights and obligations differently.

Translation should seek enough functional equivalence for the interaction at hand without requiring identical internal legal philosophy.

> **Rights Translation != Forced Conceptual Uniformity**

Where no safe translation exists, the system should preserve the unresolved incompatibility rather than fabricate equivalence.

## 14. Information

Cross-civilisational information transfer should be purpose-bounded and use the current Concord information-governance architecture.

> **Permission To Receive != Permission To Redisclose**

> **Interoperability Need != Universal Data Access**

Protected information should remain protected through transfer unless a legitimate protection-state transition separately occurs.

## 15. Shared history

A shared event may produce:
- Concord's record;
- another civilisation's record;
- jointly agreed facts;
- technical telemetry;
- third-party accounts;
- later interpretations.

> **Shared Event != Single Historical Perspective**

> **Shared Factual Record != Shared Historical Interpretation**

Historical custody of Concord's record does not create authority over another civilisation's archive or narrative.

## 16. Jurisdiction and control

Keep separate:
- claimed jurisdiction;
- accepted jurisdiction;
- disputed jurisdiction;
- effective control;
- responsibility;
- liability;
- authority actually activated.

> **Effective Control != Legitimate Jurisdiction**

> **Responsibility != Jurisdiction**

> **Cross-Boundary Movement != Automatic Transfer Of Civil Identity Or Allegiance**

## 17. Security interface

Intercivilisational Relations does not absorb Defence.

It can represent:
- communication;
- status;
- agreements;
- warnings;
- negotiation;
- peaceful-distance conditions;
- bounded information exchange.

Defence retains defensive capability/security functions under its own authority architecture.

> **Diplomatic Failure != Automatic Military Authority**

## 18. Economic interface

Trade/resource interaction belongs substantively to Economy/Commerce architecture.

Intercivilisational Relations supplies the relationship, recognition, agreement and boundary context.

> **Trade Relationship != Political Subordination**

## 19. Historical interface

Historical should preserve time-indexed relationship state without converting political decisions into timeless truth.

> **Later Recognition != Earlier Recognition**

> **Later Consensus != Earlier Consensus**

> **Later Resolution != Earlier Certainty**

## 20. Current operating flow

A generic bounded flow is:

**External Entity / Civilisation Identified**
→ **Communication Standing Established Where Legitimate**
→ **Relationship / Recognition State Represented**
→ **Specific Function Identified**
→ **Authority And Representatives Verified**
→ **Wrapper / Protocol / Agreement Applied**
→ **Interaction Conducted Within Scope**
→ **Outcomes / Disputes / Externalities Recorded**
→ **Review / Correction / Continuation / Peaceful Distance / Termination**

No arrow automatically creates the next authority state.

## 21. Current genuine gaps

This operating architecture does not claim completion of:
- general diplomatic service implementation;
- treaty lifecycle machinery across all classes;
- universal rights-translation protocol;
- first-contact protocol;
- foreign representation/mission architecture;
- sanctions or comparable coercive economic measures;
- complete border/mobility architecture;
- intelligence interfaces;
- comprehensive hostile-civilisation protocol;
- empirical validation across real non-Concord civilisations.

These remain legitimate development areas.

## 22. V1.2 disposition

V1.2 contains a large development corpus, comparative wrapper cases, adversarial work and historical source-resolution material.

Do not migrate that corpus wholesale.

Recover current living boundaries and graduated mechanisms; preserve developmental provenance in V1.2.

> **Case Study != Operational Architecture**

> **A Portable Intercivilisational Mechanism != A Completed Intercivilisational Domain**

## 23. Central rule

> **Civilisations May Communicate, Cooperate, Trade, Negotiate, Recognise Limited Functions And Share Bounded Systems Without Any Of Those Acts Silently Becoming Government, Sovereignty, General Recognition Or Authority Over One Another.**
