# Mental Health Compulsory Assessment and Detention — Predetermined Authority Envelopes 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework
**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Status:** INITIAL LEGAL FRAMEWORK / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL / SPECIALIST LEGAL AND CLINICAL VALIDATION REQUIRED
**Domain:** Law
**Interfaces:** Health / Judiciary / Civil Security / Constitutional Core
**Sources:** Decision Capacity and Apparent Subjective Reality; Exceptional Mental Health Intervention; Mental Health Exceptional Authority Health-Law-Judiciary Handoff; Emergency Health; Self-Harm Protective Action Matrix; Bounded Contextual Authority; Multi-Key Authority

## 1. Purpose

This framework defines candidate predetermined legal envelopes for compulsory mental-health assessment and liberty restriction.

It deliberately avoids creating a generic power to detain a participant merely because they:
- have a mental-health diagnosis;
- appear unusual;
- refuse treatment;
- make a harmful choice;
- disagree with clinicians;
- have previously been in crisis.

> **Mental Health Status != General Detention Authority**

## 2. Why predetermined envelopes

Exceptional authority should be defined before the individual case wherever reasonably possible.

> **Precompute The Authority Rule; Verify The Current Facts**

This reduces the risk that institutions construct a justification around the participant after deciding the desired outcome.

Each envelope therefore identifies:
- triggering facts;
- protected function;
- decision-authorship state;
- evidence requirement;
- permitted acts;
- prohibited acts;
- review;
- sunset.

## 3. Separate authority objects

The following are distinct:

**A — compulsory contact / welfare verification**
**B — compulsory assessment**
**C — emergency transport**
**D — temporary assessment location restriction**
**E — continuing protective detention**
**F — public-protection detention**
**G — treatment**
**H — restraint/seclusion**

> **Authority A != B != C != D != E != F != G != H**

Some may coexist. None automatically supplies another.

## 4. Envelope LA-0 — ordinary voluntary route

### Trigger
Concern exists but no independent compulsory predicate is established.

### Function
Offer Health support, assessment, information and voluntary protective options.

### Authority
Ordinary participant consent.

### Permitted
- offer contact;
- offer assessment;
- offer transport;
- safety planning;
- participant-authorised supporter involvement.

### Prohibited by this envelope
- compulsory transport;
- detention;
- forced treatment;
- restraint;
- search/seizure;
- non-consensual disclosure except under another authority.

### Sunset
Not exceptional authority.

> **Concern Alone Routes To Offer, Not Capture**

## 5. Envelope LA-1 — bounded compulsory verification / assessment initiation

### Candidate trigger
Credible evidence creates a material question whether:
1. grave harm may be sufficiently imminent or rapidly developing; and
2. decision authorship may be materially impaired or presently unresolvable; and
3. voluntary clarification cannot reasonably be obtained; and
4. failure to clarify within the available time could expose the participant or others to grave harm.

### Function
Resolve the minimum factual/authorship uncertainty necessary to determine the correct route.

### Permitted
Only those acts separately authorised as necessary to achieve bounded verification/assessment.

### Not established by LA-1 alone
- diagnosis;
- incapacity;
- treatment authority;
- continuing detention;
- general record access;
- public-protection detention.

> **Authority To Ask The Capacity Question != Authority To Predetermine Its Answer**

## 6. Envelope LA-2 — emergency transport

### Candidate trigger
The LA-1/Health emergency question cannot be adequately resolved in place and remaining there creates material time-sensitive danger or prevents necessary emergency assessment.

### Function
Move the participant to an appropriate assessment/emergency setting.

### Required conditions
- transport materially necessary;
- less restrictive adequate route unavailable;
- destination suitable to function;
- intervention proportionate;
- current evidence recorded.

### Sunset
Arrival or material change in circumstances.

> **Transport Completion -> Authority Revalidation**

LA-2 does not itself justify detention at destination.

## 7. Envelope LA-3 — short assessment-location restriction

### Candidate trigger
At destination/current setting:
1. a material decision-authorship or immediate grave-risk question remains unresolved;
2. it cannot safely be resolved without a short location restriction;
3. C0/C1 has not been sufficiently established for the relevant decision;
4. less restrictive adequate assessment is unavailable;
5. delay/release before resolution creates a sufficiently grave and proximate risk under the eventual legal threshold.

### Function
Permit only the minimum time/location restriction necessary to resolve the defined question.

### Required properties
- exact question recorded;
- initial evidence recorded;
- participant account recorded;
- active assessment rather than passive confinement;
- defined maximum initial duration;
- repeated review;
- access to challenge/advocacy appropriate to duration.

### Sunset
The earliest of:
- C0/C1 sufficiently established and no independent authority exists;
- relevant risk predicate falls below threshold;
- assessment question resolved;
- maximum lawful period expires;
- less restrictive adequate route becomes available.

> **Assessment Detention Exists To Resolve A Question, Not To Preserve Institutional Control**

## 8. LA-3 anti-storage rule

A participant must not remain detained merely because:
- no bed is available elsewhere;
- transport is delayed;
- staffing is insufficient;
- a clinician has not completed paperwork;
- the next reviewer is unavailable;
- the institution prefers observation.

> **Administrative Availability != Liberty Authority**

Operational failures must route to Operations/resource escalation.

## 9. Envelope LA-4 — diminished-authorship protective detention

### Candidate trigger
This envelope is only potentially available where:
1. C3/C4 decision-specific authorship is sufficiently established;
2. grave harm is sufficiently serious/proximate;
3. the impairment is materially connected to the threatened harm;
4. detention is necessary to perform a legitimate protective function;
5. less restrictive adequate protection is unavailable;
6. the legal threshold for liberty restriction is met.

### Function
Protect the participant during a period in which they cannot presently author the relevant grave-harm decision, while supporting restoration where possible.

### Critical limitation

> **C3/C4 != Automatic LA-4**

Incapacity is one predicate, not the complete authority.

### Sunset
When any required predicate ceases, including restoration to C0/C1 for the relevant decision unless another independent legal authority applies.

> **Authorship Restoration -> Incapacity-Derived Detention Sunset**

## 10. Envelope LA-5 — full authorship / no independent coercive predicate

Where C0/C1 is established and the participant maintains a grave self-directed decision, incapacity-derived detention is unavailable.

The Constitutional source resolution `Continued Existence and Participant Sovereignty — No Enforced Existence Source Resolution 001.md` finds that the apparent LA-5 authority gap is not a missing generic civil power. It is the state reached when the prior contextual authority has correctly sunset and no new independent predicate exists.

### State
C0/C1 sufficiently established.

### Function
Voluntary support, dignity, information, accessible alternatives and participant-directed reconsideration.

### Coercive authority from self-harm alone
None established.

### Independent authority
Evaluate separately where another protected function exists, including protection of others or another independently derived legal obligation.

> **C0/C1 + Grave Self-Directed Harm + No Independent Authority Predicate -> No Concord Authority To Compel Continued Existence**

> **No Remaining Authority Predicate -> No Remaining Coercive Authority**

Law must not manufacture replacement authority by reclassifying the participant as C2/C3 without evidence, treating outcome severity as an authority predicate, or converting respect for life into institutional ownership of life.

## 11. Envelope LA-6 — independent third-party protection

Where a participant poses a qualifying threat to others, ordinary/public-protection Law and Civil Security may provide independent authority.

Mental-health diagnosis is not the authority source.

> **Danger To Others Must Be Evaluated As A Protective-Law Question, Not A Diagnostic Shortcut**

A participant may be C0/C1 and still be lawfully restricted under independently valid public-protection authority.

## 12. Compulsory assessment evidence threshold

Exact evidential standards remain for Law/Judiciary development.

However, the architecture requires more than:
- diagnosis;
- history;
- unusual behaviour;
- anonymous unsupported allegation;
- treatment refusal;
- clinician disagreement;
- distress alone.

Evidence should be:
- current enough for the claimed context;
- proposition-specific;
- provenance-visible;
- capable of contradiction;
- interpreted with temporal context.

> **Past Crisis != Present Compulsory Predicate**

## 13. Capacity uncertainty and precaution

C2 can create a genuine problem where irreversible harm may occur before capacity can be clarified.

Bounded precaution may justify LA-1 to LA-3 where the legal predicates are satisfied.

But uncertainty cannot be made permanent.

> **Unresolved Capacity Can Justify Time To Resolve Capacity; It Cannot Justify Indefinite Unresolved Capacity**

## 14. Least restrictive adequate route

Before compulsory restriction, consider whether the protective function can be achieved through:
- supported decision-making;
- participant-chosen supporter;
- voluntary safe location;
- home/community assessment;
- temporary environmental change;
- participant-authored crisis plan;
- communication support;
- rapid follow-up;
- other adequate lower-intrusion routes.

> **Less Restrictive != Less Protective If It Adequately Performs The Function**

## 15. Decision-authorship independence

The assessor must be capable of finding C0/C1.

The legal reviewer must be capable of finding that detention authority is absent.

The participant need not adopt the clinician's preferred decision to demonstrate capacity.

> **Agreement Is Not The Exit Test**

## 16. Treatment separation

No LA envelope in this document automatically authorises treatment.

Treatment requires:
- consent; or
- independently established emergency/substitute/legal treatment authority.

> **Detained != Consented**

## 17. Restraint separation

Detention does not automatically permit physical restraint, chemical restraint or seclusion.

Each requires its own immediate function, necessity and proportionality.

> **Authority To Prevent Exit != Authority To Restrain For Convenience**

## 18. Information separation

Compulsory assessment does not create general access to the participant's Health record, communications, devices or private spaces.

Access must be function-specific and independently authorised.

> **Need To Assess != General Investigatory Authority**

## 19. Duration architecture

Exact times require later legal design.

The structural rule is:

**Greater Intrusion + Longer Duration -> Stronger Evidence + Stronger Review + Stronger Independence**

Initial emergency/assessment windows should be short and purpose-bound.

Renewal must require current predicates rather than historical activation.

> **Renewal != Administrative Continuation**

## 20. Burden of continuing authority

Once the state restricts liberty, the institution asserting continuing authority should retain the burden of showing the current predicates for that authority.

The participant should not have to prove normality or harmlessness to escape an expired authority.

> **Authority Holder Must Justify Continuing Authority**

## 21. Review events

Mandatory review should be triggered by:
- arrival after compulsory transport;
- initial assessment completion;
- C0/C1 finding;
- material capacity change;
- material risk change;
- participant challenge;
- proposed renewal;
- proposed change of authority purpose;
- restraint/seclusion use;
- proposed transfer;
- maximum interval expiry.

## 22. Authority-purpose change

If detention begins for assessment but later becomes public protection or another function, the authority cannot silently mutate.

> **Purpose Change -> New Authority Verification**

The participant must be able to know the current basis.

## 23. Multiple simultaneous authorities

A participant may simultaneously be subject to:
- a Health emergency authority;
- a Law detention authority;
- a public-protection authority.

These must remain separately recorded.

If one ends, the others do not automatically end — but neither may they inherit the expired authority's scope.

> **Concurrent Authorities != Merged Authority**

## 24. Judicial interface

Judiciary should be able to test:
1. which envelope is claimed;
2. whether its predicates currently exist;
3. whether evidence supports each predicate;
4. whether contrary evidence was preserved;
5. whether C0-C4 was assessed without circularity;
6. whether less restrictive adequate routes exist;
7. whether the intervention stayed within permitted acts;
8. whether the authority has sunset;
9. whether the institution changed purpose without reauthorisation.

## 25. Failure modes

### Diagnostic detention
Diagnosis treated as authority.

### Capacity laundering
Disagreement converted into incapacity.

### Risk laundering
Possibility of harm described as sufficient imminence.

### Administrative detention
Operational scarcity preserves restriction.

### Treatment detention
Participant held primarily to force treatment without independent treatment authority.

### Authority inheritance
Transport/assessment/emergency authority silently becomes continuing detention.

### Purpose drift
Assessment becomes containment without new authority.

### Historical capture
Past crisis becomes permanent present risk status.

## 27. Result

Compulsory mental-health intervention can be represented as a set of narrow, predetermined authority envelopes rather than a single status-based detention power.

The key boundary is:

**Uncertainty may justify bounded inquiry.
Diminished authorship may contribute to bounded protective authority.
Full authorship removes incapacity as that authority source.
Independent threats to others route to independent public-protection law.**

LA-5 remains deliberately unresolved rather than allowing Health or Law operations to invent a competent-self-harm detention power by practice.

### Cumulative-duration safeguard

Nominally short repeated orders must be evaluated by their cumulative effect:

**Increasing Cumulative Restriction
-> Increasing Review Independence
-> Increasing Evidential Burden
-> Increasing Need To Re-establish Function
-> Increasing Representation/Remedy Safeguards**

### Authority provenance object

Each exceptional restriction should carry:

**<Participant, Current Envelope, Protected Function, Predicate Evidence, Decision-Authorship State, Permitted Acts, Start, Review Due, Sunset, Authority Source, Responsible Actor, Independent Reviewer/Route>**

Authority changes create a new provenance event rather than silently overwriting the prior state.

## 29. LA-5 non-assistance boundary

LA-5's absence of coercive authority does not create assisted-death authority.

For C0/C1:

**No incapacity-derived detention authority
!= authority/duty to facilitate self-directed death.**

Law must preserve:

> **Respect != Endorsement**

> **Non-Coercion != Facilitation**

> **Authority To Refuse Intervention != Authority To Require A Lethal Intervention**

Voluntary care, relief of suffering, information, alternatives and participant-directed support remain available. Refusal to provide lethal facilitation must not be converted into abandonment, punishment or withdrawal of otherwise legitimate care.

Any future proposal for third-party assistance in death would require a separate constitutional and legal derivation; it is not generated by LA-5.
