# Compact Basic Law Coverage Audit — Protected Interests, Compositional Offences and Remaining Derivation Gaps 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Domain:** Law  
**Date:** 9 October 2026  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / COVERAGE AUDIT / NON-CANONICAL  
**Method:** Ethical derivation + protected-interest analysis + compositional legal classification

---

## 1. Purpose

The Law domain has now derived a substantial first set of base legal architectures.

This audit asks:

> **Does Concord still need a conventional catalogue of named offences, or can most ordinary wrongdoing be represented by a compact set of ethically derived protected interests plus reusable classifiers?**

The audit distinguishes:

1. **BASE PROTECTED INTEREST** — genuinely distinct legal interest requiring its own derivation;
2. **SPECIAL IMPLEMENTATION** — important specialised treatment of an existing base interest;
3. **COMPOSITION** — familiar offence/state produced by combining existing base laws;
4. **PROCEDURAL / INSTITUTIONAL OFFENCE** — protects operation of a legitimate civil function rather than a new participant-level interest;
5. **REGULATORY / DOMAIN-SPECIFIC RULE** — specialised safety/coordination implementation;
6. **REMAINING DERIVATION GAP** — protected relation not yet sufficiently represented.

The design goal is:

> **Minimum Sufficient Law, Not Minimum Possible Law.**

Compactness is valuable only where it preserves legal legibility, fair notice and complete protection.

## 2. Existing general classifier layer

The following are not base offences. They are reusable legal classifiers/architectures:

- mental state / culpability;
- attempt and completion;
- participation and complicity;
- consent and withdrawal;
- aggravation and mitigation;
- capacity/authorship;
- justification/BCA;
- sentencing/consequence;
- pattern-level conduct;
- causation;
- risk/endangerment;
- contextual wrappers.

These allow factual variation without creating near-duplicate laws.

> **Classifier != Protected Interest.**

## 5. Compositional test

A familiar named offence should not become a new base law merely because history gives it a distinct name.

Candidate test:

1. What protected interest(s) are violated?
2. Can existing base laws represent each violation?
3. Do existing classifiers represent intent/result/context?
4. Is there any legally material remainder?
5. If not, classify as composition or specialised implementation.

> **Named Historical Offence != Necessarily Fundamental Legal Category.**

## 31. Escape from custody

## 35. Discrimination

This exposes a genuine potential gap.

BL5 Fairness and CR-BL04 Fair Comparable Treatment already exist.

Existing offences can address some discriminatory acts when they involve:
- property;
- employment contract;
- coercion;
- falsehood;
- harassment;
- authority abuse.

But **unjustified unequal treatment in consequential civil systems** may itself violate a protected relation even without those additional harms.

Potential protected interest:

**Fair Comparable Treatment / Protection Against Illegitimate Discriminatory Exclusion**

Classification:

**REMAINING DERIVATION GAP / HIGH PRIORITY**

Must preserve:
- legitimate distinctions;
- competence requirements;
- contextual eligibility;
- private association/autonomy;

## 52. Remaining genuinely distinct gaps

The audit identifies the following strongest remaining candidates.

### GAP-A — Integrity Of Entrusted Authority / Fiduciary Function

Covers:
- corruption;
- bribery;
- abuse of office;
- misuse of entrusted capability;
- some fiduciary exploitation/conflicts.

Core question:

**What duties arise when a participant accepts authority or stewardship over interests they do not own?**

---

### GAP-B — Fair Comparable Treatment / Illegitimate Discriminatory Exclusion

Covers:
- unjustified discrimination;
- unequal access by protected/irrelevant characteristic;
- discriminatory official/service decisions.

Core challenge:
distinguish unlawful discrimination from legitimate differentiation, competence, eligibility and private autonomy.

---

### GAP-C — Positive Duties And Culpable Omissions

Not itself a protected interest but necessary legal grammar.

Covers:
- duty of care;
- custodial duty;
- parental/developmental duty;
- professional duty;
- duty created by causing danger;
- infrastructure/operator duty;
- rescue where duty exists.

Core rule already suggested:

**Capability To Help != Automatic Legal Duty To Act.**

Need architecture for how duties arise, scope, transfer and terminate.

---

### GAP-D — Integrity Of Legitimate Civil / Judicial Process

Covers:
- perjury;
- evidence fabrication;
- obstruction;
- corrupt records;
- interference with lawful adjudication;
- some valid-order violations.

This may be better derived after Judiciary procedure is developed, because:

> **No Legitimate Process → No Legitimate Process-Protection Offence.**

---

### GAP-E — Preparatory Participation

Covers:
- solicitation;
- incitement;
- conspiracy;
- agreement;
- recruitment;
- preparation by multiple actors.

Likely extension of participation/attempt/endangerment rather than a base interest.

---

### GAP-F — Commons / Ecological Integrity

BL8 and SD-BL01 already supply the ethical root.

Need to decide whether:
- Environment-domain rules are sufficient; or
- a general legal protected interest in commons/ecological integrity should be explicit.

This matters because ecological harm may be legally real before identifiable participant injury.

## 54. Architectural finding

The compact-code hypothesis is presently supported.

A large range of familiar offences can already be represented without creating new fundamental ethical categories.

Examples:

**Robbery**  
= Property + Coercion/Threat + possible Physical Harm

**Kidnapping For Ransom**  
= Restraint + Extortion

**Burglary**  
= Protected-Space Intrusion + Intent/Attempt + possible Property Harm

**Trafficking**  
= Restraint + Coercion/Deception + Forced Labour/Self-Ownership + possible Sexual Interference

**Identity Theft**  
= Fraud + Privacy + Property + False Attribution

**Arson**  
= Property Damage + Endangerment + possible Physical Harm/H5

**Evidence Fabrication Against A Person**  
= False Factual Attribution + Process Integrity

This suggests:

> **Many Traditional Offence Names Are Useful Human-Facing Shorthands For Compositions, Not Fundamental Legal Building Blocks.**

## 55. Warning against over-compression

Compactness must not become opacity.

A citizen should not need to solve an abstract legal ontology to know that robbery, stalking or unlawful detention are prohibited.

Therefore a mature Concord code may contain:

### Layer 1 — Fundamental derived law
Compact protected interests and general classifiers.

### Layer 2 — Recognised compositions / named applications
Human-legible mappings such as robbery, burglary, kidnapping, trafficking.

### Layer 3 — Domain/regulatory law
Transport, medicine, environment, infrastructure, commerce, etc.

### Layer 4 — Procedure
Investigation, prosecution, adjudication, remedy, sentencing, appeal.

> **Compact Architecture != Inaccessible Law.**

Named offence labels can remain as indexes/templates without becoming independent ethical roots.

---

## 56. Relationship to legal certainty

The law must provide fair notice.

A purely generative system that invents prohibitions after conduct occurs would be unacceptable.

Therefore:

> **Ethical Derivability != Retroactive Criminalisation.**

Ethical derivation determines legitimacy.

Promulgated law determines enforceable notice.

Newly recognised applications should be reviewed for whether they were reasonably encompassed by existing published rules before punitive liability attaches.

This strongly supports a later legal promulgation/versioning architecture.

---

## 57. Relationship to BL10

BL10 — Remain Open to Correction — means the code must be corrigible.

But:

> **Corrigibility Of Law != Retroactive Expansion Of Liability.**

Correction can:
- amend future law;
- correct invalid rules;
- expand remedies where legitimately available;
- identify gaps.

It must not silently rewrite the legal state that governed past conduct.

Historical must preserve versioned law.

---

## 58. Recommended next derivation order

Based on distinctness and architectural leverage:

1. **Positive Duties and Culpable Omissions** — general grammar needed by Health, custody, child/dependent protection, infrastructure, rescue and negligence.
2. **Entrusted Authority and Fiduciary Integrity** — closes corruption/abuse/bribery gap and links directly to Governance.
3. **Fair Comparable Treatment / Discrimination** — directly derives BL5 into operational law.
4. **Commons / Ecological Integrity** — operationalises BL8 where no individual victim is yet identifiable.
5. **Preparatory Participation** — solicitation/conspiracy/incitement.
6. **Institutional Process Integrity** — best developed alongside Judiciary procedure.

The first is not another offence catalogue entry. It is missing **legal grammar**.

---

## 59. Conclusion

The Law-domain development has reached an important transition.

The initial question was whether basic law could be derived directly from the Ethical Kernel.

The current evidence supports a stronger proposition:

> **A relatively small set of ethically derived protected interests, combined with reusable factual and responsibility classifiers, can represent a very large portion of ordinary substantive law without proliferating mechanism-specific offences.**

The remaining work should therefore prioritise missing legal relations and grammar rather than historical offence names.

Immediate next target:

# **Positive Duties and Culpable Omissions — Creation, Scope, Breach and Termination of Legal Duties**

because omission liability cannot be safely derived from capability alone and is already required by multiple existing base laws.
