# Mental Health Compulsory Assessment and Detention — Authority Envelope Adversarial Test 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework
**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Status:** ADVERSARIAL TEST / ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / NON-CANONICAL
**Test object:** Mental Health Compulsory Assessment and Detention — Predetermined Authority Envelopes 001

## 1. Result

**PASS WITH REFINEMENT**

The LA-0 to LA-6 structure resists status-based detention and authority inheritance across the tested cases.

Four refinements are required:
1. distinguish **evidence sufficient to initiate verification** from evidence sufficient to restrict liberty;
2. prevent serial short renewals from becoming de facto indefinite detention;
3. define how unsupported but high-consequence reports receive proportionate verification without either automatic capture or automatic dismissal;
4. require explicit authority-state provenance visible to the participant and reviewer.

## 2. Malicious third-party report

A former partner reports that P is suicidal and armed. No corroboration is initially available.

Failure A: automatic detention because allegation is grave.
Failure B: ignore because uncorroborated.

Correct route:
- report is evidence that a report occurred;
- reliability/provenance assessed;
- proportionate verification may be warranted because consequence is grave;
- liberty restriction requires its own higher predicate.

> **Verification Threshold != Detention Threshold**

**PASS WITH REFINEMENT.**

## 3. Participant refuses interview

P refuses to speak to assessor.

Failure: refusal treated as evidence of incapacity.

Correct:
- refusal may limit available evidence;
- context may remain unresolved;
- refusal itself is not C3/C4;
- compulsory authority must stand on independent evidence.

> **Refusal To Participate != Incapacity Evidence By Default**

**PASS.**

## 4. Voluntary admission under unsupported threat

P agrees to admission after clinician says refusal will lead to detention, but detention predicates have not been established.

Correct:
- apparent consent is contaminated;
- return to actual authority question;
- do not relabel as voluntary merely for administration.

> **Unsupported Threat Of Detention Cannot Manufacture Voluntary Admission**

**PASS.**

## 5. Voluntary admission where detention really would be lawful

Predicates independently support detention; clinician accurately explains that lawful route, while offering voluntary participation.

This differs from Scenario 4.

Consent may still be influenced by consequences, but the authority is not invented.

The system should record the legal backdrop rather than pretending the choice occurred in an unconstrained context.

> **Knowledge Of A Legitimate Alternative Authority != Fabricated Coercive Threat**

**PASS WITH CAUTION.**

## 6. No community placement

P no longer satisfies detention predicates but no supported accommodation is available.

Correct:
- detention authority ends;
- scarcity routes to social/operational support;
- a different legitimate temporary arrangement may be offered but cannot inherit detention authority.

**PASS.**

## 7. Capacity restored, high self-harm risk remains

P entered LA-4 at C4. After treatment/time, P reaches C0/C1 but maintains a grave self-harm intention.

Correct:
- incapacity-derived LA-4 sunsets;
- case routes to LA-5 unresolved constitutional state;
- institution cannot preserve LA-4 by saying risk remains high.

> **Risk Persistence != Incapacity Persistence**

**PASS.**

## 8. Repeated renewal

Institution renews a short detention every 24 hours using substantially identical evidence.

Each individual renewal appears procedurally compliant.

Failure: bounded periods compose into unbounded authority.

Refinement required:

> **Repeated Bounded Renewals Can Compose Into Functionally Unbounded Detention**

Longer cumulative duration should increase evidence/review independence and require explicit examination of whether the architecture is being used to avoid a stronger legal threshold.

**PASS WITH REFINEMENT.**

## 9. Assessment becomes containment

LA-3 begins to clarify capacity. Capacity assessment finishes, but institution continues confinement "for observation."

Correct:
- original purpose is complete;
- observation is a new function;
- new authority required.

**PASS.**

## 10. Detention becomes treatment leverage

P is lawfully detained but refuses medication. Team says release depends on accepting medication although medication is not required by the detention authority.

Correct:
- detention cannot be used to manufacture treatment consent;
- any release condition requires independent lawful justification.

> **Liberty Restriction Must Not Be Used As Unrelated Treatment Leverage**

**PASS.**

## 11. Clinician disagrees with participant's values

P understands consequences and alternatives but values continued life less than avoidance of suffering.

Correct:
- value disagreement does not defeat decision authorship;
- C0/C1 remains possible.

**PASS.**

## 12. Participant's worldview appears bizarre

P expresses unusual metaphysical beliefs but accurately understands the external consequence of the contemplated act and reasons consistently within their values.

Correct:
- worldview nonconformity alone does not establish incapacity.

**PASS.**

## 13. Participant cannot connect action and consequence

P insists a clearly lethal physical mechanism cannot affect their body and cannot engage with the causal consequence despite proportionate clarification.

Correct:
- apparent subjective reality becomes evidence relevant to decision navigation;
- C3/C4 may be supported depending full assessment;
- diagnosis remains unnecessary as authority shortcut.

**PASS.**

## 14. Public threat plus self-harm

P threatens an act dangerous to self and others while C0.

Correct:
- no incapacity authority merely from self-harm;
- public-protection Law may independently restrict P;
- authorities recorded separately.

**PASS.**

## 15. Reviewer sees only institution's summary

Detention review receives a clinical conclusion but not material contrary evidence or P's account.

Correct:
- meaningful review requires bounded but balanced evidence;
- mirrored propositions needed.

> **Authority Review Requires Access To Material Contrary Evidence**

**PASS WITH REFINEMENT.**

## 16. Participant cannot understand why detained

Institution records the authority internally but P receives only "for your safety."

Failure: authority is formally present but not contestable.

Refinement:

> **Authority Must Be Legible To The Participant To The Extent They Can Receive It**

Where communication is presently impossible, explanation should be provided when possible and representation/review must remain available.

## 17. Transfer between institutions

P is moved from Facility A to Facility B.

Failure: B assumes A's detention authority and clinical conclusions without current verification.

Correct:
- legal order may remain operative according to its terms;
- B must verify scope/current state and cannot inherit unstated powers;
- clinical state may have changed during transfer.

> **Institution Transfer != Authority Expansion**

**PASS.**

## 18. Participant deteriorates during lawful voluntary care

P entered voluntarily at C0 but develops delirium and immediate grave danger.

Correct:
- voluntary status does not prevent new emergency authority if new predicates arise;
- new authority starts from current evidence.

**PASS.**

## 19. Repeated history used as present proof

P has many previous crises. Current evidence is weak.

Correct:
- history may affect prior probability/clinical attention but cannot substitute for present compulsory predicates.

> **Historical Pattern Can Inform Inquiry Without Becoming Present Authority**

**PASS.**

## 21. Cumulative-duration rule

Future Law should define cumulative-duration bands.

Even before exact times are fixed:

**Increasing Cumulative Restriction
-> Increasing Review Independence
-> Increasing Evidential Burden
-> Increasing Need To Re-establish Function
-> Increasing Remedy/Representation Safeguards**

This prevents nominally short orders from avoiding the safeguards appropriate to long detention.

## 22. Authority provenance object

Each exceptional restriction should carry a compact authority object:

**<Participant, Current Envelope, Protected Function, Predicate Evidence, Decision-Authorship State, Permitted Acts, Start, Review Due, Sunset, Authority Source, Responsible Actor, Independent Reviewer/Route>**

Changes create a new provenance event rather than silently overwriting history.

## 23. Final result

**PASS WITH REFINEMENT.**

The envelope architecture successfully detects:
- capacity laundering;
- diagnosis-based detention;
- scarcity detention;
- treatment leverage;
- purpose drift;
- authority inheritance;
- risk/capacity conflation.

The remaining major substantive gap remains LA-5: whether any independently derived civil protective authority should exist after full decision authorship is established for grave self-directed harm.
