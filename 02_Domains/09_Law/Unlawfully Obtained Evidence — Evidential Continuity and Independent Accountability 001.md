# Unlawfully Obtained Evidence — Evidential Continuity and Independent Accountability 001

**Project:** The Concord  
**Domain:** Law / Civil Security / Judiciary  
**Date:** 2 October 2026  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / CANDIDATE EVIDENCE PRINCIPLE / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL

## 1. Purpose

This note resolves the first major downstream gap identified by the initial Policing Lifecycle stress test: what happens when relevant evidence is discovered or obtained through an illegal or unlawful act.

The candidate answer separates the evidential question from the authority/accountability question.

> **Evidence remains evidence. Misconduct remains misconduct.**

The truth-finding process should not pretend relevant evidence does not exist merely to punish the way it was obtained. Equally, evidential usefulness must never retrospectively justify unlawful acquisition.

## 2. Evidential continuity

Candidate primary principle:

> **Evidence does not cease to be evidence merely because the act through which it was discovered or obtained was illegal or unlawful.**

Therefore:

**Unlawful Acquisition != Non-Evidence**

**Illegal Source Act != Automatic Evidential Exclusion**

**Acquisition Unlawful != Evidence False**

**Evidence Useful != Acquisition Lawful**

Relevant evidence remains capable of assessment for authenticity, reliability, integrity, provenance, corroboration, contradiction and evidential weight.

## 3. Burglar discovery test

A burglar unlawfully enters a protected home and discovers evidence of a substantially more serious crime.

The burglary remains independently subject to Law.

The discovered evidence does not cease to exist because its source encountered it while committing an offence.

**Burglar's Unlawful Entry != Nullification Of Observed Evidence**

The information may legitimately trigger police investigation if the applicable threshold is met.

But:

**Burglar's Unlawful Entry != Police Authority To Repeat The Entry**

Police must establish their own lawful authority for any subsequent search, access, apprehension or other consequential interference.

An unlawful actor cannot transfer authority they never possessed.

## 4. Source status and evidential value

The moral or legal standing of a source should not itself determine truth.

Assessment may instead consider relevant properties including:
- directness;
- authenticity;
- reliability;
- integrity;
- provenance;
- opportunity to observe;
- incentive to deceive;
- contamination;
- coercion;
- alteration;
- corroboration;
- contradiction;
- acquisition conditions where those conditions affect reliability.

A criminal source may provide reliable evidence.

An institutional source may provide unreliable evidence.

**Source Status != Evidential Truth**

**Institutional Status != Evidential Reliability**

A source's circumstances may legitimately affect credibility where they bear upon reliability, but this is not a general moral ranking of persons.

## 5. Acquisition lawfulness is a separate dimension

Evidence should preserve provenance concerning how it was obtained.

Candidate acquisition states may include:
- LAWFUL;
- LAWFULNESS DISPUTED;
- UNLAWFUL;
- ACQUISITION STATE UNRESOLVED.

These states do not themselves determine truth or reliability.

**Evidential Weight != Acquisition Lawfulness**

However, an acquisition method can affect reliability where there is a causal connection.

For example, coercive acquisition may undermine the reliability of a statement, while an unlawfully accessed but independently authenticated record may remain highly reliable.

Therefore:

**Unlawful Acquisition != Automatically Low Evidential Reliability**

**Acquisition Method May Affect Reliability Where Relevant**

## 6. No automatic inadmissibility merely as punishment

Candidate Concord principle:

> **Unlawful acquisition alone should not make otherwise relevant evidence inadmissible merely as an indirect punishment for acquisition misconduct.**

Evidence can still fail ordinary evidential requirements because it is irrelevant, unreliable, inauthentic, contaminated, misleading or lacks sufficient provenance.

Those are evidential defects.

They should not be manufactured solely because a separate authority violation occurred.

## 7. Two-track architecture

### Evidence track

**Evidence -> Authenticity -> Reliability -> Integrity -> Provenance -> Corroboration / Contradiction -> Evidential Weight -> Truth-Finding Use**

### Authority track

**Acquisition Act -> Claimed Authority -> BCA Envelope -> Lawfulness Review -> Culpability -> Individual / Supervisory / Institutional / System Accountability -> Remedy / Consequence**

The tracks remain linked through provenance but logically distinct.

**Evidence Useful != Acquisition Lawful**

**Acquisition Unlawful != Evidence False**

## 8. Audit as the primary anti-abuse mechanism

The Concord policing architecture already requires examination of public-authority conduct alongside participant conduct.

Unlawful evidence acquisition should therefore be discoverable through ordinary authority provenance and audit, rather than depending entirely upon the accused discovering the violation.

Relevant records should permit legitimate reconstruction of:
- actor;
- acquisition/access event;
- authority claimed;
- authority source;
- purpose;
- information reasonably available at the time;
- permitted scope;
- actual action;
- timing;
- authority changes;
- sunset;
- onward evidence handling.

Where unlawful acquisition occurred, review should examine individual conduct and, where relevant, supervision, procedure, incentives, technology, policy and institutional conditions.

## 9. Direct accountability rather than evidential erasure

Candidate principle:

> **Correct or punish the unlawful acquisition directly rather than attempting to punish it by making relevant evidence disappear.**

Depending upon future Law and proportionality, consequences might include:
- correction;
- retraining;
- authority restriction;
- professional consequences;
- participant remedy;
- compensation;
- disciplinary liability;
- civil liability;
- criminal liability for sufficiently serious misconduct;
- supervisory consequences;
- institutional correction;
- system redesign;
- enhanced independent oversight.

Exact consequences remain undeveloped.

## 10. Culpability of authority misconduct

Not every unlawful authority exercise has the same culpability.

Candidate continuum:

**Error -> Negligence -> Recklessness -> Deliberate Misconduct -> Deliberate/Systematic Abuse Of Authority**

This is a culpability dimension, not an automatic sentencing scale.

The seriousness of the underlying interference, harm, role, circumstances and other legal factors remain independently relevant.

### Error
An actor makes a good-faith error despite acting with the care reasonably required.

The act may still require correction or remedy even where personal culpability is low or absent.

### Negligence
The actor fails to exercise the care reasonably required in establishing or remaining within the authority envelope.

### Recklessness
The actor consciously disregards a substantial or otherwise legally significant risk that the authority does not exist or that the action exceeds it.

### Deliberate misconduct
The actor knows the legitimate boundary and intentionally crosses it.

This is more serious than merely committing the same unlawful act through mistake.

**Misconduct != Deliberate Misconduct**

**Knowing Boundary Violation -> Increased Culpability**

### Deliberate/systematic abuse
Repeated, coordinated, policy-supported or institutionally tolerated deliberate violations may indicate a problem beyond the individual incident.

**Repeated Deliberate Misconduct -> Pattern Evidence -> Supervisory / Institutional / System Review**

A pattern does not automatically establish the culpability of every associated individual, but it creates a legitimate object of investigation.

## 11. Evidential usefulness cannot retrospectively create authority

A successful unlawful search cannot justify itself through its result.

**Useful Result != Retroactive BCA**

**Successful Unlawful Acquisition != Lawful Acquisition**

**Evidence Of Serious Crime != Automatic Excuse For Acquisition Misconduct**

Particularly serious is the reasoning:

> I know I lack authority, but I will obtain the evidence anyway because it can still be used.

Where established, this is evidence of deliberate authority misconduct rather than an argument for excluding the evidence.

The truth discovered remains available to truth-finding.

The deliberate violation receives its own legal consequence.

## 12. No institutional profit from authority drift

Evidence remaining usable must not silently expand police powers.

Therefore:

**Unlawful Success != Precedent For Future Authority**

**Evidence Discovery != Ratification Of Acquisition Method**

**Repeated Useful Results != Expansion Of Police Power**

Operational policy may not treat previous unlawful success as a legitimate authority source.

Recurring violations should increase scrutiny of:
- supervision;
- training;
- incentives;
- policy;
- performance metrics;
- legal guidance;
- review effectiveness;
- institutional culture;
- deliberate circumvention.

## 13. Independent review

Because the evidence-gathering institution may operationally benefit from what it discovered, serious or credible acquisition violations cannot be finally resolved solely through self-investigation.

**Internal Review != Independent Review**

**Self-Investigation != Sufficient Final Accountability**

Independent review itself remains bounded. It receives the information necessary for the review function; it does not acquire universal access.

## 14. Judiciary

Judiciary may need to answer two simultaneous questions:

### Evidential question
What does the evidence reliably establish?

### Authority question
Was the evidence obtained through legitimate authority?

A coherent result may therefore be:

**Evidence Authentic And Highly Probative + Acquisition Unlawful**

There is no contradiction.

The Judiciary can recognise the evidence while separately recognising the unlawful interference and applying whatever remedy or consequence Law provides.

## 15. Interaction with participant conduct

The architecture must remain symmetrical.

A participant who commits one offence while discovering evidence of another offence remains accountable for their own act.

A police officer who commits an authority offence while discovering evidence of another offence likewise remains accountable for their act.

Neither wrong erases the other truth.

**Offence A != Erasure Of Evidence Of Offence B**

**Evidence Of Offence B != Justification Of Offence A**

## 17. Resolution

The initial unlawful-evidence gap is provisionally resolved as:

**Evidence Discovered**  
-> **Preserve Evidence + Preserve Acquisition Provenance**  
-> **Assess Evidence On Evidential Merits**  
+ **Assess Acquisition On Legal/BCA Merits**  
-> **Evidence Remains Available To Truth-Finding Where Otherwise Evidentially Valid**  
+ **Unlawful Acquisition Receives Direct Accountability / Remedy / Consequence**  
-> **Deliberateness Increases Culpability Where Established**  
-> **Repeated Misconduct Triggers Pattern / Institutional Review**  
-> **Unlawful Method Does Not Become Authority Or Precedent**

In compact form:

> **Truth is not punished for misconduct. Misconduct is not excused by discovering truth.**
