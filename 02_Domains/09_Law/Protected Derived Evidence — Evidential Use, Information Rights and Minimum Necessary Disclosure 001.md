# Protected Derived Evidence — Evidential Use, Information Rights and Minimum Necessary Disclosure 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Date:** 6 October 2026  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / LAW-JUDICIARY COMPANION / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL  
**Interfaces:** Unlawfully Obtained Evidence; Compelled Participant Evidence; Relational Information Rights; Derived Information Governance; CIBB; PLE; Reality Trees

## 1. Purpose

Evidence may be relevant and reliable while also being protected information.

The Law domain therefore needs to separate:

1. whether an informational object is evidence;
2. whether it was lawfully obtained;
3. whether it may be used for the legal function;
4. how much protected information must be disclosed to perform that function.

## 2. Core distinction

Existing Law already establishes:

> **Evidence remains evidence. Misconduct remains misconduct.**

This companion adds:

> **Evidence Status != Unlimited Disclosure Authority**

and:

> **Protection Status != Automatic Evidential Exclusion**

## 3. Derived evidence

A derivative may include:
- bounded medical result;
- model output;
- statistical inference;
- transformed digital record;
- aggregate reconstruction;
- AI-generated analysis;
- relational information.

Its derivation should remain provenance-visible enough to assess authenticity, reliability, uncertainty and challenge.

## 4. Minimum necessary evidential disclosure

Where the legal question can be answered using a bounded projection, do not disclose the entire protected source merely because the source exists.

Possible topology:

`Protected Source`
-> `Legitimate Evidential Query`
-> `Bounded Evidential Projection`
-> `Judicial Evaluation`

If challenge requires deeper inspection, access can expand through separately justified procedure.

## 5. Adversarial fairness

Minimum disclosure must not prevent a participant from meaningfully challenging consequential evidence.

Therefore:

> **Privacy Minimisation != Authority To Hide The Basis Of Accusation**

Where source protection and fair challenge conflict, the judiciary must resolve the actual competing rights/functions rather than silently sacrificing either.

Possible mechanisms include:
- protected judicial inspection;
- independent expert review;
- bounded disclosure;
- redaction;
- summary plus challenge route;
- staged access.

Exact procedure remains Law development.

## 6. Relational evidence

Evidence may concern several participants.

One subject's privacy does not create universal veto over another participant's legitimate defence, report or claim.

Conversely, legal relevance does not make unrelated intimate material automatically public.

> **Legal Relevance != General Publication**

## 7. Derived inference

A model/inference remains an inference.

Its evidential use should preserve:
- input classes;
- transformation/model;
- uncertainty;
- validation;
- known limitations;
- alternative explanations;
- contest history.

> **Derived Confidence != Adjudicated Fact**

Mirrored Reality Trees can place derived evidence on both inculpatory and exculpatory structures according to actual effect.

## 8. Independently discoverable evidence

A protected source may lead investigators to evidence independently obtainable through lawful authority.

The later evidence should preserve enough provenance to determine:
- whether it is genuinely independent;
- whether source misconduct affected reliability;
- whether independent authority existed.

Do not manufacture independence merely by adding an intermediate step.

## 9. Public source reconstruction

Public facts can combine into protected or highly consequential derived evidence.

Public availability of inputs does not eliminate:
- inference uncertainty;
- legal thresholds;
- privacy interests;
- authority requirements.

## 10. Court record transition

Information legitimately used in a case does not automatically become unrestricted public information.

Court/public-record status is a separate protection-state transition requiring applicable Law authority.

> **Admitted As Evidence != Automatically Public Without Limit**

## 11. Correction

If a source or derivative is corrected:

`Correction -> Candidate Review Of Dependent Evidential Conclusions`

Do not automatically invalidate an outcome where independent evidence still supports it.

## 12. Central rule

> **The Judiciary May Need Access To Protected Truth Without Converting Every Protected Source Into An Unrestricted Public Object. Evidential Relevance, Acquisition Authority, Disclosure Scope And Adjudicative Weight Must Remain Distinct.**
