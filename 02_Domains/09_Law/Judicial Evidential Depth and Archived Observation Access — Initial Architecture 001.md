# Judicial Evidential Depth and Archived Observation Access — Initial Architecture 001

**Project:** The Concord  
**Domain:** Law / Civil Security / Judiciary / Historical  
**Date:** 2 October 2026  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / EVIDENTIAL ACCESS ARCHITECTURE / PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL

## 1. Purpose

This note develops the evidential-access transition that occurs as a matter moves from ordinary Civil Security investigation through prosecution into independent adjudication.

The Concord is expected to generate and preserve substantial contemporaneous operational evidence, including near-continuous police body-worn video, vehicle/dash camera footage, police-vehicle external cameras, relevant CCTV and other legitimate sensor/system records.

The existence and preservation of evidence must remain distinct from authority to inspect or use it.

**Evidence Exists != General Access Authority**

**Evidence Preserved != Evidence Disclosed To Everyone**

**Technical Availability != Legitimate Evidential Access**

The central principle is:

> **As a case moves into prosecution and adjudication, the legitimate evidential inquiry may deepen and widen, but access expands only through a new purpose-bounded authority envelope serving independent truth-finding.**

---

## 2. Routine contemporaneous recording

Subject to later detailed operational and privacy rules, Civil Security should ordinarily operate with extensive contemporaneous recording of consequential public-authority activity.

Candidate sources include:

- police body-worn cameras;
- vehicle dash cameras;
- external/on-vehicle cameras;
- interview-room recording;
- custody-area recording;
- police-facility CCTV where appropriate;
- public-space CCTV lawfully available to the system;
- dispatch and incident communications;
- system alerts and relevant operational logs;
- other legitimate contemporaneous sensor records.

The intended function is not merely evidence against participants.

The same records may establish:

- participant conduct;
- participant innocence;
- another responsible actor;
- officer conduct;
- lawful police intervention;
- unlawful police intervention;
- system failure;
- witness reliability;
- chronology;
- environmental context;
- evidence provenance.

Therefore:

**Civil Recording != Prosecution-Owned Evidence**

**Police-Created Record != Evidence Only Against Participant**

---

## 3. Near-continuous recording and evidential integrity

Where practicable, recording should be sufficiently continuous that consequential events are not represented only by manually selected fragments.

The architecture should eventually specify:

- activation expectations;
- justified interruption;
- equipment failure;
- retention;
- integrity verification;
- timestamps;
- device identity;
- chain/provenance;
- editing/derivative copies;
- access logging;
- deletion/retention rules;
- privacy masking and bounded projections.

A missing recording is not automatically proof of misconduct.

But an unexplained or improper interruption may itself become relevant evidence.

**Recording Failure != Automatic Misconduct**

**Intentional Unauthorised Recording Suppression != Evidentially Neutral**

---

## 4. Archive first, access by authority

Potentially relevant records may be preserved before their significance is known.

This produces:

**Preservation Envelope != Inspection Envelope**

A system may legitimately preserve an operational record under one function while later access requires a different function and authority.

This is particularly important for large archives containing information about participants who are not subjects of the investigation.

CIBB applies:

**Authority To Preserve != Authority To Inspect Without Bound**

**Authority To Inspect Relevant Segment != Authority To Explore Entire Archive**

---

## 5. Investigative evidential envelope

During investigation, Civil Security may access evidence within the authority generated by the legitimate investigative function and any separately required search/access authority.

The investigative question remains:

> **What happened, and which materially plausible explanation best fits the evidence?**

MRT can structure:

- supporting evidence;
- opposing evidence;
- neutral/unresolved evidence;
- dependencies;
- contradictions;
- competing actors/hypotheses;
- root-question revision.

Police access remains bounded by investigative authority.

The mere possibility that an archive might contain useful evidence does not create unlimited exploratory access.

---

## 6. Transition to prosecution and adjudication

Once the legally defined referral condition is reached and independent prosecution begins its threshold assessment, the evidential function changes.

If prosecution authority activates and the case moves toward Judiciary, additional evidence may become legitimately relevant to:

- test the referred hypothesis;
- test alternative hypotheses;
- verify or challenge witness accounts;
- test reliability and provenance;
- establish chronology;
- test claimed justification or defence;
- examine police/prosecution authority;
- evaluate material historical context;
- identify patterns relevant under Law;
- determine whether another participant may be responsible.

This may justify a broader evidential-access envelope than was necessary during initial investigation.

But:

**Later Case Stage != Unlimited Retrospective Search**

The expanded envelope derives from adjudicative relevance and lawful authority, not institutional curiosity.

---

## 7. Fiduciary symmetry

Where prosecution and defence operate as fiduciaries of the Concord rather than victory-oriented advocates, both require legitimate ability to test the evidential state.

Candidate principle:

> **Material evidence should not be structurally available to one fiduciary merely because it supports that fiduciary's assigned proposition while being unavailable to the other where legitimate examination requires equivalent access.**

Therefore:

**Prosecution Evidential Access != Proprietary Access**

**Defence Evidential Access != Secondary Access**

The exact information presented to each fiduciary may differ where CIBB, third-party privacy, safety or protected information requires bounded projection, but the system must not use those protections as a mechanism for one-sided evidential advantage.

Where full exposure is unnecessary, Judiciary or an authorised evidence function may provide a minimum-necessary projection capable of answering the legitimate evidential question.

---

## 8. Judicial evidential envelope

Judiciary may require evidence beyond the material originally relied upon by police or prosecution.

This is particularly important if Judicial MRT identifies:

- unresolved contradiction;
- hidden dependency;
- unsupported proposition;
- alternative actor;
- incomplete timeline;
- missing contextual evidence;
- evidence suggesting the root question is wrong.

The court's evidential function therefore cannot be limited to choosing between two frozen advocacy packages.

**Judiciary != Passive Selector Between Prosecution And Defence Narratives**

The Judiciary may lawfully require resolution of material evidential questions within its authority.

This does not convert Judiciary into general police investigation.

Its evidential access remains tied to adjudication of the matter before it.

---

## 9. Historical and metrics evidence

Once a matter reaches the appropriate legal stage, evidence may include relevant historical or metrics data not required during initial investigation.

Possible classes include:

- prior operational records;
- historical sensor/video records;
- system reliability metrics;
- equipment failure/error history;
- model false-positive/false-negative performance;
- recurring location/event patterns;
- prior relevant institutional incidents;
- evidence-quality metrics;
- legally relevant behavioural/event history;
- other longitudinal evidence where Law permits and relevance is established.

Such evidence must not be admitted merely because a pattern exists.

The proposition it is claimed to support must be explicit.

**Historical Correlation != Present-Case Fact**

**Metric != Individual Guilt**

**Pattern Evidence != Permission To Ignore Present Evidence**

Historical/metrics evidence may inform a proposition without replacing proof of that proposition in the current case.

---

## 10. Evidence concerning other people

Relevant evidence may concern people other than the accused.

This may include:

- witnesses;
- victims;
- officers;
- other participants;
- alternative suspects;
- co-actors;
- people whose records establish chronology/location;
- people whose activity affects reliability of another evidence source.

Third-party status does not make relevant evidence invisible.

But neither does relevance eliminate third-party rights.

Candidate rule:

> **Third-party evidence may enter the adjudicative evidential state where materially relevant, but access and disclosure remain bounded to the legitimate proposition being tested.**

Therefore:

**Not Accused != Evidentially Irrelevant**

**Potential Alternative Suspect != Permission For Unbounded Investigation**

**Third-Party Relevance != General Access To Third-Party Life**

CIBB/minimum-necessary projection should be used where possible.

---

## 11. Alternative-suspect evidence

Evidence indicating another materially plausible responsible actor is directly relevant to the accused hypothesis.

It must not be excluded from the evidential state merely because the alternative person has not been charged or formally designated a suspect.

**Evidence Another Actor May Be Responsible -> Evidence Relevant To Current Attribution**

MRT is particularly useful here.

Judicial and fiduciary examination may compare:

- accused-responsibility hypothesis;
- alternative-actor hypothesis;
- multiple-actor hypothesis;
- unknown-actor hypothesis;
- no-offence/accident/system-error hypotheses where materially plausible.

This prevents legal procedure from freezing the hypothesis space at the moment prosecution began.

---

## 12. Evidence discovery after prosecution begins

Prosecution does not close evidence discovery.

New evidence may emerge through:

- fiduciary examination;
- judicial evidential inquiry;
- archive search under new lawful authority;
- newly available historical records;
- metrics analysis;
- participant disclosure;
- third-party evidence;
- technical reanalysis;
- contradiction exposed by MRT.

New evidence feeds back into the continuing evidential state.

Therefore:

**Prosecution Initiated != Evidence State Frozen**

**Material New Evidence -> MRT / Threshold / Legal Classification Reassessment**

If new evidence collapses the prosecution threshold, prosecution authority must contract or terminate.

### Evidential reversal and investigative handback

In an extreme but important case, deeper authorised evidential access may do more than weaken the prosecution.

Example:

- the current accused was originally supported by sufficient evidence for prosecution;
- adjudicative-stage access lawfully exposes additional archived CCTV;
- the footage establishes a reliable alibi for the accused;
- the same or related footage identifies another materially plausible actor.

The correct transition may then be:

**New Evidence -> Current Accused Hypothesis Collapses -> Prosecution Threshold No Longer Met -> Prosecution Halts -> Current Accused Released From Prosecution As Law Requires**

and separately:

**Alternative-Actor Evidence -> New/Returned Civil Security Investigation -> MRT / Whole Evidential State -> Independent Referral Process If Later Warranted**

The prosecution function must not simply substitute the newly identified person into the existing prosecution.

**New Suspect != Inherited Prosecution**

The alternative actor is entitled to the same neutral investigative process, hypothesis testing, evidential development and independent threshold assessment.

Likewise, the fact that the new evidence emerged during adjudicative preparation does not require the current accused to remain under prosecution until Judiciary reaches a final acquittal where the legal basis for prosecution has already disappeared.

**Judicial Process Begun != Duty To Continue Unsupported Prosecution**

**Evidence Establishing Alibi != Merely Defence Material Where It Collapses Attribution**

**Collapsed Attribution -> Prosecution Authority Sunset**

If it indicates another responsible actor, that creates a separate or returned investigative route subject to its own authority.

---

## 13. Evidence about public authority

Archived observation is also evidence about Concord institutions.

Bodycam, CCTV, vehicle footage and system logs may test:

- police account accuracy;
- use of force;
- search scope;
- detention conditions;
- disclosure;
- evidence handling;
- instruction provenance;
- timing of authority activation/sunset.

The institutional actor does not own the evidential interpretation of its own records.

**Police Record != Police-Controlled Truth**

Where prosecution conduct is later material, its own decision/provenance records may similarly become reviewable under appropriate authority.

---

## 14. Privacy and evidential minimisation

A high-recording civilisation creates substantial privacy risk if preservation becomes universal inspectability.

The architecture therefore requires layered control:

**Record -> Preserve -> Protect -> Index/Locate Under Authority -> Minimum Necessary Access -> Evidential Use -> Audit**

Possible protections include:

- encryption;
- access logging;
- role/purpose separation;
- judicial authorisation for deeper access;
- redaction;
- masking;
- bounded projections;
- third-party anonymisation where identity is unnecessary;
- time/subject/query bounds;
- independent audit;
- retention/sunset rules.

The detailed technical implementation remains for CIBB, Historical, Civil Security and Judiciary development.

---

## 15. Judicial MRT interface

Judicial MRT can consume the expanded evidential state while preserving proposition-level discipline.

Candidate structure:

**Adjudicative Question**  
-> **Material Legal/Factual Propositions**  
-> **Mirrored Supporting/Opposing Structures**  
-> **Evidence + Provenance + Dependencies + Contradictions**  
-> **Alternative/Coexisting Hypotheses**  
-> **Historical/Metrics Evidence Where Material**  
-> **Third-Party/Alternative-Suspect Evidence Where Material**  
-> **Root-Question/Hypothesis-Space Review**  
-> **Applicable Adjudicative Standard**  
-> **Judicial Finding**

The judicial tree is not merely a copy of police or prosecution MRT.

**Investigative Classification != Judicial Classification**

**Prosecutorial Classification != Judicial Classification**

## 17. Development conclusion

The Concord's evidential architecture should exploit the fact that a highly instrumented civil system can preserve substantially more contemporaneous truth-bearing information than historical justice systems.

But the correct architecture is not universal surveillance access.

It is:

**Broad Legitimate Preservation + Narrow Stage-Appropriate Access + Increasing Evidential Depth Where Lawfully Required + Fiduciary Symmetry + Independent Judicial Control + CIBB Protection**

The progression is:

**Event / Police Recording**  
-> **Protected Evidential Archive**  
-> **Bounded Investigative Access**  
-> **Whole Evidential State / MRT**  
-> **Referral / Prosecution**  
-> **Expanded Adjudicative Evidential Envelope Where Materially Required**  
-> **Prosecution + Defence Fiduciary Examination**  
-> **Judicial MRT / Independent Adjudication**

Evidence concerning another person, including another potential suspect, remains available where materially relevant to the question before the system. It is neither excluded merely because that person is outside the current prosecution nor made universally accessible merely because some relevance can be asserted.

This architecture should next be integrated with the Judiciary fiduciary model and tested against privacy, alternative-suspect, missing-recording and historical-pattern scenarios.
