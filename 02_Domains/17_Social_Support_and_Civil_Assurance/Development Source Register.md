## Identified development material

### Participant Support and Autonomy Architecture

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Layer_Zero_and_Minus_Zero/Candidate_Architecture/CA-01 Participant Support and Autonomy Architecture.md`

This is the strongest currently identified direct architectural seed.

Potentially reusable architecture:
- support should preserve autonomy rather than convert need into general authority;
- refusal of support should not automatically escalate authority;
- support-system exit may be evidence of success;
- support should continue under failure of predictive or metrics systems;
- support institutions require anti-capture boundaries;
- vulnerability indicators require contextual examination;
- cross-substrate support must be possible.

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Layer_Zero_and_Minus_Zero/Source_Systems/00_SYNTHESIS_AND_SOURCE_DOCUMENTS/Systems Extracted from the Layer-Zero and Layer −0 Development Cycle.md`

Directly relevant extracted systems:
- Handrail Civil Support Architecture;
- Supported Decision-Making and Capacity Architecture;
- predictive support;
- trajectory analysis;
- case learning;
- privacy-bounded support information;
- exceptional-access safeguards.

The source explicitly prioritises developing participant-protective architecture before architecture capable of increasing institutional power over participants.

### Adversarial evidence

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Evidence/Layer_Zero_Development/Adversarial Failure Audit — Six Candidate Architectures from the Layer-Zero - Layer −0 Development Cycle.md`

Relevant findings:
- CA-01 survived first-stage adversarial examination with strengthened boundaries;
- support institution capture is a recognised failure mode;
- support must not become surveillance;
- participant refusal is not itself sufficient justification for escalation;
- false-positive vulnerability requires protection;
- resource scarcity and emergency conditions must not erase autonomy safeguards.

### Candidate principles relevant to support

**Source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Evidence/Layer_Zero_Development/4 Adversarial Critical Examination — 12 Candidate Principles from the Layer-Zero - Layer −0 Development Cycle.md`

Potentially reusable candidate constraints include evidence-sensitive treatment of outliers/failures, contextual treatment of indicators, purpose limitation and evidence-conditional adoption.

## Current development implication

This domain should not be treated as architecturally empty. It has a substantial candidate architecture and adversarial evidence base already present in Research. The next development pass should determine what can be adapted/promoted into a domain-specific Social Support architecture and what remains research-only.

## Cross-domain and portable mechanisms available for adaptation

### Civil Attention

**Source:** `04_Portable_Modules/Civil Attention Reporting Petition and Resolution Module v1.0.md`

Provides an existing participant-triggered intake, receipt, routing, resolution and feedback architecture suitable for support requests and reports. Domain-specific companion methodology can add support triage and safeguards without rewriting the core intake method.

### Contextual Wrapper Architecture

**Source:** `04_Portable_Modules/Contextual Wrapper Architecture — Portable Module.md`

Useful for support settings, protected information, role-specific access and bounded support permissions. Its rule that bounded permission should be used before manufacturing authority aligns closely with CA-01's autonomy-preserving support architecture.
