# Participant Support and Autonomy — Existing Architecture Source Resolution 001

**Project:** The Concord Framework  
**Date:** 6 October 2026  
**Domain:** Social Support and Civil Assurance  
**Status:** SOURCE RESOLUTION / LIVING-RESULT MIGRATION BASIS / NON-CANONICAL

## 1. Question

The V1.3 Social Support and Civil Assurance domain is structurally established but almost operationally empty. Before developing new architecture, this source resolution asks whether the required living result already exists elsewhere in V1.3.

## 2. Source located

Research contains a substantial Layer-Zero / Layer −0 development lineage originating in the homelessness systems-failure study.

The central consolidated result is:

02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Layer_Zero_and_Minus_Zero/Candidate_Architecture/CA-01 Participant Support and Autonomy Architecture.md

Its component lineage includes the Layer-Zero Systems Interface, Predictive Support System, Handrail Civil Support Architecture, Supported Decision-Making and Capacity Architecture, and Support-to-Investigation Firewall.

Research evidence includes Candidate Architecture extraction, consolidation/dependency and adversarial failure audits, candidate-principle audits, and the methodological case study derived from homelessness.

## 3. Source-resolution result

The Social Support domain does **not** require a new architecture invented from scratch.

CA-01 already answers the domain's primary operational question:

> **How should civilisation connect actual participant conditions to useful support while preserving the greatest practicable autonomy?**

The Research lineage should remain in Research as developmental provenance/evidence. The living operational result should be represented in Social Support and Civil Assurance.

This follows:

> **Research Develops; Domains Operate; Historical Remembers.**

and:

> **Migrate The Living Result; Archive The Developmental Path.**

## 4. Validated boundaries recovered from Research

The adversarial lineage established several mandatory boundaries:

1. support-system exit can be a success;
2. Support-to-Investigation Firewall is required;
3. refusal must remain separate from incapacity, wrongdoing and dangerousness;
4. emergency escalation requires independent authority;
5. prediction may expand options but does not grant authority;
6. support must remain possible without predictive systems;
7. scarcity must distinguish nominal service existence from practical accessibility;
8. substrate-specific wellbeing/support assumptions require caution.

These are part of the surviving living result.

## 5. Domain boundary

Social Support owns accessibility of legitimate support, the support relationship, voluntary assistance, participant-facing coordination, support progression, autonomy restoration, support exit, and visibility of unmet legitimate need.

It does not own Health diagnosis/treatment, Education, employment markets, housing/infrastructure construction, policing/investigation, Judiciary, general participant-information governance, emergency authority, or civil metrics as a whole. It interfaces with those domains.

## 6. No forced central welfare institution

Nothing in the source architecture requires all support to be delivered by one central institution. Support can arise through participants, families/communities, voluntary organisations, enterprises, domain services, local civil structures and Concord services.

The civil-assurance function is the backstop that legitimate support need is not simply abandoned because voluntary/local routes fail.

## 7. Migration disposition

**Disposition: REPRESENT LIVING RESULT IN DOMAIN; RETAIN RESEARCH LINEAGE IN PLACE.**

Do not delete, move or rewrite the Research source corpus. Do not mark the architecture canonical. The domain result remains provisional and should continue to expose unresolved questions and validation needs.

## 8. Central conclusion

> **Social Support and Civil Assurance is not an undeveloped blank domain; V1.3 already contains a substantial support architecture whose living result had not yet been expressed in its operational domain.**
