**Status:** CURRENT OPEN-QUESTION REGISTER / ACTIVE RESEARCH MAP / NOT CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE

---

# 1. Purpose and Scope

This document is the **current V1.2 Open Questions register**.

It replaces the need to use the many dated V1.1 audits, reconciliations, closure audits and question-update documents in this folder as though they were simultaneous descriptions of the present state.

Those earlier files are temporal research records. Their full developmental provenance is already preserved in frozen Concord V1.1 and does not need to be duplicated in the active V1.2 question register.

This register therefore preserves only the provenance needed to understand:

- which area of Concord work exposed the question;
- why the question matters;
- its current research state;
- what remains unresolved;
- and, where useful, the next form of testing or development required.

It deliberately does **not** preserve the full history of every status change.

The controlling distinction is:

> **Historical audit record ≠ current open-question state.**

And:

> **Architecture is not proof. A question may be architecturally addressed while remaining empirically open.**

---

# 2. Current Status Language

The following labels are used where useful:

- **FOUNDATIONAL OPEN** — the underlying problem remains unresolved.
- **MAJOR OPEN PROGRAMME** — substantial work exists but the domain remains materially incomplete.
- **ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT** — current architecture or hypotheses are still being developed.
- **PARTIALLY ADDRESSED** — important components are understood but the question remains materially unresolved.
- **ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED / VALIDATION OPEN** — a candidate architecture exists; testing, falsification or empirical validation remains necessary.
- **READY FOR TESTING** — the main next step should be experimental, adversarial, comparative or operational rather than additional conceptual expansion.
- **METHODOLOGICALLY DECOMPOSED / EMPIRICAL VALIDATION OPEN** — the problem has become testable but has not yet been empirically resolved.
- **PERMANENT ADVERSARIAL QUESTION** — the question should remain available as a continuing attack on the Concord rather than being designed to close permanently.

Closed premises and retired broader questions are not included as active questions except where needed to prevent an obsolete question being accidentally reopened.

# 3. Foundational Questions

## FQ-01 — Does the Concord Actually Work?

> **Can a civilisation based substantially upon Concordian principles and mechanisms produce better outcomes than realistic alternatives across long periods and under adverse conditions?**

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL OPEN / EMPIRICAL VALIDATION REQUIRED  
**Why it matters:** This is the central V1.2 question. Internal coherence, simulation, AI agreement and architectural completeness cannot answer it.  
**Arises from:** V1.2 transition; full Concord architecture; comparative analysis; simulation; empirical validation; Ratchet and correction architecture.  
**Needs:** comparative testing, simulations, bounded pilots where ethical, long-horizon modelling, hostile cases, external criticism and observed outcomes.

## FQ-02 — Ethical Kernel Sufficiency and Minimality

> **Are the present eight Ethical Kernel laws sufficient, and are all eight actually necessary?**

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL OPEN / ABLATION AND GENERATIVE TESTING REQUIRED  
**Why it matters:** V1.1 showed that a large candidate architecture can be generated from the current Kernel. It did not demonstrate that eight is the minimum, that none are redundant, or that important moral categories are absent.  
**Arises from:** Ethical Kernel; Unified Paths; Candidate Principles; V1.2 README founding-question reassessment.  
**Needs:** ablation testing, reconstruction from reduced kernels, unknown-category attacks, cross-substrate testing and comparison with alternative ethical foundations.

## FQ-03 — Long-Term Stability and Longevity

> **Will Concordian principles and institutions remain stable, corrigible and rights-preserving over decades, centuries and major civilisational change?**

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL OPEN  
**Why it matters:** Architectural possibility is not evidence of long-term survival. Semantic drift, institutional adaptation and changing participants may alter the system in ways short-term tests cannot reveal.  
**Arises from:** Recursive Constitutional Architecture; Constitutional Clock; Ratchet; continuity; civilisational regeneration.  
**Needs:** long-horizon simulation, historical comparison, drift modelling, regeneration tests and institutional succession scenarios.

## FQ-04 — Founding and Constituent Legitimacy

> **How does a Concord polity acquire legitimate founding authority without assuming the very authority it seeks to create?**

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL OPEN / ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT  
**Why it matters:** A coherent constitution does not by itself establish who may legitimately found, ratify, join, bind or represent a polity.  
**Arises from:** Concord Nation Foundation; constituency and consent; territory and digital jurisdiction; ratification; citizenship; transition to polity; external legal wrappers.  
**Needs:** explicit constituent model, consent and non-participant boundary analysis, secession/fork testing, recognition rules and anti-capture stress testing.

## FQ-05 — Foundational Failure, Replacement and Regeneration

> **What happens when a foundational Concord mechanism, institution or interpretation fails rather than merely needing ordinary correction?**

**Status:** MAJOR OPEN / REGENERATION HYPOTHESIS EXISTS  
**Why it matters:** Corrigibility requires a route for replacing failed implementations without making institutional continuity the supreme value.  
**Arises from:** Participant Primacy; Institutional Non-Self-Primacy; Recursive Oversight; Ratchet; civilisational regeneration; Ethical Seed concept.  
**Needs:** formal failure thresholds, replacement procedures, continuity boundaries, legitimacy of successor structures and testing of the hypothesis **Ethical Seed → Civilisational Regeneration**.

## FQ-06 — Civilisational Complexity and Governance Latency

> **Can the Concord remain understandable and usable without creating an expert procedural class whose command of the architecture becomes informal power?**

**Status:** ACTIVE SYSTEMIC RISK / HIGH PRIORITY  
**Why it matters:** A system intended to distribute power can recreate hierarchy through complexity, interpretation cost and procedural dependency.  
**Arises from:** full-system integration, KCS, repository development, governance, Recursive Oversight, V1.2 simplification programme.  
**Needs:** complexity metrics, interface tests, participant comprehension testing, governance-latency measurement, simplification and removal trials.

## FQ-07 — Validation Thresholds

> **What level and kind of evidence is sufficient to move an idea, principle or architecture toward implementation?**

**Status:** PRINCIPLE-LEVEL ANSWER EXISTS / FORMAL THRESHOLDS OPEN  
**Why it matters:** “Evidence conditional” is a useful rule but does not determine replication requirements, field evidence, acceptable simulation evidence or how to act under conflicting evidence.  
**Arises from:** Adoption Must Remain Evidence-Conditional; Candidate Principle/Architecture workflows; research methodology.  
**Needs:** domain-sensitive evidence thresholds, replication rules, uncertainty handling and explicit reopening/demotion procedures.

# 17. Important Questions No Longer Treated as Open in Their Old Form

The following should not be reintroduced as broad unresolved questions unless new evidence invalidates their present disposition:

- **Metaphysical personal identity:** outside required Concordian civil scope; use civil identity, provenance, verification and succession instead.

- **Does duplicated historical identity evidence prove fraud?** No.

- **Does preference-like behaviour alone prove subjective preference or sentience?** No.

- **Is participant contradiction during emergency necessarily mere disobedience?** No; it may contain relevant evidence.
- **Can aggregate normality alone invalidate credible evidence of serious local or minority harm?** No.
- **Does geographical agreement necessarily prove epistemic independence?** No.

These are retained here only as **anti-regression notes**, not as active research questions.

# 19. Current Research Posture

The V1.2 Open Questions programme should increasingly ask four things of every major unresolved problem:

1. **Is architecture still missing?**
2. **If architecture exists, what would falsify it?**
3. **What evidence would change our confidence?**
4. **What would justify simplifying, replacing or removing the mechanism?**

The governing research posture remains:

> **Keep what survives. Correct what fails. Remove what is unnecessary. Record what is learned. Test again.**

And:

> **An unresolved contradiction is preferable to a false solution.**

The purpose of this register is therefore not to make the Concord appear complete.

It is to ensure that the remaining uncertainty is visible at the correct layer, in a form that can guide real research rather than merely preserve an accumulation of historical question lists.
