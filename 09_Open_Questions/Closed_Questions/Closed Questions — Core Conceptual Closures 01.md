# Closed Questions — Core Conceptual Closures 01

**Status:** CLOSED-QUESTION RECOVERY / CURRENT DISPOSITION MAP / NOT CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE  
**Framework version:** Concord V1.2

This file records a first recovered subset of questions whose original form is no longer treated as open.

Closure does not imply that an entire domain is empirically validated. It means that the specific proposition, premise, scope question, or architecture-generation question recorded here has reached a different research state.

The closed classes used here are:

- **CLOSED — ANSWERED**
- **CLOSED — OUT OF CONCORDIAN CIVIL SCOPE**

- **CLOSED — RETIRED / DECOMPOSED BROADER QUESTION**

The last class means that the architecture-generation question is no longer open, while testing, falsification, implementation or empirical validation remains open.

## CQ-01 — Must the Concord Determine Metaphysical Personal Identity?

**Disposition:** CLOSED — OUT OF CONCORDIAN CIVIL SCOPE

The Concord need not determine which theory of metaphysical personal identity is true in order to perform legitimate civil functions.

Civil consequences can instead be represented through narrower objects including current participant recognition, civil identity, provenance, verification, consent, rights, obligations and succession.

A participant's internal conception of self is presumptively protected as autonomy and self-expression rather than being replaced by a compulsory state metaphysics.

**Reopening condition:** reopen only if a legitimate civil function is demonstrated that cannot be performed using the narrower civil objects, genuinely requires a metaphysical identity theory, and cannot be achieved through a less autonomy-intrusive representation.

**Arises from:** Identity Development; Identity Closure Audit; civil identity, succession and verification work.

## CQ-02 — Does an Emergency Itself Create Legitimate Authority?

**Disposition:** CLOSED — ANSWERED: NO

Emergency is a condition under which previously legitimate bounded powers may activate. It is not itself a source of legitimacy.

The relevant distinction is:

**Legitimate institution ≠ legitimate activation ≠ legitimate action.**

The current conceptual relation is:

**Emergency authority = prior legitimate authority + valid activation conditions + constitutional constraints + necessity + proportionality.**

Operational questions remain open concerning evidence, discretion, communications failure, compulsion thresholds, termination, residual duties and extreme cases.

**Arises from:** Constitutional Emergency; Peaceful Heterogeneity emergency work; Bounded Contextual Authority.

## CQ-03 — Does a Directive Necessarily Imply Compulsion or Criminalisation?

**Disposition:** CLOSED — ANSWERED: NO

The Concord now distinguishes advisory, directive and compulsory states.

**Directive ≠ compulsion ≠ criminalisation.**

A directive may create a strong presumption of compliance while preserving contextual deviation, explanation and later review. Escalation requires separate authority and justification.

Open questions remain about when compulsion becomes justified and how deviation, obstruction, incapacity and sanction should be treated.

**Arises from:** Governance refusal; emergency architecture; Peaceful Heterogeneity.

## CQ-04 — Does Duplicate Historical Identity Evidence Necessarily Imply Fraud?

**Disposition:** CLOSED — ANSWERED: NO

Legitimate duplication can produce multiple current participants possessing equally authentic evidence of the same historical identity.

Therefore:

**Duplicate historical evidence ≠ fraud.**

and:

**Authentication success ≠ unique current participant identity.**

The replacement question is how current civil identity should be verified when historical evidence itself can be legitimately duplicated.

**Arises from:** civil identity and identity-verification development.

## CQ-05 — Does Provenance Determine Current Identity, Ownership or Liability?

**Disposition:** CLOSED — ANSWERED: NO

Provenance records what happened. It may supply evidence to later adjudication but does not itself determine self-conception, current civil identity, ownership or liability.

Therefore:

**Provenance ≠ identity judgement.**

**Shared provenance ≠ shared ownership.**

**Shared provenance ≠ shared liability.**

**Arises from:** provenance, identity, continuity, ownership and responsibility work.

## CQ-06 — Does Preference-Like Behaviour Prove Subjective Preference or Sentience?

**Disposition:** CLOSED — REJECTED PREMISE / INVALID INFERENCE

Preference-like behaviour alone does not establish subjective preference or sentience.

The Concord distinguishes observed behaviour, functional aversion and subjective experience.

This closure does not answer whether any particular AI or other intelligence is sentient. The sentience domain remains open.

**Arises from:** Participation and Intelligence; artificial preference; sentience research.

## CQ-07 — Is Clock Time Inherently a Substrate-Neutral Measure of Punitive Burden?

**Disposition:** CLOSED — REJECTED PREMISE: NO

Clock time and experienced time are not necessarily equivalent across substrates.

Therefore identical sentence duration cannot be assumed to create equivalent punitive burden.

The replacement question concerns what legitimate consequences remain proportionate across participants with radically different temporal experience, embodiment, values and restoration possibilities.

**Arises from:** cross-substrate jurisprudence; Judiciary; equality/equivalence work.

## CQ-08 — Is Participant Contradiction During an Emergency Necessarily Mere Disobedience?

**Disposition:** CLOSED — ANSWERED: NO

Participant contradiction can contain locally available evidence that higher-level authority lacks.

Contradiction should therefore be capable of entering an upward reality-correction path rather than automatically producing punishment.

This does not create a general right to ignore legitimate instructions.

**Arises from:** emergency architecture; local evidence versus higher-level instruction; Peaceful Heterogeneity.

---

## CQ-09 — Can Higher-Level Aggregate Normality Alone Invalidate Credible Evidence of Serious Local or Minority Harm?

**Disposition:** CLOSED — ANSWERED: NO

Aggregate normality does not establish absence of serious local harm.

Credible local anomalies should instead trigger appropriate resolution change and investigation.

This does not establish that every anomaly is true. It establishes that averaging alone is insufficient to dismiss it.

**Arises from:** Size 9 Shoe Problem; GTP; Triadic Epistemics; aggregation work.

---

## CQ-10 — Does Agreement Across Geographically Distributed Regions Necessarily Constitute Independent Confirmation?

**Disposition:** CLOSED — ANSWERED: NO

Geographical distribution is not equivalent to epistemic independence.

Multiple regions may share the same models, sensors, software, infrastructure, assumptions, definitions, institutions or incentives and therefore share a common-mode failure.

The relevant question becomes: independent with respect to which plausible failure?

**Arises from:** GTP; Triadic Epistemics; Independence of AI Evidence; epistemic-mesh work.
