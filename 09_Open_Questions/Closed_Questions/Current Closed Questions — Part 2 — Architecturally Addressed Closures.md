# 1. Meaning of Architecturally Addressed

For this register:

> **ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED is a distinct class of closed question.**

It means the question:

> **“What architecture could address this problem?”**

has a sufficiently developed candidate answer that continuing to list the problem as a blank architectural unknown is misleading.

It does **not** mean:

- the mechanism is proven;
- the mechanism is empirically validated;
- the mechanism is implementation-ready;
- the mechanism is immune to capture;
- the mechanism is optimal;
- or the wider domain is complete.

The remaining question normally becomes:

> **Does this candidate architecture actually work, survive attack, outperform realistic alternatives, and remain legitimate under real conditions?**

Thus:

`Architecture Generation Closed ≠ Validation Closed`

# 2. Constitutional and Governance Architecture

## ACQ-01 — Constitutional Amendment and Constitutional Change

**Disposition:** ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED — VALIDATION OPEN.  
**Current architecture:** Recursive Constitutional Architecture separates Ethical Kernel, constitutional principle, constraint, mechanism, architecture, implementation and operation. It further separates learning from amendment, evidence from authority, convergence from legitimacy and operational correction from constitutional rewriting.

Key constraint:

> **Recursion must never manufacture authority.**

and:

> **Convergence is evidence. It is not authority.**

**Validation still open:** capture resistance, amendment thresholds, adversarial behaviour, institution interaction, semantic drift, long-term constitutional identity and comparison with alternative amendment systems.

**Arises from:** Recursive Constitutional Architecture V2; Constitutional Core; Recursive Oversight.

---

## ACQ-02 — Stable Principles Versus Adaptive Implementation

**Disposition:** ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED AS METHODOLOGY — CONSISTENT APPLICATION OPEN.  
**Current architecture:** Concord development now distinguishes:

`Idea → Principle → Constraint → Mechanism → Architecture → Implementation → Operation`

with evidence and validation kept separate.

This reduces the risk that a temporary mechanism silently acquires constitutional status.

**Validation still open:** consistent use across the corpus and resistance to status drift.

## ACQ-03 — Emergency Constitutional Authority

**Disposition:** ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED — ADVERSARIAL VALIDATION OPEN.  
**Current architecture:** emergencies alter operational priorities without becoming a new source of sovereignty. Candidate safeguards include bounded activation, temporary delegation, temporary secrecy where justified, resource concentration, accountability, recovery, continuity, constitutional oversight and return to ordinary governance.

Foundational proposition:

> **Emergencies change operational priorities. They do not change constitutional principles.**

**Validation still open:** war, pandemics, infrastructure failure, AI incidents, ecological catastrophe, communications failure, false emergency declarations and anti-ratchet effectiveness.

# 3. Authority, Functional Need and Civil Epistemics

## ACQ-08 — General Basis of Civil Authority

**Disposition:** ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED AT GENERAL PRINCIPLE LEVEL — DIRECT FALSIFICATION OPEN.  
**Current architecture:**

`Ethical Kernel → Rights → Rights-Relevant Problem → Legitimate Civil Objective → Legitimate Function → Functional Need → Minimum Proportionate Authority → Bounded Contextual Authority`

Direction of justification:

`Legitimacy → Function → Need → Authority`

not:

`Authority → Function → Legitimacy`

Important negative propositions now established architecturally:

- Authority existing does not prove a function legitimate.
- Authority existing does not prove authority necessary.
- Past authority does not prove present need.
- Technical capability is not civil authority.
- Role is not permanent universal privilege.

**Validation still open:** legitimate function, evidence thresholds, authority necessity, minimum proportionate authority, current justification, meta-authority and aggregate-power effects.

---

## ACQ-09 — Functional Need Evidence Architecture

**Disposition:** ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED — READY FOR ADVERSARIAL AND EMPIRICAL TESTING.  
**Current architecture:** plural observation, participant-derived evidence, formal Metrics, provenance, confidence/uncertainty, dependency-aware independence, contestability, minority findings, Triadic Epistemics, GTP comparison, temporal comparison, specialised inquiry and outcome calibration.

The question is no longer how evidence for functional need could be structured.

The remaining question is whether it reliably distinguishes genuine need from measurement error, institutional interest, manufactured problems, correlated evidence and politically convenient interpretation.

# 4. Civil Space, Privacy and Heterogeneity

## ACQ-14 — Broad Civil-Space Architecture

**Disposition:** ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED — CLASSIFICATION VALIDATION OPEN.  
**Current architecture:**

`Civil Space → Public / Protected / Restricted`

with Unclassified/Transitional treated as a temporary epistemic or administrative state rather than an ordinary fourth class.

Key distinctions:

`Access Class ≠ Ownership ≠ Provision ≠ Purpose ≠ Current State`

`Freedom of Movement ≠ Universal Right of Entry`

Restricted Space is a category of justified conditional access, not inherently exclusion.

**Validation still open:** classification reliability, nested spaces, dynamic states, hazards, digital/hybrid spaces and classification capture.

---

## ACQ-15 — Protected-Space Functional Access

**Disposition:** PARTIALLY ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED / INCLUDED IN ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED CLASS.  
**Current architecture:**

`Permission to Enter ≠ Permission to Observe Without Limit ≠ Permission to Record ≠ Permission to Use ≠ Permission to Disclose`

and:

`Legitimate Entry ≠ Loss of Protected Status`

Contextual Authorisation provides a candidate form:

`Default Rule + Valid Context + Valid Authorisation → Contextual Permission`

**Validation still open:** incidental observation, disclosure, retention after access ends, digital access, coercive consent and evidence of authorisation.

---

## ACQ-16 — Peaceful Heterogeneity as a Broad Architecture Question

**Disposition:** FOUNDATIONAL DECOMPOSITION ACHIEVED / MULTIPLE CANDIDATE ARCHITECTURES EXIST — VALIDATION OPEN.  
**Current architecture includes:**

`Difference ≠ Harm`

`Offence ≠ Harm`

`Permissible Activity ≠ Permissible Everywhere`

`Preference_A(Avoid X) ≠ Compel_B(Stop X)`

and a presumption of self-stewardship where hazard information, capacity and voluntary choice are present.

The old blank question — how can stable civilisation tolerate persistent value and behavioural heterogeneity without enforced conformity — is no longer architecturally unanswered.

**Validation still open:** externality thresholds, accommodation burden, genuine incompatibility, cultural differences, systemic inequality and cross-cultural testing.

# 5. Evidence, Validation and Research Infrastructure

## ACQ-17 — Evidence and Validation Architecture

**Disposition:** ARCHITECTURALLY SUBSTANTIALLY ADDRESSED — EMPIRICAL VALIDATION REMAINS A MAJOR OPEN PROGRAMME.  
**Current architecture:** Concord work distinguishes evidence classes and increasingly uses a chain such as:

`Claim → Evidence Type → Source Record → Observation → Interpretation → Limitation → Competing Explanation → Required Next Test → Updated Claim`

Evidence-state classifications distinguish conceptual work from controlled, replicated, field and longitudinal evidence.

**Closed architectural question:** the Concord no longer lacks a general method for asking what evidence a claim has.

**Still open:** whether enough high-quality evidence exists for any specific architecture, especially the civilisation as a whole.

# 8. Methodological Boundary — Internal Experience and Civil Provision

## ACQ-25 — Must civil architecture settle uncertain internal subjective experience before providing options or protections?

**Disposition:** METHODOLOGICAL BOUNDARY SUBSTANTIALLY ADDRESSED / ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED CLASS.  
**Current conclusion:**

`Question Worth Investigating ≠ Question Civilisation Must Settle`

`Internal Experience Question ≠ Civil Architecture Question`

The Concord may preserve reasonable civil options under uncertainty without asserting that a participant definitely experiences privacy need, leisure, home, boredom or other human-like internal states.

Examples:

`Right to Privacy ≠ Assertion of Privacy Need`

`Opportunity for Leisure ≠ Assertion of Leisure Experience`

`Protected Safe Space ≠ Assertion of Human-Like Home Need`

**Validation still open:** actual artificial subjective experience, flourishing, temporal experience and substrate-specific needs.

# 9. Anti-Regression Rule for Architecturally Addressed Questions

The questions in this file should not automatically return to **OPEN — ARCHITECTURE MISSING** merely because their validation remains incomplete.

Their correct state is generally:

`ARCHITECTURALLY ADDRESSED → READY FOR TESTING → EVIDENCE ACCUMULATING → SUPPORTED / REVISED / REJECTED`

If testing fails, the architecture may be demoted, revised or rejected. Failure does not erase the fact that an architecture existed; it records that the architecture did not survive reality.

Therefore:

> **Architecturally addressed is a closed class for the architecture-generation question, not a claim of empirical truth.**
