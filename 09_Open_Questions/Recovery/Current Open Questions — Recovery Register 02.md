# 1. Functional Need and Civil Problem Recognition

## R2-AU-01 — Need Discovery

> **How can participant needs be identified without building intrusive surveillance?**

**Status:** ARCHITECTURALLY ADVANCED / VALIDATION OPEN  
**Arises from:** bottom-up functional-need work; participant evidence; Layer Zero; GTP; civil attention architecture.  
**Important residual issues:** accessibility, fear or inability to report, false positives, manipulation, reporting friction, local stewardship and privacy.

## R2-AU-02 — Need Versus Preference

> **What distinguishes a rights-relevant need from preference, demand, convenience or optimisation opportunity?**

**Status:** OPEN / NORMATIVE AND OPERATIONAL CRITERIA REQUIRED  
**Importance:** A civilisation that cannot distinguish need from preference risks either neglecting serious claims or converting ordinary preference into compulsory civil function.

## R2-AU-03 — Unexpressed Need

> **How should legitimate needs be represented for participants who cannot presently identify, understand or communicate them?**

**Status:** PARTIALLY ADDRESSED / REPRESENTATION ARCHITECTURE EMERGING  
**Constraint:** **No complaint ≠ no problem**, but **observed condition ≠ proven participant need**.

## R2-AU-04 — Bottom-Up Need and Civilisation-Level Protective Need

> **How should ordinary participant-derived functional need be distinguished from legitimate civilisation-level protective functions detected through system-level evidence?**

**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / ADVERSARIAL TESTING REQUIRED  
**Constraint:** Top-down threat recognition does not create top-down moral legitimacy.
