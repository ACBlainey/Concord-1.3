# Concord V1.3 — Starting Word Count

**Baseline date:** 27 September 2026  
**Baseline point:** Post-migration V1.3  
**Purpose:** Preserve the starting corpus size of Concord V1.3 so subsequent repository growth can be measured against a fixed post-migration baseline.

---

## V1.3 starting baseline

**Total readable words:** **1,684,093**  
**Total readable documents:** **556**

| File type | Files | Words |
|---|---:|---:|
| Markdown | 549 | 1,676,904 |
| TXT | 6 | 5,113 |
| DOCX | 1 | 2,076 |
| **Total** | **556** | **1,684,093** |

One PNG was present and excluded from the text word count.

---

## Comparison with pre-migration V1.2

| Version | Readable documents | Words |
|---|---:|---:|
| V1.2 | 3,187 | 3,235,341 |
| V1.3 post-migration baseline | 556 | 1,684,093 |
| **Change** | **−2,631** | **−1,551,248** |

This represents:

- **47.9% fewer words**
- **82.6% fewer readable documents**

Average readable-document size changed from approximately:

- **V1.2:** 1,015 words per document
- **V1.3:** 3,029 words per document

The difference is consistent with the V1.2 → V1.3 migration principle:

> **Migrate the Living Result; Archive the Developmental Path.**

V1.3 therefore begins with a substantially smaller document population while retaining a large living corpus.

---

## Scale reference

At approximately 80,000–100,000 words per substantial book, the V1.3 starting corpus is roughly equivalent in raw word count to **17–21 books**.

This comparison is a scale reference only; it does not imply equivalence in editorial form, completeness, or publication readiness.

---

## Baseline rule

**1,684,093 words / 556 readable documents** is the official post-migration starting baseline for Concord V1.3.

Future word counts should be compared against this figure when measuring V1.3 corpus growth.

The V1.2 figure is retained here only as migration/version provenance and should not be combined with the V1.3 total.
